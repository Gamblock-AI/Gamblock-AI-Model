How Crazy Time Works — Game Mechanics

Three moving parts define the game: the wheel, the Top Slot, and the betting system.

The Wheel of Fortune — 54 Segments

The wheel is split into 54 segments. Roughly two thirds are numbers — the rest are the four bonus games. Number 1 dominates with 21 segments (38.89%). The Crazy Time bonus takes up just one segment (1.85%). Every spin is independent of the one before it.

Top Slot and Crazy Time Multipliers

Two reels spin above the wheel: that is the Top Slot. On every round, they assign a random multiplier to a specific segment. If there is a horizontal match — the Top Slot symbol lines up with the wheel result — the multiplier is applied to the win. Up to 50x extra before the bonus round even begins.

How to Bet on Crazy Time

At the start of each round, you get 15 seconds to place your bets. You can choose one or more segments: numbers 1, 2, 5, 10 or any of the four bonus games. The minimum stake is £0.10 and the maximum is £5,000.

You can place multiple bets at the same time. If the wheel lands on your number, you win your stake multiplied by that value. If it lands on a bonus you backed, you enter the bonus round. No bet on that segment? You can watch, but you are not in the game.

Top Slot — Random Multiplier
