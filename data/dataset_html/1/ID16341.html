How to download a YouTube video?

  1. Go to YouTube and search for a video you would like to download. Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar. Please note: A valid video URL has the following format: youtube.com/watch?v=KMU0tzLwhbE
  2. Paste the video URL into our converter above and select a download format. You can choose between MP3 or MP4. If no format is selected, the video will be converted to an MP3 file by default. Click the „Convert” button.
  3. The conversion process will start. Depending on the length of the video, this may take some time. Once the conversion is finished successfully, you can download the converted file by clicking the „Download” button.
