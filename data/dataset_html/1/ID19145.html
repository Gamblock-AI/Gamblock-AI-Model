What is Asianhandicap?

All soccer tips will be based in asianhandicap. With asian handicap betting you eliminate the possibility of a draw in a soccer game. The underdog will be given a head start of a handicap, and the favourite will be handicapped by the same handicap. The predetermined number of handicap will be added to the real number of goals  and make up the right bet.
