Why you should buy Overwatch:

  • You like fast-paced shooters,
  • You're looking for a game with a vast range of colorful characters,
  • You want to join thousands of players.

Overwatch is a fast-paced hero shooter in which two 6-player teams fight against each other. You'll learn the basics thanks to the tutorial and Practice Range that let you test different abilities. It's also the best way to get familiar with a new hero. After training, you're ready to jump into action and pick one of the numerous exciting game modes, such as Arcade, Competetive Play, or Custom Game. 

Chose your hero

Overwatch allows you to choose from over 30 diverse heroes divided into three classes. Become a tank, damage, or support. Use your character's unique abilities to help your team dominate the battlefield and win! Each character has not only individual skills but also a reach backstory. For instance, you can play as super-intelligent gorilla Winston, a brilliant scientist Mercy, or ruthless gang leader Ashe, to name only a few. 

Master your skills

With every match, you will sharpen your skills, gain levels and unlock precious items that allow you to customize your heroes to make them look unique and stand out on the battleground. 

What is Overwatch?

In the game's universe, Overwatch is a multinational task force. Its main purpose is to maintain world peace after the global struggle known as the Omnic Crisis.
