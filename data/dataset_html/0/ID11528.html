Discover a paradise of your own with The Samaya Bali

With two luxurious properties that showcase the best of the island of the gods, The Samaya Bali allows you to experience both the classic and modern side of paradise. Escape to the hills of Ubud at The Samaya Ubud and reconnect with nature while exploring the vast gardens and tranquil setting of the villas. Enjoy life like the rich and famous at The Samaya Seminyak; the beachfront villa resort merely steps away from where it all happens with designer boutiques, posh spas, trendy beach clubs, and famous eateries showcasing what Bali is made of - the melting pot of world cultures.

Escape life, reconnect, celebrate, and find the ultimate getaway only at The Samaya Bali.
