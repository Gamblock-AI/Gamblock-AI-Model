Dear Colleague,

The purpose of the International Conference on Renewable Energy Research and Applications (ICRERA) is to bring together researchers, engineers, manufacturers, practitioners and customers from all over the world to share and discuss advances and developments in renewable energy research and applications.

ICRERA will continue promoting and disseminating the knowledge concerning several topics and technologies related to renewable energy systems and sources.

The coverage of the Conference on Renewable Energy includes the following areas, but not limited to:

Energy Categories Technologies
Wind Energy Power Electronics
Solar Energy Energy Storage
Biomass/Geothermal Smart Grids
Hydro Power Electric Vehicles
  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • Energy Storage Systems
  • Energy Management
  • Artificial Intelligence in Energy
  • Smart Grid Applications
  • Environmental Impacts
Long Digest Submission: IEEE format, around 3 - 5 pages, single column, including figures and references.

Selected papers will be published in following journals:

  • IEEE Transactions on Industrial Applications
  • International Journal of Renewable Energy Research (ijrer)
  • The International Journal of Smart Grid (ijSmartGrid)
  • Artificial Intelligence Research and Applications (AIra)
