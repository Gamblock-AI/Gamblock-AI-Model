What are your rights and how can you exercise them?

In accordance with the applicable regulations, you have the following rights, which may be exercised at any time and free of charge, except in the case of abuse:

  • right to access (A)
  • right to rectification (B)
  • right to erasure (C)
  • right to object (D)
  • right to request the restriction of processing (E)
  • right to portability (F)
  • right to withdraw your consent (G)
  • the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (H)

    A. Right to access

You have the right to request confirmation from Proxistore as to whether or not personal data relating to you are being processed and, where such data are being processed, you may request access to it. The following information will also be provided:

  • the types of personal data concerned and the purposes of the processing
  • the recipients or types of recipient to whom the personal data have been or will be disclosed, in particular recipients located in third countries
  • if possible, the intended storage period for personal data or, failing that, the criteria used to determine that period
  • the rights you have with regard to your personal data, including the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority
  • where personal data have not been collected from you, any available information as to its source
  • automated decision-making or profiling and, where applicable, useful information regarding the logic behind them

B. Right to rectification

You have the right to have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected at any time.

C. Right to erasure

You have the right to ask Proxistore to have personal data about you erased in the following circumstances:

  • the personal data are no longer necessary in relation to the purposes for which they were collected or otherwise processed
  • you have withdrawn your consent on which the processing is based and there is no other legal basis for the processing
  • you have exercised your right to object as explained in point D below
  • your personal data have been unlawfully processed
  • your personal data must be deleted to comply with a legal obligation

D. Right to object

You have the right to object at any time, due to your particular circumstances, to the processing of your personal data, including profiling, carried out by Proxistore in the pursuit of its legitimate interests. In this case, Proxistore will cease the processing unless it is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims or if Proxistore can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds which override your interests and your rights and freedoms.

You also have the absolute right to object to your personal data being processed, including profiling, for commercial purposes.

E. Right to the restriction of processing

Where processing is restricted, with the exception of storage, personal data subject to restriction may only be further processed within the limits set with your consent or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, or for the protection of the rights of another person.

You have the right to ask Proxistore to restrict processing where any of the following apply:

  • you dispute the accuracy of the personal data
  • the processing is unlawful but you do not wish your personal data to be deleted
  • Proxistore no longer needs your personal data for the purposes of processing, but your personal data is still necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims
  • you have objected to the processing and it is being verified whether the legitimate reasons pursued by Proxistore prevail over yours

F. Right to portability

You have the right to have your personal data transferred to you or to another controller if this is technically possible.

G. Right to withdraw your consent

You have the right to withdraw your consent to the processing of your personal data at any time, as easily as you gave it.

Multi-device tracking

To measure and report ad conversions when they occur after multiple engagements on different devices, Proxistore’s third party service providers may set and/or access cookies or other data collection technologies that collect information (not including your name, address, email address or telephone number) about your visits to this or other websites and/or mobile websites on your different devices, in order to deliver content and advertisements about goods and services of interest to you on those different devices.

Our Partner uses a probabilistic model by matching anonymous signals to try to identify a user. These signals include:

  • Location (IP addresses)
  • Date
  • Type of conversion
  • Device ID
  • Destination page
  • Interests and browsing history

If you would like more information about this practice and wish to learn about your choices on this matter, please refer to the confidentiality policies of the third parties concerned. (Smart Traffik)

In such a case, the advertisers for whom the advertising campaign is published by Proxistore act as the data controller and Proxistore acts as the data processor (as defined in Article 4 of the GDPR).
