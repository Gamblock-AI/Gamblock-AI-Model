MEMORIAL FC BARCELONA

Now and forever, eternal

A unique and welcoming space where the ashes of a loved one can rest forever alongside their passion, their club, keeping an eternal feeling alive.

Just a few metres from the pitch, in a space steeped in symbolism, fans can forever link their passion for Barça, its colours and all the moments spent celebrating the successes that form part of the club's history.

COMMEMORATIVE
PLAQUES

The commemorative plaque area covers 180 m² so that, during their lifetime, FC Barcelona members and supporters can link their name to the Blaugrana colours and the history of the club.

MORE INFORMATION

COLUMBARIUMS

The columbarium space, located in Spotify Camp Nou, has capacity for 26,600 columbariums.

Unforgettable memories that are also part of the lives of all those Barça fans who rest in each of the columbariums.
