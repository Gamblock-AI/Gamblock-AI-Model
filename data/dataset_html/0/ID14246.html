WHO
WE
ARE

The Marwest Group of Companies is a family operation founded in Winnipeg, Manitoba. Since the late sixties, Marwest has engaged in Development, Construction, and Management of income producing properties, prioritizing excellence in quality and service. Our experienced team has allowed Marwest to capture significant activity across Canada, as well as Minnesota and Arizona. Over the many years, we have honed a mastery in construction and finance crafted carefully by experience, innovative thinking and hard work; all while becoming one of the first companies to offer private syndicated  commercial real estate in Canada.

Property Development

Today Marwest proudly boasts over $1 billion of developed and built commercial, industrial, and residential  real estate. Now operating in our third generation of ownership, we still thrive off of the hard work and entrepreneurial instincts engrained by our families back in the late sixties. Our offices in Winnipeg and Calgary allow us to operate with agility and precision across western Canada, while growing our strong family company for success today, tomorrow and for the many generations hereafter.
