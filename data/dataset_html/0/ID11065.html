About the Tour

ANTINRML TOUR 2026 brings a new live concert experience to audiences across Indonesia. Designed as an immersive performance, the tour combines music, visual storytelling, and high-energy production to create a powerful connection between the artist and the crowd.
 

Beginning in Jakarta, the tour will continue to several cities, inviting fans to experience the music in a way that goes beyond a typical concert.
 

ANTINRML TOUR 2026 is a celebration of sound, identity, and the shared energy between performers and the community that surrounds them.
