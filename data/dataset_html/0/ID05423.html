  • Journal of Business and Professional Studies (JBPS)

    Established in 2007, the Journal of Business and Professional Studies (JBPS) (ISSN: 2676-2870), formerly known as the Journal of Business Research (JBR) (ISSN: 0855-0806), is an International journal published by the University of Professional Studies in collaboration with several universities and professional institutions. The journal publishes high quality original research relating to Business and Professional Studies, including but not limited to accounting, marketing, banking, law, information technology, and communication, which are refereed by Specialists and Experts in the fields of Business Management and the related fields.

  • UPSA Africa International & Comparative Law Journal (UAICLJ)

    The UPSA Africa International & Comparative Law Journal, established in 2021, is a platform for the publication of rigorous and cutting-edge legal scholarship that evaluates current and emerging issues in the law. It is a peer-reviewed journal dedicated to the promotion of academic work and the dissemination of knowledge in the field of law at the highest level of research and doctrinal analyses. As a periodical, the Journal is focused on maintaining the highest editorial standards and will accept only publications that make important contributions to scholarship and legal developments both internationally and within Ghana.

  • Journal of Sustainability in Management (JSIM)

    The JSIM is a scholarly publication that focuses on sustainability research across important management domains with the goal of advancing knowledge in emerging markets.  

    The journal publishes empirical and conceptual research articles that advance sustainable development goals. It aims to promote innovative research in management studies, bridge academic insights with practical management challenges, and provide a platform for scholarly discourse in emerging market contexts.

  • Journal of Language, Communication and Media Studies (JLACOMS)

    The Journal of Language, Communication and Media Studies (JLACOMS) is an international open-access online peer-reviewed journal focusing on theory, research, application, and developments in the fields of Language, Communication, Media Studies, and emerging trends. The journal emphasizes rigorous studies using quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods on topics related to theories and applications of Language, Mass Communication, Media Studies, and New Media, as well as case studies across various organizational settings, including both formal and informal contexts. JLACOMS publishes Twice a year in June and December. Each article is reviewed by two blind reviewers from an internationally recognized pool.

  • Journal of Information Systems and Computer Science (JISCS)

    JISCS is a peer-reviewed, multidisciplinary journal dedicated to advancing knowledge in information systems, computer science, and digital transformation. The journal will contribute to theory, practice, and policy by publishing impactful research across various domains.

    JISCS seeks to be a leading African-based journal with global relevance, offering insights into:

    ·       Digital transformation and innovation in Africa

    ·       Information systems for sustainable development

    ·       FinTech and e-governance solutions

    ·       Cybersecurity challenges in developing economies

    ·       Artificial intelligence and machine learning applications

    ·       Data privacy, ethics, and governance in the African context

     

    The journal welcomes submissions in diverse formats, including empirical research, theoretical frameworks, simulations, case studies, literature reviews, and policy discussions. 

    All submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review to ensure fairness, quality, and integrity. The editorial board will include regional and international experts in IS, IT, and computer science. To enhance credibility and fairness, the board will be segmented into two main tracks:  Information Systems and Computer Science.
