OUR VISION

Our Primary Vision is to assist the Students to get themselves into new world of Regular School Education by imparting a High Quality Comprehensive and Meaningful Education Pattern for all Students. Each & Every Student will be expected to succeed within the bounds of their Capabilities & Goals already set by them. Each Student will be treated as an individual, given the tools to be a Lifelong Learner and taught to act as a responsible and productive member of the Society.

OUR MISSION

Our School keeps the Aim & Mission to facilitate learning at all Age Groups by enhancing capability based Syllabus up to Middle Class (VIIIth) & acquiring a new teaching instruction which has been targeted at developing Competency among all kinds of Students so as every Child and every Teacher can prove Himself & Herself as a learner of Modern Time Who is ready to accept all Challenges of Life and build up a better world for all to live comfortably. The Points given below highlight Our Mission Criteria more clearly :
  • To make Students Responsible, Law Abiding & Civilized Responsible Citizens of Global Society.
  • To equip the Child to meet all Challenges - Social, Moral, Intellectual and Comprehend Human Values.
  • To develop Scholastic Personalities and impact Knowledge to maximize Skills & Competencies.
  • To construct Traits of Team Work, Empathy, Patience, Preservance and respect of Elders and Mentors.
