Introduction

The introduction of video tutorials has revolutionized how information and training content is delivered to the target audience.

Many individuals would opt to watch a video tutorial rather than spend so much time studying lengthy training manuals or documentation to learn about a product or service. 

Research has proven that about 83% of people prefer watching instructional videos to reading instructional or informational content through text or audio.

Screen recording is one of the many methods businesses, teachers, and individuals have adopted to provide information to their clients effectively.

Calipio is a safe and free online screen recorder where individuals and businesses can create and share screen records with their clients or recipients.

This high-quality screen recorder does not require installation and registration before being used. Calipio is end-to-end encrypted to protect users’ important information. You have access to cloud storage for your recordings, and you can easily share a screen recording with one link.

What Users Need to Know About Calipio

Calipio is a great online screen recorder that is free to use; registration is optional, requires no installation, provides end-to-end encryption, cloud storage, and the opportunity to share your screen records through customized links.

Users can choose to use just the free online screen recorder, or they can grant access to the microphone to record their voices alongside the screen recording. The free screen recorder also allows users to access their cameras to include a live video of themselves in the screen capture.

With Calipio, you can massively improve efficiency when creating tutorial videos for educational purposes, screen recording for team collaboration, and your helpdesk.

Calipio is a high-quality screen recorder; therefore, it is compatible with any browser. So, that is required of users is to open the website in their browser and start recording immediately.

Select your screen, camera, and microphone without any installation, create your recording using any browser and share with anyone by sending a link.

Calipio online screen recorder makes registration optional for visitors who wish to create fast screen records and share them immediately. Your file is saved in cloud storage, too, so you need to download the file if you don’t want to do so.

Unique Features of Calipio

Calipio has all the necessary features that make high-quality screen recording easy. You can also share your encrypted recording with your co-workers, clients, or whoever you wish by simply sending a link.

Some of the main features of Calipio are:

  • Screen Recorder SDK can be integrated into any website for easy communication with clients.

How to use Calipio?

Calipio free online screen recorder is very easy to use such that you can decide to start recording right away without signing up, or you can choose to sign up.

  • To sign up, click the SIGN IN tab at the top right of the website’s homepage.
  • Log in with an available Google account or select the SIGN-UP icon beside where it says “Don’t have an account?” to create an account. 
  • An activation link will be sent to your email to verify your account. Click the link provided to confirm and activate your account and proceed to create your account. You will be asked to provide your correct name and create a secure password.
  • Agree to the free online screen recorder app’s terms and conditions.
  • On your dashboard, select CREATE RECORDING to begin the screen capturing process.
  • You can grant the application access to your camera and microphone by clicking ALLOW.
  • Click RECORD to begin the recording process and share your screen however you choose.
  • After the recording, click DONE to save your recording. Select the play icon on the video of your screen recording to preview. Another tab will open up for you to watch your screen record before deciding if you would save it or not.
  • At the top right of your preview video, you will see the link shared with others who need to see your screen record. Please copy the link and share it through different social media platforms.
  • After previewing, you can go back to the page where you did the recording and add a description using the EDIT icon before closing the page. If you are unsatisfied with the recording, click DISCARD RECORDING.
  • Your video will be automatically added to your Library.
  • To create a folder in your Library, click CREATE NEW FOLDER. Give the folder a title and tap the ENTER key on your keyboard.
  • When you click the three dots beside your folder, you can edit the folder permissions recorder tokens, move the folder to another folder, rename the folder, or delete the folder.
  • To download a screen record video, you have saved, click the three dots beside the file and select DOWNLOAD to begin the downloading process. You will see all the included sources from your video. Select the download icon to download the application into your system or PC. You can access the downloaded file in your Downloads folder.
  • You can also share your video using the same three dots. Select the SHARE icon, and a small screen will show you the link to copy and share.
  • To add users who you would like to see the screen records:
  1. Select the USER & ROLES tab from the menu box on the left side of your screen.
  2. Click ADD USER and provide the individual’s name, email address, and select a role for the person either as an administrator, or a viewer, creator, or owner.
  3. Click SAVE afterward to save changes.
  • You can also edit your settings from the menu box, access your License information, switch Library, edit your account, and sign out.
