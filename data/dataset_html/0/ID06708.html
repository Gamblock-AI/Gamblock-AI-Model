What is RUBI Community?

RUBI Community - Respecting Understanding Beauty Influencer is beauty community owned by Avoskin Beauty. Estabilished on August 5, 2017 as a representation of Avoskin Beauty in realizing the mission of "Empowering Women"

Get To Know RUBI Ambassador

RUBI Community Ambassador merupakan member yang dipilih secara khusus melalui serangkaian seleksi yang memiliki tugas menjadi representasi pilar RUBI Community yang terdiri dari 4 pilar yaitu Empowerment, Entrepreneurship, Sustainability, dan Creativity serta menjalankan campaign Avoskin Beauty.
