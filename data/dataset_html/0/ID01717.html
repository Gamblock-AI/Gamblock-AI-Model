TeacherWorks - online support for IEB Information Technology teachers

If you are new to the IEB, new to teaching IT, or find running a single person department challenging then this novel initiative is for you! TeacherWorks is designed for teachers using the Exploring IT series published by FunWorks. Get access to valuable resources and expert guidance from Delia Kench, to make your role as an IT Teacher more rewarding.

  • Delia is an IT teacher with more than 25 years of IT teaching.
  • Delia has consistently achieved a matric class average of above 80%.
  • Delia redesigned the IEB SAGs, and was an IEB practical examiner.
  • Delia is also an IEB portfolio moderator and internal moderator for IT.
  • Delia has co-authored the Exploring IT series of textbooks since 1996.
