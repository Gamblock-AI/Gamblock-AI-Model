Journal Midwifery Jurusan Kebidanan Politeknik Kesehatan Gorontalo

Journal Midwifery Jurusan Kebidanan Politeknik Kesehatan Gorontalo with ISSN 2407-8506 (Print), E-ISSN 2808-523X (Online) published by Politeknik Kesehatan Kementrian Kesehatan Gorontalo since 2015. This journal is published twice in March and September. This focus includes areas and scopes such as pregnancy, labor, baby, childbirth, family planning, reproductive health, complementary midwifery care and continuity of mother care. The journal employs a peer-review mechanism where each submitted article should be anonymously reviewed by expert peers appointed by the editor. Articles published in this journal could be in the form of a research article and an invited review article. This journal is open acces and free for subscription, submission and publication.

The journal registered in the CrossRef with Digital Object Identifier (DOI) prefix 10.52365/JM. The Journal Midwifery had a collaboration and MoU with an organization professional named Ikatan Bidan Indonesia (IBI)

Journal of Experimental and Clinical Pharmacy (JECP) is a journal that is issued every six months (February–August) in the field of experimental pharmacy and clinical pharmacy nationally. Experimental research in this journal includes in vivo, in vitro, and in silico drug and food development research, formulation, and food quality inspection. Meanwhile, clinical research includes clinical trials, case reports, case series, cohorts, and cross-sectional studies on the use of drugs and cosmetics. We also accept research articles regarding community pharmacy and entrepreneurial pharmacy (pharmapreneurs). Apart from research articles, we also accept work in the form of review articles, mini-reviews, and short communications. This journal is an open-access journal. This journal is an open access journal. Since July 2024 this journal charges the following author fees. This journal collaborates with organizations APDFI and PAFI.

Journal of Noncommunicable Diseases (JOND)

is a health journal that publishes scientific work on non-communicable diseases in the fields of nursing, midwifery, nutrition, pharmacy, environmental sanitation, and other health disciplines. This journal, which is under the auspices of the Health Polytechnic of Gorontalo, publishes articles regularly twice a year, namely in April and November. Articles that can be published in this journal can be the results of experimental research in laboratories or clinical research in the form of clinical test results, case reports, case series, cohorts, and cross-sectional studies regarding noncommunicable diseases. Apart from research articles, this journal also publishes other scientific works in the form of community service results, article reviews, mini reviews, and short communication. This journal is an open access journal. Since July 2024 this journal charges the following author fees.
