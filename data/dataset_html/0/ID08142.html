Meet Our Principal

Ar. Ida Bagus Gede Sasra Bhanutama, ST., IAI

Ida Bagus Gede Sasra Bhanutama, a proud Balinese architect, founded Emporio Architect in 2006. After earning an architecture degree from Udayana University, he had been involved in the world of property agency and construction, then chose to focus on Professional Architectural Services Company with many requests from all over Indonesia and even abroad. Various experiences in designing resorts, hotels, villas, campuses, apartments, spas, offices, guest houses, clinics, convention halls, places of worship and including homes.

Emporio Architect Villa specializes in designing commercial villas and private villas in tropical style. So with the experience and expertise they have, Emporio Architect Villa can design your dream villa according to safety standards, the best quality, attractive appearance and the right price.

Behind every successful project is a dedicated team of experts, including Architects, Civil Engineers, and MEP specialists who each have professional certifications. Their combined expertise ensures that your villa is designed to the highest aesthetic standards, structurally sound, and equipped with efficient support systems, giving you peace of mind.

We provide villa design architect services in various areas in Bali, such as: Canggu, Pecatu, Ungasan, Uluwatu, Ubud, Pererenan, Seminyak, Jimbaran, Kerobokan, Bedugul, Gianyar, Tabanan and others area.
