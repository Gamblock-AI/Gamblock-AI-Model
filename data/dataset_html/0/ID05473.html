ARENA MILANO

The Arena Milano Santa Giulia will be the largest indoor arena in Italy, with a modular capacity of up to 16,000 seats, conceived as a multi-purpose structure for sports events and shows. The arena, and its related system facilities, have been designed to guarantee optimal conditions of thermohygrometric, acoustic, and lighting comfort, and high indoor air quality.
