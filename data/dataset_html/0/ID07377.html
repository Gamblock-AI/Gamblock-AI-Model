FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does it work?

We team up with the biggest clubs and teams worldwide across sports like football (soccer), basketball, cycling and rugby. We work closely with their kit managers who collect game-worn and signed soccer shirts and jerseys directly from the players. These items are then sent to us where we authenticate every piece before putting them up for auction. Auctions usually start right at kick-off or game time so you can bid live as the game unfolds. At first, you will see digital mock-up images which are later replaced with high-quality photos showing the signatures and special details clearly. If you are the highest bidder when the auction ends, we ship the item straight to your door.

How do you authenticate the jerseys?

Once the jerseys arrive, our team performs a final verification check to ensure authenticity and accurate listing. We compare signatures with official footage and verify all match details. Each soccer jersey is then paired with a digital certificate of authenticity, powered by Fabricks’ technology, which includes detailed info about the athlete and event.

Do you offer worldwide shipping?

Yes! We offer safe and secure international shipping to wherever you are in the world. You can find all the shipping and delivery information you need on our FAQ page.

Do the proceeds go to charity?

Most of our auctions see a portion of the proceeds go to club foundations or associated charitable initiatives, something that’s been important to us from the very beginning. By owning a game-worn & signed soccer jersey, you’re not only adding to your collection but also often supporting a great cause.

Why matchwornshirt?

Game-worn soccer jerseys in the hands of fans – the idea that started a revolution.

Since 2017, we’ve been connecting fans and collectors with authentic, signed, game-worn jerseys through live online auctions. Today, we’re proud partners of over 350 clubs worldwide, including Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea FC, and AC Milan.

From global icons like Messi, Ronaldo, and Bonmatí, to the biggest club sides and national teams in the world, MatchWornShirt is the collector’s choice for match-worn memorabilia. And it’s not just soccer – we also feature exclusive worn and signed collectables in cycling, cricket, rugby, and many more top sports. Passionate fans and collectors come here for the chance to get their hands on memorabilia that can’t be found anywhere else.

These jerseys come with a story – straight from the field to your collection.
