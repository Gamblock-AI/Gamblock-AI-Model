The perfect home for business and leisure guests Welcome to Hotel Chanti Enjoy our outstanding services in the heart of Semarang City. Hotel Chanti offers an affordable luxury in a modern yet warm atmosphere where guests could feel at ease.
Manage and operated by the most dedicated and passionate hoteliers, Hotel Chanti is the perfect home for business as well as leisure guests.

Strategically located on the well-known Gajahmada street, at the heart of Semarang City, Hotel Chanti offers 106 comfortably appointed Guest Rooms, Restaurant & Coffee Shop, Swimming Pool, Fitness & Spa, well-equipped Meeting Rooms and Private Karaoke Rooms for a wonderful and relaxing business or leisure stay.
