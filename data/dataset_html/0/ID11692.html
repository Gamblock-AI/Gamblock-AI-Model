Featured 

Elizabeth II (1926–2022), queen of Great Britain, Northern Ireland, and the Commonwealth realms beyond the seas

Queen Elizabeth II was the longest-reigning monarch in British history. During the course of her reign, the United Kingdom ceased to be a great power, and the country in which she died was very different from that in which she had been born. She adapted, slowly and not always enthusiastically, but on the whole wisely and well.

Featured 

Philip, Prince, first duke of Edinburgh [Philip Mountbatten; formerly Prince Philip of Greece and Denmark] (1921–2021), consort of Queen Elizabeth II

As consort of Queen Elizabeth II, Prince Philip had few role models, and the case of Prince Albert was very different. Overcoming a great deal of initial hostility from the court and political establishment, he carved out a role for himself in which support for his wife, the queen, was always central.

What’s new: April 2026

Welcome to the 133rd update of the Oxford DNB, which adds biographies of 229 individuals who died in the year 2022: 220 with their own entries and nine added to existing entries as ‘co-subjects’.

What’s New: March 2026

Welcome to the 132nd update of the Oxford DNB, which comprises eight newly-added articles, containing ten new biographies, with a special focus on civil engineers. Three new portrait likenesses are added.

FEBRUARY 12, 2026

What’s New: February 2026

Welcome to the 131st update of the Oxford DNB, which comprises twelve newly-added and two updated articles, containing thirteen new biographies, with a special focus on operatic singers and the theatre. Nine new portrait likenesses are also added.
