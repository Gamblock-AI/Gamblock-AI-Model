About Us

  

Regal Home's knowledge and expertise in home building derives from a heritage of fine craftsmanship, building experience, and training passed down in the family for four generations in the Clemson, SC area.  We are a home builder servicing Upstate South Carolina.  Our commitment is to deliver maximum quality in the building process at an affordable price.  We provide distinctive design, unsurpassed craftsmanship, meticulous attention to detail, and superior customer service and satisfaction.  The combination of these enviable qualities allows the building of your home to be an enjoyable and rewarding experience.  Our team of experienced professionals is eager to assist you with every facet of your home building experience.  Regal Homes guarantees you will receive superb quality and service without compromise.  We make it a pleasure to build your home.  It is easy!  Ask any of our thousands of satisfied customers.

We build new construction homes, lake homes, mountain homes, vacation homes, condominiums, townhouses, villas, colonial homes, two story homes, and  one level homes in Upstate South Carolina.  Whether you want to live in a planned development, traditional neighborhood, city dwelling, country living, or suburban living, Regal Homes can meet and surpass your dreams.

About the Upstate

Build the home of your dreams with Regal Homes located in beautiful upstate South Carolina.  The upstate is abundant with:

· Mountains, including the famous the Blue Ridge Mountains.  Our mountains hold nature's beauty including waterfalls and the adventures of hiking, jogging, hunting, horseback riding, hunting and fishing.

· Lakes and rivers, including lakes Keowee, Hartwell, and Jocasse. Our lakes and rivers are great for white water rafting, fishing, swimming, boating, sailing, and skiing.

· Sports, including NCAA sports through Clemson University, Furman University, and the University of South Carolina.  Minor league sports are also popular and include teams such as the Greenville Drive, the Anderson Joes, and Greenville Growl. Whether you love baseball, basketball, football, tennis, soccer, golf, or other sports you will love Upstate South Carolina.
