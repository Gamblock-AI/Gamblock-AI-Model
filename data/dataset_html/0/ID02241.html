ENJOY NEIGHBORHOOD

  1. Central Jakarta

    Central Jakarta displays a  different character from other regions  in the capital. As the center of government and business, bustling atmosphere is a common sight.

  2. North Jakarta

    Tucked  away  is a fact revealed  from the ancient site, that humans have inhabited the  coast of  Jakarta since prehistoric times.

  3. West Jakarta

    The number of historical sites spread across West Jakarta  is a sign that this area once had an important role in civilization and the process of forming the City of Jakarta

  4. South Jakarta

    The impression of shady and cool will come to mind when referring to the South Jakarta area. It is commonplace  because it has always been known as a green area with a lot of trees and gardens.

  5. East Jakarta

    There is a special value when doing recreation with family, in addition to warmth, of course there is a promised excitement.

  6. Seribu Island

    The Thousand Islands are rich in marine tourist attractions, the panorama on land to under the sea displays extraordinary charm.
