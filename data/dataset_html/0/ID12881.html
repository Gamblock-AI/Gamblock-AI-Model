GDPR Compliance

Nexd adheres to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union. Although it was adopted by the European Union, it imposes obligations on organizations everywhere in the world, as long as they target or collect data relating to people in the EU. We work constantly to make sure we follow all the requirements of the GDPR.

We have a Data Protection Officer (DPO) to oversee our GDPR compliance and to serve as a point of contact for supervisory authorities and data subjects in case of questions or concerns about our processing of personal data.
