How to join

We’ll be using Zoom to host this session. To ensure the best experience, follow these simple steps:

1. Register now
Sign up via the registration form to secure your spot.

2. Check your confirmation
Look for your unique Join link in the confirmation email and add it to your calendar. We’ll also send a “Starting Now” reminder 15 minutes before the event.

3. Set up your tech
New to Zoom? You can join via your browser, but we recommend the Zoom client for the best experience.

4. Jump in early on the day
The Waiting Room opens 10 minutes before the start time. Use this time to test your audio and video, settle in, and grab a coffee.

FAQ

What is the format of the event?

The event will be fully virtual. All sessions will be streamed online, and participants can join remotely from any location with internet access.

Is the event free to attend?

This specific event is free to attend.

What language will the event be in?

The primary language is English.

How do I join the event?

Once registered, you will receive a confirmation email with your personal access link. Please keep this email for the day of the event. No additional registration is required.

Can I share my registration with a colleague?

Registration is unique to each individual attendee. Please have your colleagues register separately.

Which platform will the event use?

The event will be broadcast on Zoom Events.

Do I need to install any software?

You can join the event from your browser, however, we recommend joining via the Zoom client for the best viewing experience. You can download the client here ahead of time. If you already have it, make sure you’re running the latest version to access all our interactive features.

Will my camera or microphone be on?

No, camera and microphone will not be enabled for attendees during the event.
