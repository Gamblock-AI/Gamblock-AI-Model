SCAM ALERT - WEBSITE

7 Aug 2025

We have identified a fraudulent website impersonating CHARLES & KEITH Saudi Arabia.

Please be aware that this website is not associated with our brand. Our official website is www.charleskeith.sa

We are actively investigating and will take appropriate action. We kindly urge the public to exercise caution and refrain from making purchases through domains not associated with the brand.

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

SCAM ALERT - email

15 July 2025

We have recently been made aware of emails from charles-keith.com domain, falsely purporting to represent CHARLES & KEITH.

It is important to note that the email domain charles-keith.com is not affiliated with the brand. Our official email and website domain is charleskeith.com.

In response to this issue, we have promptly suspended the charles-keith.com domain and initiated necessary measures.

We kindly request the public to exercise caution when encountering such misleading claims and refrain from opening or downloading any attachments from such emails.

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

SCAM ALERT - VIETNAM

21 April 2025

It has come to our attention that some customers have received calls from individuals impersonating delivery personnel, requesting deposit, advance or additional payment prior to the delivery of their orders. These individuals may reference actual order details, such as product names and prices, in an attempt to appear legitimate.

Please be advised that CHARLES & KEITH and our authorised courier partners will never request for any form of payment before a parcel is delivered. 

Our official delivery partners include:
Tiki, J&T, LEX, and Grab

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

 

SCAM ALERT - ADS (THAILAND)

20 January 2025

We have recently been alerted to shopping accounts and advertisement links falsely purporting to represent CHARLES & KEITH. In response to this issue, we have promptly suspended such accounts and initiated necessary measures.

We kindly request the public to exercise caution and refrain from clicking from such unknown third party sites. Additionally, please avoid engaging in conversations with them.

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

 

SCAM ALERT - EMAIL

2 January 2025

It has recently came to our attention that scammers are impersonating CHARLES & KEITH employees, offering fake collaborations and requesting payments, such as shipping fees. These emails, often from third party domains such as “gmail.com”, are not legitimate. CHARLES & KEITH will never ask for payment in collaborations. If you receive suspicious emails, avoid sharing personal information or making payments.

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

 

SCAM ALERT - EMAIL

29 October 2024

We have recently been alerted to fraudulent emails claiming to represent CHARLES & KEITH for collaboration opportunities, seeking interviews with candidates and attempting to solicit funds and personal data. Please be advised that any emails from “gmail.com” addresses are not associated with our brand. Our official email and website domain is charleskeith.com.

In response to this issue, we are implementing additional security measures to safeguard our customers. We encourage the public to stay vigilant and to avoid opening or downloading attachments from any suspicious emails.

To verify the validity of such claims, please connect with us at corporatecommunications@charleskeith.com.

SCAM ALERT - FACEBOOK/INSTAGRAM (THAILAND)

27 June 2024
