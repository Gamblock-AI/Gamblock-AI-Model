What The Locksmith Agency Can Do For Your Locksmith Business

Your customers use the web for everything these days, and our job is to make sure your customers can find you quickly and easily. We’ve been in the business of digital marketing for a long time and know exactly how to turn site visitors into customers.

When you’re ready to take your locksmith business digital marketing to the next level, here’s what we can do to help:



Design a Great Looking Website that Converts Visitors to Customers

Businesses spend a lot of money building websites that look good but don’t convert visitors to customers. Some of the reasons why they’re not converting include bad navigation, ineffective CTAs, lack of SEO optimization, broken links, a bad mobile experience, poorly written content, and more.

When you hire The Locksmith Agency, we will create a custom website that looks great, has well-written and informative content, provides a great mobile experience, ranks well in local search results, and converts visitors to customers.



Boost Your Search Rankings With Local SEO

Local SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is all about getting your website to rank at the top of local search results so more people will click on your listing and become new customers. SEO can be very intimidating for most locksmith business owners, but we’re here to help you learn about it and improve it so you can grow your business.

According to Search Engine People, approximately 75% of clicks are on the first page of search results, with the first listing receiving as many as 33% of those clicks. The Locksmith Agency specializes in local locksmith SEO, and our specialized knowledge and experience allow us to choose the right keywords and create the best content to boost your site’s rankings, get more clicks, and convert visitors to customers. 



Bring in More Customers With Google Maps Optimization

Your Google Business Profile (aka your Google Map listing) is a key part of your online presence and can make or break your conversion rate. When someone searches online for a local locksmith shop or mobile locksmith service, they expect to see your location, hours, phone number, map, and reviews all in one place. If they don’t, they’re likely to click on another locksmith.

When you work with The Locksmith Agency, we will use our expertise to optimize your Google Business Profile so local customers can find your listing, call you, get Google Maps directions, get to your website, and see reviews with just a few easy clicks.



Create a Strong Social Media Presence For Your Business

In the eyes of today’s consumers, if you’re not using social media, you don’t exist. In addition to Google reviews, BBB, and Yelp, people rely on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and others to research a locksmith company and evaluate their reputation and trustworthiness before hiring them.

If you’re not using social media because the options are overwhelming, you don’t know which sites are the best for your business, or you don’t think you have the time, we can help. The Locksmith Agency will work with you to develop a customized and manageable social media presence that drives visitors to your website and converts them to paying customers.

Explore Paid Digital Marketing Options

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) marketing is a form of online advertising in which advertisers only pay when customers click on their ads. Although PPC marketing produces results, not all paid traffic is good traffic.

The Locksmith Agency has years of experience designing and deploying effective PPC campaigns that utilize the best keywords and optimization techniques. Work with us, and we’ll ensure you get the most bang for your buck.
