The Kanban Method pioneered by David Anderson, is the second most used agile method after Scrum. It is a method for evolutionary change that not only improves the management and flow of work but also evolves organizational maturity for greater business agility, resilience, and customer satisfaction.

 

Discovering Kanban is the ﬁrst in a four-volume ‘Better with Kanban’ series providing definitive coverage of the Kanban Method and its implementation at very large, enterprise scale implementations that span the entirety of modern enterprises.
