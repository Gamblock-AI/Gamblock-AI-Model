Welcome to Aurangabad Hotels

Aurangabad in Persian means 'built by the throne'. Art reigns here in the form of several ruined monuments proudly narrating its rich historical journey. The city named after the famous Mughal emperor, Aurangzeb is the base camp to reach out to Ajanta and Ellora-the names synonymous with amazing wealth of beautiful caves, frescos of multiple colurs and several other artistic splendour surrounded by an air of enigma. In the recent past the city has gone through a make over. Being the largest city of Maharashtra, Aurangabad is developed but at the same time it is a beautiful introduction to the remnants of ancient civilization.

Geography of Aurangabad

Aurangabad District is situated mainly in Godavari Basin and its some part towards North West of Tapi River Basin. The Aurangabad districts North Longitude ( Degree) is 19 and 20 and East Longitude (Degree ) is 74 to 76.

 

AREA
The Aurangabad Districts total area is 10,100 Sq. Kilo Mtrs.

 

FORESTS
In Aurangbad district total Forest Area is 135.75 Sq.Km.

 

MOUNTAINS
There are four mountains namely

1) Antur : its height is 826 Mtr.
2) Satonda : 552 Mtr.
3) Abbasgad : 671 Mtrs

4) Ajintha 578 Mtrs. Average Height of Southern portion is 600 to 670 mtrs.

 

RIVER
The main rivers are Godavari and Tapi and also Purna, Shivna, Kham . Dudhna, Galhati and Girja rivers are the sub rivers of Godavari.

 

CLIMATE
Rainy season begins from the month of June to September. Winter Season is from October to February and March to May is the season of Summer. The Average rain fall of Aurangabad District is 734 mm and the Minimum Temperature is 5.6°C. Mximum Temperature is 45.9°C.

 

LANGUAGES
Marathi, Hindi, English and Urdu language.
 


Ellora Ajanta Festival Aurangabad

The Ellora Ajanta Aurangabad Festival is one of the most colorful festivals in the city of Aurangabad. Aurangabad Festival Committee under the chairmanship of the Divisional Commissioner Aurangabad organizes the most celebrated festival in the city. It is usually held in December and it is one of the biggest crowd pullers. The artistic caves of Ellora used to be the venue earlier but recently the splendid Soneri Mahal built in 17th century has become the stage for the cultural extravaganza.

 

Ellora Ajanta Aurangabad Festival aims at bringing the historical and pictorial sites in and around Aurangabad in the spotlight.To grace the occassion, noted dancers and exponents of Indian music present finest performances. Classical folk dance, instrumental,vocals, Mushaira, Qawalli, Ghazals create a rhythmic environment. The festival also invites craftsman to showcase various local handlooms and handicrafts, the treasures of India.Several competitions are also held which include Rangoli, Mehendi, Cookery, Essay, Bhajan singing anda lot more.


Weather & Climate of Aurangabad

Hemmed in by hills on all the four side, the city experiences a moderate climate through out the year beacuse of its geographical location.

 

Summer in Aurangabad

Summers arrive in mid March and continues till mid July with the temperature rising up to 40 degree C.

 

Monsoons in Aurangabad

Monsoons set in from July and lasts till September characterized by moderate rainfall.

 

Winters in Aurangabad

Winters usually begin in November and stretches till February with the average temperature hovering around 24 deg C to 13 deg C.
