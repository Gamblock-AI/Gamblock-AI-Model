1.1 Which Permissions Are Required?

Petal Ads needs access to your device information (such as device identifier) for one or several of the purposes presented below.

Additionally, the permissions obtained by the publisher, which is the app operator displaying ads from Petal Ads, determine the types of ads we can serve to you. More specifically, the Location permission allows us to deliver location-based ads, and the Calendar permission enables booking ads, for example, allows you to set an app release reminder in your calendar when interacting with an ad that promote a soon-to-be-released mobile app. We never store your precise device location on our servers. Furthermore, any information from your calendar is neither transmitted to nor stored on our servers.

In addition, Petal Ads will read app installation status on your device to show appropriate app ads (e.g. the ads will not encourage you to install apps you have already installed on your device), and to enable measurement and reporting of app downloads for Petal Ads advertisers as a campaign metric.

1.2 Is Your Data Collected Through Third-Party Technologies?

The Petal Ads SDK does not directly embed any third-party code, such as other SDKs, that would enable remote access to or processing of your data by third parties, whether acting independently or on our behalf.

Advertisers may use tracking links to facilitate the collection and analysis of data relevant to advertising campaigns. To utilize tracking links, advertisers must input the tracking URL in the designated field within the Petal Ads platform. Once configured, the tracking link records user interactions, such as clicks and downloads, in real-time and transmits the data to the advertiser or the advertiser's chosen Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP) platform (see below for more details).

Also, by controlling frequency of ads, users benefit from seeing less repetition in ads. In order to protect children from any potential harm caused by advertising, we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children and aim to filter and limit ads which may not be suitable for children. 5.1 Access your data

The Petal Ads platform creates user segments for advertisers based on users' HUAWEI ID account information and other interactions with HMS and partnerthird-party apps.

If you are using an HMS device, you can see segments which you have been included in (based on your HMS device's advertising identifier) and which are available to Petal Ads advertisers by going to Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy > More information > Ad information, or Settings > HMS Core > Ads > More information > Ad information, or Settings > Privacy > HMS Core > Ads > More information > Ad information, or Settings > Security & privacy > More settings > Device identifier > More information > Ad information on your device depending on your operating system version. To safeguard this information against misuse, the segments will only be visible on the Ad information page if you are logged in to your HUAWEI ID account. You may also log in to your HUAWEI ID account and go to device Settings > Account center > Privacy center > Configure Your Privacy Settings > Ads personalization to access the segments.

You can also access these Privacy center settings from the HUAWEI ID web portal.

If you do not have a HUAWEI ID account and want to access such segments, including segments created based on your GMS device's advertising identifier, or have other requests about accessing your data, please contact us.

5.2 Port your data

You can request your personal data, which you have provided to us, to be ported to you in a commonly used machine readable format by contacting us.

5.3 Erase your data

You may at any time:

  • Opt-out from consent-based processing as detailed in Section 5.5. After you have opted out, if we no longer have legal basis to continue processing your data, we will erase or anonymize the relevant data.

  • Object to the processing of your data, as detailed in Section 5.4. If your objection is valid, and we no longer have legal basis to continue processing your data, we will erase or anonymize the relevant data within your effective objection.

  • Choose to reset your device's advertising identifier by referring to the following instructions:

    • If you are using an HMS device, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy, or Settings > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > More settings > Device identifier depending on your operating system version. On your smart TV, go to Settings > System > HMS Core > Ads. In this case your device will generate a new random identifier, and the data associated with your previous advertising identifier will not be linked to the new identifier.

    • If you are using a GMS device that does not have the above mentioned HMS device settings, please use Google's advertising settings from Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID on the device.

  • Contact us if you think the processing of your personal data is unlawful and your data should be erased.

We shall erase or anonymize your personal data without undue delay in accordance with the retention periods detailed in Section 3. When you disable personalized ads, it may take us up to 7 days to fully erase or anonymize your data from our ads personalization systems. If you object to the processing for analytical and development purposes, it may take up to 14 days to fully erase all data processed for such purposes.

5.4 Object to processing

You can at any time opt-out from processing of your personal data for personalized ads by referring to the following instructions:

You can object to the use of your limited HMS data for creating profiles for personalized ads by referring to the following instructions:

  • If you are using an HMS device, select Disable personalized ads from Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy, or Settings > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > More settings > Device identifier depending on your operating system version. On your smart TV, go to Settings > System > HMS Core > Ads. If you object to ads personalization, your Huawei device's advertising identifier will be replaced with a non-unique value of all zeroes to prevent targeted ads being served to you.

  • If you are using a GMS device that does not have the above mentioned HMS device settings, please use Google's advertising settings from device Settings > Google > Ads > Opt-out of Ads Personalization or Delete advertising ID depending on your operating system version.

If you have disabled ads personalization, the ads will be delivered based on general factors such as the content you are viewing, device language settings and device model, or as smart ads.

You can at any time select Disable smart ads from Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy > More information, or Settings > HMS Core > Ads > More information, or Settings > Privacy > HMS Core > Ads > More information, or Settings > Security & privacy > More settings > Device identifier > More information, depending on your operating system version.

If you want to object to the processing of your data for analytical and development purposes, please contact us.

You can also choose to reset your device's advertising identifier as instructed in Section 5.3. In this case your device will generate a new random identifier, and the data associated with your previous advertising identifier will not be linked to the new identifier.

In relation to the data we have collected about you based on our or others' legitimate interests for other purposes, you can opt-out from such processing by contacting us.

5.5 Withdraw your consent

In relation to the data we have collected about you based on your consent, you can opt-out to such processing by referring to the following instructions:

  • If you are using an HMS device, select Disable personalized ads from Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy, or Settings > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > HMS Core > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > More settings > Device identifier depending on your operating system version. On your smart TV, go to Settings > System > HMS Core > Ads. If you object to ads personalization, your Huawei device's advertising identifier will be replaced with a non-unique value of all zeroes.

  • If you are using a GMS device that does not have the above mentioned HMS device settings, please use Google's advertising settings from device Settings > Google > Ads > Opt-out of Ads Personalization or Delete advertising ID depending on your operating system version.

If you have consented to a third-party app for sharing your data with Petal Ads for purposes mentioned in this Privacy Statement, you need to withdraw your consent from that app to indicate you no longer want that app to share data with us.

If you have given your consent on an HMS device for the processing of your location data for serving location-based ads, you can withdraw your consent by going to Settings > Privacy > Location > System services on the device.

5.6 Restrict processing

If you want to restrict the processing of your personal data, please contact us. You have the right to restrict the processing of your data in the following cases:

  • Your data is unlawfully processed but you do not want to erase it.

  • You have a legal claim that you need to establish, exercise and defend, and you requested us to keep your data when we would not keep it otherwise.

  • You have contested the accuracy of your personal data and the accuracy of your data is pending our verification.

  • Your request for objection is pending our verification.

When submitting the request, please specify the scope and the basis of the request. We will contact you to verify your identity in order to proceed with your request.

5.7 Rectify your data

You can access and modify the segments you are included in based on your HMS device's advertising identifier and which are available to Petal Ads advertisers by going to Settings > Account center > Privacy center > Configure Your Privacy Settings > Ads personalization. By touching any segment, you can disable it (e.g. if you think the segment is not accurately depicting your interest or demographic information is not accurate) and it will no longer be associated with your device. You can also modify HUAWEI ID account information based segments by updating your account information by going to Settings > Account center > Personal info.

You can also access these Privacy center settings from the HUAWEI ID web portal.

If you do not have a HUAWEI ID account and want to access and rectify such segment data, including segments created based on your GMS device's advertising identifier, or have other requests about rectifying your data, please contact us. When submitting the request, please specify which data you think is inaccurate or incomplete.
