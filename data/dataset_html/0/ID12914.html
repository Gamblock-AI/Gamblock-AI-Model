Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1st May 2025

1. Introduction

Pixels AI Ltd (“Pixels”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal and anonymous data in connection with:

  • Our website pixels.ai
  • Our video and ad-serving technologies
  • Our integrations with Demand-Side Platforms (DSPs) and third-party media partners
  • Services including analytics, targeting, personalisation, and reporting

Pixels operates as a Data Controller and Processor under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, depending on the specific use case and service implementation.

2. Who This Policy Covers

  • Advertisers & Agencies using our video ad formats via DSPs or direct deals
  • Publishers embedding our technology via SSPs or header bidding setups
  • End-Users (Consumers) who are served ads via Pixels’ technology across partner sites and apps

3. What Data We Collect

3.1 End-User Data (Collected via DSP/Programmatic Ad Delivery)

CategoryExamples
Device InformationIP address (truncated), device type, user agent, OS, browser

We do not collect or process special categories of personal data (e.g., health, biometric, or sensitive information).

3.2 Customer & Partner Data (B2B)

  • Name, title, company name
  • Business email and phone number
  • Technical integration information
  • Payment and billing details (if applicable)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Legitimate Interests (e.g., frequency capping, reporting, fraud prevention)
  • Consent (when required by law, such as under the ePrivacy Directive)
  • Contractual Necessity (to deliver agreed services to partners)
  • Legal Obligations (such as for accounting or regulatory compliance)

5. How We Use the Data

For End-Users:

  • Serving video and display ads in real time
  • Measuring ad delivery, engagement, and effectiveness
  • Frequency capping and fraud detection
  • Ensuring compliance with user consent (via IAB TCF v2.2 signals)

For Customers & Partners:

  • Providing analytics, dashboards, and reporting
  • Technical support and integration
  • Account administration and communication

6. Use of Third-Party Vendors

We work with compliant data processors and sub-processors who support ad serving, analytics, hosting, customer support, and fraud prevention. All vendors are assessed for GDPR compliance and bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

Key categories include:

  • Cloud & Infrastructure: AWS, Cloudflare
  • Measurement & Analytics: Google Analytics, Snowflake
  • CRM & Communications: HubSpot, Zendesk
  • Tag Management & Consent: OneTrust or equivalent CMPs
  • Ad Serving/Tech: DSPs, SSPs, verification vendors (e.g., MOAT, IAS)

International data transfers (e.g., to the U.S.) are secured via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other appropriate safeguards.

7. Cookies & Tracking

Pixels may set cookies or use similar technologies in accordance with applicable cookie laws. Where required, we rely on prior consent obtained via compliant Consent Management Platforms (CMPs).

8. Data Retention

  • End-user ad data: Retained for up to 13 months unless a shorter retention period is required by law or partner contract
  • Business data: Retained as long as necessary for our relationship and to comply with legal obligations

9. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to ensure data security, including:

  • Encryption (in transit and at rest)
  • Access controls and monitoring
  • Regular third-party audits and compliance checks

10. Your Rights (for UK/EU Users)

You may:

  • Access or correct your data
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request data deletion or portability
  • File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

Pixels does not maintain sufficient information to re-identify individual end-users without cooperating DSPs or publishers.

To exercise your rights, email: privacy@pixels.ai

11. Children’s Data

Our services are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" and publish the updated version on our website.
