Hello. I'm Holly. I'm a person who loves to make things: art in my house, clothes, personal software. I currently live in San Francisco, in a little patch of woods. I have a deep fascination with folk art — things made by human hands, and believe that taste is the most sacred part of being human. This here website is a little creative project of mine that I try to garden now and then!

In between weekend trips finding art or hacking together custom furniture, I contemplate AI that enable new possibilities and change what we can hope for. I'm the founding PM for Figma Make, Figma's design-to-code AI product. Watch me launch it at Config 2025, or see my conversation with Anthropic. Previously: I was at a PM at Adept and Google Creative Lab. Say hi on LinkedIn.
