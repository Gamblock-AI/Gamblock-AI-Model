Welcome to the ASEAN Judiciaries Portal (AJP), the website of the Council of ASEAN Chief Justices (CACJ).

The AJP is intended to showcase the laws of the ASEAN Member States, foster judicial cooperation and encourage the development of trade and investment for the ASEAN region.
