Why Erajaya Group?

Established in 1996, PT Erajaya Swasembada Tbk. (“Erajaya”) has grown beyond its origins as an integrated mobile telecommunication device importer, distributor, and retailer.

Today, Erajaya operates across multiple business verticals with large-scale retail, distribution, and multi-brand operations nationwide.
