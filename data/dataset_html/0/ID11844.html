Welcome to Resinda Hotel Karawang

 

Styled to bring tropical sophistication and impeccable service to the Karawang industrial area, Resinda Hotel Karawang, managed by Padma Hotels, represents a new era in personalised hospitality. The property features 236 smart rooms together with supporting business and leisure facilities to anticipate the needs of well-travelled executives and their accompanying family members. With its edgy design concept and lagoon-inspired swimming pool, Resinda Hotel Karawang has all attributes of an urban resort. It is a place where guests can meet in a superior business environment and also take time to unwind in true style. A choice of exciting dining venues and comprehensive lifestyle facilities are also available to enhance overall quality of stay.
