Information on processing of data for Questpass solvers

Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Questpass sp. z o.o. with its registered office in Poznań, ul. Romana Szymańskiego 8/2, 61-826 Poznań, Poland. 

You can contact us as follows:

  • by sending an e-mail to: rodo@questpass.pl,
  • by letter to the following address to: ul. Romana Szymańskiego 8/2, 61-826 Poznań, Poland.

Source of personal data

We will process various categories of your personal data that you have provided to us when solving the questpass – advertising or informational material provided by us or our partners, which may contain a link to a landing page indicated by our partner.

For what purposes do we process your personal data?

We process your data when you visit the websites that display questpasses. We will process your data to: :

  • create your individual ID for displaying you questpasses.The legal basis for the processing is our legitimate interest in being able to properly fulfil our contractual obligations to those entities that cooperate with us.
  • pass on your contact details to our partners (if you provided them when solving the questpass). The basis for the processing is your consent, which you can be withdrawn at any time.
  • defend against or pursue any potential claims. The legal basis for the processing of your data is our legitimate interest in being able to pursue or defend against claims.

Furthermore,  based on your preferences, we will use your personal data to: :

  • measure the effectiveness of the advertisements displayed on questpasses,
  • measure the effectiveness of the content displayed on questpasses,
  • understand the audience by producing statistics,
  • develop and improve our services,
  • ensure security, prevent and detect fraud and rectify errors,
  • provide and display advertisements and content on questpasses.

The legal basis for such processing is our legitimate interest that consists in:

  • correctly fulfil our contractual obligations to those entities that cooperate with us,
  • understand how questpass solvers perceive the content and advertisements we display to them,
  • ensure the proper functioning of our services.

If you would like to find out more about our legitimate interests on the basis of which we use your data for the above purposes, please contact us (contact details provided in the “Data controller” section).

If, by solving a questpass or using the functionality available on our partner’s website, you have consented to the targeting of advertisements then, based on your personal data collected by us, we will select basic advertisements, create profiles personalised to you and select advertisements personalised to you. Your consent is the legal basis for such processing.

If, by solving a questpass, you have joined the Questpass Club, we will process your personal data:

  • in order to comply with the obligations arising from your joining the Club (including the redemption of the benefits associated with joining the Questpass Club) and to manage your membership in the Club. The legal basis for such processing is that it is necessary for the performance of the agreement with us (the Regulations of the Questpass Club accepted by you) to which you are a party, as well as to take action at your request prior to the conclusion of the agreement. You can review the Terms and Conditions of the Questpass Club here: https://questpass.pl/pl/regulaminy/regulamin-klubu-questpass/
  • to enable you to take part in the “Help with Questpass” Action, as joining the Questpass Club is tantamount to taking part in the “Help with Questpass” Action. If you take part in the “Help with Questpass” Action, we will assign information to your ID which of the Beneficiaries cooperating with us in the Action you have decided to support. You can find the list of entities we work with as part of the “Help with Questpass” campaign here: https://questpass.io/pl/regulaminy/lista-beneficjentow/. The legal basis for such processing is that it is necessary for the performance of the agreement with us to which you are a party, as well as to take action at your request prior to the conclusion of the agreement. The Regulations of the “Help with Questpass” Action are available here: https://questpass.pl/pl/regulaminy/regulamin-akcji-pomagaj-z-questpass/.  We do not pass on your personal data to the Beneficiaries of the Action.

With whom do we share your personal data?

We will share your personal data with our partners (cooperating advertisers) in connection with questpass advertising campaigns conducted on their behalf.

We will share your personal data with our suppliers to whom we outsource personal data processing services, such as IT service providers. Such entities process data on the basis of a contract with us and only in accordance with our instructions.

If you have provided us with your contact details we will also share your personal data with our partners who work with us.

For how long do we process your data?

Your data will be processed:

  • for the purposes of displaying questpasses to you and using the data collected in the course of solving questpasses, until you object, unless the purpose of the processing is terminated sooner (i.e. we stop displaying questpasses),
  • to the extent that we use your data to display personalised advertising to you, we will store it until you withdraw your consent, unless the purpose of the processing is terminated sooner (i.e. we stop displaying personalised advertising),
  • in terms of our marketing activities, until you object, unless the purpose of the processing is terminated sooner (i.e. we stop to carry out marketing activities),
  • to the extent that we process it in connection with your membership of the Questpass Club, until you cancel your membership of the Club, unless the purpose of the processing is terminated sooner (i.e. we terminate the operation of the Questpass Club),
  • in terms of sharing your personal data with our partners, until you withdraw your consent to the processing of your personal data for this purpose.

What rights you have?

With regard to your personal data, you have the following rights:

  • The right to withdraw your consent to the processing of your personal data.
  • The right of access to your personal data.
  • The right of rectify your personal data.
  • The right to have your personal data erased.
  • The right to request the restriction of your data processing.
  • The right to data portability to other data controllers i.e. the right for such data controllers to receive your personal data from us, in a structured format that is commonly used and machine-readable. You can transfer this data to another controller or request that we transfer your data to another controller. However, we will do this only if such a transfer is technically possible. You have the right to data portability only in respect of the data that we process on the basis of your consent or in connection with the performance of the agreement.
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal data.

To exercise the above rights, please contact us as follows:

  • by sending an e-mail to: rodo@questpass.pl
  • or in writing by post to: ul. Romana Szymańskiego 8/2, 61-826 Poznań, Poland.

The right to withdraw your consent

To the extent that your data is processed on the basis of your consent – you have the right to withdraw your consent to the processing of your data at any time. Consent withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of the processing based on the consent before it has been withdrawn. You can withdraw your consent in any of the following ways:

  • by sending an appropriate statement to the e-mail address: rodo@questpass.pl or in writing, by post to: ul. Romana Szymańskiego 8/2, 61-826 Poznań, Poland
  • using the functionality of the questpass displayed to you or the functionality on the website of our partner, on whose website questpasses are displayed.

The right to object

As your data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest for direct marketing purposes, you have the right to object to our processing of your personal data at any time. You also have the right to object to the processing of your personal data on the grounds of your particular situation.

You can make such an objection by sending a statement by e-mail to: rodo@questpass.pl or in writing, by post to: ul. Romana Szymańskiego 8/2, 61-826 Poznań, Poland.

The right to lodge a complaint with the authority
