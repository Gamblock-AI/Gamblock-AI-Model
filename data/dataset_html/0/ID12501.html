7. What is my right to opt-out of targeted advertising?

If you wish to opt out of targeted advertising, you can do so at any time. When you opt out, we will stop using your information for personalised advertising and will take all necessary steps to ensure that no targeting based on your User ID is applied.

Opting out on websites

You can opt out by clicking the following link: Click to Opt-Out of targeted advertising. When you opt out, an anonymous “opt-out cookie” will be placed in your browser to record your choice. Your opt-out will remain active as long as this cookie is not deleted.
Please note that:
• the opt-out applies only to the specific browser and device you are using;
• if you clear cookies, use private browsing, or switch browsers or devices, you will need to opt out again;
• Some browsers (such as Safari) may restrict cookies, which may affect the functionality of the opt-out.

Opting out in mobile applications

For mobile apps (iOS, Android, and other devices), you can opt out of personalised advertising through your device settings:
• iOS devices: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.
• Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads → enable “Opt out of interest-based ads” and/or “Reset advertising ID”.
Please note that the exact steps may vary depending on your device model and operating system version.

Effect of opting out

If you choose to opt out:
• we will not use your User ID for targeting;
• we will not create or update any profiles related to your device;
• you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

8. Do you engage in automated individual decision-making, including profiling?

No, we do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which would produce legal effects concerning you.
