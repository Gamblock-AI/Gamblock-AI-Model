Privacy policy

Performax is a technological platform providing online advertising services and helping publishers optimise and monetise their advertising space. For this it leverages its own advertising technology with automatic optimisation, the largest programmatic advertising systems in the world and above all, priceless know-how gained through its many years of experience in the industry.

Because we are transparent in our business, we’d like to explain our technologies below in plain language, to help users understand how our services process personal data. This Privacy Policy describes Performax’s practices and policies related to the usage and processing of data when providing services.

To ensure online privacy, Performax is in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation of the EU (GDPR), the ePrivacy regulation and follows the Transparency and Consent Framework standard of the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB Europe), where it is also an officially registered vendor under ID 732 (Vendor List TCF).

What we do

Performax provides a platform that helps website or mobile app owners utilise the advertising space. We also have tools for processing and measuring data, which we use to check and manage the entrusted advertising space, as well as to detect and prevent fraudulent activities.

What information do we collect and use

When we use the term “information”, we mean data generated through our services, which are implemented on the websites of our clients.

This Privacy Policy describes in detail how the information managed by Performax is used in our services.

In order to operate its services, Performax may collect and use the following information:

Device identifier (px2uid)

In order to link each interaction with an individual device, Performax generates a unique ID for each new device that reacts with any form of advertising from Performax or from website tracking. The unique device ID is stored using cookies with localStorage in the user’s browser for the domain of the given website, as well as Performax’s domains.

Information associated with the px2uid:

  • px2uid creation – a timestamp from when the identifier was created. This information serves to monitor px2uid expiration.
  • latest activity – a timestamp of when the identifier was last active. This information serves to monitor px2uid expiration.
  • IP address – information about the IP address serves to detect bots, fraudulent behaviour and to estimate audience size for GeoIP targeting.
  • Approximate activity information about the number of visits regardless of web page. A maximum of one visit per px2uid per hour is counted. This information serves to determine approximate activity and estimate audience size.
  • Device and browser information about the target device read from the User-Agent property. Serves to distinguish between mobile and desktop devices and serves primarily to choose the advertising appropriate for the device and browser.
  • Visited domains information about the visited domains, allowing targeted advertising based on the parts of the website visited. Expiration of this information is 6 months from its creation regardless of px2uid activity

Expiration of a px2uid and associated information:

  • 1 day from its creation, if no further activity is recorded,
  • if inactive for 90 days.

Further identifiers

This data has no relation to a px2uid and is not stored on the servers. Information is primarily used to manage the sequence with which advertising formats are served and to comply with their frequency limits. This information is only saved to the browser’s localStorage related to the web and serves for the management of the current advertising request. It includes:

  • px_session - randomly generated ID for the given session, relating only to the relevant website. Serves to reset sequences when a given session expires.
  • px_breaktimes_SLOT – time value tied to a specific advertising slot, recording how long adverts cannot be output to the given slot. This information serves to manage time intervals between adverts served in a given advertising slot.
  • px_counters_SLOT – information about the number of advertising slot issued in a single session. Used to limit the number of times a given advertising slot is displayed to a user each day. Expiration is 1 day.
  • px_counters_date – information on the date, compared to the current day. If the value is different than today, all px_counters_SLOT are deleted.
  • px_abtest_SLOT – the user is randomly added to a test group and the group label (A, B, C …) is saved to the browser. This splitting of users into groups serves only for AB testing. Expiration is linked to the end date of the test.
  • px_seq_last_SLOT - – information about the advert (only its position in the sequence) was last served to the user in the given advertising slot. This information allows us to serve adverts based on the set sequence in the given slot.

How we collect information

Performax uses cookies, localStorage and tagging scripts to collect information associated with a browser or mobile device.

Cookies are small text files saved in the web browser by a website or advertising server. By saving information in cookies, websites and servers can remember your preferences or recognise the web browser from one visit to another or from one website to another.

LocalStorage is a local storage location used to save information that it is desirable to transfer between visits, but which doesn’t need to be sent to the server.

Tagging scripts are small code segments in a web page that are executed by the web browser and allow communication and data transfer between the browser and the server.

How we utilise cookie files

Performax uses cookie files for various purposes. When we use the term “cookies“ we are thinking about cookie files and other identifiers, such as information stored in the browser’s localStorage. Cookie files generally allow us and our clients to target adverts to users, to evaluate how well the adverts are working, to manage and limit how they are served. Detailed information about the purposes for which we use cookie files and our general commitment to personal data protection on our platform and services can be found at the address: https://perfromax.cz/cookies/

How we use information

Performax uses this information to ensure its client services. These services are include multiple functions that the information may be used for. Performax primarily processes information for the following purposes associated with advertising:

  • Serving adverts – we can use the information to display adverts on publishers’ websites.
  • Analytics and Reporting – we use the information to analyse and monitor serving and reporting on advertising performance.
  • Improving customer experience – this is for example the display of adverts in a sequence or reducing the frequency of adverts displayed to a specific user. Information about devices then help us choosing an appropriate advert and customising it to the relevant device.
  • Detection of robots and fraud – we can use the information to detect traffic that is not “real” traffic and to prevent fraudulent activities such as the use of bots, malware and other suspicious activities.
  • Product improvement – among the purposes explained in this section is the use of information for internal research and development. They may serve to analyse the usage of our services, to suggest new functions or improve existing ones. This information may also serve in the development of computer algorithms that are part of our services.

Data storage

All Performax cookies expire 90 days after the last activity is recorded. Performax stores no cookie-file-based information for longer than 13 months. In other words, all data that might be associated with your internet devices are deleted after 13 months at the longest.
