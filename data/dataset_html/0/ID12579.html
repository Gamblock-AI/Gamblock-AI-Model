Audion Privacy Policy

1. Who are we?

Audion, whose registered office is at 85 Rue Jouffroy d'Abbans, 
75017 Paris, is a French ad tech business dedicated to digital audio advertising.
We are committed to the importance of securing the protection of all personal data, and maintaining privacy.

2. Identification of the data controller of your personal data

2.1. Navigation through our website
When you navigate through our website, https://www.audion.fm/fr/ (the "Website"), we may collect and process your personal data which will be treated in accordance with Audion's privacy policy detailed below.
For these processing operations we act as a data controller and this Privacy Policy details the processing we do on your personal data, why we collect your data, what data we collect, who receives your data, your rights and our obligations to you.
2.2. Services we perform on behalf of advertisers or publishers of advertising content
As part of our service of providing the "Audion Creative" platform (the "Platform") and as part of the advertising we carry out on behalf of advertisers, we may process personal data about you on behalf of our clients.
In this case, we act as a processor of the personal data we collect and confirm to you that we only act on documented instructions from our clients and that we only process your personal data within a defined contractual framework and with appropriate safeguards.
For more information on the processing of personal data in connection with the use of the services, you can contact the publisher or advertiser of the advertisement you are interested in.

3. What data do we collect from you?

Personal data is defined by GDPR as "any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person".
3.1. Use of the website
While you are visiting the Website, we collect, automatically save and temporarily store the following information about your computer and your visit:

  • The domain and host from which you access the internet;
  • The address of the website that linked you directly to the Audion website, if applicable;
  • The date and time you entered our site, how many times you have visited the site and the pages you have viewed;
  • Your IP (Internet Protocol) address;
  • Your type of device (mobile, computer, speaker);
  • The region where you are located;
  • The operating system and browser on your device;
  • The application on your computer or device;
  • The user-agent of your application.

This information is used to enable you to access our website, as well as for statistical purposes.
The processing of this information is therefore in line with Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), under which the processing is lawful if it is in the legitimate interest of the Data Controller.
3.2. Use of the Platform
When you are registered within our Platform, Audion collects and temporarily stores the following information:

  • The domain and host from which the internet access is made;
  • The date and time of the connection to the Platform;
  • The Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • The connection device used;
  • Your identification data such as your surname, first name, email address, password and ID.

This information is used to enable access to the Platform and for statistical purposes.
The processing therefore falls within the scope of Article 6(1)(f) and Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, according to which processing is lawful if it corresponds to the legitimate interest of the controller or is necessary to execute a contract.
3.3. Use of third-party platforms
When you use third-party platforms (Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Deezer, Google Podcasts, etc.) to download content hosted and monetized by Audion, we collect and temporarily store the following information

  • The date and time of the download;
  • The IP (Internet Protocol) address;
  • The type of device (mobile, computer, connected speaker);
  • The region where you are located;
  • The operating system and browser of your device;
  • The application on your device;
  • The user-agent of your application.

This information is used to enable access to the Platform and for statistical purposes.
The processing therefore falls within the scope of Article 6(1)(f) and Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.
3.4. Contact form
If you send us a message via the contact form, we will use the personal data you provide to respond to your inquiry.
The following personal data are collected: your surname, the company you work for, email address and the personal data given in the message.
This processing is in line with Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR.
You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at the address given in this document.

4. Who processes your data?

Your data is intended for Audion.
In order to respond to your requests or provide our services, we may share your data with our service partners.
We may also use third parties acting as contractors, including:

  • Google Cloud Platform – Google LLP (server infrastructure provider).

These providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Where is your data processed?

The data collected and processed by Audion is processed within the European Union.
In the event of cross-border data transfers outside the EU, Audion ensures an adequate level of protection through Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission.

6. How long is your data stored?

Audion stores your data for a maximum of three (3) months following the date of its collection.

7. How do we protect your data?

Audion takes all necessary precautions to preserve data security and confidentiality.
However, no system is entirely secure and there is always a risk when transmitting data over the Internet.

8. Social media plugins

Our website uses social media plugins provided by Twitter and LinkedIn.
For more information, please consult their privacy policies:

  • Twitter: https://twitter.com/fr/privacy
  • LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy

If you do not wish these platforms to link your visit to your account, please log out before accessing our website.

9. What are your rights?

In accordance with European regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access, rectify or erase your personal data;
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
Our supervisory authority is the CNIL (France): www.cnil.fr.
