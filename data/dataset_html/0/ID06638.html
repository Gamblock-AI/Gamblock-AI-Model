5 PEAKS ADVENTURES

In addition to our signature 5 Peaks Trail Running Series events, we host a variety of other trail running adventures that provide additional opportunities to gather with likeminded adventurers and test oneself. While most of our series events max out with the Grit distance category (roughly a half marathon), some of our non-series events, like the Powderface Mountain Marathon and Half Marathon, THE STOKED ULTRA™️, and THE STOKED SCRAMBLE™️ offer up longer distances and greater elevation gain.
