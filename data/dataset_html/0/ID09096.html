What does Grab do?

What we do

Grab is Southeast Asia’s leading superapp.

We are a dedicated driving force behind positive change in the communities we serve and we have a vision to create a better life for millions at the core of everything we do.

A mission led by values

Our mission is to create a better life for millions and innovate to push the boundaries of what's possible. To achieve this, we live life by our 4Hs – hunger, heart, humility, and honour.

Heart: We lead with Heart by putting our users and communities first.
Hunger: We are visionary disruptors and builders. We build fast, test early, and embrace bold ideas.
Honour: We are accountability partners for each other. We take ownership of our words, decisions, and actions—because trust isn’t just given, it’s earned.
Humility: We coach, not criticise. We believe that getting 1% better every day adds up to something extraordinary.
