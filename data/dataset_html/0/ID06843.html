New design tools to elevate your Linktree

We’ve rolled out a fresh set of design updates to help you create a Linktree that feels personal, looks professional, and stands out.

What’s new:

  • Magically Enhance with AI: Instantly refresh your profile with Enhance - a new tool that provides personalized design recommendations of layouts, wallpapers, color schemes and more, all based on your brand and top-performing Linktrees.
  • ⚡ Smarter profile editor: No more stressing about design changes. You can now enjoy live previews before pressing publish. With Suggested Titles, you can generate more engaging link names automatically.
  • New creative options: Explore fresh themes, fonts, and layouts, plus advanced visuals like video profile pictures and looping backgrounds for Pro subscribers.
  • ️ Reimagine your profile image: With the power of AI, you can now adapt your profile image with different art styles, instantly.
  • Enjoy Canva Pro, for free: Linktree Premium annual subscribers will automatically receive a Canva Pro subscription, making it easier than ever to access professional-grade design tools. Explore all that Premium has to offer on our pricing overview page. 
  • ️Integration improvements: We’ve made improvements to our Canva integration. Now you can create and edit designs directly in Canva from your profile editor and sync them back to Linktree seamlessly.

These updates were created to make high-quality design accessible to everyone. Look polished, feel authentic, and grow your audience with ease.

Send your links directly with automatic Instagram DMs


Directly deliver your important links to your followers by setting up an automated DM response to their Instagram comments.

  • Target specific comments: Make sure your automated DMs get sent to the right people by defining relevant keywords as a trigger.
  • Set it up before you post: On Linktree Pro and Premium plans you can set up your automation in advance. Your auto-DM can be active in time for early comments, so nobody misses out.
  • Get all your data in one place: When your followers click on links sent in DMs, you'll see it in your analytics with all of your other stats.

Click the Share icon on any of your links and tap "Send Instagram DM" to get started!
