Building Bridges Between Global Innovators and Investors

InTeahouse is excited to announce its new affiliate organization Boston Tech Initiative (BTI)! In partnership with FIRST, For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology, BTI will deliver the FIRST Tech Challenge program to Massachusetts! Please visit https://bostontechinitiative.org/ for more information.

We help investors and entrepreneurs communicate, network, and do business more effectively, easily, and quickly.

InTeahouse is a global network that helps power an inclusive innovation economy. We connect innovators with trusted investors to raise capital and help them do business more effectively, easily, and quickly.

InTeahouse understands the challenges of international business partnerships and knows the importance of establishing strong relationships between leaders in business, government, and academia. InTeahouse has the experience and insights to help clients connect and succeed around the world.

Headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the company maintains an active presence in many of the world’s other technology centers, including Beijing, Guangzhou, Hangzhou, London, Munich, Shaoxing, Tel Aviv, and Vancouver. InTeahouse has hosted more than 500 companies at industry-specific Summits, which have led to more than $40 million in investments, countless partnerships, and stronger personal relationships among participants.
