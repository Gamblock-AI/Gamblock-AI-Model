Feature Story

Why Business Research?

Due to an accelerating increase in human activity, there are many destructions on our planet, such as ozone depletion, creation of ocean dead zones, forest loss and loss of land fertility. These activities have also caused adverse impacts on ecological systems leading to extreme weather events, such as floods, droughts, and tsunamis.

Business and social science researchers work on various agendas aiming to resolve many societal and global issues. 

Our training program may create new research knowledge amongst young and novice researchers. Their research outcome may provide new directions to the policy makers. Implementing the outcome of such research may bring peace and harmony in the society as well as create the liveability in this planet.
