How VIDA Protects Banking Transactions

Signing Up

VIDA Identity Stack safeguards your sign-up process by blocking fraudsters from submitting fake KTPs for account openings and using deepfake injection attacks to bypass liveness detection.

 

Securing Login Access

Prevent unauthorized access with VIDA Identity Stack by securing logins from credential theft through phishing and social engineering.

 

Seamless Payment Security

Allow legitimate users to make secure, password-free credit card payments with VIDA Identity Stack, ensuring a seamless transaction experience built on trust, protection, and convenience.

 

Protecting High-Risk Transactions

VIDA Identity Stack protects high-risk transactions by blocking account takeovers and preventing unauthorized fund transfers executed with stolen credentials.
