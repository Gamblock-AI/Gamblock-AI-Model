iNi ViE Hospitality

iNi ViE is guided by eight mantras that honor people, culture, and nature while crafting deeply personalized and meaningful guest experiences. With sustainability at our core and a pioneering spirit that dares to create the unseen, we transform every moment into a story of purpose, beauty, and lasting impact.

What Makes Us Different

Ini Vie Hospitality stands as a visionary management company that blends innovation, design, and technology to create transformative experiences—anchored by rapid growth, pioneering concepts, smart innovation, and a complete multi-journey ecosystem. Through this integrated journey, Ini Vie Hospitality stands as Bali’s signature of modern luxury, where every moment is crafted with purpose, beauty, and soul. When you think Bali, think Ini Vie.

Locally Rooted, Globally Inspired

As a Bali-founded company, our strength lies in agility, innovation, and a deep understanding of local culture creating world-class experiences with Bali’s essence in every detail.

Pioneering Concepts

Our portfolio introduces first-in-Bali experiences that redefine contemporary luxury and elevate the way people stay, dine, and celebrate on the island.

Technology Integration

Innovation is at the heart of our brand. Through seamless smart systems and personalized digital touchpoints, we enhance guest comfort, efficiency, and satisfaction while preserving the warmth of Balinese hospitality.

Multi-Journey Experience

We curate a complete lifestyle ecosystem encompassing villas, resorts, restaurants, spas, beach clubs, wedding venues, and family attractions. Each thoughtfully designed to deliver a connected and inspiring experience.

Built In-House, Delivered Fast

We ideate, design, brand, build, and operate under one roof, moving concepts from idea to guest-ready with consistent quality and tighter cost control.

Fast-Growth Company

We are one of Bali’s fastest-growing hospitality groups, expanding with agility and precision across the island’s most desirable destinations while maintaining consistent excellence in every property.
