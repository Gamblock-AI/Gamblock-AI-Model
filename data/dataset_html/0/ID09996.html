Welcome to

Indo Defence 2027 Expo & Forum

Indo Defence Expo & Forum is Indonesia’s leading international exhibition and strategic forum for the defence, aerospace, maritime, and security industries. The event brings together global industry leaders, government representatives, and defence professionals to present the latest technologies, capabilities, and solutions shaping modern security.

 

Designed as a platform for collaboration and innovation, the expo offers opportunities for business networking, knowledge exchange, and strategic partnerships. Discover advanced defence technologies, explore new opportunities, and connect with the global defence community — all in one dynamic event.

 

Join us for a new chapter of defense excellence at Indo Defence Expo & Forum. Be part of the evolution, where innovation meets opportunity, shaping the future of global defense and security.
