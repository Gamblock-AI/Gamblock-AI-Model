    Looking for individual support in the classroom?

    Individual attention and focus on students at IAS is one of the key ingredients to its success. Small class sizes allow our teachers to professionally concentrate their efforts on student progress. Adaptation and assimilation of students coming from different cultures and educational systems is what our school is prepared for.

    Transition for students, especially coming from other countries can prove to be a challenging tasks. Our teachers are prepared to aid with this transition. Over the years, we have harnessed this experience by observing and instructing. IAS teachers and students, helped countless of children in this transition and assimilation period.

    Your child does not speak English?

    At IAS, diversity is embraced and every student is given equal opportunities to succeed. For non-English speaking students, this can mean extra support in the form of our English as an Additional Language (EAL) program. Our dedicated team of teachers and support staff are trained to provide effective language instruction, so that every student can feel confident and succeed academically.

    But that's not all! At IAS, we go above and beyond with our personalized approach to education. Every student is given customized support, through our differentiated instruction program, to meet their unique needs and strengths. Whether your child is a confident English speaker or still mastering the language, they will receive the support they need to thrive at IAS.

    It is worth noting that students attending the EAL program may not attend other language classes as a result of the intensive support they receive in the program.

    Accreditations

    IBO - International Diploma

    Students in the IBDP choose six subjects from established IBO groupings in addition to the obligatory course in Theory of Knowledge (TOK). IBDP ranges from Pre-IB in Grade 10 to Grade 11 & 12 as the main years of the course.

    Cognia - American Diploma

    Cognia is a globally recognized accrediting body that evaluates and recognizes high-performing schools for their academic excellence and commitment to continuous improvement. This recognition showcases IAS's dedication to providing a rigorous and high-quality education to its students.

    MEN - Polish Diploma

    We offer the Polish MEN program to ensure our Polish students have a strong foundation in their education and are well-prepared for future academic and professional pursuits. Our curriculum combines the best of both American and Polish educational systems to provide a well-rounded and challenging education for our students.

    CollegeBoard

    I.A.S. is an officially recognized S.A.T. Test Center. If students want to join Universities in the United States of America then this is the test for them. Registration for this exam can only takes place on www.collegeboard.org

    What are IAS accreditations?

    IAS is accredited by three unique governing bodies: the International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO), Cognia (American Diploma), and the Polish Ministry of Education (MEN). We leverage the expertise of each organization to provide an aligned curriculum across Elementary, Middle and High School programs. The IB Diploma Program (DP) is offered at the High School level, while our Elementary and Middle Schools are designed to prepare students to meet the IBO standards
