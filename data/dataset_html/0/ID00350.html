How do solar panels work

1

Sun

The sun generates energy packets called photons.

2

Solar Panels

Photovoltaic (PV) solar panels converts photons into direct current (DC) electricity.

3

Inverter

Solar inverter converts DC into usable alternating current (AC) electricity to power your home.

4

Electrical Panel

The energy created goes directly to the main electrical panel to be distributed in the home for immediate use.

5

Meter

The utility meter tracks both electricity production and household consumption.

6

Electricity Grid

All excess energy created is delivered to the electrical grid. This allows your home to receive power at night after the sun goes down. On cloudy days, your solar panels will continue to produce power. On days when your system does not produce enough power, your grid connection allows it to continue without interruption. And the energy you don’t use is sent back to the grid, giving you a credit on your electricity bill.
