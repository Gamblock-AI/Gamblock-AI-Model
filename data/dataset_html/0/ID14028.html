How to understand Downdetector statuses

Downdetector shows each service in one of three statuses. The status depends on how many user reports we receive compared to normal levels.

  • No problems

    There is no evidence that the company is experiencing an incident

  • Possible problems

    There is some evidence that the company may be experiencing an incident

  • Problems

    There is strong evidence that the company is experiencing an incident
