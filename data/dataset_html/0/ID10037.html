#BisaBerdaya

The #BisaBerdaya program empowers individuals through skills training and entrepreneurship support. Our mission is to help people become self-sufficient and improve their economic conditions.

Learn more

1,200+ Underprivileged

Gain jobs through vocational training

40 NGOs incubated

With structured support and mentoring

40%

Have entered the workforce
More than 1,200 youth and people with disabilities were trained in customer service, sales, entrepreneurship, and other job-ready skills, leading to job placements and sustainable incomes in over 100 cities across Indonesia.
By combining vocational access in cities with regenerative agriculture in villages, #BisaBerdaya created dual pathways for economic resilience—enabling youth to enter formal jobs and women farmers to increase income through sustainable food systems.

#BisaBerdaya

#BisaJagaLingkungan

#BisaJagaLingkungan is Kitabisa’s commitment to long-term, community-driven environmental action. It promotes holistic solutions—from waste management to clean energy—by restoring ecosystems, empowering communities, and driving cross-sector collaboration for sustainable impact.

#BisaSehat

#BisaSehat is a public health initiative that fosters lasting behavior change, empowers local resources, and builds resilient communities. Reaching vulnerable groups in urban and rural areas, it delivers tailored interventions to address health needs and reduce long-term risks.

Approximately 35,607 individuals received healthy and nutritious meals through 82 distributions under the Pemadam Kelaparan program. This initiative supported students at Islamic boarding schools (santri), school children, marginalized communities, and mosque congregations, enabling them to carry out daily activities more optimally.
Roughly 2,299 individuals, including vulnerable groups, participated in mass tuberculosis screening, helping to detect early signs of infection and break the chain of transmission in communities.

#BisaSehat

#BisaSekolah

#BisaSekolah is Kitabisa’s commitment to inclusive, future-ready education. It tackles Indonesia’s education gap by addressing financial, infrastructure, and digital barriers—while promoting sustainability and well-being. Through strong partnerships, the program builds integrated, lasting solutions tailored to students’ real needs.

Students Scholarships for 12 Provinces

15 University Students

Driving Circular Climate Action

40 Students

Schooling with Eco Impact
By merging academic scholarships with environmental literacy, #BisaSekolah empowered over 180 students to not only continue their education but also lead real-world solutions—from biodiversity conservation to climate-positive school projects.
Through leadership training, funding, and collaboration with local actors, students implemented impactful initiatives such as upcycling programs, waste bank activation, research on endangered species, and nature-positive behavior campaigns in their communities.

#BisaSekolah
