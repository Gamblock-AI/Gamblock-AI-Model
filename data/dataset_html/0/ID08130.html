How Meet & Greet Works

A transfer through the airport to your connecting flight.

We meet you

You’ll be greeted by a friendly marhaba representative kerbside when you arrive at the airport - or at your arrival gate when you get off the plane following your flight.

Meet & Greet: Frequently Asked Questions

Meet & Greet: Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in marhaba Meet & Greet?

You’ll receive varying levels of support and features depending on the marhaba Meet & Greet service you decide to book.

The tiers available can change from airport to airport, but you can typically pick from Silver, Gold, Family, Elite and Family Elite for arrivals, transfers and departures.

All tiers include a meet and greet kerbside or at your arrival gate, an escort through check-in or immigration and buggy car transfers on request.

Gold, Family, Elite and Family Elite and are all dedicated one-to-one services with increasing levels of porter assistance depending on which tier you book.

Lounge access is included in Gold, Elite and Family Elite while a luxury chauffeur-driven car to or from the airport is complimentary for Elite and Family Elite packages – but can be booked as a paid add-on for other tiers.

For transfers, you can usually select from Meet & Greet, Family or Personalised. Meet & Greet includes assistance through transfer formalities until the transfer desk, while Family provides multilingual guides and a dedicated one-to-one service.

Personalised is the top tier, including assistance through transfer formalities and an escort right to your boarding gate, as well as buggy car transfers on request.

Are meet and greet services on departure and arrival?

You can book marhaba Meet & Greet Services for both departures and arrivals, depending on availability at the airport you’re flying from and at the airport you’re arriving at.

You will need to book the service separately for your departure and arrival by inputting your flight details – and the size of your travelling party – at the top of this page.

You can also book marhaba Meet & Greet Services for airport transfers. For this, you’ll need to provide details for both of your flights.

Who should use airport meet & greet?

Airport meet & greet services are suitable for a wide range of travellers. They’re particularly popular with first-time travellers, who may be nervous about the airport process, and families who have lots of bags and want to avoid any tantrums from their young ones in long, boring queues.

Meet & greet services are also great for VIPs and those travelling on business, who  wish to make their journey as comfortable, quick and stress-free as possible.

Elderly people  can benefit massively from meet & greet services too, as they can get help with their bags and confusing airport processes, and avoid walking long distances thanks to buggy transfers.

These services can also help People of Determination who need specialist support to navigate the airport more easily.

Whether you’re booking a meet & greet service for yourself or for a family member or friend, you’ll have peace of mind when it comes to arriving, transferring or departing at an airport.
