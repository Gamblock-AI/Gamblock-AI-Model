EU Digital Services Act Information

EU Monthly Active Users

In compliance with the Digital Services Act (DSA), OneFootball GmbH (“OneFootball”) publishes semi-annually on its average monthly active users within the European Union, based on a six-month period. For the period ending June 30, 2025, OneFootball recorded approximately 6.40 M average monthly active users. This information is compiled in accordance with the DSA and may therefore differ from other user metrics published by OneFootball.

DSA Point of Contact – European Commission and Member State Authorities

In accordance with Article 11 of the DSA, OneFootball has designated DigitalServicesAct@onefootball.com as the single point of contact for direct communications with the European Commission, Member States’ Authorities, and the European Board for Digital Services regarding the application of the DSA. English or German is the preferred language for communication with this point of contact.

When sending messages to DigitalServicesAct@onefootball.com, please include your full name and the name of the EU-based authority on whose behalf you are contacting us. Additionally, provide an email address associated with the relevant EU-based authority for us to contact you.

This point of contact is reserved for engagement with the aforementioned authorities. For other inquiries, please use the mechanisms described below.

Point of Contact for OneFootball Users

OneFootball provides a number of ways for users to contact us. If you have a concern about certain URLs or other information you encounter on OneFootball, you can report this to OneFootball using the “Contact Us” page on our website/ within your profile in our app.
