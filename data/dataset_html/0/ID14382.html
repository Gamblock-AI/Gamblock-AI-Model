About Us

Welcome to Steril Craft, a leader in advanced sterilization and surgical instrument management in India.

We empower hospitals and healthcare institutions with ISO-certified processes, cutting-edge sterilizers, and expert CSSD professionals.

Our mission is simple — to minimize surgical site infections (SSIs), reduce operational costs, and help hospitals deliver safe, high-quality patient care.
