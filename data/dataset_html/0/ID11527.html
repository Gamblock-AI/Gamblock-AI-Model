YOUR PLACE TO GROW

The history of Santika Indonesia Hotels & Resorts can be traced back when Kompas-Gramedia Group, the largest media company in Indonesia, diversified its business portfolio holding in 1981.


PT Grahawita Santika was established as the owning and management company of Santika Indonesia Hotels and Resorts. Their first opening was Hotel Santika Bandung, and after a successful entry into the market development continued to other strategically placed cities around the Indonesia Archipelago, namely, Jakarta, Bandung, Semarang, Cirebon, Surabaya, Palembang, Medan, Batam, Pekanbaru, Banjarmasin, Padang, Samarinda, Yogyakarta, Bali and others.


PT Grahawita Santika have developed their brand of unique boutique Villa properties known as The Royal Collections under the property names of The Samaya and The Kayana.


The inventory of five star properties will be listed as The Anvaya, whilst four star properties will be listed as Hotel Santika Premiere and the three star business hotels will remain as Hotel Santika.


And also Kampi, is a new concept of hospitality that targeting the fresh, young and dynamic souls. With tagline “Crafted for you” emphasize the message of crafting which has strong relation with Indonesian traditional culture, We treat each of our guests as friends.


The other addition to portfolio is the Amaris brand, which will complete the sustainable growth underpinning Santika’s competitive edge which has now repositioned to cater to a wider client base, from two star budget hotels to luxurious villa properties.
