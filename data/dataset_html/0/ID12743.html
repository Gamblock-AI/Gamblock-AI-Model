Legitimate Interest Policy: Save and Communicate Privacy Choices

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Venatus, we are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is managed responsibly. In compliance with the Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) 2.2, we rely on legitimate interest solely for the purpose of saving and communicating your privacy choices. This document outlines our approach and the benefits it provides to you.

What is Legitimate Interest?

Legitimate interest is one of the lawful bases for processing personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It allows us to process your data in ways that you would reasonably expect and that have a minimal impact on your privacy. We carefully balance our legitimate interest with your rights and freedoms, ensuring fair and transparent data processing.

Why We Use Legitimate Interest for Privacy Choices

Purpose

Our sole use of legitimate interest is to save and communicate your privacy choices. This is essential for:

  • Remembering your consent and preference settings for cookies and data processing.
  • Ensuring that your choices are respected across multiple sessions and devices.
  • Providing you with a seamless and user-friendly browsing experience without the need for repeated consent requests.

Benefits to You

By processing your data for this purpose, we ensure that:

  • Your privacy preferences are consistently applied, enhancing your control over your data.
  • You experience fewer interruptions and a smoother interaction with our website and services.
  • Your consent choices are securely stored and communicated to our partners, maintaining the integrity of your privacy settings.

Your Rights and Control

Even though we use legitimate interest for saving and communicating your privacy choices, you maintain full control over your data. You can:

  • Manage Your Preferences: Update or change your privacy settings anytime through our Consent Management Platform.
  • Object to Processing: If you prefer that we do not process your data based on legitimate interest, you have the right to object. We will honor your request and cease processing as required.
  • Access and Portability: Request access to the data we hold about you and receive a copy in a portable format.

Ensuring Fairness and Transparency

To ensure that our legitimate interest in saving and communicating your privacy choices is fair and transparent, we:

  • Regularly review our data processing activities to align with your expectations and the GDPR.
  • Implement strong security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse.
  • Provide clear and accessible information about how we use your data for this purpose.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our use of legitimate interest for saving and communicating your privacy choices, please contact us at privacy@venatus.com. We are here to assist you and ensure your privacy is respected. By understanding and supporting our legitimate interest in saving and communicating your privacy choices, you help us create a more efficient and user-friendly online experience. Thank you for trusting us with your privacy.
