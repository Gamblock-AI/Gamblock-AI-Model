Get To Know MD Entertainment

MD Pictures is the largest film company in Indonesia, being the first national film company listed in the IDX (Indonesia Stock Exchange) with integrated data management. Our vision is for the stories we tell to inspire and find a place in the homes, hearts, and memories of audiences worldwide. Our mission is to be a front-runner in the local film industry and launch global advancement through innovation, collaboration, and consistent production of high-value films.

 

MD Foundation

MD Foundation was recently built in 2023 as an extension to MD Entertainment with the aim to enrich our community through our existing assets such as entertainment.

Vision & Mission

Vision

Bridging Hearts, Empowering Lives: Enriching Communities Through True Stories and Tangible Compassion.

Mission

Our mission is to not only transform real stories into compelling films that inspire change but also to extend our impact to underserved communities. We are committed to providing nourishment for both the body and the soul, by feeding the hungry and offering access to entertainment and cultural experiences for those in need.

Through the magic of cinema and the generosity of our foundation, we aspire to create a world where stories touch lives and lives are transformed by stories.
