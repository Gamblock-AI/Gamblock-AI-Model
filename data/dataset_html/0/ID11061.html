NEWS

Indonesia BOICE, brace yourself as CNBLUE will be making their long-awaited return to Jakarta this April. Presented by FriedRice Entertainment, the 2026 CNBLUE LIVE WORLD TOUR ‘3LOGY’ IN JAKARTA will take place on 18 April 2026 (Saturday), 6PM at ICE BSD Hall 1.
 

K-Pop band CNBLUE officially launched their new world tour, 2026 CNBLUE LIVE WORLD TOUR ‘3LOGY’, with a powerful two-night kickoff in Seoul on 17 and 18 January. The tour will then follow with stops in Macau, Taipei and more, before coming to Jakarta on 18 April.
 

The concert title “3LOGY” signifies a perfect system and harmony created by the three members of CNBLUE, heralding a special performance that will be brought to life with an even more powerful synergy as a complete group.
 

CNBLUE has been dominating various university festivals and festivals in Korea with their signature explosive energy, once again proving their prowess as ‘performance masters’. Their latest title track “Killer Joy”, from their third full-length album “3LOGY” released in January, has also garnered significant attention through its viral challenge. With participation from numerous artists, the “Killer Joy” challenge created a wave across social media, while the track entered multiple global music charts.
 

Marking their return to Jakarta after nearly two years, this tour serves as a highly anticipated reunion with Indonesia BOICE. A special highlight at every tour stop, the Challenge Zone will also be featured at the Jakarta show, allowing fans to freely take part in the excitement firsthand.
 

In addition to experiencing live performances of new tracks from the “3LOGY” album for the very first time in Jakarta, fans can look forward to reliving their timeless hits such as “I’m A Loner” and “LOVE”, as the group celebrates their 17th debut anniversary this year.
 

Ticket sales for 2026 CNBLUE LIVE WORLD TOUR ‘3LOGY’ IN JAKARTA will begin at 10AM (WIB) on 13 March 2026 via loket.com.
 

Stay tuned for the latest updates by following FriedRice Entertainment on Facebook, Instagram and X.
