Data retention period:

• Maximum 90 days.

Profiling

Based on your consent, we infer insights from your Personal Data provided to us by our demand side partners combined with aggregated statistics we obtain from other service providers using our technology. Your profiles are then processed to show you relevant ads, based on your preferences and interests. You can adjust your preferences at any time in your privacy settings. If you choose not to give consent for this purpose, Infinia would not be able to show you personalized ads.

Data retention period:

• The data will be deleted within 30 days from the moment we stop receiving data from a specific user.

Indoor Tracking

Based on legitimate interest, we process technical information of your device (MAC address) along with the information related to the store, provided by our service provider through its devices located into the stores of the partners we work with. Based on legitimate interest, we are processing this data to be able to provide aggregated reports back to our clients on the performance of the campaigns run.

Data retention:
