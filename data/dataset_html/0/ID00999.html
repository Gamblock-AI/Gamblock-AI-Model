WHO WE ARE

With over 60 years of engineering excellence and dedicated team of 1000+ highly skilled professionals, we have established ourselves as a trusted multinational engineering company. Our commitment to quality, safety, and innovation is reflected in our prestigious certifications, including AS 9100 Rev D, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 9001:2015, and ASME (U & R Stamps). We are also approved by leading regulatory bodies such as EIL, IBR, and PESO, reinforcing our capability to deliver world-class solutions across diverse industries.
