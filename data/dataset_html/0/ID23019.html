Need Help with Anything?

Is Filmora free to use?
Filmora offers a free version where you can explore most of its editing tools, but videos exported using the free version will include a watermark. To unlock additional features such as advanced effects and premium assets, and to remove the watermark, you can subscribe to one of their paid plans: annual, monthly, or a one-time purchase.
How do I remove the watermark from Filmora videos?
To remove the watermark from your edited videos, you need to upgrade to a paid plan. Once you subscribe, you can re-export any videos without the watermark. Your previous projects remain saved and can be re-edited without starting from scratch.
Does Filmora support 4K video editing?
Yes, Filmora supports the import, editing, and export of 4K resolution videos. This ensures that your high-definition content retains its sharpness and clarity throughout the editing process and when shared across platforms.
What operating systems are compatible with Filmora?
Filmora is available on both Windows and MacOS platforms. It offers seamless performance on both systems and is compatible with a wide range of hardware configurations. Additionally, Filmora has a mobile version, which is available on iOS and Android, allowing you to edit videos on your smartphone or tablet.
Can I add music and sound effects to my videos in Filmora?
Absolutely! Filmora has a built-in royalty-free music library that you can browse and add to your videos. You can also import your own audio files, including background music, sound effects, or voiceovers. There's even an AI Audio Denoise tool to remove unwanted background noise for a professional finish.
How do I add subtitles or captions to videos in Filmora?
Adding subtitles or captions in Filmora is simple. You can use the Text Tool to manually input captions or take advantage of Auto Captions which automatically detects spoken words and generates subtitles accordingly. Filmora offers various styles and fonts for subtitles, ensuring they match the theme of your video.
Can Filmora handle multi-camera editing?
Filmora supports multi-camera editing, allowing you to import footage from different cameras and synchronize them for seamless editing. You can switch between camera angles and make precise cuts, offering a professional touch to interviews, performances, or multi-perspective videos.
