We are the ecosystem of Indonesia's aviation and tourism, bringing the nation's hospitality to the world.


InJourney is the first holding company of state-owned-enterprises in aviation and tourism, under Danantara Indonesia sovereign wealth fund.


Transforming and connecting Indonesia’s Airports, Aviation Services, Hotels, Destinations and retail - we are both a strategic holding company and Indonesia’s tourism orchestrator, driving collaboration amongst diverse stakeholders.
