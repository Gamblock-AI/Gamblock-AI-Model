Applebee's Grill + Bar

Me and the crew were checking out the Fishermen's Wharf area and I noticed there's a Applebee's right down here! I think it's one of the few Applebee's in the inner Bay Area as I didn't think SF and associated areas were very chain restaurant friendly, like you know, there's no Walmart's in SF at all. So, anyways, I'm looking up at this huge Applebee's and it's all on the second floor of a big building right here with a view of the water and wharf. This has got to be the biggest fanciest Applebee's I've ever seen! I'm a big fan of Applebee's of late since they revamped their menu and the service seemed to be a bit better than it used to be. WE go in and get seated for lunch. This is kinda of a wild place inside, it sure dosen't look like any Applebee's I've been in. The menu looks familiar and the new Big Easy options are on the menu, but slightly higher prices than most Applebee's, I guess you gotta pay for this prime location! Service was fine and we got our drinks and placed our orders. I got my usual Bourbon sauce penne pasta with andouille sausage, chicken breast served with a mix of onions and red and green bell peppers. One of my crew ordered the sizzling platter of mash potatoes, chicken and shrimp with mushrooms. All the food was good as expected, so no weird experiences being that this is SF. Glad we came here, but I think this will be a one time thing ... I can't stand a place if it doesn't have regular parking!

Hologram

Came by during the soft opening and really wanted to love this place. There's a lot of potential here. The space itself is great. The decor and overall vibe are super cool, with some vintage pieces and records from Freddies (which I was excited to see since I love that shop). The staff was also friendly and welcoming. That said, the execution still feels like it's a work in progress. I ordered a cappuccino and waited about 22 minutes, while several people who ordered after me received their food first. It seemed like the food was coming out much faster than the drinks, and there was some congestion with people waiting around the trash area for orders, which made things feel a bit disorganized. The cappuccino itself missed the mark. It didn't have the right foam and was more like an espresso with milk rather than a traditional cappuccino. On the positive side, I overheard a lot of people raving about the Filipino/Islander bites, so I'll definitely be back to try those. Overall, I think this place has a strong concept and great energy, but could use some refinement in service flow and drink execution. Looking forward to coming back after the soft opening to see how things improve.

Serafina

My pilates instructor is Italian and says Serafina is some of the best Italian in SF. I agree! I live in the neighborhood but didn't remember seeing Serafina before! There's good views and parks within block (and hills.) Boy made a reservation for a rainy night and it was packed! Cozy and intimate, good service, get some wine. We've also gone for SF Restaurant Week and can confirm that it is always packed. Seems like a good place for date night or for a girls' night out. He enjoyed the Trofie con porcini e pomodorini and I really liked my Reginette al Pesto di Spinaci con Pinoli Tostati. Rich, creamy, and flavorful, but not too much. Everything was fresh. We've also enjoyed the tortelloni and risotto. We were surprised that the calamari appetizer was not fried but it was also good. Would also recommend the bruschetta. Service is wonderful. Love the owners! Great neighborhood gem. It's small and intimate, but very cozy and warm. Great views of the Bay Bridge. Will be back!
