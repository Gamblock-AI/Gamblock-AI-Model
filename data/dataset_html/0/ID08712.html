The BEE: the Carbon Emissions Platform Built for Impact

Accessible, Affordable Carbon Management Software

Reducing emissions is complex. Measuring them shouldn’t be. The Business Emissions Evaluator (The BEE) helps you do more with less. Get quick, credible emissions data, reduction planning tools, and the flexibility to keep advancing as your climate work evolves — without the cost or complexity of enterprise software.

Used by thousands of companies to measure and manage emissions. Trusted by retailers, suppliers, and NGOs.
