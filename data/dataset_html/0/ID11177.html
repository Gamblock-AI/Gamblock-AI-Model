Saint Paul Bike Map

The Public Works Department updates the map of existing bicycle facilities on an ongoing basis as improvements are made to infrastructure in the field. You can see the current bike map by clicking below.

  • Interactive Bike Map (2024)
  • PDF 2024: Saint Paul Bicycle Map

System Mileage

There are 234 miles of bikeways in Saint Paul:

Bike lanes: 77 miles
Bikeable shoulders: 15 miles
Bike boulevards: 12 miles
Shared lanes: 23 miles
Separated bikeways and paths: 107 miles
