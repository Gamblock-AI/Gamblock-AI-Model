What Else You May Want to Know
FAQs
Is WPS AI free to use?
WPS AI, part of the WPS Office suite, is available with a free trial, allowing users to experience its AI-powered features before deciding whether to upgrade to a premium plan.
How to use WPS AI?
Just download WPS Office and access WPS AI by clicking on the WPS AI icon in the top right corner of the interface. You can use WPS AI in WPS Word, PowerPoint, and PDF components on both Windows and Android platforms.
How is WPS AI different from other AI tools?
WPS AI is not the only AI tool available, but here's what makes it special: WPS Office itself is an all-in-one document processing tool that includes Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF components, all accessible from a single software interface. With the integration of artificial intelligence into WPS Office, your productivity is greatly enhanced regardless of the type of document you are working on. There is no need to switch between note-taking and separate AI tools. Over time, WPS AI will leverage more features of WPS Office, and we will continue to expand its capabilities to further enhance productivity for you and your team. This is just the beginning!
How does WPS AI use my data?
Your data is encrypted and kept confidential according to our standard data protection practices.WPS AI does not use your data to train our models. We do not allow any partners or third parties to use your data to train their models or for any other purposes. For specific information about WPS AI, please refer to the WPS AI Supplementary Terms.
