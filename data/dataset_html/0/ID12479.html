Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)

Zillian S.A.

Date: May 10, 2025

Public Attestation of TCF Compliance

Zillian S.A. (Vendor ID: 1428) confirms its full adherence to the Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) Policies and technical specifications, version 2.2, developed by IAB Europe.

As part of its registration in the Global Vendor List, Zillian S.A. declares the following:

- Purpose 1: Store and/or access information on a device – Legal basis: Consent

- Purpose 2: Deliver and present advertising and content – Legal basis: Legitimate interest

- Special Purpose 2: Ensure security, prevent and detect fraud, and fix errors – No legal basis required

All activities are carried out in compliance with the signaling, control, and processing requirements defined by the framework.

Purpose of the Processing

Zillian S.A. provides a technical service for the delivery of advertising creatives (such as HTML files, images, and videos) integrated into publisher websites. No personal data is collected, stored, or processed from end users during this activity. The service is strictly limited to technical delivery of creatives and does not involve:
- Cookies or tracking technologies
- Profiling or behavioral targeting
- Collection of IP addresses
- Device fingerprinting

Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest

Although no personal data is actively collected or processed, Zillian S.A. applies legitimate interest as the legal basis for processing under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, as a precautionary measure. This precaution accounts for the possibility that certain technical information (e.g., server access logs generated during content delivery) could be interpreted as personal data under GDPR definitions.

The legitimate interests pursued are:
- Technical necessity to deliver advertising creatives;
- User expectation of receiving visual advertising as part of normal web browsing;
- No undue impact on the privacy, rights, or freedoms of users.

Necessity Test

- Is the processing necessary? Yes. Delivery of advertising creatives requires minimal technical interaction with user devices to load visual assets into the publisher’s webpage. No other methods could achieve the same result without such interaction.
- Is there a less intrusive alternative? No. The delivery is purely functional and does not involve any data collection beyond what is strictly necessary for the content rendering.

Balancing Test

- Nature of the data processed: No personal data is intentionally collected. Minimal technical requests may occur, but without identifying individuals.
- Impact on individuals: None. There is no identification, profiling, or behavioral tracking of users.
- Reasonable user expectations: Users expect websites to load content, including advertising creatives.
- Safeguards implemented: No use of cookies, identifiers, fingerprinting, or tracking.

Conclusion

After conducting and documenting this Legitimate Interest Assessment, Zillian S.A. concludes that:
- The processing is necessary and proportionate for the technical delivery of advertising creatives;
- The legitimate interests pursued are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the data subjects;
- Appropriate technical and organizational measures have been implemented to minimize any potential privacy impact.

Accordingly, Zillian S.A. relies on legitimate interest as a lawful basis for its limited and non-intrusive technical processing activities.
