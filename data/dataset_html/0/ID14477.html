Mekari Talenta overview

What is Mekari Talenta?

Mekari Talenta is a cloud-based software that aims to help small and medium businesses manage their HR processes including attendance, leave requests, overtime and payroll. It encourages efficiency, transparency, and self-sustenance for businesses.

Key benefits of using Mekari Talenta

1. Online Attendance with GPS, Face Recognition, and Comprehensive Workforce Management
2. Automate Payroll Calculation, Recap, and Payslip Distribution Instantly
3. Integrated Workforce Management: From Recruitment and Appraisal to Development
4. Customizable AI-Powered Reporting & Analytics with Open System Integration
