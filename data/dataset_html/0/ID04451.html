About us:

Stampapp was founded in 2017 to give small high street businesses access to the kinds of technology being used by big brands. We released our first apps for customers a year later in 2018.

With years of experience in hospitality, most of it working with small, owner-operated businesses, the team at Stampapp are passionate about the importance of businesses like yours to the future of the High Street and the economy, so we built and priced our apps for you, the heart and soul of the High Street!

We don’t like middle men, we don’t like complexity and we don’t like hidden fees and commissions, so you won’t find any of that in our app. .
