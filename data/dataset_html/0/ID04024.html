      Welcome to Rise & Shine Breakfast Networking Club

      What we offer

      If you’re looking for an enjoyable, informal way to make new business contacts, or to bring clients to, then Rise & Shine is the ideal networking club in Guernsey for you.

       

      Meetings are normally held on the first Tuesday of each month, starting at 7.30am and finishing around 8.45am but definitely no later than 9.00am.

       

      We typically have around 30 guests each month representing a wide variety of local businesses, big and small.

      "These events offer me the opportunity to network with a diverse and growing group of local business people. Speakers are always relevant and interesting, plus meeting for breakfast doesn’t eat into my working day."

       

      Gillian Mabbett, The Red Carnation Hotel Collection
