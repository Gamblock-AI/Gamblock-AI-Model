Privacy Policy

Updated and Effective: Nov 12, 2025

This is the Privacy Policy (“Policy”) of Continental Expeditions Inc. d/b/a CloudX (collectively, “we,” “us,” “our,” or “CloudX”). This Policy is here to help you understand how we collect, process, and share your Personal Data (as defined below) on our website, platform, and related services. We also describe our security practices with respect to your Personal Data, as well as your rights & choices with respect to how we process that Personal Data. Please read this Policy carefully.

Scope & acknowledgment

This Policy applies to our “Services”, which consist of:

  • our advertising platform for app developers, publishers, and advertisers, including our related SDKs, pixels, or other integrations (the “Platform”) and related services (collectively, “Advertising Services”); and
  • our corporate website located at https://www.cloudx.io/ and any other website where we post or link to this Policy (the “Website”).

This Policy does not apply to information processed by third parties, for example, when you interact with advertisers or publishers and their advertisements, websites, and services, except to the extent the enable the operation of our Advertising Services. Please review any relevant third party’s privacy policy for information regarding their privacy practices.

Your use of any portion of our Services indicates your acknowledgement of the notices provided in this Policy.

Categories and sources of personal data

Categories of Personal Data we Process

As part of our Services, we may collect and process information that relates to identified or identifiable individuals (“Personal Data”). We collect and process the following categories of Personal Data (note, specific Personal Data elements are examples and may change):

Contact Data - Identity Data used to contact an individual, e.g. email address, physical address, or phone number.

Device/Network Data - Personal Data relating to your device, browser, or application e.g. device identifiers, advertising IDs, HTTP header information, vendor or advertiser IDs, tracking preferences, identifiers from cookies, session history and web navigation metadata, app or website utilization or interaction data, and other similar data generated through applications and browsers, including via cookies and similar technologies.

General Location Data - Non-precise location data, e.g. location information derived from IP addresses.

Identity Data - Personal Data about you and your identity, such as your name or IP address, online identifiers, or unique personal IDs.

Inference Data - Personal Data we create or use as part of a profile reflecting your preferences, characteristics, aptitudes, market segments, likes, favorites, or your interests, as well as data relating to inferred contexts in which you interact with a service, such as products or interests that may be inferred from your interaction with our Platform through a publisher’s website or application.

Transaction Data - Information about the products and services we provide to you and about transactions you make either through us or our Advertising Partners (defined below), e.g. advertisement click through, conversion, and the like.

User Content - Personal Data included in content provided by users of our Services in any free-form or unstructured format, such as a text in a “contact us” box, in a file or document, or any other message to us.

Sources of Personal Data

We collect Personal Data from various sources based on the context in which the Personal Data will be processed:

You- We collect Personal Data from you directly on behalf of yourself or, in some cases, your organization, for example, when you create an account, input information into an online form, or contact us directly.

Automatic Collection - We may collect certain Personal Data automatically. For example, we collect Device/Network Data automatically using cookies and similar technologies when you use our Services, access our Services, or when you open our marketing communications.

Service Providers - We receive Personal Data from service providers performing services on our behalf, including payment processors, web hosting service providers, or our delivery and installation partners.

Data we create or infer - We, certain partners, and third parties operating on our behalf create and infer Personal Data such as Inference Data based on our observations or analysis of other Personal Data processed under this Policy, and we may correlate this data with other data we process about you.

Advertising Partners -  We receive Personal Data from advertisers, ad networks, exchanges, demand side platforms, merchants, other advertising partners, as well as optimization and measurement/attribution partners that provide services to us or customer and other social media companies when you interact with our Advertising Services on the Advertising Partner’s own advertisements or services or otherwise in connection with our Services.
