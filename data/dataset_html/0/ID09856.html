Information
ISO/IEC 27001 focuses on process information security. Information Security Management Systems ensures that your organization keeps information assets secure and protected in accordance with the confidentiality, integrity, and availability principles. Information security governance is established to achieve that objective by protecting information against the risks of loss, damage, or any other threats.
Organizations that have obtained ISO/IEC 27001 certification validate that the security of information assets and other assets related to information management, including information entrusted by third parties, are being well managed and continuously improved based on best practice approaches and frameworks.
The information security management system showing the CBQA Global mark is audited and certified by CBQA Global.
