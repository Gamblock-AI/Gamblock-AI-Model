EASTPARC HOTEL YOGYAKARTA

Indulge in warm Indonesian hospitality by staying at Eastparc Hotel Yogyakarta. This garden-themed 5-star hotel is located in the heart of the city and is a good base from which to explore Yogyakarta's colorful arts and culture.

IMPORTANT NOTICE!

The recreational facilities at Eastparc Hotel are provided to guests according to hotel rules and regulations. It is the responsibility of every guest to assess suitability of each facility, to consider and accept all possible risks. The Hotel Management is NOT responsible in any way for any accident, physical injury, trauma, or any other consequence whatsoever to the user. The Hotel Management reserves the right to deny usage of its facilities to any guest at any time or to invalidate any vouchers given.

For the convenience of all guests, the use of bikinis (two-piece swimwear) is not permitted within hotel premises, including in water facilities area.

Eastparc Hotel supports the global campaign against single-use plastic products that harm the environment. As part of our efforts, we will not provide single-use plastic toiletries including toothbrushes and combs and will also replace single-use plastic bottles and bedroom slippers. Thank you for your understanding, commitment and participation in this global effort. 

Room rates are dynamic and may change at any time. The agreed price after the reservation process is complete cannot be changed with additional fees or refunds for the difference with the latest high/low price in the same period. 

The maximum of room capacity is for 2 adults and 2 children up to 8 years old. The Hotel may charge you an additional fee for each extra person after the reserved room's capacity.

Deposit Required at check-in. 

Hotel does not provide baby cribs.

Waiting rooms are not available in the lobby area, please use Verandah Resto or The Canopy (near the drop off lobby area) as waiting rooms. Welcome drinks are available from 1pm - 5pm at Verandah Resto.

Our restaurant only serves halal food. We do not serve alcoholic beverages in our restaurant and room minibars. Consuming alcoholic beverages in public areas including the lobby, restaurant, ballroom and pool terrace is strictly prohibited. However, guests are permitted to consume their own alcoholic beverages in the privacy of their rooms. Cigarettes and cigars are not sold in our restaurant. We also do not serve vegetarian and vegan food. 

Smoking is only permitted on the balcony of each room. Smoking is strictly prohibited in the bedrooms and it may trigger the smoke alarm. Smoking is not permitted in all public areas of this hotel including the restaurant, poolside terrace and outdoor recreation areas except in designated smoking areas. 

Check-in time starts at 15.00. Requests for adjoining rooms, opposite rooms, connecting rooms, certain rooms, certain views, or certain floors depend on availability at check-in and will of course result in a lot of late check-in times after 15.00. Early check-in depends on room availability and readiness.
