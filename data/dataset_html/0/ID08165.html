Vibration Sensors

Next-generation Quartz-based 3-axis
vibration sensors, compliant with
ISO10816/20816 for structural and
machinery health monitoring applications.

Features and Lineup

Accelerometers

Next-generation Quartz-based 3-axis
accelerometers delivering high precision
and durability. Designed specifically for
long-term use in structural health monitoring,
seismic observation, vibration control and
motion analysis applications.

Features and Lineup

IMU

Compact and lightweight Quartz-based 6-axis sensors
which output high-resolution inertial data in even the
most challenging environments. Offering superior
stability over temperature and through shock and
vibration, these sensors deliver reliable, high-precision
sensing for a range of applications.

Features and Lineup

NEW : G570PR20

With industry-leading noise performance
and sensing accuracy, the G570PR20 IMU
is also waterproof, dustproof and ruggedized
the most demanding applications.

NEW : G570PR20

Gyro Sensor

The unique double-T structure of the quartz sensor
element provides excellent noise performance,
bias stability, shock resistance, and vibration resistance.
The lineup also includes products that are compatible with
automotive applications and feature self-diagnosis functions.
