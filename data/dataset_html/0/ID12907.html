Cookie Police

We use cookies and other similar technologies to help operate our website and enhance your experience as a user of the myGaru website. myGaru OÜ is the company which operates mygaru.com.

myGaru takes your privacy seriously and wants to help you to understand what technologies we use to operate our website. This Cookie Policy sets out further details about how and why we use cookies.

By using this website, you agree that we can store and access cookies on your device, in accordance with this Cookie Policy.

You should read this Cookie Policy alongside our Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies and other tracking technologies?

Cookies are small pieces of data (alpha-numerical files) which are downloaded to your device when you visit a website to collect information about your use of the website. Cookies are sent back to the originating website on each subsequent visit, or to another website that recognises that cookie. Cookies are useful because they allow our website to remember information about you to make it easier for you to use our website. Other tracking technologies may use unique identifiers to track and facilitate your use of our Website.

You can find more information about cookies and other tracking technologies at www.allaboutcookies.org and www.youronlinechoices.eu.

2. Why do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

We use cookies and other tracking technologies [such as Google Analytics] for a variety of purposes, includingenabling you to navigate between pages efficiently, collecting statistical information about the usage of our websiteand to optimise your use of our website generally.

The Cookie Table outlines why we use specific types of cookies.

3. What cookies and other tracking technologies do we use?

Where myGaru uses cookies, these are called first party cookies. We use different types of cookies and other trackingtechnologies. Some of these are strictly necessary for our website to operate effectively. Others are not necessary butare used for a variety of purposes to facilitate your experience on our Website such as remembering your preferencesand analysing your use of our Website.

The [Cookie Table] set out below contains a full list of the cookies and other tracking technologies used on ourwebsite

4. How long do the cookies stay on your device?

Some cookies, known as "session cookies", will stay on your device only for as long as you are accessing our website during a single browsing session and so are temporary. However, others, known as "persistent cookies", are stored on your device for longer periods to enable our website to "remember" you and your preferences.

Please see our [Cookie Table] for details on how long our specific types of cookies stay on your device for.

5. How to change your preferences so that cookies are not seton your device

Most devices allow you to block cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser. However, if you use your browser settings to block all cookies (including essential cookies, such as those allowing you to log-in) you may not be able to access all or parts of our website or our website may not function as effectively.

For further information about how to block cookies, please refer to your browser 'help' section or see www.allaboutcookies.org

6. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We will inform you of any changes to this Cookie Policy by posting them on this page and updating the [Cookie Table]. By continuing to use our website after any such changes have been made you are accepting the new Cookie Policy, so please ensure you regularly check this page.

This privacy notice was last modified on September 4th 2025.

7. Contact Us

If you have any further questions in relation to this Cookie Policy, please contact us at: info@mygaru.com
