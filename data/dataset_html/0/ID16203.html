About DFC Intelligence

DFC Intelligence was established in 1994 and its core team has over 100 years of combined experience in the game and digital media industry. DFC has done major research and consulting projects for most of the largest companies involved in the industry. Over 300 companies in over 30 countries worldwide have bought our syndicated research reports and or engaged our consulting and advisory programs.

In addition to our own internal gaming panel that we regularly survey for insights into trends, opinions, usage, and emerging opportunities, DFC Intelligence partners with leading data providers.  We can provide full or summarizations of complex data sets to help investors, executives, and decision-makers make high-level strategic moves.  DFC avoids the latest hype and helps clients understand and avoid pitfalls and find real market opportunities.
