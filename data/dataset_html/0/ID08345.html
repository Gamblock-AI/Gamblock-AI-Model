About us

Voyaging Vet & Tech Services began first serving the Chippewa Valley in the summer of 2012, as a house call only practice.

Dr. Erin and I (Patrice) met while I was attending Globe University to become a Veterinary Technician and Dr. Erin was one of my instructors. We quickly discovered how likeminded we were about Veterinary medicine and shared the same vision for bringing Veterinary care to people and their pets, right at home!

We feel incredibly fortunate to have been able to serve in this capacity until the Fall of 2020.

As we all know with recent world events, life can completely change in the blink of an eye…

Due to unexpected family circumstances, we had to make the heart wrenching decision to close our practice. As difficult as it was to do, we know it was the right choice at the time.

Now almost 2 ½ years later and more life changes, we are feeling called to continue our journey, by once again providing in home euthanasia services.

Home euthanasia was always the service we felt most passionate about and although we know we can’t help everyone, we want to help those that we can.

Our deepest gratitude for the trust, love and support you have all shown us throughout the years. We are humbled and honored to be a small part of theVeterinary community once again.
