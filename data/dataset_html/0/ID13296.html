Apartemen Plaza Senayan

Apartemen Plaza Senayan is an exclusive residential complex located within the prestigious Senayan Square Complex, together with the Plaza Senayan Shopping Centre, the Plaza Senayan Arcadia Entertainment Centre, Sentral Senayan I, Sentral Senayan II and Sentral Senayan III Office Buildings, and Fairmont Jakarta.

Welcome to Apartemen Plaza Senayan, the ultimate residential address for convenience.

Tower A & B

Apartemen Plaza Senayan Towers A & B started operation in 1998, consist of 24 stories for Tower A and 28 stories for newly renovated Tower B (in 2024), with total of 205 units.

Towers A & B offer a range of one-, two- and three- bedroom units, with floor areas ranging from 74.6 to 202.3 square metres. In addition, each tower has two penthouse units, totaling four penthouse units, with floor areas ranging from 434.45 to 545.78 square metres.

A Housekeeping Service facility is provided for the one-bedroom units and is available for other units as an additional option.

Tower C & D

At the end of 2012, Apartemen Plaza Senayan offers for lease 2 additional towers of residences, Towers C & D. Towers C & D consist of 30 stories for Tower C and 24 stories for Tower D, with the total of 217 units.

Towers C & D offer a range of one-, two- and three- bedroom units, with floor areas ranging from 98.41 to 225.25 square metres. Two units of 3 Bedroom Penthouse with the size of 389.56 and 308.40 square metres and one unit of 4 Bedroom Penthouse with the size of 615.67 square metres are located in each tower C and tower D.

A Housekeeping Service facility is provided for the one-bedroom units and is available for other units as an additional option.

Location

Apartemen Plaza Senayan is well located at South end of Jakarta's Central Business District (CBD), positioned next to Jalan Sudirman and near Jalan Gatot Subroto – two of Jakarta's main thorough- fares – and is within the same complex as the Plaza Senayan Shopping Centre, Plaza Senayan Arcadia Entertainment Centre, Sentral Senayan I, Sentral Senayan II and Sentral Senayan III Office Buildings, and Fairmont Jakarta, overlooking the tranquil Senayan Golf Course and surrounded by several of Jakarta's exclusive residential neighbourhoods.
