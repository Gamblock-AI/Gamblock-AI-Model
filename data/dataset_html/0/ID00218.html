about us

Alas Harum Bali Group

Founded in 2014 as a humble coffee plantation nestled in the heart of Ubud, Alas Harum Bali Group has blossomed into a premier destination for dining, entertainment, and unforgettable experiences in Bali.

Over the past decade, we have grown exponentially, driven by a clear vision: to create lasting memories for our visitors. By offering diverse, high-quality experiences, we aim to provide something extraordinary for everyone who steps through our doors.

At Alas Harum Bali Group, we are passionate about excellence. From the authenticity of our hospitality to the creativity in our offerings, we strive to deliver unparalleled moments that inspire and delight. Each interaction with us is designed to be exceptional, leaving you with cherished memories that will last a lifetime.
Discover the spirit of innovation, culture, and warmth that defines Alas Harum Bali Group.

ACTIVITIES

What To Do

To those who seeking for adventure and relaxation, in Alas Harum Bali you can enjoy fun day activities like swing, sky bike, flying fox/zip line.

Alas harum bali
COFFEE TOUR
Discover the art of Luwak coffee at Alas Harum Bali! Learn how civet-fermented beans, a tradition from Indonesia&#039;s colonial era, are crafted into one of the world’s most luxurious coffees, priced at over $1,500 per pound.
Alas harum bali
Rides And Attraction
Experience the thrill of nature at Alas Harum Bali! From jungle swings to flying fox and sky bike, our rides and attractions offer an unforgettable adventure amidst stunning landscapes. Perfect for thrill-seekers and nature lovers alike!
Alas harum bali
Srikandi
Treat yourself to delightful pastries and bakery creations at Srikandi Cafe, Alas Harum Bali! Enjoy a cozy ambiance paired with breathtaking views of lush rice terraces—a perfect spot for a sweet escape!
Alas harum bali
Instaworthy Photo Spot
Alas Harum Bali offers Instagram-worthy spots like the Glass Flooring, Dancing Bridge, Gorilla Cave, bird nests, and the iconic 19m Soekarno statue. Don’t miss the award-winning Alas Topeng Cave, honored by MURI in 2022.
Alas harum bali
Gift Shop
Discover unique treasures at the Gift Shop, Alas Harum Bali! From Luwak coffee powder and essential oils to stylish T-shirts and handmade crafts, find the perfect memento to take a piece of Bali home with you.
Cretya Ubud
Day Club
Cretya Ubud is a luxurious restaurant set amidst nature, featuring three unique floors—Yudistira, Arjuna, and Bima—each offering open-air comfort and stunning terraced rice field views. With a 3-tiered adults-only pool, it’s an ideal serene escape.
Cretya Ubud
Restaurant
Discover a unique dining experience at Cretya Ubud, featuring a stunning rice terrace view and iconic three-layered infinity pool. Enjoy everything from a lavish breakfast to French-inspired dinner and international favorites.
Alasan Adventure
ATV And Activities
Experience the ultimate adventure at Alasan Adventure with thrilling ATV rides, eco-friendly E-Bikes, scenic cycling, and exciting paintball perfect for team building and unleashing your inner beast!
Cretya Sunset
Day Club
Cretya Sunset by Alas Harum Bali, just 15 minutes from Ubud, offers stunning rice field views, a serene ambiance, gourmet cuisine, crafted cocktails, and a luxurious jacuzzi perfect for unwinding and enjoying Ubud’s ultimate sunset experience.
