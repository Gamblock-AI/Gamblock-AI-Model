MITV

MITV is a celebration of all aspects of Myanmar culture from the ancient to the brand new, in english language.Launched on 31st March 2010 based in Yangon. MITV is a broadcast TV brand intended for international and local English speaking consumers, launched on 31st March 2010 based in Yangon. It is a celebration of all aspects of Myanmar culture from the ancient to the brand new. A platform to represent the people of Myanmar, in english language, free to view and available 24 hours a day. An accurate, balanced, independent and up to date source of Myanmar information.The soul of the MITV television channel is born from its local news, that gives a stage and a voice to local people and their stories. A chance for Myanmar to speak to the world and the world to connect with Myanmar.
