Our Leadership

Secretary-General

H.E. Moussa S. Batraki

H.E. Moussa S. Batraki, elected OACPS Secretary-General in February 2025, is known for his leadership in governance, peace, and development. He previously served as Chad’s Minister of Economy, Development Planning, Land Affairs, Housing, and Urban Development.
