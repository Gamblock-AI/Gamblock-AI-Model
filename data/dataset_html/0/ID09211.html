What is Rife Free?

Rife Free is a WordPress theme that best suits people who want a well-designed site with features that help to show their work off as best as possible.

It’s particularly good for portfolio sites, to showcase photography, art, architecture, design and any other project-centred work or professions.

Rife Free also has a single-page website option, a cool way to present small businesses like dance studios, private chess lessons, local poker clubs, or anything else along those lines.

Just check out these two example designs for a dance studio: Dancer 1 and Dancer 2.

As we love art blogging, Rife Free also comes with features that will make your blog look like eye candy.

To see examples, check out our Designs section.

Rife Free is a lighter version of Rife Pro WordPress Theme, so if you need more, you can always switch to Pro version at any time, as both themes are fully compatible with each other.
