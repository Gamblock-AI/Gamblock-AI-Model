What is Text to Speech (TTS) and how does it work?

Text to Speech (TTS) technology converts written text into natural‑sounding audio. Our AI voices process your input instantly, applying pitch, tone, and emotion settings to generate lifelike speech that can be used for learning, productivity, or content creation.

Is TextaVoice’s TTS converter free?

Yes. It is completely free. You can generate unlimited audio, convert long text, and download MP3 files without subscriptions, credits, or usage limits.

Are there any usage limits?

Almost none. Each conversion supports up to 2,000 characters, far above most competitors who only allow a few hundred. There are no limits on the number of generations, downloads, or files you can create.
