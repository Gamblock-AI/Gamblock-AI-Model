Our Story

Mavericks Sports Grill is the areas favorite sports grill located in the heart of the downtown area! We are just minutes away from the Levitt Pavilion, AT&T Cowboy Stadium, and The Ballpark in Arlington – Globe Life Park.

We are a Live Music Venue - Event Space inside a cozy sports bar and grill in Downtown Arlington, TX. We are one block north of Abram St. behind Shipley's Donuts. We are open for College Night w/ DJ Cat Lopez on Thursday Nights, Live Bands on any given occasion, Doors open at 7pm on these 4 Nights.
