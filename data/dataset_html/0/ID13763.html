Technical Questions

What is the difference between HD, Full HD, and Original quality?

SD (480p): 480×854 resolution, ~5–15 MB — best for saving data.
HD (720p): 720×1280 resolution, ~15–40 MB — recommended for standard viewing.
Full HD (1080p): 1080×1920 resolution, ~40–100 MB — ideal for high quality and editing.
Original: Matches the upload quality, file size varies — maximum quality preservation.

How do I download audio/music only from a TikTok video?

The audio file will be saved in MP3 format with quality ranging from 128–320 kbps (depending on the original source). You can also visit our dedicated TikTok to MP3 page.

Troubleshooting

Download failed or is slow — what should I do?

If you experience download issues, try these steps:

  1. Refresh the page and paste the link again
  2. Check your internet connection — make sure it's stable
  3. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
  4. Try a different browser — Chrome is usually the most stable
  5. Disable VPN if currently active

If the problem persists, contact our support with the video link that's causing issues.
