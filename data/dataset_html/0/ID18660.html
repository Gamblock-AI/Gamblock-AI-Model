Travis Beals

A lifelong Alaskan, Travis has been passionate about dogs and mushing his entire life. When not in the dog yard or on the trail with his team, Travis enjoys hunting, fishing, and snow machining.

Sarah Stokey

Sarah moved to Alaska in 2010 to pursue her childhood dream of racing in the Iditarod. Little did she know, she’d meet the love of her life and they’d start an incredible business sharing their passion for dogs together. Sarah loves her dogs, writing, and the challenges of running a small business and managing. a competitive race team.
