Legitimate Interest Statement

Effective Date: January 01, 2026

Backstage Technologies Ltd (“Backstage”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) relies on legitimate interest as a legal basis for certain processing activities under the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF). This page explains which activities we conduct on that basis, why we consider them necessary and proportionate, and the safeguards we apply to protect users’ rights and freedoms.

Processing we perform on the basis of legitimate interest

We rely on legitimate interest as the legal basis for the following TCF-defined purposes and special purposes:

Special Purpose 1

TCF SP1
Ensure security, prevent and detect fraud, and fix errors

We process data to protect our systems and users from fraudulent activity, malicious traffic (bots), security threats, and technical errors. This includes IP rate limiting, bot detection, and system diagnostics.

Special Purpose 2

TCF SP2
Deliver and present advertising and content (technical delivery)

We process data to technically deliver advertisements to users’ devices, ensure proper rendering, and manage frequency capping to prevent excessive ad repetition.

Special Purpose 3

TCF SP3
Save and communicate privacy choices (TCF signals, consent strings)

We process and store users’ privacy preferences as communicated through Consent Management Platforms (CMPs) and honor TCF signals to respect user choices across websites.

Purpose 10

TCF P10
Develop and improve services

We process aggregated and anonymized data to enhance service reliability, improve measurement accuracy, perform diagnostics, and develop new features and capabilities.

Note on Purpose 7 – Measure Advertising Performance (TCF P7):

Processing for Purpose 7 (Measure advertising performance) may rely on consent or legitimate interest depending on the CMP/publisher configuration and applicable law. Where consent is required, we only process after consent is obtained and signalled via the TCF.

Necessity and proportionality

We have assessed that our legitimate interest processing is both necessary and proportionate:

Service necessity

The above activities are necessary to technically deliver ads, ensure security and integrity, respect users’ TCF choices, and maintain/improve core service quality. Without these processing activities, we could not provide our ad serving platform or protect users from fraud and security threats.

Reasonable expectations

Users reasonably expect:

  • Basic technical delivery of advertisements when visiting ad-supported properties
  • Security measures to protect against fraud and malicious activity
  • Their privacy choices to be respected and stored
  • Service improvements based on aggregated performance data

Limited scope

Our processing is strictly limited. We do NOT:

  • Create user profiles for personalised ads or personalised content
  • Use precise geolocation or GPS data
  • Perform cross-device linking or tracking
  • Engage in device fingerprinting for ad targeting
  • Share user-level data between different clients/advertisers
  • Collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

Safeguards we apply

To ensure users’ rights and freedoms are protected, we implement the following safeguards:

1. Data Minimisation

We collect only the minimum data necessary:

  • IP addresses (for fraud prevention and non-precise location)
  • Device characteristics (browser type, OS, user agent)
  • Non-precise location data (derived from IP: city/region level only)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, unique counts, views)
  • We do not collect sensitive data, PII, or precise geolocation.

2. TCF Compliance

We fully comply with the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework:

  • We store and communicate privacy choices via TCF signals
  • We honor consent strings and legitimate interest objections
  • We respect user preferences as communicated through CMPs

3. Data Retention

We implement strict retention limits:

  • Maximum retention: 12 months (31,536,000 seconds)
  • Raw logs: 30 days
  • Aggregated data: 12 months, then deleted or anonymised

4. Security Measures

Appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect against:

  • Unauthorised access, disclosure, or use
  • Accidental loss, destruction, or damage
  • Data breaches and security incidents
  • Including: HTTPS encryption, secure servers, access controls, regular security audits, and fraud detection systems.

5. Transparency

Full details are provided in our Privacy Policy, including:

  • What data we collect and why
  • How long we retain it
  • Who we share it with
  • How users can exercise their rights

Your rights and how to object

Right to Object

Users have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time. You also have rights to access, erasure, and restriction under applicable law.

How to Object / Opt Out:

1. Via Consent Management Platform (CMP)

On TCF-compliant websites:

  • Look for “Privacy Settings“, “Cookie Preferences“, or “Manage Consent“ (usually in the footer)
  • Revisit the privacy choices dialog where you initially saw our ads
  • Adjust your legitimate interest preferences for each purpose
  • Your choices will be stored and honored across TCF-compliant sites
2. Browser Cookie Settings

Disable cookies directly in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
3. Industry Opt-Out Tools

Use industry-standard opt-out mechanisms:

  • NAI: https://optout.networkadvertising.org
  • DAA: https://optout.aboutads.info
  • EDAA (EU): https://www.youronlinechoices.eu
4. Do Not Track (DNT)

We honor browser “Do Not Track“ signals. Enable DNT in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Send a “Do Not Track“ request
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Send websites a “Do Not Track“ signal
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Ask websites not to track me
5. Contact Us Directly

Email us at main@bstage.io to exercise your rights or object to processing.

Other Rights Under GDPR/Applicable Law:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten“)
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format (where applicable)
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent (e.g., Purpose 7 in certain configurations), withdraw consent via the CMP

Effect of Objection/Opt-Out:

  • Processing based on legitimate interest will cease
  • All tracking cookies will be deletedv
  • No new cookies will be set
  • Ads may continue to appear (non-targeted, contextual only)
  • Performance measurement continues at aggregate level only

Response Time:

We will respond to your request within 30 days of receipt.

International data transfers

Data is processed in Israel, which benefits from an EU Adequacy Decision issued by the European Commission. This means Israel provides adequate protection for personal data under EU law, allowing lawful data transfers from the EU/EEA to Israel.

If applicable, additional safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) may be used with other destinations to ensure adequate protection.

Balancing Test and Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)

We have conducted a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) balancing our interests against users’ rights and freedoms:

Our Legitimate Interests:

  • Operating a secure and functional ad serving platform
  • Protecting our systems and users from fraud and security threats
  • Honoring users’ privacy choices as communicated via TCF
  • Improving service quality and reliability based on aggregated data

Users’ Interests and Rights:

  • Privacy and data protection
  • Freedom from intrusive tracking or profiling
  • Control over their personal data
  • Right to object to processing

Balancing Conclusion:

We have determined that our processing is necessary, proportionate, and does not override users’ fundamental rights and freedoms because:

  • We collect minimal data (no PII, no precise location, no sensitive data)
  • Data is used only for technical delivery, security, and service improvement
  • We do not create user profiles or engage in invasive tracking
  • Users have effective opt-out mechanisms and can exercise their rights at any time
  • We implement strong safeguards (encryption, retention limits, access controls)
