Many public spaces and offices provide QR codes to access Wi-Fi. Instead of typing long passwords, just scan the code and connect instantly. ME-QR Scanner makes this process effortless across all devices.

QR codes are also used to share PDFs, guides, or presentations. Teachers, students, and businesses can all benefit from this feature. By using ME-QR Scanner, you can quickly open files and links with one scan — a perfect example of how a QR code reader download can save time.
