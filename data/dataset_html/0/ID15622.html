Why Indonesia Blockchain Week 2026 Matters?

Since 2019, Indonesia Blockchain Week (IDBW) has established itself as Southeast Asia’s leading institutional Web3 conference. Entering its 6th edition, IDBW 2026 arrives as blockchain moves from experimentation to real-world deployment and measurable impact.

This is the moment when technology, regulation, and institutional adoption converge. IDBW 2026 connects global innovators with policymakers, enterprises, financial institutions, and builders shaping the future of digital infrastructure.

For partners, IDBW provides direct access to decision-makers, high-value audiences, and real market opportunities across payments, tokenization, identity, and on-chain infrastructure.

Join IDBW 2026 and position your brand at the center of Southeast Asia’s Web3 ecosystem.
