Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about video compression

Will compressing reduce video quality?

Our smart presets balance file size and quality. You can choose higher quality settings if needed, though this will result in larger files.

What video formats are supported?

We support MP4, MOV, WebM, and AVI formats. The output is always optimized MP4 for maximum compatibility.

What's the maximum file size I can compress?

For optimal performance, we recommend files under 200MB. Larger files may take longer to process or cause browser memory issues.

Is it safe to use? What about my privacy?

100% safe. All compression happens locally in your browser - we never see or store your files. No uploads to our servers.

How long does compression take?

Processing time depends on file size and your device. Most videos under 100MB compress in 1-3 minutes on modern devices.

Is this really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden costs. We support the service through optional ads and premium features for power users.
