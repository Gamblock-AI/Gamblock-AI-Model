    Dstillery is helping keep the Internet free by enabling marketers to target their ads to the right audience in a way that protects the privacy of users.     THE TYPES OF DATA PROCESSED BY DSTILLERY

    Dstillery thinks about the data we process in terms of whether or not the data may be used by Dstillery to identify a data subject. Accordingly, we place the data we process into two categories: identifiable personal data and pseudonymous personal data. We recognize that identifiable personal data and pseudonymous personal data are considered personal data under the applicable privacy or data protection laws in many places. However, we believe that data which may not be used to identify an individual is inherently less risky. Accordingly, Dstillery endeavors to use pseudonymous personal data via our advertising technology Platform in most places and we use data minimization and de-identification techniques where it is feasible for us to do so.

    Identifiable personal data such as a name, a postal address, a telephone number or an email address is personally identifiable information (“PII”) in that can be used to identify or locate a particular person or entity.

    Pseudonymous personal data such as a mobile ad ID, a cookie ID or an IP address is not personally identifiable (“Non-PII”) in that it cannot be used by Dstillery to identify or locate a particular person or entity.

    We believe that distinguishing the data processed on the basis of whether it is identifiable is an important privacy distinction.

    TECHNOLOGY OVERVIEW

    Dstillery’s ad technology Platform uses “network neighbor” data to help our clients deliver relevant digital advertisements on websites and mobile apps. “Network neighbors” are generally two or more people that visit the same website pages, utilize the same wifi network or use the same mobile apps. For example, a network neighbor might be someone whose social networking profile page you’ve visited, used the same mobile application you’ve used or someone that visits the same website that you visit. Network neighbors are likely to share many similar interests, hobbies, and other key demographic characteristics with you. Dstillery uses artificial intelligence to enable us to draw general inferences about network neighbors as described below, although we don’t typically know what (if any) specific interests or hobbies are shared by any group of network neighbors. Rather, Dstillery believes that if you’re interested in a particular product or service, your network neighbors are likely to also be interested in that product or service. Therefore, by understanding whom your network neighbors are and what they like, the company’s proprietary technology Platform will do a better job determining the digital ads that are likely to be of interest to you.

    Dstillery’s technology Platform collects information about the website(s) that you visit (including this Website), the mobile apps that you use and the ads that you see. We also obtain data from third-party partners. When we receive de-identified data, we won’t attempt to identify any of the data subjects in the de-identified data set or allow any recipients of our de-identified data to re-identify the data. And where we can reasonably infer that a particular computer, device or hashed email (HEM) belong to the same household (e.g., where multiple computers or devices use the same IP address), we may extend our advertising and targeting capabilities across multiple devices. You may encounter our technology Platform when: Dstillery or one of our clients purchases digital ads on a website that you visit or a mobile app that you use or when one of our clients places a web beacon on a website that you visit.

    The company uses the information collected in order to help make the digital ads and marketing messages you see more relevant to you, and for ad delivery and reporting purposes. Some jurisdictions consider this type of ad targeting to be targeted advertising, profiling, use of automated decisioning technology and/or involve the sale/sharing of personal information. If you wish to not have this information used for such purposes, you may opt-out by clicking here. By opting out in this way, you will be telling our Platform to stop profiling, ad targeting, engaging in automated decision-making and/or selling/sharing data associated with this browser. Please note our opt-out only applies to the browser or device that has opted out and does not opt you out of being served advertising. You will continue to receive ads, but those ads will not be targeted to your browser or device via our technology Platform. For more information about our opt-out mechanisms, including our mobile application choice mechanisms, please scroll down to the section of this Privacy Policy entitled: “Controlling The Use of Your Information”.

    Overview of Dstillery’s use of AI and Chatbots

    Dstillery uses agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI) powered tools including chatbots to help our clients ascertain which of the data product(s) and solutions available on our Platform are most likely to meet their needs. These tools are made available to clients at their request and don’t involve the processing of personal data. These agentic AI tools are designed to create a simple to use natural language interaction layer for our clients to interact with all our various audience building capabilities – ranging from audience selection, insights, custom modeling, and activation.

    Dstillery also utilizes AI as part of our advertising Platform.  We create models by feeding certain advertising criteria (e.g., target audience or outcomes) into an AI tool designed to train an ad targeting model. Each model can be informed by data such as digital web journeys, LLMs, CTV signals, search signals, purchase intent signals and more.  Each model helps us generate a list of pseudonymous User IDs which are  scored based on the likelihood that a particular User is interested in a particular advertising message.  This information is then segmented into audiences or inventory deal IDs based on a performance/scale ranking and prepared for activation. 

    ID-free Custom AI

    Dstillery offers an additional technology Platform solution which collects a limited data set called ID-free Custom AI (“ID-free”) which does not process personal data and does not involve the use of cookies, HEMs or mobile advertising IDs for ad targeting or profiling purposes. Rather, ID-free helps us ascertain a list of digital properties which are likely to contain Internet users that are more likely to be receptive to a particular advertising message. This technology uses artificial intelligence to help us find patterns across large data sets without creating user-level profiles or ad targeting. For more information, please contact  privacy@dstillery.com.
