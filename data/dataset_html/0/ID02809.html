About Us

Originating in 1955, Mr. Wong Jin found inspiration for the name "Kawan Lama" from a casual conversation. This simple moment laid the foundation for the birth of Kawan Lama Group, which now operates in six business pillars: Industrial & Commercial, Consumer Retail, Food & Beverage, Property & Hospitality, Commercial Technology, and Manufacturing & Engineering. Our goal is to be more than just a family business, but a business for the family. We are determined to provide added value and enhance the quality of life through a variety of products we offer and sustainable business growth. For us, the meaning of added value always revolves around three aspects: product quality, excellent service, and delivering the best to customers amidst increasingly fierce competition. We also instill added value within the organization through the implementation of a corporate culture that prioritizes the human aspect, formulated with the term "I AM ELITE," aiming to become an organization that continuously learns, innovates, and adapts to the ever-changing times.
