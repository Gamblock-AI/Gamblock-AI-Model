Legitimate Interest for IP Address Processing

One of the legal bases for processing personal data, including IP addresses, is legitimate interest. We believe that our legitimate interest aligns with the expectations of B2B companies and serves as a lawful basis for receiving and processing IP addresses in order to serve IP-based ads. Our legitimate interest in processing IP addresses is based on the following considerations:

  • Relevance of IP-Based Advertising: B2B companies rely on IP-based advertising to reach their target audience effectively. By processing IP addresses, we can provide tailored ad content and deliver relevant advertising campaigns to these businesses.
  • Business-to-Business Nature: As a platform catering exclusively with targeting to B2B companies, our services and advertisements are specifically designed to support their professional needs. Processing IP addresses allows us to offer an efficient advertising experience that targets only audiences connecting to the internet via their B2B IP computer network, hence the audiences are always represented as a cohort of users and never as individual.
  • Necessity for Ad Delivery: IP addresses are crucial for the delivery of IP-based ads. By processing IP addresses, we can ensure that our B2B clients’ advertisements are displayed to the intended audience, optimizing ad performance and promoting their products or services effectively.
  • Balanced Data Processing: We take utmost care to ensure that the processing of IP addresses is conducted in a balanced manner, respecting the privacy rights and interests of individuals. We employ strict technical and organizational measures to safeguard against any unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of IP address data.

 

While we recognize the importance of legitimate interest, we understand that individuals may have concerns regarding their privacy. Therefore, we also offer mechanisms to exercise individual rights and provide clear instructions on how to exercise these rights in our comprehensive privacy policy.
