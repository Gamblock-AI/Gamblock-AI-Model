Learning Begins With Us

LEIT (Leadership-English Institute) was established on September 18, 2005 in Tangerang. Since then, LEIT has grown significantly with new students enrolling each day. LEIT has been educating thousands of students for the past 20 years and most of the students have gained significant improvements.

LEIT always adheres to its basic philosophy: the 5 important leadership values (ICISD – Integrity, Compassion, Intelligence, Self-confidence and Determination) to help students improve their English. If these 5 leadership values are realized, students are able to acquire excellent English skills in a shorter time, since they have conquered the obstacles of diffidence and fear that otherwise inhibit the production of accurate English.

Curriculum Overview

Listening and speaking are perhaps the two skills used most frequently in daily language communication. To be able to perform these two aspects of English well will create self-confidence within learners – a quality particularly useful if they wish to interact with foreigners without inhibitions.

English for Young Learners

LEIT knows the importance of introducing language early on in a child’s development and has developed a program which is specially designed for children.

Primary Program

Children who have learned English since a very young age will find the demands of primary school much easier to bear.

Secondary Program

LEIT offers its Secondary Program based on these principles to accommodate secondary students’ needs and skills.

English for Academic Purposes

Our English for Academic Purposes (EAP) programs are designed for students who wish to apply themselves in an English-speaking academic context such as a university course.

English for Specific Purposes

The English for Specific Purposes Program (ESP), not to be confused with specialized English, is a sphere of English language teaching that includes Business English, Technical English, Scientific English, English for medical professionals, English for waiters, English for tourism, English for Art Purposes, etc.
