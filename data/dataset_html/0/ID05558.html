Your Trusted Partner for Comprehensive Electrical Testing and Maintenance Services
Welcome to DIATECH, one of the oldest and highly respected electrical testing company with over 30 years of experience serving clients across India and the Middle East. We are proud to have our satisfied clients testify to our expertise, professionalism and work experience.

At DIATECH, we cover the full range of assets in testing, including generators, transformers, motors, and all associated cables and switchgear. Our testing services help our clients prevent failures, detect incipient defects, and increase safety, ensuring that their systems are functioning optimally and in compliance with world standards.

With one of the largest resources of testing equipment in India and a team of 60+ experienced professionals, we have the capability to handle projects of all sizes and complexity. Our localized offices in Mumbai, Surat, Abu Dhabi and Saudi Arabia provide timely and efficient service to our clients.

Our top management team has collectively over 200 years of experience that gives us the depth of knowledge and the ability to every project we undertake. Our clients expect us to increase their revenue, and we always ensure that we deliver timely and accurate results for the job within budget.

We appreciate your business and hope you will choose DIATECH as your electrical testing company of choice. With our expert services, we are certain that your safety will be enhanced, failure rates reduced and your business revenue boosted. Get in touch with us today to find out how we can assist you to meet your objectives.
