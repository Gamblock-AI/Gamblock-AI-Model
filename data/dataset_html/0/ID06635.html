THE STOKED ULTRA™️ Weekend
August 28-30, 2026

TRAIL RUNNING ADVENTURES IN REVELSTOKE, BRITISH COLUMBIA

With steep ascents, rugged ridgelines, and stunning alpine views, the STOKED ULTRA™️ weekend offers challenges for those seeking to test themselves in truly WILD spaces. With varied distances, durations, and elevation profiles, the STOKED ULTRA™️ offers something for everyone seeking to experience the REAL STOKE of the mountains in Revelstoke! GET STOKED!

REGISTRATION & PRICING

The STOKED ULTRA™️ weekend features single and multi day adventures:

FRIDAY: BOTTOMS UP! (6km / 1200m)

SATURDAY: STOKED CLIMB 15K, THE STOKED SCRAMBLE™️ 25K,THE STOKED ULTRA™️ 50K

SUNDAY: TRAILSTOKE HALF MARATHON & TRAILSTOKE 30K

FRIDAY - SUNDAY: FULL STOKE and HALF STOKE
