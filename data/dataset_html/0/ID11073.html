With the objective to champion connectivity, friendship and mutual understanding among ASEAN member countries, gaining skills and strength from one another PR best practices ; ASEAN Public Relations Network will conduct regular activities that benefit the PR society in general especially its members.

Greetings from Founder & President of ASEAN Public Relations Network: Prita Kemal Gani, MBA, MCIPR, APR

The Global Alliance for Public Relations and Communications Management will launch another milestone on this new month of APRIL 2021, Global Alliance has dedicated the month of April 2021 for the second virtual ‘Education & Training Month’ as Global Alliance’s continuous support for the profession worldwide. With many PR professionals and academics finding themselves having to limit their mobility and access during the pandemic, there has never been a better time to undertake some self-improvement.

Representing over 300,000 practitioners and academics around the world, Global Alliance is inviting all our members from six continents to contribute to this program. We proudly announce that Global Alliance will deliver an insightful and compelling program of over 60 sessions of Online Webinars and video-on-demand, accessible and free to all its members, hosted by public relations and communications experts from around the world.

      The World Public Relations Forum 2024

      ASEAN Public Relations Network supports the World Public Relations Forum (WPRF) that will be held in Bali, 2024.

      The World Public Relations Forum (WPRF) stands as a premier global conference in the field of public relations and communication management, bringing together professionals, academics, and industry leaders from around the world. Hosted by Global Alliance for Public Relations and Communication Management, the forum serves as a vibrant platform for the exchange of ideas, strategies, and best practices.

      This year’s forum will be held in Bali, Indonesia, in November 2024 co-hosted by Perhumas, Indonesia Public Relations Association in collaboration with Official Event Management Katadata Indonesia. Addressing the pressing challenges and opportunities within the PR industry, WPRF is fostering dialogue on innovation, ethical practices, and the evolving role of PR in society and organizations. The forum facilitates insightful conversations and networking opportunities, enhancing professional development and global collaboration among its attendees.

      WPRF is more than just a conference; it is a global gathering that celebrates the diversity and dynamism of the public relations profession. It underscores the critical role of communication in building and maintaining trust between organizations and their publics. The WPRF brings a unique opportunity to gain global perspectives as well as to meet peers from different cultural backgrounds, and contribute to the collective advancement of the profession.
