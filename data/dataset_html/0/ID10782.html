WHY OFFICE PAKUWON

Pakuwon Group is the pioneer of the Superblock concept in Indonesia, a large-scale integrated mixed-use development of retail shopping malls, offices, condominiums, and hotels. Its successful track record and reputation within the property industry has secured a strong long-term relationship with tenants and buyers, which provides a stable platform for rapid business expansion.

In today’s congested Jakarta, daily commuting consumes a significant amount of valuable time.  Just imagine the hours that could be saved by conveniently living next door to the office.  GANDARIA 8, EightyEighty@Kasablanka, Prudential Centre and Pakuwon Tower not solely implies a location and peerless business facilities, it offers a perfect synergy between work, life-style, shopping and entertaintment.  In addition to its own dedicated parking area, Pakuwon Office’s Jakarta benefits from the parking capacity it shares with Mall.  As peak hours for business and commercial space do not overlap, an abundance of available parking space in ensured at all times.
