How to offer 1:1 appointments through your link in bio

On a Bio Site, you can set an appointment price, length, and description to match what you can uniquely offer—with up to 10 per month. Follow these steps to add 1:1 appointments to your link in bio.

In your browser:

Step 1: Create a Bio Site or log in to Bio Sites

Step 2: In My Sections, select 1:1 Appointments

Step 3: Customize your appointment: Add a title, description, and thumbnail image

Step 4: Set your price and connect to a payment provider like Stripe or PayPal

Step 5: Set your availability (days and times)

And done!

In the app:

Step 1: Open the Unfold app to either create or edit a Bio Site

Step 2: Tap the Bio Sites tab in the Unfold app’s bottom navigation bar

Step 3: Tap the three dots next to your Bio Site URL and select Edit

Step 4: Tap Sections

Step 5: Scroll down to ‘Add More Sections’ and select 1:1 Appointments from the menu

Step 6: Customize your appointment: Add a title, description and thumbnail image

Step 7: Set your price and connect to a payment provider like Stripe or PayPal

Step 8: Set your availability (days and times)

And done!
