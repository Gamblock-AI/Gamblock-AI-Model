Welcome to SEENCH India Foundation

Empowering Futures, Nurturing Dreams

At SEENCH, we believe in the transformative power of education. We envision a future where every individual, regardless of their background, has the opportunity to thrive and succeed. Through our relentless dedication to empowerment and hope, we are pioneering change in the tribal villages of Karjat and beyond.

SEENCH annually impacts nearly 600 tribal children over 25 villages/sub-villages. We believe, our collective efforts lay the foundation for thriving communities and empowered individuals.

What We Do

How We Serve The Community

At SEENCH, we work towards making the environment conducive to education and holistic growth, and work hard to ensure our kids stay excited and equipped to learn and grow.

Teachers

Our SEENCH Shakhas initiative employs dedicated teachers who deliver training to children on numeracy, literacy skills, digital education and sports.

Transportation

We operate buses and vans across two shifts daily to facilitate the journey of middle and high school students, overcoming the challenging terrain.

Material

We equip our schools, teachers and students with notebooks, books, stationery, sports equipment, computers, printers, and e-learning resources to ensure a comprehensive educational experience for all.

Requirements of Children

We provide all children with uniforms, water bottles, school bags, pencils, pens, pouches, raincoats, rain sandals, and umbrellas to ensure that the children have what they need to attend school and also stay excited to be a part of it.

Repairs & Maintenance

Rains often play havoc with the school infrastructure and we support the schools with repair and maintenance work, to keep the learning environment safe and conducive.

Engagement

We regularly engage with all stakeholders, including students, government teachers, SEENCH teachers, village elders, and village heads to keep all those involved happy to be associated with the good work being done.

How it Started

Guiding Light of Hope

SEENCH stands as a guiding light of hope, a non-profit organization dedicated to transforming lives in Karjat through the power of education. Nestled in the heart of rural Maharashtra, SEENCH was born over a decade ago out of a collective passion to uplift the underserved youth of this region. Our journey is etched with stories of resilience, determination, and unwavering commitment to creating a brighter future for the children and young adults of Karjat.

Educational Outreach

Through partnerships with local schools and community centers, we deliver educational workshops, tutoring sessions, and mentorship programs to supplement classroom learning. Our aim is not only to enhance academic performance but also to instill confidence, critical thinking skills, and a love for learning.

Scholarship Programs

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to pursue their dreams. That’s why we offer scholarships to promising students from low-income backgrounds, covering tuition fees, books, and other educational expenses. By removing financial barriers, we open doors to higher education and brighter futures.

Skill Development Initiatives

In collaboration with local businesses and vocational training centers, we provide skill development workshops and vocational courses to equip young adults with the practical skills needed for employment or entrepreneurship. Whether it’s computer literacy, tailoring, or agricultural training, we empower individuals to chart their path toward economic independence.

Community Empowerment Projects

SEENCH is more than just an educational organization; we are a catalyst for positive change in the community. Through initiatives such as environmental awareness campaigns, health clinics, and women’s empowerment workshops, we address broader social issues that impact the well-being of our beneficiaries and their families.

How it Started

Guiding Light of Hope

SEENCH India Foundation is a beacon of hope, dedicated to transforming lives in Karjat through the power of education. Established nearly a decade and a half ago in rural Maharashtra, SEENCH was born from a collective passion to uplift underserved youth and empower them to break the cycle of poverty. Our journey is filled with stories of resilience, determination, and a steadfast commitment to creating a brighter future for the children and young adults of Karjat. We believe that education is the foundation of empowerment and progress, and through our initiatives, we provide quality education, holistic development opportunities, and essential resources to foster growth, self-sufficiency, and community well-being.

SEENCH Foundation

Supporting community Growth

In the pursuit of fostering thriving communities, SEENCH has been actively engaged in various impactful activities and infrastructure projects. From repairing primary schools to donating water tankers, these endeavours aim to uplift the lives of individuals and contribute to sustainable development

Repair and Revitalization of Primary Schools
  • SEENCH undertook the repair and revitalization of primary schools in underserved areas.
  • Through thorough assessments, damaged infrastructures such as classrooms, roofs, and sanitation facilities are identified and addressed.
  • Skilled labour and quality materials are employed to ensure lasting improvements, fostering conducive learning environments for children.
  • By investing in education infrastructure, SEENCH strives to promote access to quality education, empowering future generations with the tools for success.
Creation & Maintenance of Infrastructure
  • Recognizing the vital importance of access to clean water, SEENCH constructed water tanks to schools facing water supply issues.
  • Water Tanks: To ensure consistent access to clean water, SEENCH India Foundation has installed water tanks in schools, addressing a critical need for safe drinking water and supporting the daily requirements of students and staff. This initiative enhances hygiene standards and contributes to a healthier learning environment.
  • Wall compound: constructed secure compound walls around school premises, creating a safe and protected area for students to learn and play. These walls also help safeguard school infrastructure and reinforce the commitment to providing a secure educational environment.
  • Waterproofing of roof: We’ve undertaken waterproofing projects to protect school roofs, ensuring classrooms remain dry and functional throughout all seasons. This essential maintenance work prevents leaks, enhances durability, and helps create a comfortable space conducive to uninterrupted learning.
  • Painting of schools: To cultivate an inspiring and welcoming atmosphere, SEENCH has painted school buildings, transforming them into vibrant spaces. A fresh coat of paint not only uplifts the aesthetics of the school but also fosters a sense of pride and motivation among students and staff alike.
Impact Assessment and Monitoring
  • SEENCH prioritises impact assessment and monitoring to gauge the effectiveness of its initiatives.
  • Through rigorous evaluation methodologies, both quantitative and qualitative data are collected to measure outcomes and identify areas for improvement.
  • Feedback mechanisms involving community stakeholders are integral to ensuring relevance and sustainability.
  • Transparent reporting practices further enhance accountability and enable informed decision-making for future endeavours.
Community Engagement and Empowerment
  • Central to SEENCH's approach is community engagement and empowerment, fostering ownership and sustainability of projects.
  • Consultative processes involving residents, leaders, and organizations are conducted to ascertain needs and priorities.
  • Capacity-building activities are implemented to equip community members with skills and knowledge, enabling active participation in project implementation and management.
  • By empowering communities, SEENCH catalyzes transformative change from within, nurturing self-reliant and resilient societies.
Partnerships and Collaboration
  • SEENCH recognizes the significance of partnerships and collaboration in achieving its goals.
  • Strategic alliances with governmental agencies, non-profit organizations, and corporate entities amplify the impact of interventions.
  • Through shared expertise, resources, and networks, synergies are leveraged to address complex challenges more effectively.
  • These collaborative efforts foster innovation and scalability, driving systemic change at local, regional, and national levels.
Sustainability and Long-Term Vision
  • Sustainability is at the core of SEENCH's interventions, guided by a long-term vision for holistic development.
  • Interventions are designed with consideration for environmental, social, and economic sustainability principles.
  • Investments in renewable energy, waste management, and ecosystem conservation contribute to a greener and more resilient future.
  • By fostering local ownership, building institutional capacity, and promoting inclusive growth, SEENCH lays the foundation for lasting positive change.

Through its diverse range of activities and infrastructure developments, SEENCH exemplifies a commitment to enhancing communities’ well-being and resilience. From repairing primary schools to donating water tank, each initiative is driven by a shared vision of sustainable development and inclusive progress. By prioritizing community engagement, partnerships, and long-term sustainability, SEENCH strives to leave a lasting legacy of positive impact, empowering individuals and transforming societies for generations to come.
