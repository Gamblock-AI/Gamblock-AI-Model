Welcome

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints currently operates over 350 missions in 162 nations, where more than 53,000 missionaries, colloquially known as "Mormon missionaries", are currently serving.

LDS Mission Network lists every mission ever organized since 1837, many of which have an accompanying alumni web site. Learn more about the LDS Church here.
