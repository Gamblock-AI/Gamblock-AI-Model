Frequently Asked Questions

What is a QR Code?

A QR Code (Quick Response Code) is a square barcode that stores information like a website link, text, Wi-Fi details, or files, which can be read instantly using a camera or QR scanner.

How do I generate a QR Code?

Enter your content (URL, text, Wi-Fi, PDF, etc.), customize the design if needed, and download the generated QR code. No registration is required.

Are the generated QR Codes free to use?

Yes, all QR codes generated here are completely free and can be used for personal or commercial purposes.

What types of QR Codes can I create?

You can create QR codes for websites, text, Wi-Fi, email, phone numbers, social media links, PDFs, images, and more.

Do static QR Codes expire?

No. Static QR codes never expire and will work as long as the embedded content exists.

Can I customize the QR Code design?

Yes, you can customize colors, add logos, change shapes, and adjust error correction while keeping the QR code scannable.
