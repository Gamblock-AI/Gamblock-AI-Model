Grand Galaxy Park

Grand Galaxy Park is a modern shopping and entertainment center located in Grand Galaxy City, Bekasi. In addition, Grand Galaxy Park also features thrilling rides and recreational areas. With its strategic location and various facilities, Grand Galaxy Park has become a favorite destination for residents of Bekasi and the surrounding areas.
