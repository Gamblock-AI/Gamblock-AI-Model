    Instant Likes for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube

    With around two billion active users logging into Facebook each month, it’s fair to say Facebook is the most-used social media platform available. Not only does the website/app have attention in the US, but it’s also the same in the UK, Canada, and all over the world. Why? Initially, we used Facebook to keep up with friends and even reach out to old friends we lost contact with many years ago. Over the years, the uses of Facebook have expanded to include sending invitations, making announcements, scheduling events, reading the news, and more.

    However, Facebook is more than simply a platform to talk with friends and announce an engagement; it has also evolved into an excellent marketing tool for businesses. Since users are sharing their experiences with certain products/services, a good profile on Facebook goes a long way. In fact, it has become the modern-day word of mouth. You’re in big trouble if a few customers write bad reviews on Facebook.

    What do you search for while looking for businesses on Facebook before deciding whether to contact them? You could check at locations, reviews, services, and other details. But there’s one thing that we should all check first, which is the LIKES.

    Nowadays, the number of likes of a brand, business, or even a celebrity has determined its reputation and trustworthiness. With every like, this suggests a happy experience. As most Facebook users head towards the profile with the most likes, this generates even more interest, more comments, and more instant likes. Because human nature is to follow the crowd, and crowds will always attract crowds, the page soon snowballs.
