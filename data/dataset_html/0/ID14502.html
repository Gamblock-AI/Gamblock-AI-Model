Bejarah Raya @ UNIMAS Global 2026: A Celebration of Tradition, Experience and Connection

“Bejarah”, derived from the Sarawak Malay dialect, reflects the practice of ziarah or visiting, a deeply rooted cultural tradition in Sarawak. Especially during Hari Raya, Bejarah is more than a social visit. It is a symbol of openness, unity and mutual respect, where homes are opened to welcome family, relatives and friends regardless of background. This tradition captures the true spirit of togetherness that defines Sarawak’s multicultural identity. More >>

SLUSE Joint Field Work 2026 Strengthens International Collaboration and Community Engagement in Sarikei

SLUSE-M (SLUSE Malaysia) is a field-based academic programme that focuses on sustainable land use and natural resource management. It combines classroom learning with hands-on fieldwork and close engagement with local communities. At UNIMAS, the programme is supported by the Faculty of Resource Science and Technology (FRST) and the Faculty of Social Sciences and Humanities (FSSH), showing a strong interdisciplinary approach.

During the 11-day field course, participants were placed in six rural locations in Sarikei: Kampung Hilir Lama Selalang; Rumah Ningkan (Ulu Sungai Paoh); Rumah Langgie (Sungai Tapang, Sare); Rumah Grace (Sungai Enseluai, Sare); Rumah Tayan (Sungai Lepong, Ulu Sarikei); and Rumah Sibar (Lubok Lembak, Ulu Sarikei). At these locations, participants carried out field studies, interacted with local communities, and learned about real challenges related to environmental sustainability and resource management.
