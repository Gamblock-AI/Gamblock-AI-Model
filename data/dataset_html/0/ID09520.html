Purpose

The purpose of Bali Blockchain Center (BBC) is multi-faceted. It aims to develop a safe and secure ecosystem for Blockchain and Web3, with positive implications for Indonesian society. BBC serves as a trusted source of information, an education center to produce skilled talent, a community hub to spread awareness and encourage participation, and an incubation center to support startups and entrepreneurs working on Web3-based projects. By collaborating with the global and local blockchain community, BBC aims to make a significant impact on the development of blockchain technology in Indonesia.
