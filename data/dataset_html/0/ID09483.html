Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PresenterMedia.

Are PresenterMedia templates compatible with Google Slides?

Yes. Many of our PowerPoint templates can be uploaded directly into Google Slides for easy online editing. To get started, simply open Google Drive, click New → File Upload, and select your downloaded PresenterMedia template file. Google Slides will maintain layouts, text, and most graphic elements so you can quickly start editing in a browser. While animations may appear differently in Google Slides than in PowerPoint, your slides remain fully editable, making our templates an excellent option for collaboration with teams or classrooms that use Google Workspace.

Can I edit colors, text, and images in your templates?

Absolutely. Every template on PresenterMedia is designed to be fully customizable. You can edit text, adjust colors to match your brand palette, and replace placeholder images with your own graphics or photos. Our presentation clipart and graphics can also be swapped in to instantly transform the look and feel of a template. Many of our PowerPoint animations and video backgrounds can be added into slides for extra impact. This flexibility ensures you can create unique presentations that match your theme, audience, or branding goals.

Do I need PowerPoint to use PresenterMedia content?

No, you are not limited to PowerPoint. While our content is optimized for Microsoft PowerPoint, many of our templates also work seamlessly in Google Slides and Apple Keynote. Beyond templates, you can use our video backgrounds, graphics, and animations in a variety of other software programs. You can even export images and videos to use in websites, emails, or social media posts. This makes PresenterMedia a versatile resource whether you’re presenting in PowerPoint, collaborating in Google Slides, or building visuals for marketing and digital projects.

Is the AI Presentation Builder included with my subscription?

Yes. All active subscribers get access to our AI Presentation Maker. This powerful tool allows you to instantly generate professional slide decks with the help of AI. You simply enter your presentation topic, select the number of slides, choose a theme, and our AI will generate a complete presentation outline with slide layouts ready to download. You can use the presentation as-is or customize it further with our graphics, animations, and templates. This makes creating polished, professional presentations faster and easier than ever.

Can I use PresenterMedia graphics in commercial projects?

Yes. An active subscription gives you commercial usage rights for our templates, graphics, and animations, making them perfect for business presentations, client work, marketing videos, and social media campaigns. For example, you can use our customizable clipart in branded slides or add video backgrounds to a product demo. The only restriction is that you may not resell or redistribute our raw template files or designs as stand-alone products. As long as they’re used in your own projects, presentations, or campaigns, they are fully covered under your subscription.

A subscription to PresenterMedia gives you unlimited downloads of our entire library of content, including PowerPoint templates, PowerPoint animations, presentation clipart, and video backgrounds. You also gain full access to our design customization tools like SlideClips Video Maker and the AI Presentation Builder. New content is added weekly, giving you a constant stream of fresh templates, graphics, and designs to keep your presentations engaging and up to date. With one subscription, you have everything needed to create professional, polished, and attention-grabbing presentations.
