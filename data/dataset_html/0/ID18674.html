Time2play.com: A trusted QMRA website for the United States market

Members of QMRA

Quality Mark Responsible Affiliates (QMRA) is a Quality Mark for iGaming websites that provide information for consumers. Time2play.com US is a QMRA member, compliant with our US Code. This means that Time2play.com US is compliant with the following: 

1. Channelling Objectives:

Members may only advertise licensed operators and must remain strictly factual when mentioning illegal ones, avoiding any promotional language. They are prohibited from providing services that facilitate unlicensed gambling offers.

2. Responsible Advertising: 

Members must ensure that their marketing is balanced and does not target individuals under 21 or excluded persons. They must clearly state the legal age requirement in a visible and logical manner.

3. Preventing deception:

Members must ensure transparency by providing accurate and complete information about gambling offers, avoiding misleading claims. They must clearly communicate the nature, workings, and benefits of the offer without omissions or exaggerations that could create a false impression. Statements regarding legal rights, obligations, and remedies related to transactions must be truthful and easy to understand. Additionally, affiliates must explicitly state when terms and conditions apply, ensuring that users are fully aware of any restrictions or requirements. They are also prohibited from falsely implying sponsorships, approvals, or affiliations, and must not describe gambling offers as “free” unless this is entirely accurate.

4. Contribution to responsible gambling:

Members must prominently display links to responsible gaming websites and gambling helplines on every webpage, ensuring easy access to support resources for individuals facing gambling-related issues. These references should be easily noticeable, such as in the footer or other permanent sections of the website. The affiliate must also ensure that the provided information is up to date and correctly directs visitors to the appropriate authorities or organizations. By maintaining clear and visible references, affiliates contribute to creating a safer gambling environment and encourage responsible gaming behavior among users.

Time2play.com US and Responsible Gambling 

QMRA members attach high importance to responsible gambling and addiction prevention. The website you visited contains responsible gambling information that is relevant to your country. QMRA Compliance Officers have checked the accuracy of the information provided on the website you visited. If you need help or further information on responsible gambling, the website you visited will offer this. We also offer additional insights on our responsible gambling page.

Company Verification and Relationship with QMRA

QMRA has verified the identity of the organization behind Time2play.com US and confirmed that it is an established, demonstrably reliable entity with a registered business. QMRA maintains direct contact with the management of this organization regarding the operation of the website. A contractual relationship exists between QMRA and this company, under which periodic compliance checks are carried out and clear agreements have been made concerning adherence to applicable laws and regulations.

Regular checks on Time2play.com US

To make sure the website you visited consistently aligns with the QMRA Code, QMRA Compliance Officers will perform regular checks. Annually, a QMRA member will be provided with a detailed report for recertification. Reports might contain feedback. After the feedback has been addressed, a QMRA Compliance Report will be shared with the member. Reports are based on the most current versions of the Country Codes.
