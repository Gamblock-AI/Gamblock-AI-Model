Chandra Asri Group’s strategic expansion and acquisitions have solidified its position as Southeast Asia’s leading energy, chemical and infrastructure solutions provider.  Our significance to various sectors in Indonesia has been recognized by the Indonesia government, granting us the status of a vital national object. We have the largest integrated petrochemical complex in Southeast Asia, including a pioneering Naphtha Cracker in Indonesia, along with a world-class Chlor-Alkali and Ethylene Dichloride (CA-EDC) plant that will commence operations soon, as well as a comprehensive infrastructure business. All of this is underscored by a history of more than 30 years during which we have served as growth partners for Indonesia and across Southeast Asia.
