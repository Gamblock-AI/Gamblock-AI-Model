Our Manifesto

How we drive sustainable change by bringing you awesome food
The New Green Revolution

Food supply-chain that takes care of the people and the planet. We believe that simple action like eating is the solution to reverse climate change.

 Fruit & Vegetable

Not only that we love organic agriculture, our inclusive supply-chain also supports conventional farmers to reduce synthetic chemical use through our transition program

 Plant Based Protein

We support consumers to transition into a plat based diet by connecting them to a wide variety of alternative source of protein

 Protein

We work with organic and high welfare farms that ensures no use of antibiotics, hormones, and beta-agonist.

 Sea food

Our collaboration with Ao Yor fishing village ensures fair trade and regenerative fishing through the use of small boats, primitive tools, seasonal catching, and marine restoration program with the Department of Fishery

 Healthy Food

For us, sustainable food should also heal our body and mind. It's important to curate "real food" from natural ingredients with no artificial sugars and preservatives. This is for both our in-house brands and partners.

 Carbon Neutrality

We are committed to a measurable method of carbon removal through the us of Bio Char. Our impact extends to Bio Char application in conventional farms aiming to reduce the use of synthetic chemicals among farmers around Thailand.
