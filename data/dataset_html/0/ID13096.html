6. Measurements execution & Happydemics methodology

Regardless of the type of License subscribed to, the Measurements are conducted based on Respondent Data obtained and collected by Happydemics using a unique methodology, which involves disseminating a questionnaire via online advertising spaces or other websites/mobile applications. The following options are available to increase the likelihood of exposure:

- Tag/Pixel Integration: Retargeting individuals/audiences provided by the Client;

- Points of Interest (POI): Respondents are qualified based on their presence within a specified radius around a network of display screens (D)OOH, using location data. Only individuals located within this specific radius are surveyed;

- Batch of IDs: The Client uploads a data file containing identifiers and consent variables. The Client is solely responsible for the content of this data file and for obtaining the necessary consents;

- Broadcasting Framework: The Client uploads a data file to define the dissemination parameters of their campaign.

Once generated, the Measurements are made available to the Client on the Platform and cannot be modified. The Measurements are accessible to the Client as long as they hold a License.
