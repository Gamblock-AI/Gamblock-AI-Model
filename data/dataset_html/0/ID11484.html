Introduction

YAYASAN MR. D.I.Y. is the philanthropic arm of MR.DIY Group [M] Berhad, Malaysia’s largest home improvement retailer. Established in June 2023, the foundation was born out of MR.DIY’s longstanding commitment to community support. It serves as a platform to implement impactful, community-driven initiatives that aim to create positive and lasting change in the lives of Malaysians. Supporting and uplifting communities has always been at the heart of MR.DIY’s ethos.

Feature Program And Initiative
Environment Focus

The Environment Initiative is a community-based program under Yayasan MR. D.I.Y. aimed at raising environmental awareness and promoting sustainable practices. It includes activities such as Tree planting, beach cleaning, and hiking trail conservation to inspire collective action in preserving the environment for future generations.

Stem Discovery On Wheels

STEM Discovery On Wheels is a community-driven mobile outreach program that brings interactive Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) learning experiences directly to schools and communities. Featuring STEM Tinkering Kits, it aims to spark interest in STEM through engaging and accessible learning.

MR DIY Art Competition 2025

The MR.DIY Art Competition 2025 is a nationwide, community-driven art initiative that invites the public and students to express their creativity through visual arts. Focused on themes that reflect values, identity, and community, the competition encourages talents to share their perspectives while promoting cultural appreciation and artistic expression.

DIY Made Simple 2025

DIY Made Simple 2025 is a community-driven innovation challenge that encourages secondary school students to develop simple, practical solutions for everyday problems using do-it-yourself methods. The competition fosters creativity, teamwork, and hands-on learning, empowering youth to contribute meaningful ideas that benefit their communities.
