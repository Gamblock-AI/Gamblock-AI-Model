Our Story

Welcome to Zobud Bistro

Located in the heart of downtown, Zobud Bistro brings you the finest American cuisine and the freshest seafood from coast to coast. Our passion for quality ingredients and authentic flavors has made us a local favorite since 2015.

From our signature hand-crafted burgers to our daily catch specials, every dish is prepared with care and served with pride. Our chefs combine traditional American recipes with modern culinary techniques to create an unforgettable dining experience.
