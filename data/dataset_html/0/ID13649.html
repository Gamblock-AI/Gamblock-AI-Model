    Baobab Safari Resort

    Wake Up to Spectacular African-Style Wildlife Views in East Java

    Experience a unique safari resort stay in Pasuruan, East Java, located just minutes from Taman Safari Prigen. At Baobab Safari Resort, guests can enjoy breathtaking views of roaming giraffes, zebras, rhinos, and hippos directly from their private balcony, creating an unforgettable wildlife experience.

    Featuring 148 modern safari-themed rooms and 4-star facilities, Baobab Safari Resort is one of the best family-friendly resorts near Taman Safari Prigen. The resort combines luxury accommodation, nature, and close encounters with African wildlife, making it the perfect destination for family holidays, weekend getaways, and nature lovers visiting East Java.
