WHO WE ARE

Early Stage Venture Capital Fund

GoodWorks Angel Fund is an early-stage VC fund launched by serial entrepreneurs Vishwas Mudagal and Sonia Sharma. This new undertaking under the GoodWorks group will invest and support startups & entrepreneurs across a spectrum of industry verticals. Along with the investment, GoodWorks will provide support to portfolio companies in terms of office space, technology, operations, marketing, and other support services that are critical for scaling up startups.
