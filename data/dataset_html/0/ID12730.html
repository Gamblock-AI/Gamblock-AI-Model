Privacy Notice

Effective as of April 21, 2025

Lutkan B.V. ("Lutkan," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, partners, and customers. This Privacy Notice describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you use our media buying platform, NeuBids, and our monetization product, NeuPubs.

We have prepared this Privacy Notice to describe the types of data we collect from you and explain how, why, and when we collect this data to provide our Services to our Customers. In this Privacy Notice, the term ‘Customer’ refers to any business, advertiser, or publisher using Lutkan’s NeuBids or NeuPubs platforms. This Privacy Notice also identifies what rights you have concerning the collection of this data, such as the right to opt-out of interest-based advertising (also commonly referred to as targeted or personalized advertising).

Please note that this Privacy Notice does not apply to data we collect when you visit a Lutkan website, including https://www.lutkan.com/, www.neubids.com, and https://www.neupubs.com/. To learn how we handle data collected through our websites, please refer to the Lutkan Website Privacy Notice.

About Lutkan's Services

Lutkan provides media buying and monetization technologies designed to help advertisers and publishers improve performance through data-driven optimization.

With our proprietary technology, NeuBids (for programmatic media buying) and NeuPubs (for publisher monetization), Lutkan helps businesses identify high-value audiences, improve ad relevance, and optimize return on ad spend (ROAS). By leveraging real-time data insights, neural-driven optimizations, and transparent programmatic strategies, Lutkan enables advertisers to reach the right users at the right time while providing publishers with higher yield and sustainable revenue growth.

Lutkan has two different products:

NeuBids

NeuBids is a neural-powered (‘Neural-powered’ refers to our use of machine learning and AI algorithms for real-time decision making and optimization) media buying platform designed for advertisers looking to maximize their reach and engagement across digital channels. Whether you're a brand, agency, or performance marketer, NeuBids makes it seamless and efficient to execute high-impact programmatic campaigns.

By leveraging neural-driven optimizations and real-time data insights, NeuBids enables advertisers to target the right audience, refine bidding strategies, and improve ad performance across multiple channels. The platform helps businesses attract new customers, re-engage existing audiences, and drive conversions through connected, personalized ad experiences.

In addition, NeuBids provides advanced measurement and attribution tools, allowing advertisers to gain deep insights into their campaign performance, optimize their media spend, and make data-driven decisions for continuous growth.

NeuPubs

NeuPubs is a neural-powered monetization platform designed for publishers and media owners who want to maximize their advertising revenue and optimize their digital inventory. Whether you operate a website, app, or digital platform, NeuPubs helps you connect with high-value advertisers, increase fill rates, and improve overall yield through intelligent, data-driven monetization strategies.

With NeuPubs, publishers gain access to advanced audience insights, enabling them to attract premium demand partners and deliver high-performing ad placements. The platform leverages neural-driven optimizations to match the right ads with the right audiences, ensuring higher engagement and better revenue outcomes.

Additionally, NeuPubs provides transparent reporting and measurement tools, allowing publishers to track revenue performance, analyze ad effectiveness, and refine their monetization strategies. By integrating seamlessly with multiple demand sources, NeuPubs ensures publishers can efficiently monetize their inventory while maintaining a high-quality user experience.

Interest-Based Advertising with Our Products

Lutkan’s neural-powered technology helps advertisers and publishers deliver highly relevant ads to users based on their interests and online behavior. This approach, known as interest-based advertising, ensures that ads are shown to people most likely to engage with them, improving both user experience and campaign effectiveness.

Many of these ads appear on websites and mobile apps that rely on advertising revenue to offer free content. When you visit a website or app (a “Digital Property”) that partners with Lutkan, or see an ad delivered through NeuBids on a third-party platform, we may collect certain non-personal data from your device, as outlined in our Privacy Notice. Our platform then processes this data—along with additional insights—to help advertisers serve ads that are more relevant to you.

Interest-based advertising benefits both users and businesses:

  • For users: Ads are better tailored to your interests, reducing irrelevant or intrusive experiences.
  • For publishers: Websites and apps can optimize revenue by delivering ads that are more likely to be clicked.
  • For advertisers: Brands can reach high-intent audiences in real time, making their campaigns more effective.

By leveraging neural-driven optimizations, NeuBids ensures that advertisers of all sizes—from startups to global brands—can compete effectively, reaching the right audience without requiring massive budgets. At the same time, NeuPubs empowers publishers to maximize their ad revenue while maintaining a high-quality user experience.

Example of Interest-Based Advertising

Imagine you’re researching new running shoes for your workout routine. You visit Google and search for “best running shoes for long distances.” A list of websites appears, including one from XYZ Sportswear. You browse their site, looking at different shoe models, but you don’t make a purchase just yet.

Since XYZ Sportswear uses NeuBids for its advertising campaigns, our neural-powered technology recognizes that your device visited their website and showed interest in specific products.

Later, as you browse the web, you see ads from XYZ Sportswear reminding you about the running shoes you viewed—perhaps with a limited-time discount or a special offer for first-time buyers. Additionally, you may see ads from other retailers offering related sports gear, such as athletic wear or smart fitness accessories, to complement your purchase.

This interest-based advertising approach helps brands reconnect with potential customers, improves ad relevance for users, and enables publishers to monetize their digital properties more effectively through NeuPubs.

Measurement and Attribution

Beyond interest-based advertising, Lutkan provides advanced measurement and attribution tools that help advertisers and publishers understand the impact of their campaigns and optimize their strategies.

Using neural-powered analytics, NeuBids enables advertisers to track key performance indicators (KPIs), such as conversions, engagement rates, and return on ad spend (ROAS). By collecting unique identifiers (e.g., cookie IDs, device IDs, or anonymized session data), Lutkan can measure whether users who saw an ad or visited a Digital Property later took a specific action, such as making a purchase, signing up for a newsletter, or returning to the website.

NeuBids: Media Buying Attribution

  • Tracks the effectiveness of ad campaigns across multiple channels.
  • Identifies which audience segments are most engaged with specific ads.
  • Measures conversion paths to help advertisers refine their targeting and budget allocation.

NeuPubs: Monetization Insights

  • Provides publishers with in-depth reporting on ad performance and revenue generation.
  • Uses neural-driven optimizations to determine which ad formats and placements generate the highest engagement.
  • Helps publishers understand audience behavior and improve inventory yield.

Example Use Case

A sporting goods retailer using NeuBids may want to know whether users who saw an ad for running shoes eventually made a purchase. By tracking unique identifiers from ad impressions to completed sales, NeuBids helps the retailer measure ad effectiveness and refine targeting strategies.

With real-time reporting and deep attribution analysis, Lutkan ensures that advertisers and publishers have the data-driven insights needed to make informed decisions and maximize the value of every ad impression.

  • Setting cookies related to advertising and analytics.
  • Processing data for personalized ad targeting.

1.2 Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)

Lutkan process data to fulfill contractual obligations and provide services, such as:

  • Delivering advertising and monetization services via NeuBids and NeuPubs.
  • Measuring campaign performance and optimizing ad delivery.

1.3 Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Lutkan processes certain data based on our legitimate business interests, provided that these do not override users' rights. Examples include:

  • Fraud detection and security monitoring.
  • Frequency capping to avoid excessive ad repetition.
  • Aggregated reporting and analytics for advertisers and publishers.
  • Under CCPA/CPRA: We ensure that all data classified as "personal information" (as defined under CCPA) is processed in accordance with users' rights.

1.4 Legal Obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR & CCPA Business Compliance Requirements)

Lutkan may process personal data to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, such as:

  • Responding to regulatory or governmental requests.
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws, including CCPA/CPRA provisions related to consumer rights.

1.5 Vital Interests (Article 6(1)(d) GDPR) (If Applicable)

In rare cases, we may process data to protect someone’s life or safety when legally required.

6. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data in the provision of our Services for the purposes explained below.

Data We Automatically Collect from Your Device

When you interact with websites or applications that use NeuBids (for media buying) or NeuPubs (for monetization), Lutkan may automatically collect certain non-personal data from your device—unless you have opted out or declined to provide consent. This data is used to improve ad relevance, optimize campaign performance, and enhance user experience.

6.1 Cookies

We assign a unique identifier (cookie ID) to your device when you visit a Digital Property (website or app) that uses Lutkan’s technology. This helps us understand your online behavior and allows advertisers to serve relevant ads when you visit other sites.

6.2 Device Information

Technical details about the device you use to access a Digital Property, including:

  • Operating system (e.g., Windows, macOS, iOS, Android)
  • Device type (e.g., smartphone, tablet, desktop)
  • Mobile device advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
  • Other unique identifiers (e.g., Android ID, UDID for older iPhones)

6.3 IP Address

We collect the IP address of your device when you access a Digital Property. This helps in fraud prevention, security checks, and geo-targeting for ad delivery.

6.4 Location Data

Based on your IP address, we determine non-precise geographic information, such as:

  • Country, state, city, and postal code
  • Language preferences (e.g., showing German ads if you're in Germany)
    Lutkan does not collect precise GPS coordinates.

6.5 Browser Data

Technical information about the browser you are using, including:

  • Browser type (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Browser version and capabilities (for ad rendering)

6.6 Activity on Digital Properties

We collect behavioral data about your activity on sites or apps that use Lutkan’s technology. This may include:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page.
  • Buttons or links clicked.
  • Products viewed, added to cart, or purchased.
  • What site or ad led you to the Customer’s website

6.7 Ad Data

Information about ads you have been shown or interacted with, such as:

  • The number of times an ad was displayed.
  • The website or app where the ad appeared.
  • Whether you clicked, viewed, or engaged with the ad.

Why We Collect This Data

This data allows Lutkan to:

  • Deliver relevant ads tailored to your interests.
  • Optimize campaign performance for advertisers and publishers.
  • Improve ad targeting without using personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Provide better user experience with meaningful content.

We prioritize data privacy and transparency and ensure compliance with GDPR, TCF, and other global privacy regulations.

Data We Collect from Third-Party Sources

In addition to the data, we collect automatically from devices, Lutkan also receives data from third-party sources to enhance campaign effectiveness and provide deeper insights for advertisers and publishers.

6.8 Campaign Performance Data

We collect data related to the performance of advertising campaigns, whether served through NeuBids or other advertising platforms. This includes:

  • Impressions, clicks, and conversions on ads served.
  • Engagement metrics (e.g., time spent on landing pages, interactions with ads)
  • Attribution data (e.g., determining which ads led to a purchase or site visit)
  • Cross-platform ad effectiveness (e.g., how ads perform across different channels such as web, mobile, and CTV)

This information helps advertisers optimize their bidding strategies, improve audience targeting, and maximize return on ad spend (ROAS) while allowing publishers to better understand ad revenue potential.

6.9 How We Use the Data We Collect

At Lutkan, we leverage neural-powered insights to create a seamless, data-driven advertising and monetization ecosystem that benefits both advertisers and publishers. Our two core products—NeuBids (for programmatic media buying) and NeuPubs (for publisher monetization)—work to ensure that advertisers reach the right audience with optimal campaign performance, while publishers maximize their ad revenue without compromising user experience.

Our data-driven approach helps businesses deliver relevant and effective advertising while ensuring ad-supported content remains free for users.

6.10 Optimized Ad Delivery & Performance

Our platform analyzes browser, device, and network data to ensure ads are delivered efficiently and optimized for performance:
NeuBids ensures ads load quickly, display correctly, and are served in viewable placements
NeuPubs maximizes fill rates, ensuring that publishers earn the highest possible revenue from each ad impression.

For example, if a publisher’s website receives mobile-heavy traffic, NeuPubs prioritizes mobile-friendly ad formats, while NeuBids ensures that advertisers serve properly formatted ads that fit those placements.

6.11 Audience Segmentation & Smart Targeting

  • Lutkan uses advanced neural algorithms to segment audiences into different behavioral groups, such as:
  • High-intent buyers (users likely to make a purchase)
  • Casual browsers (users still researching)
  • Frequent content consumers (users engaging with specific types of content)

This segmentation allows:
Advertisers to refine their targeting and bid more effectively
Publishers to better understand their audience and attract premium advertisers.

For example, a travel publisher can use NeuPubs to identify users researching vacation destinations and connect them with advertisers selling travel packages through NeuBids.

6.12 Frequency Capping & Ad Sequencing

To avoid ad fatigue, NeuBids and NeuPubs work together to:
Control how often a user sees the same ad (frequency capping)
Deliver ad sequences in the correct order, ensuring a smooth advertising journey.

For instance, if a user sees an initial brand awareness ad, NeuBids ensures they later receive a follow-up offer ad, leading to a higher conversion rate.

6.13 Reporting, Attribution & Yield Optimization

Lutkan provides real-time analytics to both advertisers and publishers:

NeuBids helps advertisers track ad performance through:

  • Impression and click tracking.
  • Conversion analysis (identifying which ads lead to purchases or sign-ups)
  • Attribution modeling (determining which touchpoints drive sales)

NeuPubs helps publishers improve monetization with:

  • Revenue reporting, including fill rates and CPM trends.
  • Yield optimization, adjusting bid floors to maximize revenue

For example, if NeuBids detects that an ad has low conversions, it can adjust targeting. Similarly, if NeuPubs detects high engagement on a certain ad placement, it will prioritize higher-paying advertisers for that slot.

Lutkan ensures that all advertising activity is safe, transparent, and compliant with privacy regulations, including GDPR, TCF, and CCPA.

Fraud detection – Identifies and blocks invalid traffic (IVT), click fraud, and bot activity
Brand safety – Ensures that advertisers do not appear on inappropriate or low-quality websites
User privacy protection – Adheres to industry-standard consent frameworks and ensures compliance with user preferences.

For example, if NeuPubs detects suspicious traffic patterns from a website, it can prevent advertisers from wasting budget on fake impressions.

12.3 Right to Withdraw Consent

When processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Once withdrawn, we will stop processing your personal data unless we have a lawful reason to continue.

12.4 Right to Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten")

You may request the deletion of your personal data in certain cases, such as:

  • If you have withdrawn your consent for processing.
  • If you have objected to processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds for continuing it.
  • If your data has been processed unlawfully.

12.5 Right to Rectification

If the personal data we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to request that we correct or update it.

12.6 Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to request the restriction of processing in the following cases:

  • You contest the accuracy of your personal data, and we need time to verify it.
  • The processing is unlawful, but you prefer restriction over deletion.
  • We no longer need the data, but you require it for legal claims.
  • You object to processing, and we are assessing whether our legitimate grounds override your rights.

While processing is restricted, we will not use your data unless you give explicit consent, or it is required for legal reasons.

12.7 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, allowing you to transmit it to another data controller.

12.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your rights have been violated or that your data has been processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your country.

For complaints in the European Union, you can contact your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

12.9 Response Time for Requests

We will respond to all data requests within a reasonable timeframe, which will not exceed one month from the date of receipt. If your request is complex or requires additional verification, we may extend the response period but will notify you accordingly.
