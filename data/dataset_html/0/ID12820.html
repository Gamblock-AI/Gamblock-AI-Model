2. What personal information does Azira collect about me?

We collect personal information from and about you as described in this section.  For purposes of this Privacy Policy, “personal information” refers to information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, to you, and includes “personal data” or similar terms used in applicable data protection laws.

A. Azira Services

There are several sources of personal information we collect in order to provide Azira services to our customers:

  • Third Party Data Providers:  Azira collects personal information about you from third-party data providers based on your engagement with third-party apps or advertisements.  Our third-party data partners provide your personal information to us only where you have consented to such collection and disclosure to us.
  • Ad Exchanges:  Azira acts as a Demand-Side Platform (“DSP”) in Real-Time Bidding (“RTB”) auctions. RTB auctions offer advertisers the opportunity to display their ads on digital media (webpages, mobile apps, etc.) in near real time. Acting as a DSP, Azira collects personal information about you when we bid on behalf of advertisers wishing to show you an ad based on your interests and preferences (known as “targeted advertising”).  We use the personal information we receive in a bid request for advertising placement, measurement, and audience profiling purposes, consistent with any consent instructions we receive from publishers or their agents or representatives.  
  • Pixel:  Azira collects personal information on our customer websites or advertisements integrated with our pixel.  We use our pixel to identify you and your browsing session, and to provide measurement insights to our customers.  Our customers are responsible for collecting consent for the use of cookies and pixels on their websites or advertisements where required by applicable law.
  • Trusted Sources: Azira collects personal information from trusted third-party sources, such as data brokers, to help us identify you and your browsing session.  In certain jurisdictions, we may collect your personal information from third-party sources and link this information to the information that we collect to help us identify you.
  • Aggregate Data: Azira uses aggregate information that we receive from publicly available sources, social media companies, census data providers, public directories, or other data brokers to build our products and services.  We link aggregate information or trends to personal information we have collected about you as part of the services we offer to our customers. For example, Azira may use publicly available data to determine that your ZIP Code (which counts as personal information) is associated with farmland.

What is the business or commercial purpose for collecting personal information?

  • Targeted advertising services. We help our advertising and publisher customers provide more relevant advertising and content that is aligned with users' interests. We may group users into audiences, such as sports fans, car enthusiasts, movie goers, travelers, etc., and measure the effectiveness of campaigns.
  • Staggered advertising. We assign a randomized identifier to replace your MAID which enables us and our Partners to know if your browser or device has previously been served the same advertisement.
  • Cross-device advertising. In some cases, we can infer that two different devices are used by the same user; we can then use that information to deliver targeted advertising across multiple devices. We engage in cross-device advertising with the device-user’s permission, as expressed in their privacy choices on each of the two devices, or as otherwise allowed by applicable rules. We require our Third Party Data Providers to ensure that permissions for cross-device advertising were properly collected and remain in effect.
  • Data intelligence services. Azira provides customers with human movement data, refined and enriched to help them make informed business decisions. For example, a chain of fast food restaurants may use this service to choose the sites of future outlets, based on the lunchtime locations of likely patrons. We start with raw human movement data collected by mobile apps consistent with user permissions. The data includes an identification number unique to the device, known as a Mobile Advertising ID (“MAID”). The MAID is paired with the device’s physical location at a given time of day. Prior to providing customers with datasets, Azira safeguards the privacy of device-owners by routinely encoding MAIDs, using a cryptographic algorithm in a process known as “hashing.”We provide actionable insights, patterns and commonalities to enable our customers to evaluate products and services they offer, such as enabling engagement with audiences and improving user experience of their apps and websites.
  • Improving our services. We use your personal information to improve the way we work, improve our services, and provide additional services such as insights.
