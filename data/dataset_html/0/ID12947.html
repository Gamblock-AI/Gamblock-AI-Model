Privacy policy

Introduction

Updated on December 16th, 2024

We place special emphasis on protecting your personal data.

This document aims to inform you about how we collect and process your data, as well as your rights in this regard.

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time to reflect any changes in how we process your personal data and/or to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

Who collects your data?

Nexx360 (“we,” “our,” “the platform”) is a SaaS solution that allows programmatic actors (“clients”) to exchange server-side information about website visitors (“users”) for advertising and marketing activities.

This privacy policy governs the practices related to the processing of personal data of:

–       Visitors (“you,” “your”) to our partner publishers’ websites (“clients”) for content or online services. This information is collected as part of our advertising technology services. (Chapter I)

–       Visitors (“you,” “your”) to our company website www.nexx360.io. This information is collected as part of Nexx360’s marketing activities. (Chapter II)

Nexx360 is a simplified joint-stock cohttps://web.archive.org/web/20251001163254/httpmpany registered with the Trade and Companies Register under number 891 857 674, with its registered office at 144 Avenue Charles de Gaulle – 92 200 Neuilly sur Seine.

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us at the following email address: dpo@nexx360.io

CHAPITRE I.

Privacy policy for visitors to our partner sites

This chapter of our privacy policy informs you about the purposes of data collection and the processing we perform on the personal data of users when they browse our partner websites. It also details the rights and how users can exercise their choices regarding the collection and processing of their personal data by Nexx360.

Nexx360 participates in the IAB Europe’s “Transparency & Consent Framework” and complies with its Specifications and Policies. Our identification number is ID965.

1/ How are your data collected?

Nexx360 uses text files, in the form of a character string called cookies, to collect and store data from visitors to its partners’ websites. We may place these cookies on a user’s browser, subject to their explicit consent. Cookies allow us to recognize a user’s device during their validity period. Only the issuer of a cookie can read or modify its content.

2/ What data is collected?

When a user browses one of the partner websites and/or applications, Nexx360 may collect the following personal data:

  • IP address;
  • Advertising identifier (user ID) assigned by companies offering advertising technology services (SSP) ;
  • Approximate location (country) indicated by IP address.

Nexx360 does not collect or process, directly or indirectly, any information that could directly identify a user, such as their name, first name, address, email, or date of birth. We do not collect or process sensitive information, such as health data, political or religious beliefs, sexual orientation, or ethnic origins of users.

3/ What are the purposes and legal bases for processing personal data?

Nexx360 collects and processes the personal data of visitors to its partners’ websites for the following purposes:

PurposesLegal Bases
Store and/or access information on a deviceConsent
Develop and improve our servicesConsent

4/ To whom are personal data transferred?

The service provided by Nexx360 enables the optimal transmission of data concerning the visitors of its clients’ websites to Intermediaries (SSP) operating in the programmatic advertising inventory chain. These Intermediaries can thus have the necessary information for auctioning the programmatic advertising of Nexx360’s clients.

The nature of the operations carried out on the personal data of users is:

–       Their transmission to Intermediaries;

–       Their analysis by our clients so that they can decide whether to delete a request (in case of suspicion of bots or fraud, quality or carbon impact issues) before transmission to each of their contractual partners (Intermediaries).

Furthermore, Nexx360 may share information with third-party service providers such as:

–       Internet hosting (OVH)

–       Cloud servers (Scaleway)

5/ Are data transferred outside the European Union?

Personal data may be transferred to an Intermediary located outside the European Union (EU) or the European Economic Area (EEA), including to third countries for which the European Commission has not yet adopted an adequacy decision. In their contractual relationship with these Intermediaries, our partners implement appropriate protection measures to ensure an adequate level of protection for your personal data.

6/ What is the data retention period?

Cookies deposited by Nexx360 have a retention period of 6 months. At the end of the data processing services, Nexx360 ensures the destruction of these cookies. Nexx360 does not retain any personal data after transmitting them to the Intermediaries of its partners.

7/ Rights of users under the GDPR

In accordance with articles 14 to 22 of Regulation 2016/679 of April 27, 2016, any natural person has the right to exercise the following rights:

–       Right of access, rectification, and deletion of collected data,

–       Right to object to the processing of their data,

–       Right to limit processing,

–       Right to data portability,

–       Can formulate directives regarding the retention, erasure, and communication of their personal data after their death.

However, Nexx360 collects and processes personal data on behalf of its clients, publishers of content or online services. It is therefore recommended for users to exercise their rights directly with the publishers of the websites they have visited. Nexx360 undertakes to promptly and in writing respond to information requests made by its clients to enable them to respond to users’ requests to exercise their rights.

If necessary, you can address your requests to dpo@nexx360.io.  The response time is one month. This period may be extended by an additional two months depending on the complexity and number of requests received. We may refuse to respond to your request, subject to providing clear and legitimate explanations.

In case of non-response or unsatisfactory response, the user can contact the supervisory authority of their country of residence, for France, the CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr/

Finally, if we detect a personal data breach that may pose a high risk to the rights and freedoms of our users, we undertake to inform the affected users as soon as possible.

The withdrawal of consent to the placement of Nexx360 cookies on the user’s device can be exercised directly from the consent management platform of the publisher.

You can also accept or refuse certain or all cookies (e.g., by blocking all third-party cookies) directly in your browser settings. Nexx360 is committed to implementing the following security measures:

–       Means to restore the availability of personal data and access to them within appropriate timeframes in the event of a physical or technical incident;

–       A procedure to regularly test, analyze, and evaluate the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of processing, and to provide proof at the first request of the client or the data protection authority.

CHAPITRE III.

What are cookies for?

Cookies are used to monitor users browsing patterns or to analyse user behaviour, and in particular:

  • To measure the traffic to our website;
  • To save information related to the client account when users are logged in;
  • To personalise the content available to users.

There are several categories of cookies, some are issued directly by NEXX360 and its service providers, others may be issued by third-party companies.

1.Cookies issued by NEXX360 and its service providers

  • Necessary cookies:

Necessary cookies help make a website usable by enabling basic functions such as browsing pages and entering secure areas of the website. The website cannot operate correctly without these cookies,

  • Preference cookies:

Preference cookies allow a website to retain information which change the way the site behaves and displays information, such as your preferred language or region,

  • Performance cookies

Performance cookies help website owners by collecting and sending anonymous data in order to understand how visitors interact with websites.

  • Marketing cookies:

Marketing cookies are used to monitor visitors through websites. The aim is to display ads which are relevant and interesting to the individual user and therefore valuable for third-party publishers and advertisers.

2.Cookies issued by third party companies

The issue and use of cookies by third parties on our site, are subject to the privacy policies of those third parties. These cookies are not essential for browsing our site.

3.Cookies issued by third-party applications integrated into our site

On our website, we may include third-party software applications, which enable you to share the content of our site with other people or inform other people about your visit or your views concerning a specific content on our site. This is notably the case for the ‘Share’ and ‘Like’ buttons from social media platforms such as Facebook or Twitter

The social media platform providing such an application button is able to identify you through this button, even if you have not used this button while visiting our site

Indeed, this type of application button may allow the social media platform to monitor your browsing journey through our site, simply because your account on the social media platform in question was open on your device (open session) while you were browsing our site.

We have no control over the processes used by social media platforms to collect data about your browsing history on our site and linked to the data they hold.

We suggest that you read the personal data protection policies of these social media platforms in order to learn more about their purposes of use, including advertising, for the browsing data they may collect via those application buttons.

These privacy policies must allow you to exercise your preferences directly with these social media platforms, notably by configuring your user accounts for each of these social media platforms.

How can you manage the cookies placed on your device?

Storing non-essential or unnecessary cookies on your device is subject to your consent.

At any time, via the settings in your browser software, you can simply and without cost, choose to allow or refuse cookies to be stored on your computer. You can configure your browser as you wish, such that the cookies are accepted and saved on your computer or rejected.

You can configure your browser so that:

  • Before a cookie is stored, you will be offered the opportunity to accept or refuse it.
  • You can systematically refuse to store cookies on your computer.
