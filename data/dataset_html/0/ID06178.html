our mission

We’re building a world where adult industry professionals are seen, supported, and valued.
As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, we raise funds to cover the cost of therapy and make mental health care accessible to everyone who needs it.
By connecting individuals with expert, sex-worker-affirming therapists and nurturing a community rooted in understanding, we’re breaking stigma and reshaping what care looks like.
So that no one ever has to struggle alone.
