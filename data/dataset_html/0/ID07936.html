Lisa James Otto Country Properties is a boutique real estate firm by the river’s edge. Since 1986, the company’s dedicated agents have been helping clients find a place they can call home. With offices in beautiful Stockton, NJ and New Hope, PA, Lisa James Otto Country Properties specializes in antique historic houses, charming country houses, farms, estates and riverfront cottages in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and New York. The company also has many commercial, land and international listings.

Over the last 28 years, Lisa James Otto has pursued her dream of matching buyers and sellers in an atmosphere of elegance, grace and style. Professionalism balanced with client sensitivity is paramount in today's constantly evolving marketplace. In a world of mergers and takeovers, Lisa James Otto is proud to remain an independent.
