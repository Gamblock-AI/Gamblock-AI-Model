Borneo Studies Network

Borneo Studies Network (BSN) is an International Partnership of Research Institutions for Borneo, allowing the coordination across the institutions of higher education and research centers in the Island of Borneo with a partnership approach. As a precursor of interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary research in Borneo studies, BSN constitutes a bridge towards Borneo’s research, innovation, and studies capacities to foster Borneo competitiveness. BSN has committed to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 2030, which has been executed through the holistic approach by aligning with the 2030 Agenda for sustainable development focusing resolutely on people, planet and partnerships via its research and academic collaborations.
