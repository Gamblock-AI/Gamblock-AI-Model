Our framework for success

GRIT
Passion and perseverance in pursuit of understanding

We're fueled by GRIT, our performance excellence framework that guides our work and drives incredible outcomes. Demonstrating GRIT means setting and achieving goals, delivering results, thinking innovatively, and strengthening the broader team. By weaving GRIT into our performance conversations, hiring, and goal setting, we're building a team of motivated, high-performing individuals.

Lead With Care
Leadership grounded in empathy and accountability

Lead With Care is Scribd, Inc.’s leadership philosophy. We ask leaders to demonstrate care across four essential areas: our people, our work, our culture, and our business. When leaders role model care, transparency, and trust, we create an environment where individuals feel empowered, collaboration thrives, and meaningful results follow.

Pillar Model
How we collaborate to do our best work

Our Pillar Model is the operational framework that powers how work gets done at Scribd, Inc. By strengthening cross-functional collaboration, giving teams clear ownership, and empowering every employee to own problems and drive solutions, we expand our capacity to pursue ambitious ideas and execute with focus, speed, and creativity.

Benefits

Scribd Flex

Choose the workstyle and location that enable you to perform at your best. Built on trust and outcomes, with intentional moments for in-person connection.

Health & Wellbeing

Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents, with employee premiums fully covered. Plus fully funded access to leading mental health support.

Time Off

Generous paid time off designed for real rest and real life. Includes vacation and personal days, flexible sick time, company holidays, a winter break, volunteer time, and milestone sabbaticals.

Parental & Family Support

Fully paid parental leave, along with fertility and reproductive health benefits. Support that shows up when it matters most.

Retirement & Equity

A robust retirement matching plan and equity for every employee from day one. We invest in your long-term financial future and shared ownership in what we build.

Growth & Learning

Ongoing investment in your development through education support, certifications, conferences, coaching, and culture-forward training. Your growth is part of our long-term strategy.

Wellness Stipend

A flexible annual stipend to support your wellbeing, workspace, and personal comfort. Designed to help you do your best work wherever you are.

AI Tools & Product Access

Fully paid access to best-in-class AI tools, plus complimentary subscriptions to Everand, Scribd, Slideshare, and Fable. We equip you with the tools and knowledge to move faster and think bigger.
