MEET LISA STELLY

Founder & CCO of FANCY SPRINKLES®

Meet Lisa Stelly, founder, and Chief Creative Officer of FANCY SPRINKLES®. Lisa quickly started to build her sprinkle empire in 2016, turning her love for hobbies into entrepreneurial fuel and food for thought. Lisa fused pop culture, trends, and her own creative intuition into a lifestyle brand that dares to be unapologetically different.

As a mother to 3 girls, an astrology buff (Pisces gang!), and a passionate lifestylist who is always in search of the next best thing, Lisa Stelly is the fearless female behind FANCY SPRINKLES®.
