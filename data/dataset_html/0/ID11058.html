News

Get ready, MOODZ! WOODZ is entering a bold new era and returning to the global stage.

 

Singer-songwriter WOODZ (Cho Seung-youn), known for his powerful storytelling and genre-blending sound, is bringing his long-awaited solo concert to Indonesia with the 2026 WOODZ WORLD TOUR＇Archive. 1＇IN JAKARTA. This tour marks his highly anticipated return to the world stage and marks a new chapter in his musical journey.

 

Since debuting as a solo artist in 2016 and redefining himself as WOODZ, he has showcased his artistry through powerful live vocals, captivating stage presence, and music that seamlessly blends multiple genres. His viral hit “Drowning” topped major Korean music charts, alongside well-loved tracks such as “Love Me Harder,” “I Hate You,” and “I’ll Never Love Again”, further cementing his reputation as one of K-pop’s most versatile and compelling performers.

 

MOODZ, this is your moment. Witness WOODZ’s raw vocals, emotional depth, and explosive performances live in Jakarta!

 

For the latest updates and ticket information, follow @ckstar.id on Instagram.
