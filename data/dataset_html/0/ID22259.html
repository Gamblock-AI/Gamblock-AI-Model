PC/Windows System Requirements*

- Windows ME, Windows 2000, Windows XP, Windows Vista, or Windows 7
- Pentium or higher processor (233 MHz or above required)
- 32-bit SVGA Video card with at least 2MB of Video RAM
- 16 MB RAM
- DirectX 5 or above (DirectX 10 or higher recommended)
- Approximately 410 MB free disk space
- Up to 100 MB additional free space for save-game files
- DirectX compatible digital sound card


Apple Mac System Requirements*

- Mac OS X 10.4.11 Tiger/Mac OS X 10.5 Leopard/Mac OS X 10.6 Snow Leopard
- nVidia 8xxx Series or AMD 3xxx or 4xxx Series Video Card recommended
- Intel Core Processor CPU Required
- 512 MB RAM
- Apple's X11 must be installed
- Approximately 590 MB of Hard Drive Space required
- Up to 100 MB additional free space for save-game files
- Compatible digital sound card


*If your system fails to meet these requirements, you may experience slowness and choppy game-play. A Pentium (or equivalent) system above 500 Mhz is generally recommended.


Translation Packs
The King’s Quest II Remake does not support translation packs.
There will be no future translation packs released for the King’s Quest II Remake.
