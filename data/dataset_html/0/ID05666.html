1. Can I renew my subscription through magazines.com?

Absolutely! Whether you want to take advantage of our everyday great prices or consolidate all your orders into one place we are happy to help! Simply look up your magazine title on magazines.com and complete the order information by listing your name and address as it appears on your current magazine mailing label. Your renewal order will be added to our system. You will be charged immediately for your renewal order by magazines.com and your renewal subscription will start as soon as your current subscription expires.

If your subscription was not placed through magazines.com or you are unsure, contact us.

back to top

2. I placed a renewal through your site, but now I am receiving two copies of the same magazine.

Simply contact us or give us a call 1-800-MAGAZINES. We will be happy to take care of it for you!

back to top

3. What is Automatic Renewal?

Automatic Renewal allows you to continue to receive your magazine subscriptions without the hassle of dealing with renewal notices and payments. Before each magazine renewal term you'll receive an email notice reminding you of your upcoming renewal. At that time you can choose not to renew your magazine. Or, as always, you can log in to my account or contact us

back to top

4. How do I turn off Automatic Renewal?

To turn off your auto-renewal status, please log in to my account. Once logged in, click on Recent Orders to view the Auto Renew Status for your subscription .

Turning off Auto Renew will cancel your subscription for future terms and you will not be charged again. You will continue to receive the remaining issues, and/or have access to your subscription features, until the end of your current subscription period. Please note that by changing your auto-renewal status, the publisher of your magazine(s) may send you subscription renewal requests by mail or attached to your magazine. To learn more about cancelling your current order or refund eligibility, click here.

back to top

5. Can I change my method of payment for my renewal?

To change your method of payment, please log in to my account. Once logged in, select "Update Payment Info." Enter your updated payment information under "New Payment Method."

back to top

6. Will I be notified of upcoming renewals?

Before each renewal, you'll receive an emailed reminder notice specifying price plus processing (and any applicable sales tax), the date of the upcoming charge, and billing terms for the next subscription term. For annual terms billed more frequently, you will receive a payment confirmation notice with each transaction and an annual renewal notice. Billing frequency and price are subject to change. The email provided with the transaction will be the email utilized to deliver all future notices and communications regarding your subscription. If you need to update your email, please use this form. As always, you may also update your auto-recurring status by managing my account or contacting us at 800-624-2946.

Please note monthly billed subscriptions will not have notices prior to renewal but future charges can be cancelled at any time.

back to top

7. Do I have access to digital editions of my magazine subscription?

To access your digital library, you must be opted in to auto-renewal. To view your digital library, please login to "my account" under 'Manage Subscriptions', select 'Digital Magazine' to read content related to your purchase.

back to top

8. Is my Instant Start digital issue free?

Yes! There is no cost to this issue – it's on us as our way of saying thank you for your purchase and to help pass the time while you await the start of your print subscription.

back to top

9. Does it count against the total number of issues in my subscription?

No! It's truly a freebie courtesy of magazines.com. As an example, if you order a 12-issue subscription of your favorite magazine today featuring Instant Start, you'll immediately receive the current newsstand issue digitally and then receive 12 print issues from the magazine's publisher in the mail.

back to top

10. What issue will I receive via Instant Start?

You'll be receiving the digital version of the issue currently available on newsstands. Note that depending on the date the magazine's publisher begins fulfilling your print subscription, it is likely that there will be a gap in issues between the current newsstand issue delivered by Instant Start and your first issue arriving in the mail. Once your print subscription begins arriving your issues will be delivered consecutively until all issues in the subscription have been served.

back to top

11. How do I get access to my free Instant Start issue? Do I need a username and password or app?

Once you complete your purchase of any print subscription featuring Instant Start the link to view your digital issue will be available on both the confirmation page and in the order confirmation email you receive from magazines.com. Simply click the link from whatever device you're on to enjoy your magazine – no username, password or app download needed. Instant Start issues can be viewed on any device (smartphone, desktop, laptop, tablet, etc).

back to top

12. Do I need to be connected to the internet to view my Instant Start digital issue?

Yes, an internet connection is required.

back to top

13. Is there a limit to the number of devices I can use to access my free Instant Start issue?

You can access your free Instant Start issue on up to 5 different devices. Once you've reached your 5 device limit, additional attempts to access you Instant Start issue from any new device will be denied.

back to top

14. What if the subscription is a gift?

Good news! If your subscription is a gift and features Instant Start you have two choices:

1. Upon reaching the order confirmation page, click "email a gift message" to notify your gift recipient of the subscription your purchased for them. You can then copy and paste the link to the Instant Start issue in that email.

2. Keep the free Instant Start bonus issue for yourself. We promise not to tell!

18. How do I cancel my order?

To change your auto-renewal status, please log in to my account. Once logged in, click on Recent Orders to update the Auto Renew Status for your subscription.

The goal of Auto Renew is to continue your magazine delivery service or digital subscription features uninterrupted. If you turn off Auto Renew, you will continue to receive any remaining issues of your magazine and continue to have access to digital subscription features through the end of your current active term. Turning off Auto Renew will prevent future renewals and charges.

To cancel your current subscription or determine refund eligibility please contact us.

If your subscription was not placed through magazines.com or you are unsure, click here.

back to top

19. How do I deliver multiple copies of the same magazine to the same address?

Ordering more than one subscription of a specific magazine to the same address on our website will result in extending the length of your subscription. You will NOT get 2 copies of the magazine. If you want to receive multiple copies of a magazine at the same address, please contact us and a Customer Service representative can help.

back to top

20. What is this charge on my credit card?

Check to see if auto-renewal is turned on. To view your auto-renewal status, please log in to my account. Once logged in, click on Recent Orders to view the Auto Renew Status for your subscription.

You will receive an email reminder in advance of each renewal providing you with information to manage your subscription. Your credit card or method of payment will be charged at the time of the purchase, and before the start of each new term, at then current rates stated in the notice. This is applicable to gift and non-gift subscription orders.

If you see a charge on your billing statement for a purchase on Magazines.Com that you didn't make and your auto-renewal status is turned off, please contact us.

back to top

21. How long will it take to receive my order?

Our delivery dates are based on multiple factors, including the type of magazine you ordered and the publisher of the magazine.

Publishers generally ask that you allow:
– 4-6 weeks on weekly magazines
– 6-8 weeks on monthly magazines
– 8-10 weeks on seasonal or bi-monthly magazines
– Up to 12 weeks on quarterly or less-frequent magazines

However, you can anticipate faster delivery on many of our top-selling magazines. If you have not received your first order within the estimated delivery time stated above, please contact us or call Customer Service at 1-800-MAGAZINES.
