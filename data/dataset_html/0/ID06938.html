Global Most Innovative Knowledge Enterprise (MIKE) Award

The Global MIKE Award (Most Innovative Knowledge Enterprise Award) is an international recognition program that honors organizations worldwide that excel in the creation, sharing, and application of knowledge to drive innovation, sustainable success, and business impact. The award showcases best practices from leading companies, public institutions, and non-profits, providing a global benchmark and a platform for knowledge and innovation leaders to learn from one another.
