Become part of our success story

As part of group.one, a leading European provider of online presence, cloud hosting, and digital marketing services. Over 2 million customers trust us with their online success. Our global team consists of 1600+ employees operating from 20+ offices across 15+ countries, with our headquarters in Sweden.

At one.com, we're committed to empowering individuals and businesses online. Since 2002, we’ve delivered reliable and affordable web hosting and domain registration services, making it easy for anyone to build and maintain an online presence. With a focus on integrity, innovation, and customer-first service, we help our customers succeed online.

When you join one.com, you become a part of a global network that values collaboration, growth, and excellence. Join us in creating online success stories!

Workplace & Culture

We are an international company with 400+ employees spread across our one.com offices in Sweden, Denmark, UAE, India, the Philippines, Norway, and the Netherlands. We embrace diversity in nationalities, geographies, and cultures. We work collaboratively and celebrate achievements together to create shared experiences across our global offices.

Culture and Benefits

At one.com, we believe in creating a workplace where you can thrive. Beyond the local benefits explore our international common culture and benefits here.

  • Flexible work

    We promote work-life balance through a hybrid work model, combining office time with remote work, allowing employees to work from home up to 2 days a week.

  • Career growth

    Thrive with internal career opportunities. At one.com, we invest in your professional development, ensuring a path for advancement within our global team.

  • Social international environment

    Embrace diversity for a stronger team. Our international environment celebrates varied perspectives, creating a collaborative workplace that values uniqueness.

  • Free domain and hosting

    Get familiar with our cutting-edge products with a free domain and hosting. Explore your creativity and build your dream website.

Inclusion & Belonging (I&B)

one.com is a humanistic and value-driven company. We bring our full selves to work, so we can be who we are, and contribute our uniqueness towards company culture and business.
Building inclusion and belonging means for us to respect each other’s differences – visible and invisible, such as opinions and ways of being – whilst appreciating them. With an ambition to capitalise on the benefits of working with a team formed by diverse backgrounds and perspectives serving a diverse customer pool, we believe we need to integrate I&B work in three dimensions: Employees; Customers and Society.

Our Hiring Process

Application

At group.one, submit your application online. Showcase your skills and experience. We value diversity and look for passionate individuals to join our international team.

Interviewing

Engage in 1-3 video and office interviews with our team. We aim to understand your strengths and align them with our company culture. Your journey with group.one starts here.

Reference check

We contact the provided references to gain insights into your past performance and work behaviour.

Job offer

Congratulations! You’ve impressed us. Expect a comprehensive job offer. At group.one, we support your international career aspirations.

Onboarding

Welcome aboard! Dive into our onboarding process tailored for success. Connect with colleagues, learn about our culture, and embark on a fulfilling journey at group.one.

FAQ

  • If you come across a job opening of interest, simply complete the required information, submit your resume (or apply using your LinkedIn profile), answer any questions, and then submit. That's all! We are looking forward to reviewing your application.

  • We'll reach out to you via email or phone if you have progressed to the next stage.

  • The initial interview is designed to get to know you better, learn about your background, and determine how well you align with the company culture. During this stage, we’ll ask about your past experiences, skills, and your interest in the role. We will also share details about one.com and the position, and address any questions you may have.

  • We will make every effort to keep you informed throughout the recruitment process. While we cannot guarantee personal feedback, it may be available based on the duration of your involvement in the process. You are always welcome to ask for additional feedback.

  • This depends on the specific role. Some positions may require you to complete skills assessments, personality tests, or technical evaluations relevant to the job. If so, this will be mentioned in the job advertisement.

  • Our goal is to keep the recruitment process efficient while maintaining a high standard of quality. The timeline can vary significantly depending on the position.

Sustainability

Sustainability is an integral value embedded within our business operations. We firmly believe that we are responsible for shaping an inclusive, secure, resilient, and sustainable future for all.

Our commitment to sustainability means that we are actively taking tangible measures to push the sustainable agenda forward and take responsibility for our environmental impact. Through our Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) initiatives, we are actively working to minimise our carbon footprint and create a meaningful positive impact.

One of the tangible ways we make a real difference is through our commitment to renewable energy. All our domains are powered by renewable energy sources, which shows our dedication to contributing to the global transition towards cleaner and more sustainable energy solutions.
