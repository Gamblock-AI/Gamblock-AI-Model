Mission

Building tools that give creators more power

My mission is to help creators move faster, own more of their process, and build without unnecessary friction. Too many creative industries still rely on bloated systems, outdated licensing models, and layers of gatekeeping that slow people down. I believe the future belongs to platforms that are direct, intelligent, fast, and genuinely useful.

That means building products that do real work. Not just flashy interfaces or recycled ideas, but systems that help creators find sounds faster, generate better assets, experiment more freely, and publish with less resistance. Whether that means music libraries, creator utilities, media platforms, or AI-driven tools, the goal is the same: make high-value creative infrastructure available to far more people.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of AI, ownership, search, distribution, and creator freedom. The next generation of platforms should not only save time, they should expand what individuals are capable of doing on their own.

Bio

About Josh

Josh Linsk is an American founder, executive, and creative entrepreneur working at the intersection of music, AI, publishing, and internet product design. His background spans years of work in media, music-related ventures, online business, and brand-building, giving him a broad perspective on both creative industries and digital infrastructure.

Known for thinking independently and building aggressively, Josh has focused much of his work on identifying where old industry models are weak and replacing them with faster, more scalable systems. His approach is shaped by hands-on product work, marketing intuition, editorial thinking, and a long-running obsession with the future of creative tools.

As CEO of Sound Stock, he is working to build a major destination for royalty-free sound powered by AI and modern search systems. His broader work reflects a simple philosophy: strong ideas matter, execution matters more, and the internet still rewards people who build real things with conviction.
