2. What are “cookies”?

Cookies are unique identifiers, usually consisting of small pieces of text or code. Cookies are typically stored on your device or in your browser. They send certain information back to the party that set the cookie. When we say “cookies,” we actually refer to a wide range of technologies other than cookies, including invisible pixels, invisible GIFs, pixels, and similar technologies. When we refer to first-party cookies, we mean cookies set by Armis. When we refer to third-party cookies, we mean cookies set by parties other than Armis.

3. What are cookies used for?

In general, cookies are used to better understand how applications and websites function and to optimize online and mobile experiences.
