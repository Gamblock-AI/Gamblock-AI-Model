What Is a Bitsight Security Rating?

Similar to a credit rating, Bitsight Security Ratings measure an organization's cybersecurity risk and security management effectiveness. They are generated daily based on one year of supporting data. Leveraging over 200+ billion externally observable events gathered from more than 120 different data feeds and processed daily, each individual security event is analyzed for confidence, severity, and duration, and then mapped to a specific company.

Bitsight rates organizations on a scale of 250 to 900, with 250 being the lowest measure of security performance and 900 being the highest. A portion of the upper and lower edge of this range is currently reserved for future use. The currently achievable range is 300-820.

Each organization's rating falls into one of the three rating categories: Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced, with Basic (up to 630) being the lowest performing and Advanced (740 or above) the highest performing. Organizations in the Advanced rating category typically have a strong security performance and the lowest risk.

Higher Rating Means Lower Risk

The Bitsight Security Rating is the industry's only cybersecurity rating independently correlated to the likelihood of a cyber breach and an organization's stock performance. Also linked to an organization's susceptibility to a ransomware attack, the Bitsight Security Rating is a meaningful metric that measures the overall performance of the organization's cybersecurity program over time.

Companies with a rating lower than 600 are 6.4 times more likely to be a ransomware victim compared to those with a 750+ rating, and companies with a rating between 600-650 are 4.6 times more likely.

Companies with higher ratings are also less likely to experience a public data breach. Specifically, companies with a rating of 400 or lower are five times more likely to experience a publicly disclosed data breach than companies with a rating of 700 or higher.

Industry Benchmarking

Bitsight Has Over 445,000 Human-Validated Companies

Bitsight has more than 445,000 human-validated companies from across the globe. Each company is grouped into one of 23 industry sectors based on their website or firmographic data. In addition, Bitsight has 40+ million actively monitored organizations worldwide.

It's important to understand how a company's rating fits into the industry standards and expectations. Overall, it's best to have a higher rating. Although in certain industries, it's possible to have a lower rating compared to the entire Bitsight catalog but still be highly rated within the company's industry.

Less than 1% of the companies in the Bitsight inventory have a rating of 800 or higher.

Bitsight's extensive rating portfolio allows you to easily compare a large group of similar companies for a bigger picture of company and industry performance.
