Bidease Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 1TH OF AUGUST 2024

01. INTRODUCTION

Bidease Inc., operating under the brand name «bidease» (hereinafter referred to as “Company”, “Bidease”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a mobile advertising company that provides customers with a platform and software to deliver more targeted advertising to their consumers.

Bidease’s technology enables its partners to show mobile application users more relevant advertising that is based on the users’ interests and behaviors by means of online services focused on providing and administering the advertising platform and software owned and operated by Bidease ("Services"). In order to provide these Services, we use certain information about user activity and preferences, which we receive from our supply partners and advertisers (together – “Advertising Partners”). We understand that such users (“Consumers) care about their personal information and how it is used and shared, and we take their privacy very seriously.

Bidease has a website (https://bidease.com/, “Website”) that may collect data of those users who access it and / or perform certain actions on that Website (“Website Users”).

Bidease has created and enacted this Privacy Policy to help Website Users and Consumers (together – “Users”), as well as its Advertising Partners (“Users” and “Advertising Partners” together – “you”), understand its privacy practices.

Please read this Policy carefully to understand our practices regarding User personally identifiable information that we gather when Users access the Website or use our Services (“Personal Information”) and how we will treat it.

Since we are unable to perform the Services without User Personal Information, if you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our Website or Services. By using Bidease Website or Services in any manner, you acknowledge that you accept the privacy practices and policies we outline in this Privacy Policy, and you hereby consent that we will collect, and/or use, and/or share your information in the ways outlined in this Policy. This Policy may change from time to time. Your continued use of Bidease Website or Services after we make changes is deemed to be acceptance of those changes, so please check the Policy periodically for updates.

Any capitalized terms used herein without definition shall have the meaning given to them in the Company Terms of Use. Bidease voluntarily commits to comply with The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

02. WHAT DOES THIS PRIVACY COVER?

This Privacy Policy applies to the Website and the Services. This Privacy Policy describes how Bidease collects, uses and secures the Personal Information of Users, their choices regarding the use, access and correction of their Personal Information.

Please note that this Policy does not apply to the practices of companies that we do not own or control, or to individuals that we do not employ or manage.

This Privacy Policy covers the following:

  • The information Bidease collects or receives;
  • Bidease’s use of this information;
  • Your ways to opt out of these practices or to dispute such practices.

03. THE INFORMATION BIDEASE COLLECTS

Bidease collects two main types of information through the Services and the Website - Personal Information and anonymous information, and we may also generate a third type of information, aggregate information, as set forth in more detail below.

Depending on your interaction with us, we may receive information from our Advertising Partners, collect information automatically and/or when Website Users voluntarily choose to provide it. We may also acquire information from other trusted sources to update or supplement the information that Website Users voluntarily provide to us or that we automatically collect or receive from our Advertising Partners.

User Data Supplementation
We may receive information about Users from other sources, including publicly available databases or third parties from whom we may purchase data, and combine this data with information we already have about Users. This helps us to update, expand and analyze our records, identify new customers, deliver our marketing services and provide products and services that may be of interest to you. If you provide us with Personal Information about others, or if others give us your Personal Information, we will only use that Personal Information for the specific purpose for which it was provided to us.

Examples of the types of Personal Information that may be obtained from public sources or purchased from third parties and combined with information we already have about Users, may include purchased marketing data from third parties that is combined with information we have been provided by Advertising Partners, to create more tailored advertising and products.

A. Information Bidease Collects from Website Users

If you are one of Bidease’s Website Users, Bidease may collect the following categories of Personal Information from you:

  • The Personal Information you may provide to us via the contact form on the Website – your name, your email, your company name and any other information you choose to provide us with via this form. If you have provided us with a means of contacting you, we may use such means to communicate with you. For example, we may send you promotional offers on behalf of other businesses, or communicate with you about your use of the Services. Also, we may receive a confirmation when you open a message from us. This confirmation helps us make our communications with you more interesting and improve our Services. If you do not want to receive communications from us, please indicate your preference by clicking “UNSUBSCRIBE” in marketing emails or by emailing us at: privacy@bidease.com. Please note that if you do not want to receive legal notices from us, those legal notices will still govern your use of the Services, and you are responsible for reviewing such legal notices for changes.
  • We may also collect certain types of information automatically through the use of cookies and other similar tracking technologies. Namely, we use Google Tag Manager (through which we have also LinkedIn Insight Tag, META pixel, X website pixel and Yandex website pixel installed). Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Our Advertising Partners use cookies or similar technologies to analyze trends, track User movements around the Website, and to gather demographic information about our User base as a whole.

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer by websites that you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as provide information to the owners of the site.

You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level, but if you choose to disable cookies, it may limit your use of certain features or functions on our website or service. We follow guidelines set forth by the Information Commissioner’s Office with regards to European Union rules on cookie use.

Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.aboutcookies.org.

Please note that Bidease does not retain or use any information collected automatically on the Website by using cookies or similar technologies.

07. HOW CAN I OPT OUT OF SEEING INTEREST- BASED ADVERTISING FROM BIDEASE?

To opt-out please email us at privacy@bidease.com

In order for us to proceed with the request, your email must contain:

- Your advertising ID (e.g. IDFA / GAID / etc.)

Please note that in case your email does not contain such information, we will not be able to proceed with completing your opt-out request.

There are also other ways to opt out of personalized advertising, namely:

  • Adjusting your device settings to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID (instructions available in iOS and Android settings);
  • Using industry opt-out tools such as YourAdChoices (for US users) or YourOnlineChoices (for EU users).

Opting out just means that the advertisements you do receive from us will not be targeted in any sort of way. Note that even if you opt out, you may still receive content and advertising from us.

You may control how your browser responds to cookies by adjusting the privacy and security settings of your web browser.

You may opt out of certain cookies and browser-enabled, interest-based advertising. Please note that these opt-outs apply to interest-based advertising. You may still receive other types of online advertising, and the websites you visit may still collect information for other purposes. In addition, the opt-out choices you select are stored in opt-out cookies only in the browser that you use to visit the opt-out websites, so you should separately set your preferences for other browsers or devices you may use. Deleting browser cookies can remove your opt-out preferences, so you should visit these opt-out websites periodically to review your preferences or update your preferences to apply to new advertisers.

Advertising Partners may also provide ways for you to opt out from or limit their collection of information from and about you. Please note, however, that we are not responsible for the privacy practices of our Advertising Partners and other third parties.
