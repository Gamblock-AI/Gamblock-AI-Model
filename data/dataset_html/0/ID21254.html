DXI PASSPORT BENEFITS

1. Free entry to DXI Exhibition for the next 3 years – no ticket needed.
2. Fast track access to the exhibition area (DXI Priority).
3. Fast track access to all activity programs at DXI.
4. Special extra discounts at selected DXI tenants.
5. Chance to join the grand prize lucky draw at every DXI event (terms & conditions apply).

How to Get a DXI Passport?

1. Be part of a selected community invited by the DXI Program Team.
2. Show proof of purchase at any DXI tenant with a minimum total of Rp 1.000,000 (max. 2 combined receipts).
3. Purchase early bird DXI ticket.
