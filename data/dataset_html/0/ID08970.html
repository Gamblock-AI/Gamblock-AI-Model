Mission, Vision, Values

What are we doing and why we are doing it?

Mission

to create memorable learning experiences.

Vision

to build a suite of engaging, easy-to-use tools that help reshape education for teachers and students.

Values

Public and honest
Honest & open communication about everything, whether things are going well or not.
Experimentation before perfection
Quick and dirty ideas that are publicly shared and validated before investing time and resources on a perfect version.
Community at heart
Foster an inclusive and supportive community focused on improving education for educators & students.
Appreciation of why we're here
Respect and gratitude towards each and every stakeholder in the education ecosystem. (Advertisers and marketers are not part of this ecosystem.)
Focus on the long term
Prioritize sustainability over short-term gains.
