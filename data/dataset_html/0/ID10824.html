La Favela

Situated in the heart of Seminyak as one of Bali’s most famous hotspots, La Favela first opened it’s doors in 2013 and is now home to latin-music and tapas dishes.

La Brisa

Nestled by the salty sea of Echo Beach, wild and enchanting yet pleasantly soothing – La Brisa offers an experience of Bali like never before.

La Plancha

La Plancha is one of the first Chiringuito styled restaurant and bar located in Seminyak serving Spanish inspired dish and cocktails with bright umbrellas and beanbags.

La Laguna

Vintage wooden gypsy caravans and multi coloured decor makes La Laguna stand out since first opening its doors in 2014. Now under renovation – opening soon 2023.

Bokashi​

Inspired by the Japanese wabi sabi concept, Bokashi strives to deliver the best quality & health forward offerings in both our Grocer and 2nd floor Café.

Attika

A speakeasy bar located on the 3rd floor of La Favela, Attika introduces a concept of special cocktails and curated music for the lovers.

Lyd Organic

LYD Organic, is an organic farm located in Northern Bali where we supply all of our venues with organic, local and sustainable produce.

La Santa Rosa

La Santa Rosa provides an array of options for your next visit and stay in southern Bali.
