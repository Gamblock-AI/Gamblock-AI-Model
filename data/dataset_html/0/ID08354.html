BEST AYURVEDIC HOSPITAL IN KOCHI , KERALA

Parathuvayalil Ayurveda Hospital is the one of the best traditional ayurvedic treatment centres in Kerala. In 1955 it started to serve people from chronic diseases and won global fame. This NABH Accredited and CGHS Approved hospital; specialized in traditional Marma Chikitsa & Kaya Chikitsa (Orthopaedic and other general Ayurvedic treatments), which are integrated with modern facilities for diagnosis. This hospital is well known for traditional methods of healing Fracture, Dislocation, Musculoskeletal disorders, Osteoarthritis, Rheumatoid Arthritis, Low Back Pain, Knee Pain, Frozen Shoulder, Neck Pain, Neck stiffness (Cervical Spondylosis), Disk Prolapse etc.

Under the guidance and leadership of renowned Dr. Pathrose Parathuvayalil (Managing Director, Chief Physician and the son of Founder - late ayurveda practitioner Sri. P.V Poulose Vaidhyan) and his legacy & talented hands; this hospital has set many milestones. The roots of Parathuvayalil family treatment heritage, extended into 4 generations and now following a refined unique approach of treatment. This hospital (PPG Group) operates a GMP certified own herbaceutical drug manufacturing unit which produces high quality medicines, and it is recognized by ISO 9001:2000 certifications for clinical quality.Read more

For better diagnosis and treatment, we recommend that you visit our spine specialist whether or not you need surgery.
