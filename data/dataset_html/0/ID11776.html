Welcome to Harvard Library

We are the libraries and archives of Harvard University. Our librarians help you discover, use, and create knowledge.

Making Our Collections Available to the World

We digitize and describe original materials from our collections, making them freely available online for all to discover and use.

Explore What’s Possible

Harvard Library advances the research, teaching, and learning of Harvard students, faculty, scholars, and staff. With over 20 million items in more than 460 languages, our holdings range from rare and unique materials to extensive digital resources, many freely accessible online.

  • Discover Our Collections

    Harvard Library’s vast collections are renowned for their global reach and depth.
