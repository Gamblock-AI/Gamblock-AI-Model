Hardwood Floor Sanding, Refinishing and Installation

Balsdon Floor Sanding is a Third Generation Hardwood Flooring Company, Specializing in Sanding, Refinishing, Restoration and Installation. As a London Based Business we Provide Both Residential and Commercial Floor Sanding. We Offer our Customers an Environmentally-Friendly Hardwood Flooring Restoration and Refinishing Solution.



Sanding and Refinishing

Providing our customers with quality sanding and refinishing at a great rate. It is our mission and passion to meet all of your hardwood flooring needs.

Installation and removal

We do installation of Solid, Engineered & Laminate Flooring as well as removal of Carpet, Hardwood, Laminate and Tile.

Environmentally Friendly

We ensure three coats of oil based environmentally-friendly hardwood floor finish will be applied to every floor, available in 3 durable topcoats: Gloss, Semi-Gloss and satin with virtually no odor.

About the owner

Korey Basldon at the age of fourteen years old started to learn everything there is to know about floor sanding, refinishing and the family business with his father Jack and grandfather Butch. After the passing of his grandfather, Korey took possession in May 2015 using his skills to continue Balsdon Floor Sanding. Taking not only Butch’s passion and dedication, Korey prides himself in the quality and craftsmanship of every floor. Balsdon Floor Sanding is now serving the London and St Thomas area.
