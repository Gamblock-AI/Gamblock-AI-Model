Why Zoho Schools?

What we teach:

The focus here is on future-proof skills that are relevant for real software careers

What we look for:

At Zoho Schools, and at Zoho Corp, we have always looked for skillsets and abilities rather than for paper credentials and degree certificates

We don't charge, we pay!

Rs. 10,000/month during the first year, which goes up to Rs. 15,000 the second year.

Career Opportunities:

After completion of the course, you are automatically inducted as an employee into
Zoho Corporation, a world leader in cloud-based software products.

Zoho Schools now operates from four locations in Tamil Nadu, India. Applications are welcome from all anywhere in India!

What should my basic qualification be?


Your age should be within the range of 17 to 20. This two-year program is an exclusive opportunity for students looking for a meaningful alternative to conventional colleges. So, college graduates needn't apply.
