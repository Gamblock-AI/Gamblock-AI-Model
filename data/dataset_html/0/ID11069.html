Join a family-run, optimistic and independent Maison

Headquartered in Paris, Longchamp stands foremost as a sizable, optimistic, and autonomous family-owned enterprise with a global footprint.

Established in 1948 by Jean Cassegrain, Longchamp is presently overseen by the third and fourth generations of the founding family, steadfastly dedicated to preserving its contemporary essence and enduring legacy.

Spanning over 25 countries and a network of 3,000 team members, the Maison is currently in search of new talent — individuals who embody a spirit of freedom, creativity, and dedication — to drive its advancement within a demanding yet nurturing professional environment.

Becoming a part of Longchamp means contributing to the creation and distribution of fashion and leather goods that are authentic and exquisitely crafted, infused with unparalleled artistry and savoir-faire.

Let's open the Longchamp of possibilities

Opt for a company that values its people.


At Longchamp, we entrust every team member with meaningful responsibilities and engaging challenges that highlight their talents.


Progressing within Longchamp offers you a vibrant and forward-moving career journey, supported notably by our growth prospects both domestically and internationally.


As a member of our team, you'll become an integral part of a sustainable business model. Our commitment to product longevity, efforts to minimize environmental footprints, and the pursuit of innovative materials and solutions all contribute to safeguarding the world of tomorrow.

JOIN US

Together we can develop your potential...

At Longchamp, we value unconventional career paths and daring personalities.


Across our three distinct domains – production, retail, and support departments – we eagerly welcome and provide training to men and women from diverse backgrounds who are united by a shared aspiration: a passion for learning and excelling.

We are fueled by optimism and hold the belief that life presents challenges to conquer.

Being part of Longchamp means embodying both the authenticity and the energy of the Maison, two inseparable values that drive us every day.

...and your client culture

Whatever your role, you'll serve as a Maison ambassador, dedicated to delivering an unparalleled experience for both local and global clients.

Your role will play a pivotal part in championing a French way of life on a global scale, mirroring our boutiques that draw subtle inspiration from Parisian flats – sophisticated yet brimming with delightful surprises.

Our collections have continually evolved over time, distinguished by a diverse range of products and captivating narratives.


Join us and become part of our narrative.
