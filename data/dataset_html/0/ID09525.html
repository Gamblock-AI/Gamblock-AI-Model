Canggu Tropical Paradise

Imagine yourself with your toes gently sinking into soft white sand, a refreshing fresh coconut in one hand and your camera in the other, capturing a breathtaking sunset along the Canggu coastline. The warm ocean breeze, golden skies, and the soothing rhythm of the waves create a naturally calming tropical atmosphere.

You don’t have to imagine it anymore. This experience awaits you at our beachfront hotel in Canggu, a 5-star all suites hotel thoughtfully designed for travelers who appreciate space, comfort, and a sense of ease. 

Swarga Suites bali Berawa, Marclan Collection

Swarga is a Sanskrit term that means a place in the realm of life after this world. Deeply rooted on Balinese customs, we let you feel the touch of Bali in every corner of our heaven. Discover the best suites in Bali for your upcoming getaway.

Swarga Suites Bali Berawa is an all-suites hotel sitting along Berawa Beach, Canggu. Our beachfront hotel has 60 suites, each with its distinctive features like an ocean view or direct pool access. We offer everything you could possibly desire, from a stunning swimming pool to a gym and a relaxing spa. Enjoy a divine getaway here and have a little of your own “Swarga” here.
