welcome to hills & valley

Araku Valley is a familiar hill station in Andhra Pradesh due to climatic conditions. The place is a pleasure to stay cool in summer, enjoy fog in winter and feel a bunch of clouds in the rainy season. The beautiful Scenario of Araku Valley attracts tourists all over India. Hills & Valley Resorts located near the cinema hall. Our Resorts concept consists of spacious & well-furnished AC Suites (SUN, MOON, SKY, EARTH, STAR, CRAZY, LAZY), Non AC Suites (ESCAPE, ANTIC, SECRET, AKARSH), 10-bedded room, AC Deluxe and Non AC Deluxe Rooms established with Restaurant, Conference hall, Entertainment zone, Children Play Park and 24*7 Security.
At Hills and Valley, we pride ourselves on providing an array of entertainment options for our esteemed guests. From excellent gardens to thrilling play station games, we offer an unforgettable journey into the heart of Araku's vibrant beauty. To enrich your experience further, immerse yourself in the rhythmic beats of our Tribal Dance performances, a captivating cultural expression unique to the region
Hills and Valleys is a Provisional Point of Calm and Serene Holiday Executive Guest House at the lush of Green Eastern Ghats. It is just a three and half hour drive from Visakhapatnam city and three hours drive from Visakhapatnam Airport.
