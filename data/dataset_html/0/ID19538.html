    Lahaina Arts Society (LAS) showcases, markets and mentors Maui County Artists.

    Don’t you sometimes wish you could do one more art fair just for the fun of it? If you have ever been an LAS juried member, we would love to have you show and sell your art as a guest for a single weekend. You would not have to rejury if you had juried in that particular media in our 52 yr. past and would only pay the current commission. It’s a chance for another opportunity to be with your peers and old friends and reconnect! Let us know if you have an interest! We’d love to see you at the Cannery!
