FAQ

We have the answers to help you transcribe with ease.

  • How do I use RecCloud AI Speech-to-Text?
    Getting started is easy. Just upload your audio/video file, record directly online, or paste a link. Our AI will then transcribe it to text, generate a smart summary, and allow you to edit, copy, or export the transcript in various formats.
  • What's the best speech-to-text AI I can use, both free and paid?
    If you're looking for a speech-to-text tool with both free and paid options, RecCloud is a strong choice. It offers 2 free transcriptions per day, supports 100+ languages, and can also generate summaries and translations—making it useful for meetings, content creation, and research.
  • Is it accurate enough for professional use?
    Yes. With industry-leading 98%+ accuracy on clear audio, it's built to handle professional scenarios from meeting transcription to client calls with confidence.
  • Is there a free plan?
    Yes. You get 2 free transcriptions per day (up to 20 minutes each).
  • Is my data safe with you?
    Absolutely. We employ end-to-end encryption to protect your files. Your files are used solely for transcription and are never shared with any third party.
  • Does it support speaker identification?
    Yes, with Speaker Diarization. Our AI recognizes different voices and assigns labels like "Speaker 1." You can then edit these labels for a clear, multi-participant transcription.
  • What file formats do you support?
    We support all major formats, including MP3, WAV, MP4, and M4A, among others. This covers the vast majority of audio and video transcription needs.
  • Is there a file size or duration limit?
    Yes, we support single files up to 5 hours in length, with size limits of 4GB for video and 500MB for audio.
  • Which languages do you support?
    We support a global reach of 100+ languages and variants, including English, Spanish, Japanese, and French, to accurately transcribe your international content.
  • Can I copy and export the transcript?
    Once your transcript is ready, you have full control. Edit it in our tool, copy the text with one click, or export it to a Word document to seamlessly integrate into your workflow.
