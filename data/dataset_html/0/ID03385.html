About Us

Here's What We Do

Established in 1955, Oscar Wilson has evolved into one of the premier outdoor power equipment parts distributors in the Midwest. We pride ourselves on our extensive inventory, knowledgeable staff, and commitment to exceptional customer service.

From our 120,000 square foot warehouse in O'Fallon, Missouri, we provide timely delivery and competitive pricing on thousands of parts from over 40 leading manufacturers.

  • Same-day shipping on most orders
  • Knowledgeable customer service team
  • Competitive wholesale pricing
  • Extensive parts inventory
