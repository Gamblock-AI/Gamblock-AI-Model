    Meet the team fueling dreams

    Power what’s next. As global partners, we’re teaming up to drive real impact and fuel future generations’ dreams through science, nutrition, innovation and sport.

     

    Since 2021, we’ve been the global health sciences and nutrition partner of the Real Madrid Football Club and global partner of the Real Madrid Foundation. Highlights of our partnership include:

     

    • The Abbott Dream Team
    • Malnutrition screenings and nutrition education for children
    • Athlete health and nutrition leadership
    • Sports science innovation

    Our plan to beat malnutrition

    In partnership with the Real Madrid Foundation, we’re working to combat global malnutrition through screening programs and nutrition education to help improve the well‑being of children worldwide.

    This effort supports Abbott’s sustainability goal to transform care for malnutrition and improve the lives of 3 billion people each year by the end of the decade.
