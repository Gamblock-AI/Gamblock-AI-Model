Yamaha Group Lessons

Group music lessons for kids starting from age 3, based on the world renowned Yamaha Music Education System.

Private Lessons

Flexible, 1-on-1 lessons with one of our excellent teachers. We teach piano, guitar, violin, flute, ukulele, voice and a bunch more.

Music Store

We proudly carry Yamaha instruments as well as Makai Ukes, Revival Guitars, Adagio, Sandner and Vivace violins, and a host of other world instruments and accessories
