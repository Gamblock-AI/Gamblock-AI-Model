Our Services

Risk Management

We use a variety of tools and methods to manage risk, including mark-to-market functionality and real-tme monitoring as standard risk management practices.

Clearing & Settlement

We simplify and secure transactions, minimizing counterparty risk while ensuring efficient, transparent, and compliant trades while ensuring all the terms of the deal are double-checked.

Custodian Bank

We securely preserve customers' funds by collaborating with reputable banks that are qualified as margin depository banks.

GCG Principles-Based Process

Kliring Komoditi Indonesia strives to implement 5 (five) basic principles of GCG, namely: transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness.
