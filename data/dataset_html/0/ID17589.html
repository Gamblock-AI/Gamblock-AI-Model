10 Best Anime Download Sites for Free (2024)

Tarunveer Singh
-
June 27, 2024

Anime is growing fast every day, and people all over the world love to watch it because of its great story and animation. However, many people are still unaware of how and where to download anime. That’s why below, we have come up with a list of the best anime download sites for free that all anime lovers should know about.

Anime originated in Japan and offers colorful visuals, various characters, and a wide range of genres, such as action, fantasy, romance, adventure, and comedy. Its storytelling is one of the best reasons why it attracts a global fan base.

There are lots of sites on the internet that provide anime, but here we have provided you with the best and most dedicated websites for anime, where you can get every anime series and movie for free.

Best Anime Download Sites for Free Online

Here, we have compiled a list of the best free anime download websites, which every anime lover has always been searching for. If you love to watch anime, then the list below will definitely make your day.

1. Crunchyroll

 

Crunchyroll is one of the most popular anime websites that every anime lover knows about. It offers almost all famous anime series, including Dragon Ball Z, One Piece, Naruto, Ace of the Diamond, and many more. If you want the latest and most popular anime, then you must visit this site.

Crunchyroll offers dubbed versions of most anime shows and series with subtitles. If you want to download the episodes, you have to buy their premium subscription.

Apart from anime, Crunchyroll also provides music and manga series. The website has over 100 million monthly visitors, which is a huge deal.

Website Link: Crunchyroll

2. AnimeHeaven

AnimeHeaven is another website for anime that provides the latest anime faster than others. It has a huge collection of anime series and movies that you can stream online and download as well. The website’s interface is simple, making it easy for anyone to find their favorite anime.

All you have to do is search for the name of the anime, and it will show you all the episodes easily. You can also click on their menu to view dubbed anime, series, movies, and popular titles. It also offers high-definition video quality and a download button.

Website Link: AnimeHeaven

3. GoGo Anime

GoGoAnime is a very famous anime website that is used by almost every anime lover for sure. The site’s interface is simple and clean, making it easy for everyone to find their favorite anime easily. It also provides both dubbed and subtitled versions so that any anime lover around the world can easily enjoy the anime.

It will always try to provide the latest anime so that anime lovers can have instant access to their favorite anime shows. If you have just started watching anime and are looking for the best anime collection, consider this website.

Website Link: GoGo Anime

4. Funimation

Funimation is one of the best-maintained anime websites. It provides all the best and latest anime shows. You can easily find your anime show by searching its name. The website layout is clean and divided into categories like Psycho-Pass Animes, Music Related, BFF 4 Life, and Currently Popular.

With these categories, you can easily select the anime you like. Each episode is excellently rated and available in English dubbed versions. If you are finding this website for the first time, then it is a must-try.

Website Link: Funimation

5. Animension

Animation is also a great option for all anime lovers. It has a huge collection of dubbed anime content that you can download or stream online for free. The website attracts over 8 million monthly visitors, which indicates that many anime fans are enjoying this website.

The best thing about this website is that there are no ads to disturb you. Its interface is clean, and the ads will only appear when you start watching the anime. It has an option called ‘Seasons’ where you can find anime shows by their years or seasons.

Website Link: Animension

6. Animeout

AnimeOut is another option to download anime for free. The best part of this website is that it provides direct download links for all anime episodes.

It also provides links for different resolution properties, so you can select the one you prefer. Just click on the download button and after a few seconds, the download will start automatically.

If we talk about the other features of the website, then it has a huge collection of anime content in various genres.

It also provides detailed information about each anime series or episode, including anime information, release date, rating, synonyms, and total episodes. This information will help you decide whether to download it or not.

Website Link: Animeout

7. RetroCrush TV

If you want to watch old-fashioned anime shows, then you can choose Retrocrush TV. This website mainly focuses on retro and classic anime series and movies. So, don’t expect a huge anime collection from this site.

However, you can find the best retro anime content that may not be available on other sites. These are some of the best anime collections available on RetroCrush TV: Great Teacher Onizuka, Deltora Quest, Ultra Maniac, Flame of Rekka, Bubblegum Crisis, Blue Seed, and many more.

Website Link: RetroCrush TV

8. 9Anime

Another anime website has a huge collection of anime series and movies in dubbed versions with subtitles. You can easily find anime based on their genres or search by titles. The best part of this website is that it keeps on updating its library with the latest high-quality anime series and movies.

You can also search alphabetically from A to Z if you are unclear about the anime name and struggling to find a specific anime series. All you have to do is visit this site, search for your favorite anime series, and watch it online.

Website Link: 9Anime

9. AnimeKaizoku

AnimeKaizoku has a wide collection of anime series and movies across various genres, including romance, action, love, motivation, and more. You can easily download any episode you want with just one click.

The website also provides detailed information about each episode, including its release date, genre, and synopsis. The website’s interface is clean and user-friendly, making it easy to find the anime you’re looking for.

Website Link: AnimeKaizoku

10. AnimeLand

Animeland is a website where you can watch all the latest anime series and movies without any interruption. It has a huge collection of English dubbed anime that anyone can watch and download for free. It covers various anime genres, including action, fantasy, romance, and thriller.

We also realize that this website’s interface is not that great, but it will never let you down when it comes to finding your favorite anime. If you do not want a lot of advertisement promotion while watching anime, then you can consider this website.

Website Link: AnimeLand

Conclusion

That’s all for this article. Almost every website listed here provides the best anime content in dubbed versions. I hope you liked this article and that you have found the best site according to your preferences.
