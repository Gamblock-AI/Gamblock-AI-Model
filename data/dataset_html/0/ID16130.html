Frequently Asked Question

What is LinkCrane?

LinkCrane is a powerful SaaS tool designed to help you create bio link pages, shorten URLs, and generate QR codes with advanced customization options. It’s a comprehensive platform to enhance your online presence and track link performance effectively. 

It also has capabilities to Share Vcards , Transfer files & Host HTML Sites

Who can use LinkCrane?

LinkCrane is perfect for businesses, influencers, marketers, and creators looking to optimize their digital footprint, manage multiple links in one place, and analyze link performance for better decision-making.

Is LinkCrane free to use?

LinkCrane offers both free and premium plans. The free plan provides essential features, while premium plans unlock advanced capabilities like custom branding, in-depth analytics, and unlimited usage options.

Can I track the performance of my links?

Yes! LinkCrane offers advanced analytics that track clicks, user demographics, device types, and more. These insights help you understand your audience and optimize your campaigns.

Does LinkCrane Supports Custom Domain?

Absolutely, Yes. You can integrate your custom domain with LinkCrane to create branded URLs, enhancing your credibility and consistency & the good thing is you can add custom domains in free plan.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime. Your account will remain active until the end of the billing cycle.
