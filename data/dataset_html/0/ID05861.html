Start your return

Please follow our easy return procedure below: 

  1. Contact Sales Support
    Email our sales support team at support@jitsie.com and indicate the items you wish to return. Please include your sales order or invoice number. 
  2. Await Confirmation
    Returns are only accepted after confirmation from our sales team. Items must be unworn, unused, and in their original packaging with all labels attached.
  3. Return the parcel
    Return shipping costs and labels are not covered by Jitsie. You may use a courier of your choice to return the approved items. Return address:

    • Jitsie Returns, Steenweg 3 unit504, 3540 Herk-de-Stad, Belgium
  4. Refund Processing
    Once the returned parcel has been received and validated, our accounts department will issue a refund to the original payment method used for the order.
  5. Tracking Responsibility
    Please retain your tracking number at all times. Lost or damaged parcels remain the responsibility of the sender and cannot be refunded by Jitsie.
