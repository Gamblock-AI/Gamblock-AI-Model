About Us

Located in San Mateo, Calif., Manticore Games™ is the developer and publisher of Out of Time, a fast-paced co-op roguelite built for endlessly replayable action and gear-driven experimentation, and the creator of the Core Platform™.

Manticore Games was established by industry veterans from Electronic Arts, Blizzard, Respawn, Xfire, Roblox, Trion Worlds, Crystal Dynamics and Zynga.
