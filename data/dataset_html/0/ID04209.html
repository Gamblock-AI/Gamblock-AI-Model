Discover Senayan Park

Skywalk

As a shopping center located in the center of Jakarta, Senayan Park now has a new destination that can be visited by the public, the new destination is Skywalk which is located on the rooftop level. Visitors can enjoy the beauty of the city of Jakarta from the Skywalk.

Senayan Park

Senayan Park (SPARK) is the oasis in the heart of the city. Located in Senayan area, it is the urban city lifestyle mall with lots of outdoor facilities and scenery, such as huge lake, parks, jogging track, lots of photo spots, Rooftop Garden and Skywalk. During this pandemic, it is the safe and perfect place for your family getaway, even for your furry little friends!
