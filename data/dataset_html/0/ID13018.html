INTRODUCTION:

This privacy policy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”) describes how Tagoras Ltd. (“Tagoras”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects and uses, certain information, including your Personal Data and the choices you can make about your Personal Data we process.

Tagoras is a supply-side platform provider which enables digital assets owners (“Publishers”) and third-party advertisers and advertising networks (“Advertisers” and collectively with Publisher the “Partners”) to manage their advertising inventory (“Advertising Services”).

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of data collected by Tagoras in connection with: (i) visitors of our website, or other official digital assets, such as our social media accounts (“Visitors” and "website" respectively); (ii)  Publishers and Advertisers (and each of their authorized users) signing up (or log in) for and use of Tagoras Services, including a designated account (“Partner Account”) dashboard environment, marketplace, execution of marketing campaigns, etc. (“Tagoras Platform”); and (iii) Publisher's end users (“End Users”).  

Advertising Services, Tagoras Platform and the Website shall be collectively referred to herein as the “Services”. Visitors, Publishers and End Users shall be further, separately and collectively, referred to herein as “you”.

We strongly urge you to read this Policy and make sure that you fully understand and agree to it. If you do not agree to this Policy, please discontinue and avoid using our Services. You have the right to cease using our Services at any time. You are not legally required to provide us with any Personal Data, but without it we will not be able to provide you with the best experience of using our Services.

DATA RETENTION:

In general, Personal Data usually will not be retained for more than 24 months. However, we do retain the data we collect for as long as it remains necessary for the purposes set forth above, all under the applicable regulation, or until you express your preference to optout, where applicable.

The retention periods are determined according to the following criteria:

  • where we have a valid a business reason to process your Personal Data, such as the provision of our Services.
  • For as long as it remains necessary in order to achieve the purpose for which the Personal Data was initially processed. For example: if you contacted us, we will retain your contact information at least until we will address your inquiry.
  • To comply with our regulatory obligations. For example: transactional data will be retained for up to seven years (or even more under certain circumstances) for compliance with our bookkeeping obligations purposes.
  • To resolve a claim we might have or a dispute with you, including any legal proceeding between us, until such dispute will be resolved, and following, if we find it necessary, in accordance with applicable statutory limitation periods.

Please note that except as required by applicable law, we will not be obligated to retain your data for any particular period, and we may delete it for any reason and at any time, without providing you with prior notice if our intention to do so. However, retention periods will be determined by taking into account the type of information that is collected and the purpose for which it is collected, bearing in mind the requirements applicable to the situation and the need to destroy outdated, unused information at the earliest reasonable time and in a way that prevents the Data from being restored or reconstructed.
