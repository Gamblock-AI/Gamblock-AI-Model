Advertising Cookies

Always Active

These cookies use information about your use of our services to show you relevant BritBox adverts on websites and apps other than BritBox, measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns, and to limit the number of adverts that you see. These cookies may be set by us or our service providers. These cookies do not store personal data that directly identifies you, but they do collect a unique identifier that identify your browser and device. If you opt out of these cookies, you may still see BritBox adverts on other websites and apps, but those adverts will not be as relevant to you.

Always Active

These cookies allow us to collect information about use of our services, such as most and least popular pages, content viewing patterns and how users arrive at our services. We collect this information so that we can measure and improve the performance of our services. These cookies collect data in a way that does not directly identify you.

Always Active

These cookies are necessary for our services to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. For example, we may use these cookies to enable you to log in, watch content and manage your cookie settings, and also for additional security purposes. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of our services will not then work.
