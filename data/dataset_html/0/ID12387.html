Introduction

We would like to be transparent about our business and explain our technology in plain language so that users can understand how our services process personal data. This Privacy Policy describes Adform´s practices and policies with regard to the use and processing of data when providing online advertising services for the world’s largest websites and advertisers (hereinafter collectively referred to as "Services").

If you wish to find out more about Adform's processing in the context of its website and/or corporate business, please see the following link.

In specific Service and data processing scenarios, Adform and its clients and/or partners may act as joint controllers which – however – ultimately has no effect on exercising your data subject rights as described in the section ‘Your Data Protection Rights’ below.

If you want to find out more about the concept of joint controllership, what processing is affected and how this is managed at Adform, please visit this link.

What Information Do We Collect and Use

Below we provide the purposes for which we process your personal data and, for each processing purpose, the categories of personal data we use for that purpose, the applicable AdForm products or services, and our legal basis for the relevant processing. Please note that the term “legal basis” and those listed below are only relevant in those jurisdictions that recognize such concept, excluding, for example, the United States.

1. Reporting, measurement, forecasting, and attribution (IAB Purpose 7)

Brief explanation

Specific processing activities may be, for example:

  • We use combined data from cookies and the number of impressions to predict user/audience trends.
  • We gather IDs when a user visits an advertiser's website through tracking the user’s online activity.

Data Categories

1st Party IDs, Cookie IDs, and technical data related to these IDs (e.g. impressions, clicks, conversions, approximate geolocation, timestamp, URL, device information, browser information), IP address). 

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, DMP

Legal bases

We process this data by default on the basis of our legitimate interest. However, the publishers with sites that have integrated our Services can decide to obtain your consent. In this case, we process the data based on your consent.

2. Improve online advertising experiences and efficiency (IAB Purpose 2, Purpose 10)

Brief explanation

The ads you see on websites or apps that integrate our Services are chosen based on the categories of information mentioned above in (1). This means that we may use the site you're on, the kind of device you're using, and your general location in order to improve the online advertisements you see. We might also look at what you're interested in or what you've looked at before based on your interactions with websites or apps to make sure you don't see the same ad too many times. We may also use it to show you ads in a certain order, show you ads you might like, figure out the best time to show you an ad, make our ads work better, and to make your online experience better by improving our Services.

Data Categories

Same as in No.1.

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Ad Server.

Legal bases

We process this data by default on the basis of our legitimate interest. However, the publishers with sites that have integrated our Services can decide to obtain your consent. In this case, we process the data based on your consent.

3. Fraud prevention and security (IAB Special Purpose 1)

Brief explanation

Your personal data can be used to monitor for and prevent unusual and possibly fraudulent or unlawful activity (e.g. to ensure that the ads are clicked upon by real people, and not bots), and to ensure systems and processes work properly and securely.

Data categories

Online identifier (e.g. cookie ID, mobile advertising ID), browser information (e.g. URL/domain; time stamp, user agent, IP address).

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, Ad Server.

Legal bases

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests.

4. Develop and Improve products (IAB Purpose 10)

Brief explanation

We use personal data about how you use Adform clients' websites or apps, such as how you engage with ads or content, to improve our Services  and to develop new products and services.

Data Categories

1st Party IDs, Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable, as well as real-time data (e. g. information about the page content, app type).

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, Ad Server, DMP

Legal bases

We process this data by default on the basis of our legitimate interest. However, publishers with sites that have integrated our Services  can decide to obtain your consent. In this case, we process the data based on your consent.

5. Synchronize our Service with other online advertising services (Link different End User devices) (IAB Feature 2)

Brief explanation

We might use Cookie IDs and other IDs to connect our Services with other online advertising services.

Data Categories

1st Party IDs, Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable.

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, Ad Server, DMP

Legal bases

We process this data by default on the basis of our legitimate interest. However, publishers with sites that have integrated our Services can decide to obtain your consent. In this case, we process the data based on your consent.

Brief explanation

We may process your personal data to comply with our legal obligations, such as to respond to  data subject rights requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1st Party IDs, Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable.

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, Ad Server, DMP

7. Creation of personalized advertising profiles (IAB Purpose 3)

Brief explanation

We collect information about your actions on our clients' websites or apps, like the content you view or data we receive from our clients. This data may be combined with your previous actions or those of similar users, to create or enhance a profile about you, including your potential interests and personal characteristics. This profile can be used to show you more relevant ads based on your interests.

Data categories

1st Party IDs, Cookie IDs, and technical data related to these IDs (e.g. impressions, clicks, conversions, approximate geolocation, timestamp, URL, device information, browser information), IP address.

To inform the ads you see on our clients' websites or apps, we may use your advertising profiles and interests, which are based on your activity on these platforms, as well as other websites or apps

Data categories

1st Party IDs, Cookie IDs, and technical data related to these IDs (e.g. impressions, clicks, conversions, approximate geolocation, timestamp, URL, device information, browser information), IP address.

9. Use limited data to select advertising (IAB Purpose 2, Special Purpose 2)

Brief explanation

Ads on our clients' websites and apps may be tailored based on limited data, such as the site or app you're currently using, your general location, device type, and your interaction with content. This customization helps limit the frequency of ad displays.

Data categories

1st Party IDs, Cookie IDs, and technical data related to these IDs (e.g. impressions, clicks, conversions, approximate geolocation, timestamp, URL, device information, browser information), IP address.

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, Ad Server

Legal bases

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests.

10. Save and communicate your privacy choices

Brief explanation

In order for Adform to respect user privacy choices, it has to first know what those choices are. The storing and communicating of user choice signals (for instance in the form of TCF Strings), ensure that the choices of users can be respected and that users do not have to make those choices again on each subsequent use of the relevant digital property.

This results in Adform’s recording of certain consent signals, or other user preferences, for instance by means of TC Strings within the Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF).

Data Categories

General metadata about the website operator that indicate user privacy choices, such as the website operator’s integration with the TCF, including data like the relevant consent management platform’s ID and the user’s consent per purpose.

Related Adform Product and/or Service

Buy Side, Sell Side, DMP

Legal bases

We process this data by default on the basis of our legitimate interest.

What channels does Adform operate in

Adform may serve you ads on a number of a different channels, including those set forth below. As discussed further in the Your Data Rights Section, please click here to exercise opt-out choices that may be available to you.

1. Video channel

Description: Video ads placed on phones, TVs, computers, gaming consoles, digital out of home signs and more.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookie IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

2. Audio channel

Description: Programmatic audio runs on a range of services including podcasts, streaming services and digital radio.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

3. Gaming channel

Description: In-game advertising covers a range of gaming types, including e-sport, streaming and platform games. The ad placements appear as game interruptions, outside of gaming area or in places which replicate advertising in real-world situations, such as advertising boards, product placements, mobile or static billboards.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

4. DOOH channel

Description: Digital Out Of Home (DOOH) refers to ads reaching the consumer while they are outside of their home, and targets people in transit, in public spaces, or during specific activities.

Personal data categories: none.

Legal basis of data processing: Legitimate interest.

5. CTV channel

Description: Connected TV (CTV) refers to an internet-connected TV with built-in internet app capabilities.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

6. Native ads channel

Description: Native ads match the content and design of the content they appear with. Native ads can be found in gaming, news, social media or other web or apps content outlets.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

7. Display channel

Description: Non-video, non-native banners that can be seen when browsing websites or using apps.

Personal data categories: 1st Party IDs; Cookies IDs and other identifiers as applicable (e.g. MAIDs, IDFAs); IP address; Device type; Device location data (where available).

Legal basis of data processing: Please refer to the section ‘What Information Do We Collect and Use’ for more information on exact lawful basis for each purpose.

How long do we store personal Information

We retain personal information we collect from you as long as we have an ongoing legitimate business need to do so (for example, to provide our clients with a service or to comply with applicable legal, tax or accounting requirements).

In the context of information derived from tracking technologies please note:

The cookies Adform sets in browsers expire 60 days after the last interaction with the website.  

Adform does not keep any Cookie-based Information longer than 13 months. In other words, any data that can be related to your internet devices are deleted after maximum 13 months. 

International Data Transfers

Personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside of the European Union. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country and, in some cases, may not be as protective. 

Some of the recipients of personal information may operate outside the European Union (e.g. in the United States of America). This means that when we transfer your Personal Information to these third parties, your personal information will be processed outside the European Union. We have taken appropriate safeguards to ensure an adequate level of data protection in accordance with this Privacy Policy on the side of the recipients. For example, some of the transfers may occur to the so-called ‘adequate’ countries that ensure the same level of protection of Personal Information. Nevertheless, in case the data transfer occurs to a country that is not considered ‘adequate’, we have signed Standard Contractual Clauses as an international data transfer mechanism and conducted necessary supplementary measures, such as transfer risk assessments to ensure that the level of protection of your Personal Information is not lower than in the EU.  

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the jurisdiction in which you reside, you may have the right to:

    • Access the personal information we have about you,
    • Correct inaccurate personal information,
    • Delete your personal information,
    • object to processing of your personal information,
    • restrict processing of your personal information,
    • A portable copy of your personal information, and
    • if we process your data with your consent, to withdraw your consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

You can exercise these rights at any time by visiting the Data Subject Rights page via this link (or for U.S. users, the U.S. Data Subject Rights page via this link), or by sending us an email to dpo[a]adform.com.

You have the right to complain to a data protection authority about our collection and use of your personal information. For more information, please contact your local data protection authority. A list of the European data protection authorities can be found here: https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en
