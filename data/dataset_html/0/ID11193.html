      Frequently Asked Questions

      Booking your beach getaway through Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy offers several exclusive advantages:
      •    Earn Marriott Bonvoy Points: Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy is the only platform where you can book a complete vacation package, including airfare, and earn Marriott Bonvoy points, enhancing the value of your travel.
      •    Exclusive Savings: By bundling your hotel stay with airfare or car rentals, you can enjoy significant savings on your vacation package.
      •    Luxurious Accommodations: Stay at renowned Marriott hotels and resorts, guaranteeing comfort, exceptional service, and access to premium amenities during your beach vacation.
      •    Diverse Beach Destinations: Choose from a wide array of popular beach destinations, including Hawaii, the Caribbean, Mexico, and various U.S. coastal locations, ensuring a perfect fit for your travel preferences.
      These benefits make Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy an ideal choice for a memorable and rewarding beach vacation.

      Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy offers packages to several sought-after beach destinations:
      •    Hawaii: Experience the serene beauty of the islands with villas offering ocean views and easy access to beaches.
      •    Caribbean: Relax in tropical settings with villas located in destinations like Aruba and St. John.
      •    Mexico: Enjoy vibrant culture and stunning coastlines with villa options in Cancun and Los Cabos.
      •    Florida: Discover the sunny shores of Miami and the Gulf Coast, perfect for sunbathing and water sports.
      These destinations offer a variety of experiences to suit different interests, making them perfect for a memorable beach vacation.

      To optimize your savings when booking a beach vacation package:
      •    Bundle Your Booking: Combine your hotel stay with airfare or car rentals through Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy to access exclusive discounted rates, potentially saving up to 20% on your package.
      •    Book Early: Beach destinations are popular; securing your reservation in advance can help you take advantage of the best deals and availability.
      •    Travel During Off-Peak Times: If your schedule allows, consider traveling during less busy periods to benefit from lower rates and special promotions.
      •    Monitor Special Promotions: Keep an eye on Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy's Deals of the Week for additional savings opportunities.

      Your Privacy

      Cookies are pieces of data stored on your browser or device that help us provide you a smooth and personalized experience on our web properties. We use different types of cookies, which are outlined below. ​
      More information

      Strictly Necessary Cookies

      Always Active

      Strictly Necessary cookies are vital for our (Marriott) property's core functionality, such as enabling booking systems, secure payment processes, and navigation through our hotel's amenities pages.

      Functional Cookies

      Active

      Functional cookies remember user preferences, like language settings, room choices, and past reservations, enhancing user experience by personalizing interactions on our site.

      Advertising Cookies

      Active

      Advertising cookies (also known as Marketing cookies) allow Marriott to log user interactions to deliver targeted ads and promotions, showcasing special offers, events, or loyalty programs.

      Analytics Cookies

      Active

      Analytics cookies (also known as Metric or Measurement cookies) gather data on user behavior, helping Marriott optimize our website's performance, improve service offerings, and understand user needs better.
