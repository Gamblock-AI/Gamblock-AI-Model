PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 05, 2025

At Bidfuse, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Pri-vacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and secure your data when you interact with our adver-tising technology, services, and websites.

This Privacy Policy outlines how Bidfuse, along with its affiliates and partners (collectively, “we”, “our”, or “us”), handles information we collect about you when you access our services. This includes our adverti-sing platform, demand and supply partner integrations, lead generation forms, analytics services, and related offerings.

By using our services, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

a. Device and Usage Data:

Browser type, version, language

Operating system

Referring URLs and exit pages

b. Interaction Data: Ad impressions

Deliver and optimize advertisements

Improve targeting and ad relevance

Prevent fraud and enhance security

Support customer service and technical operations

Fulfill legal and contractual obligations

We may also aggregate or anonymize data for internal analysis and business purposes.

4. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

Depending on your location, our legal basis for processing your information may include:

Your consent (e.g., for cookies or personalized ads)

Fulfillment of a contract

Compliance with legal obligations

Our legitimate business interests (e.g., fraud prevention, service improvement)

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Typically:

e. Affiliates and Group Companies:

We may share information within our corporate group to enhance service offerings and provide unified support.

f. Legal Requirements:

We may disclose data when required by law, legal process, or to defend our rights or property.

g. Business Transfers:

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, user information may be part of the transaction.

h. International Transfers:

We may transfer and process data globally, including in countries with different data protection laws. Safeguards are applied as necessary.

7. DATA SECURITY

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your information. While we strive for strong security, no system can guarantee complete protection. If a breach occurs, we may notify you via our site or email.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

COOKIES POLICY

This Cookie Policy explains how Bidfuse Media Technologies Pte. Ltd. and its affiliated companies (“Bid-fuse”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and process informa-tion about visitors and users across our ad delivery ecosystem, websites, and our partners’ digital pro-perties.

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. These files are used to re-member information about your browsing behavior, such as language preferences or login details, and help deliver a more customized online experience.

1. Who Places Cookies on Your Browser?

As part of our advertising services, Bidfuse partners with publishers, ad networks, and technology ven-dors (“Partners”) who may allow our cookies to be placed on their websites and mobile platforms. In such cases, our cookies are classified as third-party cookies. These are distinct from cookies placed directly by the website owner.

Our cookies may be triggered when you interact with these websites and are designed to collect non-identifying behavioral data such as:

Pages visited

IPv4, device type, browser, and OS details

Interaction with Bidfuse-served ads (e.g., views, clicks)

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to support several key functions:

Frequency Capping: Avoiding repetitive ad exposure

Campaign Measurement: Tracking performance metrics like views and clicks

Fraud Detection: Preventing invalid traffic and ensuring authentic user engagement

System Health: Supporting diagnostics, maintenance, and service optimization

In some cases, Bidfuse may receive additional insights from its Partners or affiliated entities to enhan-ce user targeting or measure aggregated audience trends.

We do not collect sensitive identifiers such as your name, address, financial data, or government-is-sued IDs through these cookies.

3. Data Sharing and Collaboration

The data collected through cookies on Bidfuse or our Partners’ websites may be shared with:

Our affiliated group of companies

Advertisers and demand partners for campaign delivery and reporting

Third-party vendors (e.g., hosting providers, analytics platforms, and data enrichment partners)

Any such sharing is done in accordance with applicable data protection laws and contractual obliga-tions.

4. Partner and Third-Party Cookies

Partners may also set their own cookies when you visit their websites. We do not control these cookies and recommend that you review the respective privacy policies of those websites to understand how your information is being handled.

5. Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising

If you prefer not to receive behaviorally targeted advertising via the Bidfuse Ad Network or its affiliates, you may opt out at any time by visiting our opt-out page:

https://bidfuse.com/opt-out

Please note:

We need to store an opt-out cookie in your browser to recognize your choice.

If you delete or clear your browser cookies, or switch to a new browser or device, you’ll need to opt out again.

Even after opting out, you may still receive ads from Bidfuse, but they will not be personalized.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings. However, blocking cookies may limit functionality on certain sites or disrupt the delivery of relevant ads.

If you have questions or concerns about how Bidfuse uses cookies, feel free to contact us at privacy@-bidfuse.com.

OPTING OUT OF INTEREST-BASED ADVERTISING

At Bidfuse, we respect your privacy choices and are committed to providing transparency around how personalized ads are delivered. You can choose to opt out of receiving ads that are tailored to your interests across various platforms and devices.

General Interest-Based Advertising Opt-Out

Bidfuse uses signals such as the websites you visit, mobile apps you use, and your engagement with ads to infer your interests and display more relevant advertising. For example, if you regularly engage with travel-related apps or content, you may be shown more ads for travel offers or related services.

Importantly, Bidfuse does not collect personal identifiers like your name or contact details. All proces-sing is in line with our Privacy Policy, which outlines how we manage and safeguard your data.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through the following options:

1. AdChoices & Industry Opt-Out Tools

You may opt out of interest-based advertising through industry-supported initiatives:

AdChoices – Visit youradchoices.com to manage ad preferences.

NAI’s Mobile Choices – To control interest-based ads on your mobile devices, visit: https://www.ne-

tworkadvertising.org/mobile-choice

NAI’s TV Choices – For Connected TV devices, visit: https://www.networkadvertising.org/internet-con-

nected-tv-choices

2. Device-Level Opt-Out Options

Mobile App Advertising

To stop receiving personalized ads within mobile apps, you can disable ad tracking directly on your device:

Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.

iOS: Navigate to Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising and enable Limit Ad Tracking (for iOS 14 and earlier) or disable Personalized Ads (iOS 14.5+).

Please note: This action is specific to the device on which the setting is applied. You must repeat the opt-out process on each device individually.

Mobile Web Browsing

To opt out of targeted ads while browsing on your mobile web browser, you can disable tracking or adjust cookie settings in your browser’s privacy or security settings.

Connected TV Devices

Interest-based advertising can also be disabled on Connected TV (CTV) platforms by adjusting system settings:

tvOS/iOS Devices: Enable Limit Ad Tracking

Android TV: Enable Opt Out of Interest-Based Ads

Roku: Navigate to Privacy > Advertising and enable Limit Ad Tracking

Refer to your device manufacturer’s privacy policy for specific instructions, as steps may vary by model or OS version.

3. Bidfuse-Specific Opt-Out

You can request to opt out of personalized advertising across Bidfuse’s network. When you do, we will no longer use your advertising ID to serve interest-based ads. However, you may still receive general ads that are not tailored to your preferences.

To submit your opt-out request:

Via Browser Cookie – Opt out of personalized ads on mobile web.

Via Device Identifier – Submit your mobile advertising ID for Android or iOS:

Enter your Device Advertising ID:

Android ID / Google Advertising ID

iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)

If you are unsure how to retrieve your advertising ID, visit your device’s privacy settings or refer to your device manufacturer’s support documentation.

4. California Residents: Do Not Sell My Data

If you reside in California, you have the legal right to opt out of the sale of your personal data under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

To exercise this right, please submit the following:

Your device advertising ID (Android or iOS) or browser ID

Your email address (if known and voluntarily shared)

Once submitted, we will process your request in compliance with applicable privacy laws. Opting out may not stop all data collection, but it will prevent your data from being sold or used for interest-ba-sed advertising.

Contact & Additional Support

If you need assistance or have questions about opting out, please contact our SQA team:

privacy@bidfuse.com

Be sure to include your advertising ID or browser identifier if you are submitting a request, so we can locate your data and action your preferences correctly.

You may also View My Cookie ID to check the unique identifier used by our systems for interest-based advertising purposes.

1. WHO WE ARE

Bidfuse provides mobile and digital advertising technology solutions to help our clients deliver more relevant and personalized advertisements. Our services include data processing for ad delivery, mea-surement, and reporting. In some instances, Bidfuse acts as a data controller (e.g., when collecting data via our own website), while in others, we act as a data processor on behalf of our clients.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Information Collected via Our Services (as a Processor)

When you interact with ads served by Bidfuse or visit websites and apps that use our advertising tech-nology, we may collect:

Device identifiers (e.g., IDFA, GAID)

IP address

Browser type, language, and settings

Device type and model

Mobile carrier

Operating system and version

Geolocation data (limited to coarse location)

Interaction data (e.g., ad impressions, clicks)

Contact information (e.g., name, email address)

Information submitted through forms (e.g., demo requests, support tickets)

Consent – where you have given explicit consent (e.g., via cookie banners).

Legal obligations – where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws.

To deliver relevant ads and measure their performance.

To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.

To prevent fraud and ensure security.

We may share your personal data with:

Service providers – who process data on our behalf under strict contractual obligations.

Your data may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside the EEA, including countries that may not provide the same level of data protection. When we transfer your personal data internationa-lly, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

You can manage your cookie preferences via your browser settings or by visiting our Cookie Policy. Please review third-party privacy and cookie policies where applicable.

9. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Bidfuse does not knowingly collect personal data from children as defined by applicable laws (e.g., under 16 in the EEA, under 13 in the U.S.). We comply with COPPA, GDPR, and CCPA requirements for age-based data processing and apply appropriate age-gating controls.
