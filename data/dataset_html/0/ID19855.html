Supporting cast & special appearances for JTBC drama “Human X Nine Tailed Fox”

Supporting cast & special appearances announced for JTBC drama series “Human X Nine Tailed Fox” starring Gianna Jun and Ji Chang-Wook. Appearing in the upcoming drama series in supporting character roles are Cha Joo-Young, Kang You-Seok, Kim Jong-Soo, and Kim Min-Suk. Also cast to make special appearances are Hwang In-Yeop, Seol Hyun, and Lee Jae-In.

For supporting character roles, Cha Joo-Young will play Ji Woo-Kyeong. She is the CEO of Wookyeong Entertainment and Gu Ja-Hong’s (Gianna Jun) good partner. She is also the only person that knows Gu Ja-Hong is a gumiho. Kang You-Seok will play Go Tae-Seung. He is the only friend of Choi Seok (Ji Chang-Wook) and the deputy director of Oseong Museum. Actor Kim Jong-Soo will play Choi Man-Gwan. He is Choi Seok’s grandfather. Kim Min-Suk will play Tiger Beom-Yi of Inwang Mountain. He enjoys a laid-back lifestyle.

For the special appearances, Hwang In-Yeop will play Sam-Yi. He is an enemy of Gu Ja-Hong. Actresses Seol Hyun and Lee Jae-In will play monters Yeon-Yi and Bang-Sang. They lurk around Gu Ja-Hong and Choi Seok.

“Human X Nine Tailed Fox” is now set to air sometime during the first half of 2027.
