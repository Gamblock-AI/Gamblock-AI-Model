DELAMIBRANDS
Celebrates 45th
Anniversary With
Mangrove Planting
Initiative

To commemorate its 45th anniversary, PT DELAMIBRANDS KHARISMA BUSANA has strengthened its commitment to environmental sustainability by planting 100 mangrove trees at Pantai Indah Kapuk in North Jakarta on November 6, 2024. This initiative supports coastal ecosystems, helps prevent erosion, and contributes to climate change mitigation.

DELAMIBRANDS COO, Boysanto Pasaribu, shared, “We are grateful for the support we have received over the past 45 years. This mangrove planting is part of our responsibility to give back to the environment and reflects our commitment to a greener future.”

The mangrove planting underscores DELAMIBRANDS’ vision to make a positive impact on the environment and local communities. Looking ahead, the company remains dedicated to expanding its environmental initiatives and continues to bring “Pride in Local” values to life through meaningful action for Indonesia.
