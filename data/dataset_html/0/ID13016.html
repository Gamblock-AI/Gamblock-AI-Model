Who we are

Suggested text: Our website address is: https://connekt.ai.

Comments

Suggested text: When visitors leave comments on the site we collect the data shown in the comments form, and also the visitor’s IP address and browser user agent string to help spam detection.

An anonymized string created from your email address (also called a hash) may be provided to the Gravatar service to see if you are using it. The Gravatar service privacy policy is available here: https://automattic.com/privacy/. After approval of your comment, your profile picture is visible to the public in the context of your comment.

Media

Suggested text: If you upload images to the website, you should avoid uploading images with embedded location data (EXIF GPS) included. Visitors to the website can download and extract any location data from images on the website.

Cookies

Suggested text: If you leave a comment on our site you may opt-in to saving your name, email address and website in cookies. These are for your convenience so that you do not have to fill in your details again when you leave another comment. These cookies will last for one year.

If you visit our login page, we will set a temporary cookie to determine if your browser accepts cookies. This cookie contains no personal data and is discarded when you close your browser.

When you log in, we will also set up several cookies to save your login information and your screen display choices. Login cookies last for two days, and screen options cookies last for a year. If you select "Remember Me", your login will persist for two weeks. If you log out of your account, the login cookies will be removed.

If you edit or publish an article, an additional cookie will be saved in your browser. This cookie includes no personal data and simply indicates the post ID of the article you just edited. It expires after 1 day.

Embedded content from other websites

Suggested text: Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the other website.

These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, including tracking your interaction with the embedded content if you have an account and are logged in to that website.

Who we share your data with

Suggested text: If you request a password reset, your IP address will be included in the reset email.

How long we retain your data

Suggested text: If you leave a comment, the comment and its metadata are retained indefinitely. This is so we can recognize and approve any follow-up comments automatically instead of holding them in a moderation queue.

For users that register on our website (if any), we also store the personal information they provide in their user profile. All users can see, edit, or delete their personal information at any time (except they cannot change their username). Website administrators can also see and edit that information.

What rights you have over your data

Suggested text: If you have an account on this site, or have left comments, you can request to receive an exported file of the personal data we hold about you, including any data you have provided to us. You can also request that we erase any personal data we hold about you. This does not include any data we are obliged to keep for administrative, legal, or security purposes.

Where your data is sent

Suggested text: Visitor comments may be checked through an automated spam detection service.

Privacy Policy

Welcome to Connekt!

Effective Date: 17th April 2025


Connekt is the operating brand of KC Connekt Media LTD (Ireland) and KC Connekt Media Pte Ltd (Singapore), subsidiaries of Connekt Media Holding Ltd.


Connekt is a global ad-tech company that operates and provides an online supply-side platform (“SSP”). Our SSP offers a proprietary programmatic solution designed to connect third-party advertisers (“Advertisers”) with digital inventory from publishers (“Publishers”) through integrations with various digital assets, including mobile apps and connected TV environments (“Digital Assets”). We facilitate the delivery of ads using industry-standard mechanisms such as OpenRTB, header bidding solutions, and direct tag-based connections.


At Connekt, we value your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we may share it with—only when necessary.


By accessing or using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Your use of Connekt is also subject to our Terms and Conditions.


We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of those updates. Please review this page regularly.


This Privacy Policy is maintained in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR – EU) and, where applicable, the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA – US). For more information about our business, visit https://connekt.ai.

Types of Information We Collect

We may collect two types of information through our services and interactions with Digital Assets:

1. Non-Personal Information

This includes information that does not directly identify an individual. Examples include aggregated usage data and technical data such as:

  • Browser or device type
  • Page views or ad interactions
  • Referrer URLs or app bundle names
  • Approximate geo-location (e.g., country-level)

This type of information is used for operational purposes including reporting, frequency capping, and performance optimization.

2. Personal Information

In some jurisdictions, certain identifiers such as IP addresses may be considered personal information. While we do not directly store or enrich data, we may process:

  • IP addresses
  • Ad identifiers (e.g., IDFA or AAID) when passed through our systems programmatically

We treat such identifiers in accordance with applicable laws and ensure they are handled only as needed for the delivery of programmatic advertising campaigns. We do not combine this data with any other sources to re-identify individuals.

If Personal Information is ever combined with Non-Personal Information, the combined data is treated as Personal Information as long as it remains combined.

How we use your information

We use information we process through our platform for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our SSP and services
  • To optimize the delivery and performance of advertising through digital assets
  • For fraud detection, billing reconciliation, and dispute resolution
  • For internal business reporting, auditing, and analytics
  • To respond to inquiries or service-related communications
  • To protect our platform, our partners, and users from security issues or technical disruptions

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age defined by applicable data protection laws. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that such data has been inadvertently collected, we will take steps to delete the information promptly.

Data Retention

We retain non-personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, including analytics, billing, and auditing.

As a data processor, we do not store personal data for extended periods and do not use such data for profiling or marketing purposes. Where retention of any identifying data is required to fulfill contractual or legal obligations, it is handled in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or monetize personal information. We only share data in limited circumstances, and strictly for operational or compliance purposes:

  • Authorized Disclosures: We may disclose personal information to third parties when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, enforce our policies, or detect and prevent fraud or security issues.
  • Service Providers: We work with trusted third parties who assist in the delivery and operation of our services, such as cloud hosting, demand-side platforms, and billing support. These parties are contractually bound to use data only as needed to perform their functions.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, personal information may be transferred as part of the transaction.
  • Non-Personal Information: We may share aggregated or anonymized data (which does not identify individuals) with partners for reporting, forecasting, and business development purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the integrity and availability of data transmitted through our systems. While Connekt does not store personal data, we ensure that all systems involved in the transfer of data between partners follow industry-standard security practices.

These include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Encryption protocols for data in transit (e.g., SSL/TLS)
  • Regular audits and system monitoring

 

Although no internet transmission is entirely secure, we are committed to continuous improvement in data protection and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.

International Data Transfers

Where data is processed outside of the European Economic Area (EEA), including in Singapore, we ensure such transfers are conducted in compliance with applicable data protection laws. These safeguards may include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or other appropriate mechanisms.

We regularly assess the legal framework and privacy environment of third countries to ensure the protection of personal data.

Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF 2.2)

Connekt participates in the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework and complies with its specifications and policies. We act as a data processor under TCF, using data solely to facilitate programmatic advertising transactions.

Our legal basis for data processing is consent, depending on the nature of the demand partner integration.
