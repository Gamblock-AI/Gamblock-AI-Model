Protecting data across every screen and signal.

Data Controller

GSTV is the data controller under the Data Protection Act 2018 (Isle of Man) for personal data you provide. Processing is grounded in contract performance or your explicit consent, and this notice may be updated as our operations evolve.

“Processing” includes collecting, storing, evaluating, using, disclosing, and transferring your data within GSTV or to vetted partners as detailed below.

Personal Data We Collect

Across our websites, apps, and consent flows we collect information such as IP addresses and device identifiers, device settings (manufacturer, model, operating system version, status, and screen size), browser information, digital media interactions including watch time and content details, and on-site interactions such as submitted form data.

How We Use Personal Data

Personal data allows us to deliver personalised advertising and content experiences, power interactive features across devices, monitor platform performance and resolve technical issues, provide analytics and reporting to partners, and measure campaign and content effectiveness.

Contact form submissions are processed under consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and retained until the request is closed, consent is withdrawn, or statutory retention periods expire.

Cookies and Consent Management

We use Google Analytics (Google Inc., Mountain View, USA) under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR to optimise our products. Additional cookies support standard logging, attribution, and engagement use cases. Consent preferences are managed through our CMP under the IAB TCF; you can revisit or withdraw consent at any time via browser settings or supported platforms such as Freeview.

Retention of Data

Retention periods vary by purpose and legal basis. We retain data where it is contractually necessary to deliver digital media access, where legitimate interests are balanced against user rights, where consent remains active and can be withdrawn at any time, and where legal obligations or law-enforcement requirements apply.

Interaction data such as watch history and analytics is retained for analysis and service improvements before being securely anonymised or deleted.

Children’s Data

We do not knowingly process personal data from children under 16 without guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us so we can remove it promptly.

Data Sharing and International Transfers

We may share personal data with advertising exchanges, supply-side platforms, ad networks, service providers, content partners, operational vendors, affiliates involved in corporate transactions, and regulators or law-enforcement agencies when required.

Data may be stored in the UK, Europe, or North America. We enforce Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards for transfers outside the EEA or UK.

Your Rights

Under GDPR you can request access to personal data we hold, rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data, erasure under qualifying circumstances, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing including marketing activities.

Email contact@goseetv.media to exercise your rights. We respond within one month in line with regulatory guidance.
