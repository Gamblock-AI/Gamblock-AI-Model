ABOUT US
Nox was founded in 2015 and has independently innovated and developed the Nox Juxing overseas influencer marketing cloud platform, which is now the world's most-visited influencer engine. It has helped over 400,000 Chinese enterprises deliver their products and services to global users while providing high-quality AI-powered products to clients worldwide.
