FAQ

ChatGPT is a great personal assistant, but it often operates as a siloed chatbot, with disconnected context and tools. An AI platform like nexos.ai offers access to leading AI models, Agents, work tool integrations, and other AI tools in a single place.

When choosing the best AI platform for your team, prioritize integrations and execution capabilities. The best platform shouldn't just provide a chatbot or an AI-powered search engine. It should connect to your existing apps like Slack, Google Drive, or GitHub to execute tasks and answer with context, not a generic web search. 


Especially for non-tech professionals, look for no-code tools that allow anyone to create custom agents, plus an expert-built template library to build agents faster.

An AI platform acts as a productivity multiplier. It eliminates boring tasks by automating them – for example, drafting reports, summarizing meetings, or researching leads – in the background. This frees you to focus on the high-value creative and strategic work that actually needs a human touch.

nexos.ai is an all-in-one AI platform that enables business teams to reach their full potential. We move beyond simple chat by allowing anyone to build custom, no-code AI Agents and automate complex Workflows from a single, unified interface.

nexos.ai is model-agnostic, which means that you have total flexibility to use the best AI models for your specific tasks. We support hundreds of AI models from top providers like Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, and Meta.

Integration is seamless and requires zero coding. You can securely connect your Agents to Google Workspace, SharePoint, Slack, GitHub, Zendesk, and other work apps. Simply ask your Workspace Owner to enable integrations and connect them via Chat.

nexos.ai is a secure command center for your entire AI usage. Every interaction is recorded in detailed audit logs, providing the total observability needed for internal reviews and security assessments.
