  • BACHELOR DEGREE FINANCE / ACCOUNTANCY / BANKING MIN.GPA : 3
  • MINIMUM 3 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE AS ACCOUNTING STAFF/SPV OR PUBLIC ACCOUNTANT ASSOCIATE.
  • UNDERSTANDING, RECOGNIZING, AND ABLE TO RESOLVE PROBLEMS, MISTAKES, AND RISKS INDEPENDENTLY.
  • KNOWLEDGEABLE IN THE LATEST PSAK.

  • HANDLING ACCRUED ACCOUNTING.
  • PREPARATION OF QUARTERLY AND ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT.
  • REVIEW OF BALANCE SHEET LISTINGS PREPARED BY SUBORDINATES.

HEALTH, SAFETY, ENVIRONTMENT & SECURITY SECTION HEAD -  SUPERVISOR

  • BACHELOR DEGREE ENGINEERING (ENVIRONMENT / HEALTH / SAFETY/ ECOLOGY) MIN.GPA : 3
  • HAVE SIM A
  • HAVE AK3U Certificate
  • PREFERABLY HAVE OTHER AK3 CERTIFICATION.
  • PREFERABLY HAS EXPERIENCE AND KNOWLEDGE ABOUT THE HEALTH, SAFETY, AND ENVIRONMENT FIELD
  • HAVE THE ABILITY and KNOWLEDGE HOW TO IMPLEMENT HSE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
  • ABLE TO COMMUNICATE AND COORDINATE WITH TEAM.
  • HAVE WORKING KNOWLEDGE OF MICROSOFT OFFICE WORD, EXCEL AND POWERPOINT ,AUTOCAD, ETC.
  • GOOD INTERPERSONAL SKILLS.
  • WILLING TO DO BUSINESS TRIP IN EAST JAVA & NTT AREAS
JOB DESCRIPTION :
  • CONDUCT AND MONITOR EA HIRA (ENVIRONMENTAL ASPECTS HAZARD IDENTIFICATION AND RISK ASSESSMENT).
  • DO CONTINUAL IMPROVEMENT IN ISO 9001, SMK3 AND OHSAS 18001.
  • DEVELOP, IMPLEMENT AND MAINTAIN HSE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM.

LEGAL SECTION HEAD -  SUPERVISOR

QUALIFICATION :
  • BACHELOR DEGREE LAW MIN.GPA : 3
  • Contract Drafting experience, CCD (Certified Contract Drafting) certification is a plus.
  • Has experience in corporate licensing.
  • Has experience in a law firm or litigation.
JOB DESCRIPTION :
  • Supervising and implementing the process of drafting agreements and managing company licensing to fulfill the legal aspects of the company as required by applicable laws and regulations.
  • Providing input on legal steps that need to be taken by the Company to Managers that can support the implementation of the company's strategic plans.
  • Provide legal consultation to Users who will take legal action with parties outside the company in accordance with management regulations to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Monitor the implementation of information security system management and provide the necessary support to employees in operating the information security system in their work areas.

MECHANIC BOOTCAMP -  STAFF

QUALIFICATION :
  • SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL / VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL
  • Lulusan SMK Jurusan Teknik Sepeda Motor atau Teknik Kendaraan Ringan Otomotif
  • Nilai kelulusan SMK min 7.00
  • Bersedia ditempatkan di wilayah NTT
  • Bersedia untuk menjalani kemitraan selama 3 tahun
JOB DESCRIPTION :
  • Melakukan perbaikan dan perawatan unit sepeda motor sesuai dengan NOS (Network Operation Standard) dan perintah kerja yang tertuang dalam form SA dan Honda safety checksheet.
  • Memeriksa kondisi kelayakan spare part unit sepeda motor dan menginformasikan rekomendasi pergantian spare part yang diperlukan untuk memastikan kondisi unit motor dalam kondisi prima.
  • Memastikan tools dalam kondisi baik dan lengkap serta berada pada tempat yang disediakan untuk memastikan siap saat dibutuhkan.
  • Menjaga kebersihan dan kerapian area kerja bengkel untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan nyaman.
  • Mengikuti pelatihan yang disediakan oleh perusahaan untuk memastikan pemenuhan kompetensi yang diperlukan sebagai mekanik.
  • Benefit : Uang Saku (saat training), bonus kemitraan, uang kos, tiket PP ke daerah asal, komisi tambahan, dll
