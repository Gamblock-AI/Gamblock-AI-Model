Overview

We use Tracking Technologies, such as cookies, web beacons, and pixels, on this website for various purposes such as to conduct targeted advertising, measure analytics, and ensure the functionality of our website.

Through Tracking Technologies, IAS may share personal data, including identifiers, internet usage information, and inferences, it collects from website visitors to IASs vendors that provide analytics and targeted advertising services. You may manage your preferences regarding certain Tracking Technologies, including disabling or enabling them based on their purpose (including Advertising, Analytics, and Performance) by navigating to the tabs on the left-hand side.

For more information about our websites use of Tracking Technologies, see our Privacy Policy and our Supplemental U.S. State Privacy Notice. You may also submit certain privacy requests by filling out the IAS Privacy Request Portal.


More information

Advertising

These Tracking Technologies help us, and other parties, personalize ads that are shown to you on our website, as well as on other online services. If enabled, these Tracking Technologies allow you to receive cross-context and targeted advertising across online services over time. For example, if you show interest in one of our products shown on our website, you may receive an advertisement for that same product on a different online service.

Analytics

These Tracking Technologies allow us to assess how users navigate and interact with our website allowing us to measure and improve our services based on consumer interests and tendencies (such as knowing which parts or features of the website are the most and least popular). Note that if Advertising Tracking Technologies are enabled, they may interact with Analytics Tracking Technologies to improve advertising features.

Performance

These Tracking Technologies enhance the websites performance, such as detecting fraudulent access (including from bots), filtering out bots, improving latency, controlling web traffic, redirecting links or between device formats efficiently, and storing language preferences. Note that if Advertising Tracking Technologies are enabled, they may interact with Performance Tracking Technologies to prevent fraudulent bots interacting with our ads and to improve ad delivery and performance. Without these Tracking Technologies, your experience on the website may be interrupted, slower, inefficient, and certain content or portions of the website may not render or be accessible.
