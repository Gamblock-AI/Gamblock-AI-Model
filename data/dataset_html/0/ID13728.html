How EID Works

GETTING STARTED

1. Register

Creating an account sets the foundation for a secure and verifiable digital identity. An account can be created in different ways, by using an email and strong password, connecting a Web3 wallet, or signing in with a Google account. This flexibility makes onboarding fast and familiar, while optional two-factor authentication ensures protection from the very first step.

Experience the convenience of a single sign-on, allowing you to access multiple services with just one identity.

VERIFICATION

2. Verify Account

Users must confirm their email address and verify their phone number via OTP. These steps secure the account and establish a reliable channel for future verification. At this step, the EID identity is ready to be used for single sign-on (SSO) across connected applications. Instead of creating new accounts everywhere, the same EID can serve as a trusted login method for partner platforms.

Improve trust level on your profile and increase your credibility by verifying your identity.

UNIQUE IDENTITY

3. Claim EID identity

By claiming an EID identity, a unique digital name becomes yours. This unlocks more features of the platform, including the ability to create and share a public profile. With a verified identity anchored on the IDCHAIN blockchain, the EID account becomes more than just a login, it becomes a trusted representation in the digital world.

Make your identity more personal and easier to remember with a premium identity.

TRUST & CREDIBILITY

4. Verify National ID

This process elevates trust by validating National ID documents such as an ID card, passport, or driver's license, along with a quick liveness check. Data is encrypted end-to-end and securely reviewed by accredited partners. Upon approval, a verifiable credential is issued, and the EID account receives a trusted badg, unlocking advanced features and seamless verification across integrated services.
