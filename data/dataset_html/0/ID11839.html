Talents Beyond the Uniform: Meet Made Adi

At Padma Resort Legian, you’ll meet many friendly faces, but some stories go a little deeper. One of them belongs to I Made Adi Dwi Cahyana Putra, better known as De Adi.Art has been part of De Adi’s life since childhood. Growing up in the Kuta area near the resort, he often spent time alongside his father, helping with creative projects, especially making Ogoh-ogoh, a traditional Balinese art form full of character, culture, and meaning. What started as a fun pastime soon became something he truly loved.Today, that passion has taken him far. De Adi is trusted by Sekaa Teruna Teruni (Youth Association) Yowana Giri, Banjar Tegal Kuta, as an Ogoh-ogoh architect, and he has also been involved in judging various art events around Bali, from mini Ogoh-ogoh and sketches to Tapel Ogoh-ogoh.De Adi joined Padma Resort Legian as a bellboy in 2023, where he’s known for his warm smile and eye for detail. But beyond his day-to-day role, his creativity sometimes shows up in unexpected ways. As part of Padma Resort Legian’s commitment to supporting local talent, his artistic touch has found a home within the resort.You may notice his work in subtle details around the property, especially the hand-painted mirror art in selected VIP guest rooms. These small, thoughtful touches add something extra to the stay and often become a pleasant surprise.If you’re lucky enough to find a beautifully painted mirror waiting in your room, don’t forget to capture the moment and share it on Instagram, and be sure to tag @padmalegian. And if you happen to meet De Adi during your stay, take a moment to say hello. His genuine warmth and passion are part of what makes the Padma experience truly special.Watch the full story on our YouTube channel and see his creativity in action.https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94S5nNx3opE

ZERU: A New Height of Dining, Cocktails, and Music Culture Arrives in Semarang

Rising above Semarang’s skyline, a new rooftop destination is set to redefine the city’s dining and nightlife scene. Opening on 5 December 2025, the 9th floor of Padma Hotel Semarang will serve as the cherry on top of the hotel experience. Blending fire-driven Mediterranean cuisine, artisan mixology, and an immersive vinyl-led music culture, the venue promises a multi-sensory experience, positioning itself as one of Central Java’s most anticipated destinations of the year.Thoughtfully designed across indoor and outdoor settings, including a dedicated listening bar, an elegant dining room, and a skyline-facing open-air terrace offering breathtaking views of Semarang, the space transitions effortlessly from evening sophistication to nighttime vibrancy, delivering an elevated experience for dining, cocktails, and music alike.Grill Section - ZERU Sky TerraceA Culinary Journey of Fire and FlavoursInspired by the coastal Mediterranean, the venue embraces a philosophy where fire, smoke, and spice define the experience. Guests can expect premium grilled meats, fresh seafood, and spice-forward dishes rooted in Southern European and Eastern Mediterranean traditions. Select offerings are elevated through dramatic tableside presentations such as Bone Torching and the aromatic Smoking Tagine, merging craftsmanship with storytelling for an unforgettable dining experience.Dining Area - Zeru Sky TerraceCrafted Cocktails and Curated SoundFrom the warm glow of golden hour to the vibrant energy of late-night lounging, the bar serves signature cocktails crafted with seasonal ingredients and masterful techniques, each perfectly complementing the Mediterranean-inspired menu. More than a bar, this is a listening sanctuary. A space where music is as essential as the drinks in hand. Vinyl DJ sessions and a meticulously curated soundscape, spanning soul, funk, ’70s–’80s classics, adult pop, jazz, and electronic, create a layered auditory experience that moves with the night. Every note, every sip, every moment is intentionally designed to captivate the senses and elevate the evening into something truly unforgettable.Bar Area - Zeru Sky TerraceElevating Semarang’s Lifestyle and F&B SceneWith its bold culinary vision, handcrafted cocktails, and immersive music culture, Zeru at Padma Hotel Semarang promises more than just a night out. From the first spark of fire on the grill to the final note spinning on vinyl, every detail is crafted to delight the senses and create lasting memories.Discover the perfect blend of contemporary and luxury over the sky, at Zeru Sky Terrace.Reserve Your Spothttps://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84nWW5mqNXw

35 Years of Padma Hotels: A Legacy of Indonesian Hospitality

A Journey Through the YearsPadma Resort Legian: The First StepPresidential Suite - Padma Resort Legian - 1991It began in 1990, on the shores of Legian, Bali. Back then, Padma Resort Legian was a modest seaside retreat, with a single pool and just a few rooms. What it lacked in grandeur, it made up for in warmth. Guests arrived to discover something rare: a spirit of hospitality deeply rooted in Balinese culture, expressed not through extravagance, but through genuine care and attention.Presidential Suite - Padma Resort Legian - 2025Over the next 35 years, this humble beginning grew into Padma Hotels, a name that now embraces five unique properties—each with its own personality, each inspired by the place it calls home, and each carrying forward that same spirit of heartfelt hospitality. The journey from one resort to a collection of award-winning destinations is not just a story of expansion. It is a story of vision, perseverance, and the belief that true luxury is found in experiences that linger long after a stay has ended.Padma Hotel Bandung: Expansion in the HighlandsLobby - Padma Hotel Bandung- 2006In 1994, on the tranquil slopes of Ciumbuleuit in Bandung, stood a quiet retreat that—unknowingly—would mark the beginning of a new chapter for Padma Hotels. Far removed from the city’s bustle, it welcomed those willing to make the climb with crisp mountain air, sweeping valleys, and the intimacy of just 51 elegantly designed rooms.What started as a boutique hideaway soon earned a reputation as one of West Java’s most cherished escapes. Over the years, this gem evolved and, in 2009, was reintroduced as Padma Hotel Bandung. Today, it offers 124 rooms with breathtaking valley views, a heated infinity pool that seems to spill into the horizon, and the ever-popular Adventure Park—a playground not only for children, but for families to bond amidst nature.Lobby - Padma Hotel Bandung- 2020More than just a leisure haven, Padma Hotel Bandung has become a favourite retreat for business travellers seeking inspiration in the highlands. And now, with an ambitious transformation project underway, it stands ready to once again redefine hospitality in Bandung—blending Sundanese warmth with a new era of sophistication.Padma Resort Ubud: A Sanctuary in the JunglePool - Padma Resort Ubud- 2016When Padma Resort Ubud opened in 2016, it redefined luxury in Bali’s northern Payangan valley. While Ubud had long been a cultural hub, Padma introduced something new: a wellness retreat that merged eco-conscious design with elevated comfort.The now-iconic 89-metre infinity pool, perched above bamboo forests, quickly became a destination in itself. But beyond the views, guests found jungle treks, yoga programmes, an agroforestry garden, and quiet corners that nurtured both body and spirit.Pool - Padma Resort Ubud- 2024In less than a decade, Padma Resort Ubud has been recognised among the world’s leading wellness resorts—proof that the Padma philosophy, when placed in harmony with nature, resonates globally.Resinda Hotel Karawang: Hospitality in the UnexpectedLobby - Resinda Hotel Karawang - 2016If Legian and Ubud reflected Bali’s natural beauty, and Bandung reflected the highlands, then Resinda Hotel Karawang, opened in 2016, was something altogether different. Here, in the middle of an industrial hub, Padma saw opportunity: to bring warmth, style, and comfort to a place known more for factories than leisure.Lobby - Resinda Hotel Karawang - 2024The result was transformative. With its lagoon-inspired pool, Japanese bathhouse, and stylish ballroom, Resinda quickly became the city’s preferred destination, not just for business travellers, but also for families and long-stay guests. It proved that hospitality could thrive anywhere, so long as it carried authenticity and imagination.Padma Hotel Semarang: A New EraAerial View - Padma Hotel Semarang - 2022In 2022, Padma Hotels planted its newest roots in Semarang, Central Java’s bustling capital. From the outset, Padma Hotel Semarang broke new ground: the city’s first pool-access rooms, a warm salt-chlorinated pool, expansive function halls, and what is now Central Java’s largest hotel ballroom.Waterpark - Padma Hotel Semarang - 2023Soon after, the addition of a full waterpark transformed it into more than just a hotel, it became a cultural and lifestyle centrepiece. Within just three years, Padma Hotel Semarang has hosted large-scale events, introduced beloved dining venues like Goolali Gelato Bar & Patisserie, and emerged as a symbol of Semarang’s rising prominence on Indonesia’s tourism map.Beyond the PropertiesPadma Hotels is not just about buildings and rooms. It is about moments of connection, joy, and discovery.Over the years, the brand has introduced award-winning dining concepts, curated wellness programmes, and signature events such as the Padma Run, where thousands come together to celebrate community and wellbeing. The launch of the Padma Resident membership programme and the Padma Hotels App has brought guests even closer, offering exclusive rewards and personalised services that extend beyond their stay.Through it all, Padma Hotels has kept sustainability and cultural enrichment at its heart, ensuring that growth never comes at the cost of heritage or the environment.Looking AheadThirty-five years is a milestone, and also a reminder of how far Padma Hotels has come, and how much lies ahead. With new collaborations, innovative guest experiences, and cultural partnerships on the horizon, the brand continues to look forward with optimism.From that first pool in Legian to the skyline of Semarang, Padma Hotels has never stopped evolving. And as it toasts to the future, one thing remains unchanged: its commitment to making every stay memorable, meaningful, and truly Indonesian.Here’s to 35 years of heartfelt hospitality and to the journeys still waiting to be written.

Padma Run Bali 2025: A Celebration of Wellness and Community Impact

The annual Padma Run Bali 2025, organised by Padma Hotels, was successfully held, bringing together fitness enthusiasts and community supporters in a spirited event that combined wellness with purpose. This year’s event raised an impressive total of IDR 113,484,780, which has been donated to support education and environmental sustainability in several villages across Payangan, Gianyar.On Wednesday, 30 July 2025, a donation ceremony took place at Sekolah Dasar (Elementary School) Negeri 2 Puhu, attended by community leaders, school representatives, and the Padma Hotels team. The event concluded with a school tour, highlighting facilities scheduled for renovation.The funds raised through Padma Run Bali 2025 were allocated to renovating school facilities and providing two operational laptops to Sekolah Dasar (Elementary School) Negeri 2 Puhu. Additionally, 40 sets of organic and inorganic waste bins were distributed to multiple banjar (local community organizations in Bali) within Payangan, reinforcing efforts toward better waste management and environmental care.“The donation provided by Padma Hotels to Sekolah Dasar Negeri 2 Puhu is not just a one-time contribution. In previous years, we have also received numerous forms of assistance from Padma Hotels, including facilities, infrastructure, goods, and services to support education in our school. We sincerely thank Padma Hotels for always caring, and we hope that all the donations will be put to the best possible use,” said I Wayan Suryaarta, Headmaster of Sekolah Dasar (Elementary School) Negeri 2 Puhu.“Today we are very proud to hand over this donation, which reflects our ongoing commitment to support social and humanitarian programs that have a direct impact on the community,” said Kristanti Tannady, Hotel Manager of Padma Resort Legian and representative of Padma Hotels. She added, “We believe that the hospitality industry is not only about delivering service but also about contributing and taking small steps that can lead to significant changes for our environment.”This achievement underscores Padma Hotels strong commitment to giving back to the community. Through initiatives like Padma Run Bali, the organization continues to champion education, sustainability, and community well-being across Bali.
