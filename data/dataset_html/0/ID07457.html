Welcome

As business and policy collide on the global stage, and technology increasingly connects the world, leaders across functions face a fresh set of challenges. Our CNBC Councils answer the call with specialized, invitation-only collectives that bring together C-suite executives from key sectors to exchange ideas with peers, CNBC journalists, and global newsmakers. 

Benefits

NETWORKING

Exclusive access to a curated network of industry leaders, policymakers, and CNBC's editorial team, fostering strategic connections that drive real business impact.

Experiences

Members-only experiences including off-the-record dinners, digital events with of-the-moment guests, and summits featuring thought leaders

News

Access expert analysis and strategic perspectives from CNBC’s global newsroom on the trends and developments shaping industry, delivered through members-only newsletters, digital portals, and curated online resources.

Councils

CEO Council

This community harnesses the power of corporate leaders from across the globe to brainstorm, problem-solve, idea-share, and learn, all in an effort to create positive change and reshape the future of business worldwide. 

Learn More

CFO Council

The ever-expanding role of the Chief Financial Officer inspired the creation of this unique community. CNBC CFO Council members enjoy a roster of year-round events, including the in-demand Fed Matters series, hosted by Steve Liesman.

Learn More

Technology Executive Council (TEC)

CNBC TEC members gather regularly to discuss high-priority concerns surrounding breakthrough technologies that solve problems and power growth while addressing the challenges many of these innovations present. 

Learn More

Workforce Executive Council (WEC)

CNBC’s WEC community is made up of human resource, talent, and diversity professionals—the guardians of culture who oversee the people who are the power behind companies from every sector.

Learn More
CNBC Councils exclusive community of cross functional subject matter experts of experienced and former c-suite executives of accredited organizations. Helping current and former executives stay informed of timely trends by connecting to vetted executives of leading organizations.

    Naveen Agarwal

    Former Prudential Financial, Chief Customer, Marketing and Market Development Officer

    Naveen Agarwal advises boards and companies in financial services and healthcare on innovation, digital transformation, AI, and marketing. Naveen brings deep P/L operating experience and his career spans 30+ years in CEO and other C level operating roles at public companies and startups. He is passionate about using AI and technology to create new business models, serve customers better, and increase productivity.

    Prior to NavDots, Naveen was Chief Customer Officer at Prudential Financial (NYSE: PRU) and led global digital technology, customer experience, data sciences/AI, and marketing. He led company’s digital transformation efforts, setup of companies Corporate Venture Fund, and tech driven M&A efforts.

    Naveen’s entrepreneurial background includes 20+ years in Silicon Valley. He was the CEO and Board Director at Pricelock, an online marketplace for large energy buyers and sellers; served as President of E*TRADE Capital Management and ran E*TRADE’s retirement, mutual funds, investments, corporate stock plans and benefits, and wealth management businesses. He also held executive roles at startups and public companies including Renaissance Software, SunGard and TCS. He brings expertise in SaaS, stochastic and quantitative pricing and risk techniques, asset and wealth management, brokerage, credit, insurance, and retail and institutional financial services.

    Naveen frequently speaks on wide-ranging topics and is well respected in AI, growth, and transformation advisory. He serves on the board of Ascension, one of the nation’s leading health systems with 125+ hospitals, serving communities in 19+ states. He is a Senior Advisor at Boston Consulting Group; advises SY Partners, world’s leading company on corporate culture; is on CNBC Councils Advisory Board & Tech Executive Council; advises various VC/PE funds on their investments and deal diligence; is on board of 2021.ai, a startup out of Copenhagen, Denmark; is a Senior Advisor to AI4All; is a Fellow at Creative Destruction Lab at University of Wisconsin; and has served as an independent board member and chair of audit committee for Leo Holdings (NYSE: LHC).

    Naveen holds an MBA from Haas School of Business at the University of California, Berkeley and a bachelor’s degree in computer science and engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology, Varanasi, India.

    Jim Dunn

    Former Advocate Health, EVP, Chief People and Culture Officer

    Jim D. Dunn, PhD, DHA, DAST, FACHE, is executive vice president and chief people and culture officer for Advocate  Health, one of the largest non-profit and leading academic health systems in the United States. As a member of the executive leadership team, Dunn leads teams that focus on the engagement of Atrium Health teammates – from recruitment through retirement – including workforce relations, diversity and inclusion, language services, compensation, benefits, learning and organizational development, teammate health, LiveWELL, recognition, events and HR communications, social impact and health equity.

    With more than 30 years of experience, Dunn joined Atrium Health in 2018. Previously, he served as a research scientist with the Georgia Tech Research Institute and the leader of global HR operations for former President Jimmy Carter at the Carter Presidential Center in Atlanta. Additional leadership positions held throughout his career include serving as adjunct and distinguished faculty for multiple universities including the Harvard School of Public Health, MIT Sloan School of Management, Morehouse School of Medicine, Emory University, University of Chicago and the University of Texas at Dallas.

    Dunn is a state registered professional mediator, specializing in workplace conflict resolution strategies and a fellow (FACHE) and faculty member of the American College of Healthcare Executives. He was also selected by the US Congress to serve on the Congressional Award Foundation’s National Board of Directors and serves as a member and contributing writer for CNBC and Forbes Workforce and HR Councils. Jim also currently serves as Board Chair, Harvey Gantt African-American Center for the Arts and Trustee and Vice-Chair for Central Piedmont Community College, both in Charlotte, North Carolina.

    Among his many achievements, Dunn has received the Outstanding Business Leader Award from the Dallas Business Journal, the South Dallas Outstanding Leader Award, and the Global Strategic HR and OD Award from the Organizational Development Institute for his work on informal cultures. He also was named among the 2018 and 2020 Most Influential African Americans in Corporate America by Savoy Magazine. Dunn was also selected as a finalist for 2021 CHRO of the Year by HRO Today Association.

    Dunn holds Bachelor of Science degrees in chemistry and macro-environmental science from Howard University, a master’s degree in business administration from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), and a master’s degree in public health from Emory University. Additionally, he holds doctoral degrees in education, organizational development and healthcare administration from Emory University, Benedictine University and the Medical University of South Carolina, respectively.

    InaMarie Johnson

    Former Zendesk, Chief People & Diversity Officer

    Ms. Johnson is a seasoned human resource professional with experience serving at multiple companies during periods of strategic transformation. She is an inspirational leader entrusted with enabling the effectiveness and wellbeing of employees across organizations. She has guided companies and leadership teams through massive turnarounds and inflection points, recognizing opportunities to champion initiatives that go beyond awareness and transform the mindsets and behaviors that shape a culture.

    As part of her tenured career, Ms. Johnson served as Zendesk’s Chief People and Diversity Officer from 2018 to 2022, leading the company’s vision for delivering a great employee experience. She spearheaded Zendesk’s 2020 pivot to a digital-first culture where all employees can thrive regardless of location and created a leader-led diversity model for its employees, customers, and communities worldwide, gaining top-level support and establishing programs in pursuit of true social equity. Before Zendesk, from July 2015 to June 2018, Ms. Johnson was Senior Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer for Plantronics, an audio communications company, where she led a cultural transformation and successful CEO transition to jumpstart growth. Previously, from September 2011 to June 2015, she held the role of Chief Human Resources Officer at UTi Worldwide Inc., a supply chain management company. Before that, Ms. Johnson held senior leadership roles at Honeywell International Inc., an international technology and manufacturing company for 10 years, including Vice President, Human Resources Functional Transformation and Vice President, Human Resources, Communication, and Six Sigma.

    Ms. Johnson holds a B.A. in Social Sciences, with an emphasis in Human Resource Management, from the University of California, Berkeley, and an M.A. in Organizational Development and Management from John F. Kennedy University in California.

Most recently, she was elected as a member of Chief, a private network built to drive more women into positions of power and keep them there. Chief is the only organization specifically designed for senior women leaders to strengthen their leadership journey, cross-pollinate ideas across industries, and effect change from the top down.

    Mario Marte

    Former Chewy, Chief Financial Officer

    Mario Marte is the former Chief Financial Officer of Chewy, Inc. (NYSE: “CHWY”), the world’s largest online retailer of pet products and services. 

    Committees 

    • BBY. Chair – Audit Committee 
    • BBY. Member – Nominating, Corporate Governance and Public Policy Committee 
    • FIGS. Member – Audit Committee 

    Key Qualifications & Experience: 

    • Finance Expertise – As the former Chief Financial Officer of Chewy, Inc., Mr. Marte brings financial depth and breadth to a public company Board. In his current role, Mr. Marte led the successful initial public offering of Chewy in June 2019 and leads all finance, accounting, tax, capital markets, and investor relations functions for the company. Prior to becoming CFO, he led financial planning and analysis and treasury in three successful private fundraisings and the sale of Chewy to BC Partners in 2017. He has over two decades of experience in finance at American Airlines, Hilton Worldwide and Chewy. 
    • Growth / E-commerce / Transformation Experience – Mr. Marte has experience in growth and transformation, having established the financial planning, operations finance and treasury functions at Chewy Inc. He also worked closely with the leadership team to reengineer the company’s financial strategy and long-term growth plan in the first six months after joining Chewy. These steps led the company to grow from $250 million in revenue to more than $11 billion in eight years while rapidly scaling to profitability and the lead position in e-commerce for the pet category. 
    • Governance and Risk Management – In his role as Chief Financial Officer of Chewy and as a member of both the Audit and Nominating, Corporate Governance, and Public Policy committees at Best Buy, Mr. Marte has had extensive experience in corporate governance and risk management at all levels of the company from internal controls and insurance programs, to risk mitigation and recovery, and cybersecurity. In partnership with Chewy’s Chief Technology Officer, Mr. Marte helped establish the CISO organization and cybersecurity program at Chewy, building the program as the company prepared for the public markets.  
    • Global Expertise – Mr. Marte has held finance and functional roles at large, global and capital- intensive companies in travel and hospitality. He has worked internationally, based in Spain and the United Kingdom, while leading teams across several countries and regions including Asia Pacific, Latin America, North America and Europe. He has operated in a variety of cultures, regulatory and currency regimes, and has implemented processes and systems that accelerate time and improve visibility into business performance across business lines and on a global basis. 

    An ardent supporter of the performing and visual arts, Mario has served on the Boards of The Dallas Opera (as Trustee) and Creative Arts Center of Dallas where he was Board Vice President. Mario is also an avid reader, and a fitness enthusiast who has completed several marathons and half-marathons.

    Arun Sundararajan

    NYU, Harold Price Professor of Entrepreneurship and Technology

    Arun Sundararajan is the Harold Price Professor of Entrepreneurship and Professor of Technology, Operations and Statistics at New York University’s (NYU) Stern School of Business. An internationally recognized expert on artificial intelligence governance and digital strategy, his best-selling and award-winning book, “The Sharing Economy,” published by the MIT Press, has been translated into Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Portuguese and Vietnamese.

    Arun’s research studies how digital technologies transform business, government and civil society, with a recent focus on the regulation of artificial intelligence, generative AI and platform-driven change, digital strategy, market power, and the digital future of work. He has published numerous scientific papers in peer-reviewed academic journals and conferences, and op-eds in outlets that include The New York Times, The Financial Times, CNBC, TIME, The Guardian, Wired, Bloomberg View, Le Monde, El Pais, the Economic Times and the Japan Times.  His scholarship has been recognized by nine Best Paper awards, two Google Faculty awards, an Axiom Best Business Books Award, and a Thinkers50 Award. He has given hundreds of keynote and plenary talks at forums that include the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting in Davos, the World Government Summit, the Wuzhen Summit, the Global Partnership on AI Summit, and SXSW Interactive. He has testified and provided expert input to the United States Congress, the European Parliament, the United Nations, and numerous regulators globally. He is a widely sought-after commentator by top media platforms.

    Arun is a member of the World Economic Forum’s AI Governance Alliance, a member of the Carnegie Council’s AI and Equality advisory board, and serves as a board member and advisor to numerous organizations, ranging from startups to Fortune 100s.. He teaches in executive education programs in the United States, Europe and Asia, focusing primarily on AI, platforms and governance. He is a frequent angel investor. He has degrees in electrical engineering, operations research and economics from IIT Madras and the University of Rochester. Find him at https://digitalarun.io/ and  https://linkedin.com/in/digitalarun
