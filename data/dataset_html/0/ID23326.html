Tier 16 Secrets of Ulduar & New Content - 27/03/2026

⚔️ Tier 16 Secrets of Ulduar has been released ⚔️

Server Update & Changelog
- New Raid: Secrets of Ulduar is now live!
- World Bosses: 4 new World Bosses and their associated quests have been added.
- Note: Two of these bosses are located in PvP zones. You will be automatically teleported to a safe spot upon death.
- New Area: Explore the Ruins of Alterac (Note: PvP is enabled in this zone).
- 4 New Solo Quests have been added specifically for this area (Ruins of Alterac).
- A dedicated Solo Daily Quest NPC has been added. Daily Quest NPCs are now split into three categories: World Boss, Raid, and Solo.

Currency & Reward Adjustments
- All daily raid quests now reward T16.5 Medallions.
- Transmog quests (Cata) now also reward T16.5 Medallions.
- Daily quest rewards have been updated from T15.5 to Ironsoul Medallions + T16.5.
- New Exchange: Added a new daily quest: Icecrown Medallion Exchange (Convert to Ironsoul).
- PvP & Visual Tokens: Reward amounts from old dailies have been reduced; these rewards have been redistributed to the newer quests.

Progression & Balancing
- Tier 15 Changes: Quest requirements have been reduced by 25%. (Stats were also slightly nerfed last week).
- Tier 14.5 & 15.5: Quest givers have been removed as the required Heroic Medallions are no longer obtainable.
- Donor & VIP Gear: Following our new tier release standard, all Donor and VIP gear has received a stat boost.

PvP Updates
- Alterac Valley: New PvP quests added "Battle for Frostwolf" and "Battle for Stormpike".
- Season Reset: Honor and Arena Points have been reset.

Quality of Life & Misc
- Hearthstone: Now permanently spawns a Mini Teleporter upon use (right-click to despawn).
- Resurrection Sickness has been disabled.

Good luck and have fun!

Introduction of Tier 16 - 18/03/2026

We are pleased to announce the arrival of our newest Tier 16 instance!
Secrets of Ulduar offers you the chance to obtain the newest Tier sets on Unlimited-WoW.

Here is some additional information:

- Secrets of Ulduar (Tier 16) is a solo raid featuring 5 bosses. To ensure a smooth experience in this massive zone, the layout has been scaled down.
- New World Bosses: Four new bosses have arrived: Ignis the Furnace Master, Auriaya, General Vezax, and Algalon the Observer. You can find two in PvP zones and two in PvE zones.
- Six new visuals are being added, including four unique drops one from each of the new World Bosses.
- Tier 16.5 Heroic quests will be available at launch. Additionally, Tier 15.5 Heroic quests will now require fewer medallions than regular Tier 15 to help you progress toward Tier 16.
- Honor and Arena Points will be reset upon the release of the raid. You must complete the Tier 16 attunement quest to access the PvP Vendors.
- Daily and PvP quest rewards will be updated to Ironsoul Medallions, used for Tier 16.5 Heroic gear.
- We are adding new daily quests, including soloable objectives for Heroic Ironsoul Medallions.
- Donor & VIP Gear: As with every new tier release, all Donor and VIP gear will receive a power boost.

Get Started: The attunement quest for Secrets of Ulduar is now available at the Key Quest NPC located at the Mall.

⚔️ Tier 16 will be released on Friday, March 27, 2026. ⚔️

Introduction of PvP Seasons - 25/05/2025

We are pleased to introduce a new competitive feature: PvP Seasons. This system is designed to provide ongoing, structured PvP competition with monthly rewards to recognize the top-performing players.

Each PvP Season will run for one month.

 

At the conclusion of each season, the top three players on the PvP ladder will be awarded Donation Points (DP) as follows:

- 1st Place: 30 DP
- 2nd Place: 20 DP
- 3rd Place: 10 DP

The ladder will reset at the end of each season, with a new season commencing one day later, ensuring continuous competition. The PvP leaderboard will be accessible via the website sidebox and the game launcher, providing real-time visibility of rankings.

Total kills per character will continue to be displayed in the PvP statistics section.

Rewards will be automatically distributed to qualifying players at the end of each season.
Please note that the reward amounts may be adjusted in future seasons to ensure balanced and engaging competition.

The first PvP Season will start this afternoon!

We believe that PvP Seasons will enhance the competitive experience by fostering a dynamic and rewarding environment for all players.
