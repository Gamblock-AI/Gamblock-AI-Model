FRii Bali Echo Beach

Our Hotel is located in the heart of Canggu, within 5 minutes walking away to the one of the best Surfing Spot in Bali (Echo Beach & Batu Bolong Beach), and within 2 minutes drive away. FRii Hotel is a perfect home to stay, our unique property can make you feel homie. Our location makes it easy for surfers and the persons who love beach vibes. 

Bunch of local actraction you can experience when you stay at FRii, we are close to La Brisa, Tanah Lot Temple, Atlas Beach Club, and approximately 60 minutes drive away from Ngurah Rai International Airport. 

Breathtaking panorama of the Indian Ocean and all-embracing Echo Beach create a romantic nuance for every visitor. For the surfers, FRiidom is the time to ride the waves. Here, surfing has a long legacy, some surfers called it home. A home where you can be yourself, live, and share your passion. A home where you can meet and make friends, where it's impossible to feel lonely. We are to complete those dreams, together we can create and connect stories.

FRii believes life is a journey, and this is why FRii appreciates every moment of it. FRii is a unique stay, it's about people's journey and how they venture into life to collect, respect, and share these stories. at FRii, we create, share, and collect stories!.

Echo Beach Canggu

Echo Beach also call ‘Pantai Batu Mejan’ is one of Bali’s most popular surf spots. Sharing the serene rice field-landscaped region of Canggu with other wave-riding spots such as Berawa, Seseh and Pererenan, Echo Beach has become most popular due to its advanced reef breaks, 180-degree seascape and sunsets, as well as delicious seafood cafes set on a high vantage point.
