Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to comply with the GDPR?

Any website owned by an entity in the European Union, or is marketed toward citizens of the EU, should comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The GDPR is designed to protect the privacy rights of individuals in the EU. This directive mandates how personal data is collected, used, disseminated, and how users should be notified about their privacy rights. It also gives users the right to opt-out of having their cookies tracked.

Failure to comply with the GDPR can result in steep penalties. Your first infraction will likely result in a written warning, but the next incident could net you a fine up to €20 million or 4% of revenues, whichever amount is greater.

Is my website CCPA compliant?

As of January 1st, 2020, businesses that utilize data on California residents should be compliant with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

If your organization is buying, selling, renting, or disseminating data from California residents, you need to ensure you are compliant. Failure to follow the rules could result in steep penalties for your organization.

Read more

Is my business LGPD ready?

Brasil has it's own privacy law, Brazil’s Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (or LGPD).

Businesses that process the personal data of Brazilians will need to shift staffing priorities or hire new staff to meet the new law’s regulations.

Read more

What if I ignore GDPR and CCPA laws?

On your first offense, you will most likely receive a written warning. After that, you will likely be assessed a fine. GDPR fines can go as high as 20 million Euros or 4% of annual revenue, whichever is higher. CCPA fines are assessed at $7,500 per incident, yet there is not a maximum amount for how much an organization may be fined.
