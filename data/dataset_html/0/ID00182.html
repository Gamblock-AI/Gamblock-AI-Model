Advocate for your best future

AIA advocacy efforts strive to make us stronger and more impactful. We advocate at the international, federal, state, and local levels for equity across a wide breadth of policies such as: tax fairness, affordable housing production, climate action, democracy in design, and more. We champion meaningful policies that enable architects to design a better tomorrow by acting on the issues that matter most to our industry. Join us, adding your voice to ours, advocating together for your best future.
