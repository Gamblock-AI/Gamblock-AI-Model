Responsible Gambling

At Housebets it is strictly forbidden to play for anyone who is under the age of 18 years old.

Online gambling is fun, and purely for entertainment purposes only. We at Housebets have our players' best interests at heart. We support responsible gambling.

As online gambling has grown over the last decade, online gambling sites for casinos, sports, lottery and other games have become easily accessible at the touch of a button. This makes it harder for players to set boundaries.

Casino and sports betting is designed to offer maximized entertainment for pure enjoyment. However, we wish for our gamblers to play within their means.

Always Keep In Mind:

  • Gambling is intended to be fun and entertaining not as a means of income.
  • Gambling is not a way to resolve any financial difficulties.
  • Decide on a gambling budget and stick to it. Keep track of your spending and only play with the amount initially decided and the amount you can afford to lose.
  • Do not chase after losses or play with money allocated to other areas of your budget or life.
  • Check that you are only playing in your free time allocated to hobbies. Gambling is not meant to keep you from your routine daily activities. Monitor the time you spend gambling.

Are You A Compulsive Gambler

According to Gamblers Anonymous, if you answer 7 or more of these with a yes, you should seek help for your gambling addiction

  1. Did you lose track of time when gambling?
  2. Has gambling affected your home life negatively?
  3. Has your reputation been damaged from gambling?
  4. Have you ever felt remorse after gambling?
  5. Do you ever gamble to solve financial difficulties?
  6. Has gambling caused a decrease in your ambition or efficiency?
  7. After a win, do you have an urge to return and win more?
  8. Do you often gamble until your last funds are gone?
  9. Do you ever borrow money to finance your gambling?
  10. Have you ever sold anything to finance gambling?
  11. Are you reluctant to use "gambling money" for normal expenditures?
  12. Does gambling make you careless with the welfare of yourself and/or your family?
  13. Do you ever gamble longer than you had originally planned?
  14. Have you ever gambled to escape your worries?

Protect Yourself And Gamble Responsibly

If you cannot gamble responsibly, the only option is to not gamble at all. This way, you will avoid putting yourself and your loved ones at risk. Many people have ruined their lives by not listening and acting on signs of their own irresponsible gambling.

All casinos and games have a built-in RTP or return to player and a set house edge which has a favorable long-term outcome for the casino.
