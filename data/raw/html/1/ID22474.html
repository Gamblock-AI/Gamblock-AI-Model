    14 Year Anniversary Events


    Event Area & Minigames

    In celebration of the 14-year anniversary, there is an event area at Verona Beach that will host a total of six minigames and remain active until the end of February.

    You can claim a prize once per paycheck by completing / winning one of the minigames.

    You will also find some festive decorations throughout Los Santos!






    Minigame #1: Anniversary Maze

    A minigame that involves figuring your way out of a maze with several moving parts that you'll need to get past. Once you make it to the end, you'll be able to claim a prize.





    Minigame #2: Anniversary Jungle DM

    A deathmatch minigame where there can only be one winner! Every five minutes, a round can be started if there are enough participants.





    Minigame #3: Shooting Range Minigame

    The Shooting Range Minigame is a competitive minigame that can be played with up to 8 players. The objective is to be the first to shoot the target. The person that shoots the most targets, and thus has the highest score, will win the game and get a prize! New rounds will start when there are 8 participants or if 2+ participants have been waiting for 5 minutes.





    Minigame #4: Anniversary Hopscotch Minigame

    Based on a game from the Squid Game series, contestants must jump across tiles and make it to the other side to claim their prize. However, some of the tiles are not solid, and falling down eliminates you from the minigame.





    Minigame #5: Anniversary Climbing Minigame

    A brand new climbing minigame, where the objective is to climb your way to the top to claim a prize. But be careful, there are several moving parts that complicate the road to the top, and if you fall down, then you will need to start over!





    Minigame #6: Anniversary Grove vs Ballas Minigame

    Step into Grove Street and fight the classic battle between Grove and Ballas in this team deathmatch minigame. Eliminate the enemy team, and your entire team will win a prize!






    Double XP Weekends

    There will be a Double XP Weekend every weekend (Friday to Sunday) in the month of January.

    This basically means that you will get 2 respect points instead of the regular 1 respect point per paycheck and that you will be able to level up all jobs twice as fast (2 skill points). We hope you will take this opportunity to invite over some friends so that they can get a head start in the game, while of course also making use of the opportunity yourself.





    Other Events & Giveaways

    There will also be several admin hosted events throughout the next couple of weeks, where many great prizes can be won.

    Be sure to also check out our social media, as many prizes will be given away there too!





    Christmas Event Winners

    The winners of the Santa's Helper Event will be announced later this month. Stay tuned to find out if you've won any prizes!
