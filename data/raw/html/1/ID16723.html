Big vs Small Bet — What’s the Difference?

4D betting allows players to choose between BIG and SMALL bets. Here’s what each means:

1. BIG Bet

A BIG bet gives you more chances to win because it covers all prize groups:

  • 1st Prize
  • 2nd Prize
  • 3rd Prize
  • Special Prizes
  • Consolation Prizes

Because there are more chances to win, BIG payouts are lower than SMALL payouts for the same bet amount. BIG bets are good if you want a higher chance of getting something back.

2. SMALL Bet

SMALL bets only qualify for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd prizes — but the payout is much higher.

SMALL bets are ideal for players who are confident in their number and want to maximise their potential reward.
