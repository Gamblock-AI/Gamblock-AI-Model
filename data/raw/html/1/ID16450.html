What Are 777 Slots?

The lucky number 7 can be traced back to the late 19th century, when Charles Fey invented the first one-armed bandit slot machine called Liberty Bell. 777 slots are reminiscent of old-school fruit machines, and some of them are more or less exact online replicas of physical slot machines. 777 games remain popular as an alternative to more complex and modern video slots, as they combine simplistic charm with a strong nostalgic appeal.

Features & Symbols In 777 Games

Part of the charm with free slots 777 is the simple fruit machine symbols, and you typically get a blend of fruit symbols, bar symbols, and, of course, the lucky number 7 symbol. Landing a 3 or 5 of a kind 7s win usually equates to a larger payout, and the lucky number 7 has come to represent good fortune and abundance ever since the first fruit slot machine was invented. Let’s now take a closer look at the symbols and features you can expect when you play free slots 777 no download.

Symbols

You will typically find fruit symbols such as cherries, lemons, and oranges in many slots, and such symbols appeal to the gatherer in us. The bell symbol signifies good fortune with its characteristic chime, and wins used to be announced by the loud ringing of a bell back when games were first invented. The 777 casino slot symbol represents harmony and luck in many cultures, and it’s usually the top-tier symbol in this slots.

Features

Classic 777 slots come with 3 reels and 1 to 5 paylines, while offering simple gameplay with few if any features. You get wild symbols that help create wins, but you can also play 5-reel games. These are games that have the classic look and appeal, but video 777 slots can come with modern features such as bonus rounds, bonus games, and jackpots. You get the best from both worlds in a sense, and the max win is often higher as well.
