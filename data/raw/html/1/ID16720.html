HOW TO PLAY 4D SINGAPORE POOLS

To play 4D, you have to choose a combination of 4 numbers from 0 to 9 or choose QuickPick and the Lottery processing system randomly chooses set of numbers for you. The minimum cost is $1, inclusive of GST. 23 sets of winning 4D numbers across five prize categories are drawn each draw. If you have placed bets on any of the 23 winning 4D numbers, you win a prize.

4D Draws take place every Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday at 6:30PM

4D BET TYPES

QUICK PICK

If you do not have any numbers in mind, you may consider the Quick Pick option. The system will generate a random set of numbers for you.

BIG BET

If you place a Big bet, you win a prize if your 4D number appears in any of the five prize categories.

SMALL BET

If you place a Small bet, you win a prize only if your 4D number appears in the top three prize categories. You can choose to place a Big or Small bet, or both, among these bet types:

ORDINARY ENTRY

Select four digits in a specific order. You win a prize if your combination matches any of the winning numbers in the same order.

4D ROLL

Select three digits of your choice and one rolling digit in a specific order. The rolling digit, designated by 'R', represents any number from 0 to 9.

SYSTEM ENTRY, IBET

Select four digits and place bets on all its possible combinations. For System Entry, each 4D number combination costs a minimum of $1. For iBet, all 4D number combinations cost a minimum of $1 in total. Your prize money is proportionately divided by the number of combinations.
