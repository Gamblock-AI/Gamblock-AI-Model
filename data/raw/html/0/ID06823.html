FAQs

What are your costs?
Every client is different and their business needs are also different. Because of various business models and website requirements, pricing typically ranges from $1000 to $10,000.
How long does it take to develop or redesign a website?
We know website development time can cost you business, so we do our best to provide quality results as quickly as possible. The timeline for website design and development can take from a few weeks to several months.
What size companies do you typically work with?

We believe in growing together. We have worked and developed many small to large businesses, startups to non-profits, personal to corporate portfolio websites. We have developed websites for clients in Bangladesh, United States, United Kingdom, Australia, United Arab Emirates, Finland, Spain, and elsewhere. We encourage prospective clients to check our portfolio.

What’s a typical client-and-agency relationship like?

The core of a typical web development team consists of a project manager, creative director, web designer, web developer, copywriter, digital marketer, and quality assurance specialist. The size of our team can scale up with your needs and we partner with illustration and motion graphic experts as needed.

Clients can expect daily communication with their team via email, Slack, or Trello and “all-hands-on-deck” meetings once a week via video conferencing. We try to make ourselves as available as possible.

Can you work with my existing Marketing team?

Absolutely. It’s common for our web development company to collaborate with internal or external digital marketing teams.

Throughout the course of a project, we often help these teams set a better structural foundation for SEO, set up smarter segmentation, and improve goal and event-tracking.

Post-launch, we can collaborate with your marketing team to produce new landing pages, website sections, and lead magnets, or we can assist with aspects of the marketing work ourselves.

How do I decide whether to build a website or improve my current one?

Building a new website is best for organizations undergoing a major rebranding, looking to migrate from their content management system to WordPress or Shopify, or have larger, structural issues with their current web design.

Improving a website is best for organizations that already have a website on Shopify or WordPress and are looking to increase conversions, sales, and improve overall customer experience.

When was your agency founded?

Our digital agency was founded in 2016. We have over 7 years of experience building and transforming brands’ online presence.
