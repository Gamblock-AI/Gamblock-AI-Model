SoundCloud Pro comes in two tiers: SoundCloud Pro and SoundCloud Pro Unlimited. The Pro service costs users $7 per month, while the Pro Unlimited service costs $15 per month.

  1. Go to www.soundcloud.com and click the orange "Sign-up" button on the top right corner of your screen.
  2. Choose your favorite kinds of music and audio.

Can you listen to music offline on SoundCloud?

Open the playlist and click the save offline button next to the Likes button to make individual playlists available for offline listening. You'll see tracks are left to save for offline listening in the orange progress bar on the bottom of your screen on iOS, or in the pull-down menu on Android.
