    Adapundi is chosen by millions of users, a fast, easy, and safe loan solution licensed by OJK. Apply anytime and anywhere - no collateral, low interest! Adapundi stands apart from other loan applications. Adapundi is dedicated to making your life easier, committed to maintaining transparency with fast, secure, and easy services. With Adapundi, every step you take toward your goals becomes brighter and happier. Adapundi, Bring every dream to life with heart! ◉ Why Choose Adapundi？ Adapundi - A safe loan platform trusted by nearly 30 million users, with 15 million+ disbursements made, and licensed and supervised for 8 years. It offers unsecured online loans with an easy application process, high limits of up to Rp100,000,000 and low interest rates with flexible monthly installments. Adapundi is an online loan credit platform that is licensed and supervised by the Financial Services Authority/OJK (KEP-48/D.05/2021) and is certified with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for security. Adapundi guarantees the protection of user information by not providing any information without consent to third parties. Adapundi provides complete financial solutions — not only for daily needs such as school fees, emergencies, or medical expenses, but also to support your dream of starting or growing a business. The loan application process is simple, and funds can be disbursed within minutes. Special for Loyal Customers, enjoy exclusive offers with higher loan limits and longer repayment terms. Let’s borrow smart, safely, and reliably — apply now! ◉ What Are the Features of Adapundi's Loan Product? - High Loan Limit: Up to Rp100,000,000 - Loan Tenor: 91–360 days, with monthly installments - Interest Rate: 10.8%–18% per year - Unsecured: No collateral required - Fast Registration Process: Apply with your ID (KTP), and get approved quickly - Guaranteed Privacy and Security: Data is encrypted under ISO 27001:2022 standards and processed in accordance with OJK regulations and the Personal Data Protection Law For a loan of Rp50,000,000 with a tenor of 360 days and 12 installments, the fees charged are as follows: Monthly Interest Rate: 10.8% / 12 = 0.9% Monthly Interest: IDR 50,000,000 × 0.9% = IDR 450,000 Total Interest: IDR 50,000,000 × 10.8% = IDR 5,400,000 Total Repayment: IDR 50,000,000 + IDR 5,400,000 = IDR 55,400,000 Monthly Installment: IDR 55,400,000 / 12 = IDR 4,616,667 ◉ How to Get a Fast Loan from Adapundi? 1. Download the Adapundi App 2. Fill in your data accurately and completely 3. Wait for data verification 4. Wait for your application to be approved 5. Request your loan limit 6. Funds will be disbursed to your bank account ◉ How to Repay the Loan? We will send you an SMS reminder one day before and on the due date to help you avoid penalties. You can repay your loan via ATM, Mobile Banking, Internet Banking (BCA, BNI, BRI, Mandiri, Permata, Maybank, or Danamon), E-wallet (OVO or LinkAja), and Alfamart. We also support early online loan repayment!
