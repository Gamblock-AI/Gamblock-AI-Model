1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide effective advertising services to publishers, advertisers, and users. Information collected includes:            Device and Browser Information: Such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and location data.            Ad Interaction Data: Information on how users interact with ads (e.g., clicks, impressions).            Publisher and Advertiser Data: Information provided directly by our partners to optimize ad delivery and performance.            Cookie and Tracking Technologies: We and our partners use cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to gather and store information for ad targeting and personalization.        

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:            Deliver and Improve Ads: Enhance ad targeting and personalization to improve relevance for users.            Optimize Performance: Analyze and improve ad performance across publishers, advertisers, and SSP partners.            Fraud Prevention and Security: Detect and prevent fraudulent activity and ensure the security of our platform.            Compliance and Legal Obligations: Fulfill regulatory and legal obligations as required.        

3. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following ways:            With Publishers and Advertisers: To provide insights into ad performance, audience engagement, and optimization.            With Other SSP Partners: When collaborating with other SSPs, we may share information to enhance cross-platform ad delivery and targeting.            Service Providers: We work with third-party providers who assist in delivering our services and comply with our data handling standards.            Legal and Regulatory Compliance: We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or respond to lawful requests.        

4. Cookie and Tracking Policy

The websites operated by Kuantyx, as well as those of its partners, suppliers, and clients, make use of cookies and similar technologies to gather information about how users interact with and navigate our digital platforms. By accepting cookies on Kuantyx websites, you consent to our use of this data to recognise you as a user, perform internal analytics, and carry out any data processing that you have authorised as a visitor, customer, or partner. This Privacy Notice should be read together with our Cookie Policy, which provides additional details on cookie use and management.For more specific information about how Kuantyx manages cookies, please refer to our [Cookie Policy].

As part of the advertising services provided by Kuantyx—which help optimise and distribute advertising across our partner media—certain non-identifiable data may be collected. While none of this information allows Kuantyx to personally identify you, it helps ensure that advertising is more relevant and effective.

Examples of data that may be gathered include:
Device identifiers – used to measure ad delivery and frequency.
Device characteristics – used for targeting and statistical reporting.
General location data (via IP address) – used to serve ads for products and services available in your country.
‍Precise location data (mobile apps only) – only if you’ve granted permission, used to show ads relevant to your area.
Ad-related interactions – data about how often an ad is displayed or clicked, helping us track campaign performance.
Contextual website events – such as page views or content categories, used to evaluate where and how ads are displayed for Brand Safety purposes.
‍Public IP address – shared with our advertising partners only when required for transactional purposes (e.g., bid or demand platforms, Brand Safety tools). In all other cases, Kuantyx uses an anonymised version, removing the final segment (for example, 192.168.10.100 becomes 192.168.10.xxx).

Since Kuantyx collaborates with other advertising technology companies and publishers, several kinds of cookies may be used throughout these processes.
