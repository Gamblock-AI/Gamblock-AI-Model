    How Device-to-Device File Sharing Works

    Traditional file sharing services require you to upload your data to a central server, which your recipient then downloads. This process is slow, limits your file size, and raises privacy concerns.

    TransferMyFile leverages network technologies to create a direct, encrypted tunnel between you and your partner. Files flow directly from your device to theirs, meaning unmatched speed, absolute privacy, and zero file size restrictions.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is TransferMyFile really secure?

    Yes. TransferMyFile uses network technology which inherently provides end-to-end encryption. Your files are transferred directly between your browser and your partner's browser without ever being stored on our servers.

    Is there a file size limit?

    No! Since the transfer is direct (device-to-device), there are no artificial file size limits. You can send massive files as long as both devices stay connected and have sufficient memory to process the chunks.

    What happens if my connection drops?

    We built a smart chunking system. If you lose connection during a transfer, just reconnect with your partner using their ID. The transfer will seamlessly resume from the last successful chunk instead of starting over.
