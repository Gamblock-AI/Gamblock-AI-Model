About

ASRI LIVING

ASRI Living is an integrated loyalty application that provides
various advantages and benefits at malls in the ASRI ecosystem,
including ASHTA District 8, PIK Avenue, Mall of Indonesia,
Grand Galaxy Park Bekasi, and Hublife Taman Anggrek.

With ASRI Living, you can easily access various benefits such as promos,
discounts and special gifts from various shops and services in related shopping centers. This application provides convenience and comfort
in shopping and enjoying facilities at various ASRI facilities.

What is ASRI Living?

ASRI Living is your all-in-one, fully digital loyalty app designed for convenience. Formerly known as SedayuOne, it connects all ASRI malls and projects, including: 

  • Mall of Indonesia,
  • ASHTA District 8 SCBD,
  • PIK Avenue,
  • Grand Galaxy Park Bekasi,
  • Hublife Taman Anggrek Residences ,

and more to come.

With ASRI Living, you can easily register, earn rewards, and redeem offers—all in one place. We’re committed to continuously enhancing the app to provide you with an even better experience.

How to be ASRI Living member?Aruna2024-10-02T21:44:18+07:00

How to be ASRI Living member?

Becoming an ASRI Living member is simple! Just follow these steps:

  1. Download the App: Find ASRI Living on the Play Store or App Store.
  2. Register: Enter your name, email, and phone number.

That’s it! You’re now part of the ASRI Living community and ready to enjoy exclusive rewards and offers.

What are the benefits of being an ASRI Living Member?Aruna2024-10-02T21:52:46+07:00

What are the benefits of being an ASRI Living Member?

As an ASRI Living member, you’ll enjoy a range of exciting benefits, including:

  • Exclusive Promotions: Stay updated on mall events and tenant offers.
  • Koins Collection: Earn Koins with every transaction.
  • Instant Rewards: Access E-vouchers, goodies, and shopping programs.
  • Birthday Treats: Celebrate your special day with exclusive surprises.
  • Special Rewards: Enjoy perks from participating tenants and business partners.
  • Parking Privileges: Benefit from exclusive parking options.

Join us and make the most of your shopping experience!
