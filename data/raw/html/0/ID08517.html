Welcome to our Whitehen Private Limited

INTRODUCTION

Established in 2016, Whitehen Private Limited is a leading name in the egg farming, poultry feed, and cattle feed industry. Headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, Whitehen is committed to delivering premium organic white eggs and high-quality cattle feed to meet the evolving needs of health-conscious consumers. The company operates with cutting-edge technology sourced from the Netherlands and Denmark, ensuring optimal conditions for poultry farming. Whitehen also takes pride in being the first farm in Uttar Pradesh to run entirely on solar energy, reinforcing its commitment to environmental sustainability

About Us

Whitehen Private Limited was founded by a family of doctors, with a vision to provide fresh, organic, and nutrient-rich eggs while maintaining the highest standards of hygiene and sustainability. Our state-of-the-art farm is designed with modern infrastructure to regulate environmental conditions, ensuring the health and well-being of our hens.

Our Services & Offerings

Organic White Eggs

Whitehen’s organic white eggs are known for their exceptional freshness and high nutritional value. Produced in a controlled, hygienic environment, these eggs meet the highest quality standards.

Cattle Feed

Our high-quality cattle feed is designed to enhance livestock health and productivity. We supply sustaina- ble and nutrient-rich feed to farmers, ensuring improved animal well-being and agricultural efficiency.

Brown Eggs (Upcoming)

In response to growing consumer demand, Whitehen plans to introduce organic brown eggs, produced under the same rigorous biosecurity measur- -es as our white eggs.
