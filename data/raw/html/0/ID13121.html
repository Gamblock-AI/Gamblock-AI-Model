JMMobi INC. Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2025

1. Introduction

JMMobi Inc. ("JMMobi", “we”, “us”,“our”) is committed to protecting consumer privacy and providing transparent information about the collection and use of personal data.

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) outlines how JMMobi collects, processes, and shares personal information when delivering mobile advertising automation technology and solutions to advertisers, buyers, app developers, and publishers of mobile apps and websites (collectively, “Clients”). Our practices are designed to ensure market quality and transparency, aligning with regulations on data privacy. This Policy not only applies to information (also referred to as "Personal Information”) gathered through JMMobi’s Ad Services, which may include advertising, measurement, data validation, fraud protection, and related services, but also applies to information gathered through JMMobi’s website and/or platform. The Policy also explains your choices regarding the processing of your information within these services.

For purposes of this Policy, “Personal Information” refers to data that identifies or can be reasonably linked directly or indirectly to you ("Clients", "End-Users" , "JMMobi's website and/or platform Viewers") or your household. This Policy does not cover anonymous, de-identified, or aggregated information as defined by applicable law.

2. Information Collection

JMMobi collects various types of information to optimize ad delivery and provide a personalized advertising experience for End-Users (also referred to as "users"). This section describes the types of information we collect through our Ad Services and how it may be used.

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect Personal Information when you voluntarily provide it to us, such as when you give us feedback or request information. This Personal Information will only be used to respond to your feedback or fulfill your request.

2.2 Device Identifiers

To deliver relevant ads, JMMobi may identify your device using unique identifiers, including:

  • Operating System-Supplied Identifiers: Such as Google’s Advertising Identifier (GAID), or randomly assigned identifiers.
  • Device-Related Identifiers: Including IP addresses, device type (e.g., smartphone), device model, operating system (e.g., Andorid), device language, carrier (e.g., Verizon), network connection type (e.g., WiFi or cellular), and hashed MAC addresses (MD5 format).
  • App-Related Identifiers: Such as the device’s Identifier for Vendor (IFV), an identifier unique to an app publisher.

    • These identifiers help us ensure ad relevance, perform frequency capping, and optimize ad delivery based on device and operating environment.

    2.3 Location Data

    To enhance ad relevance, JMMobi may use precise location data linked to your Device Identifier, such as GPS coordinates, which may be obtained from apps and websites where you have opted to share your location. Additionally, we may use data about device movement, proximity to other devices, and other available system data. Location data is used for:

  • Ad Targeting: To deliver ads based on relevant geographic areas or locations.
  • Performance Metrics: To analyze impressions, clicks, and other ad performance indicators to evaluate marketing impact.

    2.4 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

    When you access our services through mobile websites or apps, we automatically collect certain information using “cookies” and other tracking technologies, including:

  • Cookies: Small data files stored by your device’s web browser that help us recognize your device, track user interactions, and personalize the experience. We use both session cookies (which expire once you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain until deleted) for tracking and analytics.
  • Log Files: We collect data such as IP address, browser type and language, device type, timestamp, amount of data transferred, referring page, and the type of content delivered.

    • You may adjust your browser settings to block or remove cookies; however, doing so may impact your experience with our services. This information helps us optimize ad content, track engagement, and improve the quality of our services.

    2.5 Web Beacons and Pixels

    JMMobi may permit publishers and advertisers to use web beacons or “pixels” – tiny graphics similar to cookies – within advertisements to collect non-personal information or aggregate data. Pixels help us track ad engagement, such as views or clicks, and optimize the delivery of content.

    2.6 Information from Third Parties

    Our partners may collect additional information via cookies, similar trackers and share it with JMMobi. This data, including app or website usage details, is combined with the information we collect to enrich our insights and improve ad targeting. You may have the option to modify or disable data collection in your device’s settings.

    2.7 Data Shared with SSPs and Advertisers

    For effective ad delivery, we share relevant data with our SSP partners and advertisers, including:

  • Device Information: Such as device type, manufacturer, model, operating system, carrier, and network type.
  • Geolocation Data: Town-level data (or more precise data with opt-in) is used by SSPs for targeted advertising. Users can opt out of precise location tracking if desired.
  • User Interaction Data: Information on ad views and clicks to assess ad performance.
  • Authentication-Derived Identifiers: Hashed identifiers (e.g., email or phone number) created using methods like SHA-256 or MD5 are used by DSPs to personalize ads. Note that no authentication-derived identifiers are collected from EU-based users.

    • By understanding how users interact with ads and gathering relevant device and location data, JMMobi ensures optimized and relevant ad delivery for improved user experiences.

    3. How We Use Information

    JMMobi uses the information we collect to provide, optimize, and improve our advertising services, and to deliver relevant, personalized ads to users. The information we gather is essential for the functioning of our Ad Services, including ad targeting, reporting, and analytics. Below are the key ways we use the information collected:

    3.1 Providing and Improving Ad Services

    We use the information we collect to deliver and enhance our Ad Services, including the optimization of ad delivery and ad targeting. This includes:

  • Device Identification: Using automatically transmitted device characteristics and identifiers (e.g., Device ID, IP address, operating system) to uniquely identify and link different devices used by the End-User.
  • Precise Geolocation: With user consent, we may collect and use geolocation data to serve location-based ads and to track ad effectiveness.

    • This data helps ensure ads are displayed to the right audience at the right time, improving overall ad performance.

    3.2 Delivering Ads

    JMMobi works with publishers and app developers to offer advertising inventory, while advertisers and buyers (DSPs) bid on and place ads in that inventory. We use the data collected to:

  • Tailor Ad Content: Select and display relevant ads based on user behavior, interests, and device information.
  • Ad Placement: Ensure that ads are placed in the appropriate environments, whether on websites or within apps.

    • 3.3 Behavioral Targeting

    We may use the information collected to help our advertising partners predict future user behavior and interests. This allows advertisers to select highly relevant ads for the right users, based on data from multiple devices or specific devices. This process, known as behavioral targeting, helps improve the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

  • Opt-Out Option: Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to opt out of behavioral targeting. If you wish to opt-out, you can do so through the settings on your device or by directly contacting JMMobi. For third-party publishers or advertising clients using our Ad Services, you will need to opt-out directly with them according to their privacy policies.

    • 3.4 Reports and Analytics

    We collect data on ad performance, including impressions, clicks, and conversions, to:

    A). Track Ad Effectiveness: Measure the success of ad campaigns, including metrics like click-through rates and app installs.

    B). Generate Reports: Provide detailed performance reports to our clients, helping them assess the success of their campaigns and make data-driven decisions.

    3.5 Data Validation and Fraud Prevention

    To ensure the quality and integrity of the data used in our Ad Services, we use the information collected to:

    A). Validate Data: Match End-User information across devices and platforms to ensure the accuracy of data.

    B). Prevent Fraud: Identify and filter out invalid impressions, clicks, installs, or ad queries, reducing the risk of fraudulent activities in our Ad Services.

    3.6 Legal Compliance and Protection

    We may use the information we collect for several essential purposes, including:

    A). Compliance with Legal Requirements: To comply with legal obligations or respond to requests from government authorities, including those from the End-User’s country of residence.

    B). Protection of Rights and Safety: To protect our business, our affiliates, our clients, and user privacy and safety.

    C). Audits and Security: To conduct internal audits and assessments related to security, accounting, or corporate processes.

    D). Enforcement of Terms: To ensure compliance with corresponding JMMobi’s Terms of Use and JMMobi’s Policies, preventing misuse of our services.

    3.7 International Data Processing

    Your Personal Information may be processed in the country where it was collected, as well as in other countries, including the United States, where data protection laws may be less stringent than those in your country of residence.

    3.8 Opt-Out and Data Management

    You have the option to opt out of certain data processing activities:

    A). Limit Ad Tracking: You can limit ad tracking on your device through its settings, which will prevent ads from being selected based on your activities across different websites or apps. However, you will still see ads, but they may not be as relevant.

    B). Opt-Out Cookies: For services using cookies, we offer opt-out cookies that allow you to decline ad targeting across the websites you visit.

    By using the information we collect, JMMobi ensures that ads are more relevant, helps improve our advertising services, and provides transparency and control to users over how their data is used.

These service providers process your personal information on our behalf and are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.     We may disclose your personal information to public authorities in response to lawful requests, including those related to national security or law enforcement.     We may share your personal information with third parties that you authorize or request us to send it to, such as service providers or partners with whom you interact through our services. This type of information is not linked to any specific individual and is used for general marketing, research, and analysis purposes.

    4.7 Opt-Out Options

    You can opt-out of sharing your personal information with additional third parties by using the “Limit Ad Tracking” settings on your device. By doing so, you will continue to see ads, but they will not be tailored based on your activities across different websites or apps.

    We retain various types of personal information about you for different lengths of time, depending on the purposes for processing, our legitimate business needs, and any legal requirements under applicable law.

    8. GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA Users)

    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, the processing of your personal data is subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and similar laws. The following additional rights and disclosures apply to you:

    We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For the use of cookies, location tracking, behavioral advertising, or marketing communications, where required.
  • Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): To fulfill obligations under any service agreements you enter into with us.
  • Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): For compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To improve our services, detect fraud, measure ad performance, and support our business operations, provided such interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

    • 8.2 Data Controller & Representative

    JMMobi Inc. is the data controller for personal information collected through its services and website. If required under Article 27 GDPR, we will appoint an EU representative, and their contact details will be published here once available.

    8.3 Data Subject Rights

    You have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of Access – Know what personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
  • Right to Rectification – Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") – Request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Restrict Processing – Limit how your data is used in certain situations.
  • Right to Data Portability – Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller.
  • Right to Object – Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent – Withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint – File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    • You can exercise any of your rights by contacting us at: privacy@jmmobi.com

    • 8.8 Data Security Measures

    JMMobi applies a range of data security measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. In accordance with Article 13(1)(f) of the GDPR, we are committed to protecting your data using industry-standard safeguards. These measures include:

  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel have access to personal data, governed by role-based access control and the principle of least privilege. Administrator-level access requires two-factor authentication (2FA).

  • Vendor and Processor Management: All third-party service providers that process data on our behalf are subject to GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) and undergo risk-based due diligence.
  • Security Audits and Monitoring: We conduct regular vulnerability assessments and audits of our systems to identify and resolve potential security issues. Real-time monitoring is in place to detect unauthorized activities.
  • Incident Response: In the event of a data breach, JMMobi will promptly initiate its incident response plan and, where applicable, notify data subjects and supervisory authorities in accordance with GDPR requirements.

    • These measures are reviewed and updated periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulatory requirements, and our business operations.
