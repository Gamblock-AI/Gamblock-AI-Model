FAQS

Why choose TeraAIpix?

TeraAIpix provides you with top-notch AI image editing services, while also offering free benefits, such as AI photo categorizing and AI photo filtering, to help you classify, filter, and edit your photographic works faster and better—all can be achieved in TeraAIpix without switching between other platforms.

How do I upgrade my subscription plan?

If you are currently on a monthly subscription (non-annual), your membership duration will be accumulated after upgrading your package. The total membership duration will be the remaining duration of the current package + the increased duration after the upgrade. If you are currently on a monthly auto-subscription and want to upgrade to an annual package, please cancel your current monthly subscription first. After the current benefits expire, you can upgrade to the package you want.

How many credits are included in my subscription?

All TeraAIpix plans include unlimited AI edits. If you encounter a prompt indicating that the export limit has been reached, please take a screenshot and send it to teraaipix@gmail.com.

How should I choose a TeraAIpix subscription plan?

Since all packages include unlimited usage, you only need to consider the appropriate duration. Since the longer the subscription period, the lower the monthly price, we recommend that you choose an annual or continuous monthly auto-subscription to save more money and time.

How do I cancel an existing subscription?

Please go to [Client - Personal Center - Order History] to manage your existing subscriptions. Your refund will be returned to the original path within 1-3 business days.

What is TeraAIpix's refund policy?

Typically, subscription fees are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins. However, if you were charged without your knowledge and have not used the associated benefits, please report your issue via [Help Center - Account Related] within 7 days of the charge. We will review your case, and if it meets our policy standards, we will process a refund for you.
