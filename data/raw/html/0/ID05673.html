Hosting your VPS on an SSD

Equipping your VPS with an SSD brings performance to the next level. Operations that would take minutes/hours to complete on a traditional mechanical hard disk can be completed in seconds with an SSD VPS.

No more bottlenecking your CPU with slow I/O operations, no more waiting hours for data transfers to complete - It's time to experience SSD VPS Hosting from Evolution Host. Strap in.

What makes an SSD VPS better than a standard VPS?

  • Faster boot times
  • Quicker transfer speeds
  • Improved software performance
  • Reduced risk of disk failure
  • Significantly more simultaneous disk operations
  • Faster database lookups

HDD vs SSD vs NVMe SSD VPS benchmarks

Below you can find three images comprising of the disk throughput speeds of 3 separate virtual private servers. These tests have been carried out on live production servers to ensure that the data is as true to life as possible. These benchmarks should be indicative of the disk speeds you can expect with one of our KVM VPS.

Looking at the results of the benchmark

These tests were carried out during normal server operations, i.e. the disks were in use at the time of benchmarking. While typically you would benchmark performance on an empty server, we feel it would be more transparent to showcase results of how a VPS performs under normal circumstances in order to show a realistic representation of server disk performance.

As can be seen above, the standard SSD VPS performed significantly better than the HDD VPS. However the NVMe SSD VPS blew it's competition away with blistering speeds of up to 2,010MB/s for buffered disk reads. These test results should leave no question in anyone's mind that an NVMe SSD VPS is the way to go.
