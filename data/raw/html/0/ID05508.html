Jakarta's Playground in the Sky

Rising 385 meters above Jakarta, UP at Thamrin Nine stands as Indonesia's tallest 360° observatory deck. More than just a view, UP offers an immersive experience where boundless views, architectural marvel, and thrilling sky-high attractions converge.

UPfinity

A one-of-a-kind observation space that blends mirrored reflections with interactive installations—designed to inspire, engage, and elevate your view of the city.
