Hi, How Can We Help You?
CATARACT
Clear your vision and gain a better quality of life when you go for a cataract procedure. Cataract is very common and probable for all ages, including diabetic patients and children. Some of the common symptoms of cataract include blurry visions, pain in the eyes, light sensitivity or difficulty seeing at night. Cataract surgery is a procedure to remove the natural lens in your eyes and replace it with an artificial lens. Hashmanis is equipped with the most advanced and latest bladeless or Femtosecond Laser Assisted Cataract Surgery with 100% accurate results.
LASIK
Get rid of wearing glasses or contact lenses for a lifetime with the right LASIK procedure designed for you. LASIK is one of the most commonly practiced laser refractive surgery to reshape the eye’s cornea in order to improve and correct vision problems for people who are nearsighted or farsighted, or who have astigmatism. The procedure is very quick, safe and painless. Hashmanis is known to be the first to introduce the most advanced and successfully executed LASIK procedures in Pakistan
RETINA
Protect your eyes from vision loss with a safe and effective retinal procedure. Some of the common signs of retinal problems include floating filters or flashes, blurred or distorted central vision. Retina is the thin layer of tissue at the very back of the eyeball. It is the key bridge between the light that enters the eye and the images one sees. Most Retinal diseases can be treated with early diagnoses. Retinal eye problems or diseases may cause blindness if left untreated.
CORNEA
Improve sight, relieve pain or treat any severe infection impacting the cornea with a safe corneal procedure. Cornea is the transparent part of the eye that covers the iris and the pupil and allows light to enter inside the eye. Common symptoms of corneal issues include signs of tearing, redness or blurred vision in the cornea. Corneal problems are very common and one of the leading causes of blindness. Most corneal diseases can be treated with early diagnoses.
OCULOPLASTIC
Get rid of droopy, inward or outward eyelids, tear duct problems or any other issues surrounding the eyes or face with a safe and effective Oculoplastic procedure. Oculoplastic is an ophthalmic plastic, reconstructive or cosmetical surgery that deals with conditions affecting the eyes and the surrounding areas of the eyes including eye socket, eyelids, tear ducts as well as other areas of the face. The procedure is designed to enhance the appearance and functionality of the eyelids or face.
PEDIATRIC OPTHALMOLOGY
Find out all about your child’s eye health through Pediatric Ophthalmology; a specialized branch of ophthalmology dedicated to diagnosing and treating eye diseases, visual development, and vision care among children. Some of the common eye problems affecting children include refractive errors (myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism), pink eye (conjunctivitis), eye rubbing, misalignment of eyes (squint), watery eye (Nasolacrimal Duct Obstruction), Lazy Eye (Amblyopia), among others.
GLAUCOMA
Save your vision from worsening with an advanced glaucoma procedure. Glaucoma is a disease that damages the eye’s optic nerve due to increasingly high eye pressure from fluid building up at the front area of the eye. Some of the common signs of glaucoma include blind spots in your peripheral (side) or central vision, eye redness, eye pain or headaches as well as halos around lights. Glaucoma is one of the leading causes of blindness especially in seniors over the age of 60. Hashmanis has proudly introduced the latest invention in glaucoma into Pakistan comprising Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty (SLT) that supports moving glaucoma treatment beyond eye drops and much more.
HOSPITALIZATION
Hashmanis brings 4 fully equipped state of the art operation theaters, modern intensive care units (ICU, NICU and PICU) as well as access to private wards and labor rooms (including anesthesia hygiene covers). We offer 24/7 round the clock emergency services with a team of highly trained and efficient paramedic staff dedicated to provide exclusive and continuous attention, comfort and care to critically ill patients. Hashmanis provide access to one of the biggest critical care units across Sindh comprising higher intensity monitoring and control measuresto help protect and prevent ailments and achieve optimal cure. Our caring nursing staff and certified surgeons are trained and experienced in managing patients with all kinds of critical and technical needs.
RADIOLOGY
Hashmanis Radiology assists in diagnosing and treating injuries and diseases using the latest medical imaging technology and procedures comprising advanced X-rays machines, ultrasound, computed tomography (CT), interventional radiology, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) among others. Our team of radiologist are committed to promote the highest standards of inbound and outbound patient care with constant observation and investigation in efforts to help progress radiology services nationwide
LABORATORY
Hashmanis Laboratory, Pakistan’s leading edge in diagnostic care brings the most modern tools and equipment to support wide ranges of laboratory tests, services and procedures across various disciplines including biochemistry, hematology, microbiology, coagulation and more. Our scientific lab experts provide in-depth consultation in clinical and diagnostic immunology as well as meaningful and accurate interpretation of results

We provide a wide range of authentic medications that are globally and nationally approved and recognized. Our medicines are stored under controlled temperatures to maintain quality standards. We strive to deliver exceptional service levels and the best possible patient care.
