Your playstyle, your contribution

Destroyers
Fast and stealthy, destroyers excel at daring recons, crafty ambushes, and hunting carriers and battleships. Armed with devastating torpedoes, they’re ideal if you love hit-and-run tactics and playing on the edge.
Battleships
Giants with the thickest armor and mightiest guns. Soak up damage, protect your allies, and punish foes—battleships are capable of destroying an opponent with just a couple of salvos.
Cruisers
Cruisers balance firepower, mobility, and survivability. From providing crucial support with anti-aircraft fire to dishing out consistent damage with artillery or torpedoes, you will be able to deliver whatever your team needs.
Aircraft Carriers
The choice of true tacticians, carriers deploy aircraft to scout, strike, and support their team from afar. Command the battlefield from above, shaping the fight with precision attacks and game-changing strategies.
