OUR PORTFOLIO

LE CHÂTEAU
The Essence of French Living

The Evermore masterplan marks a defining chapter in the evolution of Ras Al Khaimah—a coastal destination shaped by openness, nature, and a long-term vision. Pristine shorelines, sculpted parks, and expansive promenades intertwine with world-class resorts, creating a setting where life unfolds with purpose and quiet sophistication. Within this setting, Le Château offers a refined expression of balance and flow. Here, light, form, and landscape exist in seamless harmony, shaping a way of living defined by calm presence, natural rhythm, and understated elegance.

Hado
Sculpting balance with nature

Introducing HADO — a place where the unseen becomes experiential, and where motion and stillness exist in perfect harmony. Three sculpted towers rise with quiet confidence, opening toward the water to welcome light, horizon, and uninterrupted views. Transparent glass corners blur the line between interior serenity and the vastness beyond, allowing architecture to flow like energy — subtle, fluid, and alive. Set on Island B within the prestigious Siora master plan on Dubai Islands, Hado is the first landmark to emerge in this visionary waterfront enclave. Dubai Islands stands among the city’s most distinguished mixed-use destinations, offering refined residences, world-class hospitality, vibrant marinas, and over 20 kilometers of pristine coastline — including an exclusive Blue Flag–certified beach.

Kanyon
A living environment shaped by movement, light, and silence.

Kanyon rises as an open form, designed to capture light from every angle and let the horizon flow through its architecture. Each residence extends outward in fluid terraces that look toward the sea, transforming every view into a quiet dialogue between space and sky. From every floor, the panorama unfolds in 360 degrees — an ever-changing theatre of light, reflection, and movement. Here, architecture becomes an experience of openness and serenity, where the boundary between inside and outside fades into air.

31-Above
WORK. LIFE. BALANCE.

A sculptural landmark that sets a new standard of what it means to work well. Created by Beyond, the visionary developer renowned for some of Dubai’s most sought-after luxury residences, this is their first foray into the world of commercial design - bringing the same artistry, precision, and sophistication that define their signature style. Architecturally, the Tower is distinguished by its dynamic, stacked form; a series of considered volumes placed one above the other to create a bold vertical identity. Each section is subtly offset, forming landscaped terraces which bring light, greenery, and perspective at every level. The result is a composition of balance and proportion - a bold expression of success.
