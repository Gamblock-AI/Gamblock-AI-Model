Understanding Epoxy Resin for Adhesive

Adhesive Epoxy resin is a polymer that is very strong and thermosetting in nature and normally arrives in two components, one being a resin and the other a hardener, and to begin a curing process, one has to combine these components in a particular proportion. After this blend, it passes through a process that changes it into an extremely hard and long-lasting solid that forms a bond, which is usually stronger than the corpus it bonds. Epoxies are considered the ideal adhesive where high mechanical strength, chemical and heat resistance, and little shrinkage in the curing process are required due to their reputation of having the highest mechanical strength, resistance to chemicals and heat, and low shrinkage during the curing process when compared to most other adhesives. It is their capacity to bond a huge variety of materials, such as metals, ceramics, wood and rigid plastics, in industries such as construction and automotive as well as aerospace and electronics, that could make them an excellent industrial-grade adhesive.
