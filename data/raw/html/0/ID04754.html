Lake Victoria-Nile Marathon

The Lake Victoria Marathon is an International annual event to create awareness about the challenge of plastic pollution and how to foster responsible plastic waste management and disposal, raise resources to support initiatives that seek to foster awareness about responsible use and disposal of plastic waste along the Nile Basin.

The Marathon is part of the Refresh Environment and Climate Campaign that TRANET-Africa has been running since 2023.

The marathon will take place each in Jinja City in Uganda the source of the Nile on Lake Victoria on the 22nd of February 2025.

For more kindly visit https://tranetafrica.org/innitiative/lake-victoria-nile-marathon
