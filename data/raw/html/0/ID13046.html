Beeswax Inc. (“Beeswax”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an advertising technology platform headquartered in New York City. We respect your right to privacy. Beeswax runs a technology-buying platform for digital media. The Beeswax technology allows our customers to buy ad space on websites, mobile apps, video players, and other devices. When ad space is bought or sold our technology fulfills the transaction by delivering the ad to Internet users who visit that site, apps, or device. We also use pixel tags, which are small, invisible lx1 images on a web page. Pixel tags allow us to set, read and modify cookies when you visit the websites or apps of our partners.

If you block, delete or restrict cookies in any way, your opt-out request will not work. Also, opt-out settings are specific to each browser or device, so you will need to set your opt-out preference for each one, and if you use a different computer or Internet browser, you will need to renew your opt-out choice. If you opt-out through this method, we will not use your Personal Information in our Services; however, we may continue to use your information collected for other purposes, such as our own marketing. If you wish to opt-out of such other uses of your information, see our Website Privacy Policy.

For consumers who have opted-out from the use of your Personal Information by Beeswax, we do not share any of your information with these third parties and do not attempt to re-identify the user in any way. We do not utilize cross-device linking services when we know the user or the ad request is subject to GDPR or otherwise not permitted.
