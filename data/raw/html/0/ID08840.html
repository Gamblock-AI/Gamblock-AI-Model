FOX One offers annual and monthly subscriptions, plus add-ons. Some plans include a free trial at signup.

To start your 7-day free trial:

  1. Go to fox.com and select “TRY 7 DAYS FREE”
  2. Choose a plan that includes a free trial (note: not all plans have a trial).
  3. Create your FOX One account with a username and password.
  4. Add your payment method—you won't be charged until the trial ends.
  5. Cancel any time during the trial from the Manage Subscription page.

If you already have a FOX Nation subscription, you can keep watching just like you always have—either online at foxnation.com or in the FOX Nation App on all your favorite devices. Using FOX One with your TV provider is easy! Here's how to get started, depending on the device you're using:

On fox.com:

  1. Click TV Provider at the top of the page.
  2. Search and select your provider from the list.
  3. Sign in with your provider username and password.
  4. Once you're signed in, you'll be automatically redirected back to fox.com.

On mobile apps:

  1. Tap Connect TV Provider in the app
  2. Find and select your TV provider.
  3. Enter your username and password on your provider's site.
  4. After signing in, you'll return automatically to the FOX One app.

On Living Room apps (Smart TVs, Roku, etc.):

  1. Select Connect Provider in the app.
  2. Follow the simple instructions displayed on your screen.
  3. After signing in through your provider, you'll automatically be signed into your device.
  4. Now you're ready to start watching!
