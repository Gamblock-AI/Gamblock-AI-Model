FAQ'S

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What size events can Expo Creationss Pte Ltd handle?
We handle events of all sizes, from small corporate displays to large industry conventions.
  • Can you work within my budget?
Absolutely! We offer customized solutions that can be tailored to fit various budget constraints.
  • Can Expo Creationss help with marketing the event too?
While our primary focus is on the design and execution of the exhibition, we collaborate closely with marketing agencies to ensure your event receives the right exposure and reaches your target audience effectively.
  • How much lead time do you require to plan an exhibition?
Ideally, we prefer 3-6 months to ensure thorough planning and execution, but can accommodate shorter timelines.
  • Do you offer expo-friendly exhibit options?
Yes, we provide expo-friendly exhibit solutions including reusable materials and energy-efficient lighting
  • What happens if there is a last-minute change or emergency during the event?
Our experienced project managers are skilled in crisis management and are equipped to handle unexpected changes or emergencies efficiently to minimize any impact on the event.
