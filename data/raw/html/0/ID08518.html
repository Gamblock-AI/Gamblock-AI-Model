Small plant focus, Big company resources

National Manufacturing Group, formerly known as National Composites, is a multi-plant OEM supplier specializing in custom molded FRP, thermoformed plastics, and full metal cab fabrication and assembly. National Manufacturing Group utilizes multiple FRP molding methods, including open mold, LRTM, RTM, and compression molding. As a vertically integrated, American-based manufacturer, National supports domestic innovation through in-house metal and composite product design (as needed), pattern builds, R&D and prototyping, tool and fixture fabrication, high-volume and diversified molding capabilities, and Class A paint operations. Additionally, National is a full-service assembly organization dedicated to value-added component installation and complete system assembly.National Manufacturing Group operates over 1,000,000 square feet of production space spanning five states, providing the scale, resources, and infrastructure to support programs of any size. Our facilities function as a unified manufacturing platform, supported by centralized business development, engineering, design, finance, and executive leadership. This structure allows National to support customers from early-stage design, material selection, and prototyping through full-scale production—whether the program requires a single component or production volumes in the tens of thousands.

National’s broad manufacturing footprint and continuously expanding capabilities allow us to serve a wide range of industries with consistency, reliability, and speed. Built on American craftsmanship and driven by innovation, National Manufacturing Group is committed to delivering more than quality products—we strive to be a long-term manufacturing partner our customers can depend on.

What We Do



COMPOSITES

National Manufacturing Group specializes in advanced composite manufacturing, offering Robotic Open Mold, Light Resin Transfer Molding (LRTM), and Resin Transfer Molding (RTM) for precision and quality. Additional capabilities include Liquid Compression Molding (LCM), Sheet Molding Compound (SMC), and a dedicated painting division—providing full-service solutions for a wide range of composite applications.



Operator Cab Fabrication

National Manufacturing Group delivers full-service metal fabrication with a focus on custom cab assemblies, integrating plastics and composites for complete builds. We handle everything from controls and wiring to seating, glass, and upholstery, working with both steel and aluminum. Our in-house paint division provides everything from primer to Class-A finishes using advanced prep, prime, and coating systems for metals, plastics, and composites.



THERMOFORM PLASTICS

We deliver high-quality thermoform plastic solutions that combine visual appeal with durability. Ideal for low to high-volume production, thermoforming offers cost-effective manufacturing with minimal tooling investment. By incorporating Fiber Reinforced Plastics (FRP), we enhance strength without sacrificing appearance—resulting in parts that are both tough and visually refined.

Marine and Recreation

National Composites marine and recreation division has been supplying the nation’s top pontoon and boat manufacturers for years. Utilizing open and closed molding methods we produce helm stands, center consoles, seat bases, table/bar tops, doors, and more. With a deep understanding of the high part quality expectations and the constantly adapting colors and styles, our team are marine experts, specializing in the latest trends of poly flake and metallics gel coats, as well as complex multi gated molds. Our marine division also has a dedicated secondary component assembly group very experienced in the assembly of helm stands and center consoles.
Marine and Recreation production is located in one of our Owosso Michigan plants, Great Lakes Composites.

Electric and Specialty Vehicle

Since 1974 we have been working with some of the top innovators in the market. We have a deep understanding of the market’s quality and tolerance expectations.
