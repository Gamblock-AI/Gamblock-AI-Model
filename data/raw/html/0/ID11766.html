Shaping Digital Futures Together

A  joint initiative between the City of Linz and IT:U. A space where research meets the public and digital futures are co-created by everyone. From AI and robotics to VR, climate and urban data, and citizen science — technology becomes hands-on. Workshops, demos, and labs turn complex challenges like sustainability and digital society into everyday experiences, accessible to all generations.

Step Into Your Future Classrooms

At IT:U, learning happens in LearnLabs, not big lecture halls.

From day one, you work in small, interdisciplinary teams on real challenges, guided by experts. Whether in the Robotics Lab (ROB), the Extended Reality Lab (RITA), the Human-Computer Interaction Lab (INES), or across a growing network of labs, you learn by building, testing, and experimenting. 

This is where ideas become prototypes, and theory becomes something you can experience.

What Learning at IT:U Looks Like

At IT:U, learning is different. You’ll start in a unique Project Space, join a team of five from diverse backgrounds, and move through the program together with a rotating groups in a tight-knit cohort. You co-create your learning path with professors, coaches, and lab experts. Learning goes beyond traditional classrooms, taking place in innovative labs and real-world projects.

Climate Agents

Research Group: Geosocial Artificial Intelligence

“There is no Planet B”: The effects of climate change are increasingly permeating our everyday lives and influencing our living environments and our environment.

Together with students, teachers and other citizen scientists, the “Climate Agents” project pursues a so-called “co-creation approach”, which is researched thematically-scientifically and in participatory formats. Under supervision, students become “citizen scientists” and create their own “StoryMaps” for the presentation of results and science communication.

This expands the understanding of scientific processes and thus strengthens trust in science.
