Semua Yang Perlu Anda Ketahui

Apa itu OpenL?
OpenL is an AI-powered translation software that supports more than 100 languages, including English, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, French, German, Hebrew, and Indonesian, making it ideal for professional translations. It utilizes AI and machine learning for accurate translations and provides content creation assistance, grammar correction and language learning features.
Apakah aplikasi ini gratis?
OpenL provides 30 trial credits per day for free accounts, which can be used without logging in. To obtain more credits, you can purchase a Subscription account.
Berapa banyak karakter yang bisa saya terjemahkan sekaligus?
Anda dapat menerjemahkan 1.500 karakter sekaligus dalam rencana gratis. Membutuhkan lebih? Naikkan ke rencana berbayar, yang dapat menangani hingga 100.000 karakter per terjemahan.
Berapa banyak bahasa yang didukung oleh OpenL Translate?
OpenL Translate menawarkan terjemahan dalam lebih dari 100 bahasa, termasuk bahasa utama seperti Inggris, Arab, Prancis, Rusia dan Spanyol, serta bahasa khusus seperti Yunani Kuno, Norse Kuno, Toki Pona, dan Emoji. Rasakan terjemahan AI yang cepat dan akurat.
Metode pembayaran apa saja yang tersedia?
We currently accept Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Diners Club, JCB), Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Additional payment methods may be available based on your country.
Apakah informasi pembayaran saya aman?
Your transactions and payment details are securely handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, using advanced encryption technology and strict adherence to banking industry standards. Your credit card information is sent directly to the bank, and OpenL does not access or store any payment information, ensuring your payment security.
Apakah siswa mendapatkan diskon pendidikan?
Yes! If you're a student or teacher with an edu email, email us at hi@openl.io to receive a 30% education discount code.
Apa yang terjadi dengan dokumen saya setelah diterjemahkan?
Your privacy is our priority. We don't store any of your translation data. Once the translation is done, all data is immediately deleted. The translation history remains only in your local browser and is never uploaded to our servers.
Bagaimana cara membatalkan langganan saya?
You can cancel your subscription at any time via the Billing page. Cancellations take effect after the current billing cycle ends.
Bisakah saya meminta pengembalian dana?
Refunds can be requested according to our Refund Policy. Please refer to the policy for details on eligibility.
Apa perbedaan antara langganan web dan iOS?
Web subscriptions are for the website only, while iOS subscriptions are for the iOS app only. They cannot be used across devices. Please choose the subscription based on your device.
