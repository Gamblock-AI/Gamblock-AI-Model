Facilities

Tunjungan Plaza has complete facilities to ease the visitors; plentiful parking lots, valet service, taxi stand, concierge, ATM center, SIM corner, banks, nursery rooms, toilet and disabled toilet, smoking room, prayer room, free wi-fi at Tunjungan Plaza 5 and many more.

SMOKING ROOM
Supporting Regional Regulation no 5/2008 about Non-Smoking Area, all areas in Tunjungan Plaza has been smoke-free since 2008. However, Tunjungan Plaza provides smoking room for visitors in Foodcourt area TP 3 5th Floor.
