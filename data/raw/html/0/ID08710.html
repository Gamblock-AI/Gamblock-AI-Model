Certified impact, made simple.

The 1% for the Planet model is accessible to businesses, credible for consumers and impactful for people and the planet.

1.
Commit
Commit to donating at least 1% of annual sales to environmental organizations
2.
Give
Donate directly to one or more approved environmental organizations
3.
Certify
Submit donation receipts and revenue documentation to certify as a 1% for the Planet member
4.
Amplify
Use the 1% for the Planet network and certification to inspire businesses and individuals to take action
