Welcome to FIBAA – Foundation for International Business Administration Accreditation

Your quality assurance agency for programme and system accreditation and certification.

With many years of expertise and an extensive network of highly qualified experts, we are your reliable partner for the highest quality standards in education and science. Our work is consistently based on internationally recognised guidelines such as the 'European Standards and Guidelines for Quality Assurance', which are binding throughout Europe. As a dialogue partner for universities, we are actively committed to quality, transparency and comparability.
