frequently asked questions

What is co living?
Co-living is a modern way of living where you rent a private room and share common spaces like the kitchen and living area with others. It’s fully furnished, includes Wi-Fi, cleaning, and utilities in one monthly bill, making it convenient and affordable. Co-living also creates a sense of community through shared amenities and regular events. To learn more, visit our blog.
How do I find suitable properties?
It's super easy! Just click on the “view homes“ button at the top, filter according to your preferences and we will provide the most relevant places. You can also chat with our sales specialists and they'll be happy to share our available room or studio options.
How quickly can I move in?
The earliest move in date is displayed on each property listing. Many of our available rooms are open for immediate move-in and all properties are open for move in within 2 weeks of the listing status showing as 'available'.
Can I speak to someone at Cove?
The team are always happy to speak to you – simply drop us a Whatsapp (+6531384860).
What amenities and appliances are provided in the property?
Most of our properties feature a TV (40”-50” wide), Chromecast, Superfast 1GB Mesh Wifi, furnishing, washing machine and comprehensive kitchenware (including fridge, crockery, cutlery, utensils and kitchen knives, pots and pans, cooking dishes, serving dishes, glassware, mugs, coffee press, kettle, toaster and hob - please check the individual listings for details of whether a microwave, oven, freezer and tumble dryer are provided). Please check individual listings for information about additional apartment amenities and condo facilities as these vary by property.
Do all your properties come furnished?
Yes, all our properties come furnished with basic necessities. At a minimum you can expect in the communal areas: sofa, coffee table, smart TV or TV with Chromecast, dining table and chairs, and kitchen appliances. In the bedrooms we provide queen sized beds in most homes, or a super single or single bed if space is limited, bedside table, bedside lamp and wardrobe. In some larger rooms we try to fit WFH friendly furniture such as a desk. All rooms also come equipped with a laundry basket, 10 hangers and an extension cord.
Can you remove the furniture if I provide my own?
We're not able to remove any of the furniture provided but if there is space in your personal bedroom you're welcome to add additional small items of furniture (as long as they don't require any fixing to the walls).
Do you allow pets?
No, unfortunately we do not currently allow pets in any of our properties.
