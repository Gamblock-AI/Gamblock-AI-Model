1.  Introduction 

 

Gamoshi Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”, “Gamoshi” or “Company” and correlating terms) is creating proprietary technology for the ad tech and mar tech industries. The Company offers proprietary technology to the digital advertising industry through the development of Gamoshi service platform  (“ Product”), and certain services as described in this privacy policy for advertisers, publishers, and mediators (“Clients” or “Users”). It allows users to seamlessly manage and convert all delivery methodologies to their own needs and supply and demand partnerships. We are not collecting personal data through our Product use from our Users, besides minimal data for registration and Client operation preferences. 

In addition, Gamoshi provides certain related services, as described below. Gamoshi does not provide its Services directly to end users. Instead, Gamoshi’s Services are designed for businesses, marketers, advertising agencies, marketing technology platforms, media companies, and publishers, and enable them to participate in programmatic advertising activities. Note that when providing programmatic advertising services to its customers, Gamoshi does not use information that directly identifies anyone as a specific person (such as your name, address, email address, or phone number), but we may use other pseudonymized personal information as further described below.

Gamoshi’s Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”) describes the data collection and processing practices that give you information about how we treat your Personal Data  (as described below when you access our websites and use Our services). This Privacy Policy forms part of your agreement with us, when you access our website, available at www.gamoshi.com  or any subdomain or website that links to this Policy, (the “Site” or “Website”), or use the Product, or otherwise engage with any of our services, software, features or forms that we may make available to you from time to time through the Site or other hosting websites (each individually, or jointly with the Site and Product, the “Services”).  

Please read the Privacy Policy carefully to be sure you understand it and agree with its terms before you use the Services. If you do not agree with any of the terms provided in this Policy, and the choices we provide do not mitigate your concerns, please do not access or use our Services. If you feel you do not understand anything included in this Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us with any questions you may have at: info@gamoshi.com.

3. Gamoshi services:

 

1. SaaS Products and Services. 

Gamoshi offers cloud-based services that are offered through its software as a service platform, Gamoshi platform, including Gamoshi’s DSP solution for advertisers, and Programmatic Exchange solution. Gamoshi’s platform combines contextual-analysis algorithms, machine-learning algorithms, content-match algorithms, predictive analytics, and big-data mining and analysis features. Our Product connects all media types, including display, video, native, and audio whether they are represented as regular tags or Real-Time Bidding (RTB) for our Clients: advertisers, publishers, and mediators. 

These Services enable customers to manage or participate in a programmatic advertising auction. Clients can connect their own buyers and sellers to the auction or connect to GamoshiX programmatic marketplace. Gamoshi provides the tools that facilitate our customers’ programmatic activities and acts as a ‘data processor’ determining the purposes and the means of the processing of Personal Data on behalf of and at the direction of those clients.  

2. GamoshiX  marketplace

Gamoshi offers advertisers and buyers the option to connect to our own advertising programmatic auction via the GamoshiX marketplace service. GamoshiX marketplace has access to leading demand sources and high-quality traffic. GamoshiX marketplace service uses universal IDs such as live ramp ID to identify users across computers, phones, and other mobile devices.

3. Gamoshi’s data services

Some of Gamoshi’s Services, allow Gamoshi’s clients to utilize data to reach the right online audiences for their products and services. To provide these Services, Gamoshi collects data from third-party providers (including website owners, app owners, and SmartTV manufacturers) and makes this data available to its clients. This data is called “Third Party Data.” Gamoshi’s clients use Third-Party Data to create data segments associated with certain interests, behaviors, and attributes (for example, an interest in travel or an interest in sports). These data segments are associated with cookies and advertising identifiers so that clients can send relevant advertisements to consumers on their computers, phones, and other mobile devices.

  • Personal Data refers to information about a living individual, which means that they can be identified (a) from that data, or (b) from that data and any other information which is, relates to, describes, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular person or could in the future, come into the possession of the data controller, and as provided in this Privacy Policy below. 

  • Non-Personal Data means information that cannot personally identify or lead to identifying a natural person. For example, statistics, aggregated information, or any other type of data that can no longer be attributed to you.

  • Data Controller refers to the person, organization, public authority, agency, or other body who, either alone or with others, determines the purposes for which and the manner in which any Personal Data is to be processed, and defines the controls required for such processing.

  • Data Processor refers to any person or organization (other than an employee of the Data Controller) who undertakes the processing of Personal Data on behalf of the Data Controller.

  • Processing refers to any operation which is performed upon or applied to personal data, whether undertaken manually or by automated means, including its acquisition, organization, storage, retrieval, consultation, amendment, availability, disclosure, erasure, or destruction.

  • Data Subject refers to an individual who is the subject of Personal Data.

  • Data Subject Consent refers to the Data Subject’s approval or agreement for an activity to take place, having considered the benefits and risks of the activity. For consent to be valid, the data subject needs to be informed, have the capacity and knowledge to decide, and have given their consent voluntarily. Specific requirements need to be met in connection with the consent which is given by Children, including validating parental consent and the age of the Child. 

  • Supervisory Authority refers to (a) the national data protection authority of each EEA country, responsible for enforcing GDPR within their own nation b) any similar regulatory authority responsible for the enforcement of Data Protection Laws and Regulations outside of Europe; or (c) In Israel, the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority and the Israeli Database Registrar. The Supervisory Authority is also the reporting point for data breach notifications, and for conducting investigations.
