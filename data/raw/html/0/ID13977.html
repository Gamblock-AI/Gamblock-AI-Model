An electronic signature, or eSignature, is a digital version of a person’s signature. It’s used to verify and authenticate electronic documents like PDFs. Electronic signatures created with Sign.com are legally valid and recognized throughout the European Union and in a growing number of countries around the globe.
Electronic signatures created with Sign.com are valid and legally binding. They were accommodated into European law with the eIDAS regulation, and granted legal status in the USA with the ESIGN Act. As long as signatures are legally obtained and created with intent, they are legally recognized in the entire EU and numerous other countries.
When you create an electronic signature with Sign.com, the entire process is protected with end-to-end TLS encryption. Your documents are fully secure from upload to download and everything in between. You can rest assured that your data is safe, private, and secure.
