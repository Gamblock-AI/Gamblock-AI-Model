    It looks to me that the Indonesian translation of f-droid.org is basically ready to launch. Please check it in Weblate and on https://staging.f-droid.org/id/ to make sure everything is in order. Then let me know whe I can launch it. Someone will need to write a short blog post to announce that Indonesian is on the website. The details are being tracked here: https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroid-website/-/issues/658

    We have added a new automation to merge translations when they are ready. When this component and the "website-pages" component are 100% translated with 0% failing checks, the language will be automatically sent to be merged into production.
