    Master of Public Policy and Management (A6033) View

    The Master of Public Policy and Management (MPPM) builds practical knowledge and skills in policy-making, policy analysis and public management across the spectrum of public sector activities. The course will develop your understanding of the processes of policy formulation, implementation and delivery of public services and programs, and the political and structural realities that influence decision-making.

    The MPPM leverages Monash’s global reputation and expertise in delivering public policy education and has been especially designed for Indonesia to train the next generation of public policy leaders, practitioners and scholars keen to contribute towards addressing contemporary policy challenges. It combines knowledge of public policy theory and practice to enable professionals to be effective policy practitioners who can work in or with governments to shape how policy agendas are negotiated and how public policies are designed and implemented.

    The MPPM is for working professionals who want to pursue or accelerate their career in a policy-related field as a policy practitioner, adviser, analyst or advocate. The course will prepare you for a career at all levels of government, NGOs, international development agencies, research think tanks, consulting firms, and private businesses that interact with government and contribute to or are affected by public policy in local, national and global settings.

    This degree will equip you with industry relevant specialist skills and prepare you for working and living in a world of constant technological, environmental, political and demographic change. The course provides an immersive and interactive learning experience and is designed to foster skills to critically evaluate evidence, develop and support arguments, conduct policy research using a variety of methodological approaches, communicate with impact, and understand the possibilities and challenges of public policy practice and reform. You will learn from accomplished and internationally-recognised academics, scholars and practitioners, and benefit from their wide-ranging intellectual expertise and real-world policy experience.

    The degree offers flexibility to tailor your study to your career goals through the choice of two specialisations. The Policy studies stream will widen your disciplinary perspectives of public policy and provide you with a potential pathway to undertake a higher research-based degree. The Global studies stream will develop your skills and knowledge of public policy in international contexts, and prepare you for a global public policy career. Students taking this specialisation have the opportunity to enrol in approved elective units taught by Monash Australia, the option to take a study tour to India, and conclude their studies with a dedicated capstone focusing on a global policy challenge.

    Master of Business Innovation (B6027) View

    The Master of Business Innovation (MBI) program at Monash University, Indonesia is designed for experienced professionals looking to enhance their management and innovation skills to drive business performance.
    The program is ideal for individuals who are eager to exchange practical and theoretical perspectives, actively participate in the learning process, and integrate their professional experiences with theoretical frameworks. With a minimum of two years of working experience, applicants are hungry for knowledge co-creation in a classroom setting.

    With an average age of 30 and 5-10 years of work experience, current MBI students are well-positioned to create positive impacts and contribute to the nation's economy.

    Monash has launched a world-first business research center in the Pacific, climbed in global rankings, and received recognition for its global executive program. Additionally, we collaborate with important industry players, such as Kalbe, to strengthen leadership capacities.

    Throughout the course, students can expect to engage with a wide range of topics and themes that are essential for driving innovation in business. The program is divided into three parts: Foundation skills and knowledge (Part A), application and implementation (Part B), and mastering innovation (Part C). Students will acquire a diverse set of management skills critical to fostering innovation, including understanding regulations, market dynamics, sustainable business practices, resource allocation, and business model performance.

    Master of Global Business (B6061) View

Building upon prior experience and credentials, it advances critical analysis, problem-solving, and solution development skills through in-depth study of geo-political, trade, sustainability, social responsibility, and intercultural communication issues. This coursework program provides a holistic, innovative perspective on navigating the complexities of the contemporary global business environment to produce graduates capable of developing solutions for business, professional, social, and public policy sectors worldwide.

    Students will learn how to engage an increasingly internationalised world, exhibit cross-cultural competence, act and trade ethically, and be able to incorporate diverse world views into their strategy and operations.

    By connecting with, and learning from, a community of like-minded peers, world-leading academics and industry experts, students start building a lifelong network of people who want to drive Indonesia forward through business, giving them a compelling headstart for a fulfilling, changemaking career.

    Master of Data Science (C6009) View

    Our Master of Data Science is a technical program, but we welcome students from various levels of experience, starting from budding professionals to VP/Head level, even if they are not from a cognate background. There are no prerequisites in terms of business or information technology (IT) understanding, as we provide foundational knowledge and skills in IT during the first two terms to ensure all students can effectively engage with the course material.

    Our curriculum has been developed by Monash Australia, ensuring a comprehensive and industry-relevant education. We have already undertaken projects with industry leaders such as Tokopedia, Indonesia's largest e-commerce platform.

    During the course, students can expect to delve into topics and themes such as data analysis, data visualisation, big data modelling, and machine learning. These skills are versatile and can be applied to various domains and areas, providing students with a solid foundation to excel as data professionals in both private and public sectors.

    Urban Design Leaders working across scales, from flourishing urban villages to highrise districts, civic spaces, commercial hubs, transit networks and landscape ecologies.

    Urban Scaling

    Design across neighbourhoods, towns, cities and regions.

    Where imagination meets analysis.

Master both creative methods—drawing, modelling, spatial imagination—and analytical tools—GIS, data analysis, regulatory frameworks, feasibility assessment. Collaborate with city-makers in government, industry, civil society and local communities to create innovative visions that inspire and endure.

Studios: Design with real stakeholders on actual sites, building professional portfolios. Tools: Master creative methods and technical skills. Fieldwork: Learn from Indonesian cities through field intensives and international collaborations. Research: Develop thesis work that contributes to scholarship and practice.

    Master of Sustainability (S6010) View

    The Master of Sustainability program at Monash University, Indonesia, is designed to equip students with industry-relevant knowledge and skills to address the sustainability challenges faced by Indonesia, both in private and government sectors. As the nation rapidly transforms, there is a growing need for skilled professionals who can deliver practical solutions to issues related to ESG and the SDGs such as reducing emissions and waste, improving air quality, developing renewable energy, and combating deforestation and flooding.

    The curriculum combines evidence-based knowledge with hands-on learning opportunities and practical, transdisciplinary problem-solving skills, enabling students to develop innovative solutions and drive policy change for complex problems. The program also combines global perspectives and knowledge with local context and expertise, equipping students to address sustainability issues at national, regional, and international levels. Three parts working together to develop learners"

    1. Core studies in sustainability to understand cutting-edge issues and multiple stakeholder perspectives on global and local issues.
    2. Expert sustainability studies to learn about corporate sustainability, measurement approaches, behaviour change and innovation in sustainability.

    The students are expected to contribute to the sustainable, equitable, and thriving future of Indonesia by driving policies, projects, and practices that promote environmental, social, and economic sustainability.     What is the accreditation of Monash University, Indonesia? View

    Monash University is a Top 50 university globally. Our courses are accredited by a range of professional associations in Australia and globally. All courses offered at Monash University, Indonesia are accredited with the Indonesian Ministry of Education and relevant accrediting bodies. Monash University, Indonesia has accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Higher Education for both the institution and the courses on offer.

    All programs offered by Monash University, Indonesia have been accredited in the country of origin (Australia) through the TEQSA accreditation system and are recognized by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology. For bachelor's or undegraduate programs, Please refer to the Decree of the Minister of Education and Culture Number 664/B/O/2025. While, for posgraduate programs, please refer to the Decree of the Minister of Education and Culture Number 1097/E.E3/DT.03.02/2024.

    In addition, Monash University, Indonesia operates with official authorization from the Indonesian government, as granted since 2020 and has been updated through Decree of the Minister of Higher Education, Science, and Technology Number 28/A/O/2024, which provides the legal framework for foreign higher education institutions to establish and operate campuses in Indonesia.

    The decree also stipulates that all educational levels offered by Monash University, Indonesia are equivalent to those applied in the Indonesian education system. Furthermore, it includes a conversion table that translates Monash University's Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) to the Indonesian Indeks Prestasi Kumulatif (IPK) grading system. This equivalency and conversion ensure that degrees and academic achievements from Monash University Indonesia are recognized and can be accurately compared within the Indonesian educational framework.
