QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our AI art generation tools and services.

Our AI image tools use advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze and generate images based on text prompts. These models are trained on vast datasets of images and text, allowing them to understand and create visual content that matches given descriptions.
Penly AI offers extensive customization options including style selection, detailed prompting controls, and advanced parameters for fine-tuning your generated images. You can adjust aspects like composition, lighting, color schemes, and artistic styles to achieve precisely the visual results you're looking for. Our platform balances user-friendly simplicity with powerful creative control.
The copyright status of AI-generated images is a complex and evolving legal topic. While the AI doesn't have rights, the person who prompted the AI might have some rights to the output. We recommend using AI-generated images for personal use or obtaining legal advice for commercial applications.
While each generated image is unique in its composition, there's a possibility of similarities with other AI-generated images, especially if using similar prompts. The uniqueness depends on the specificity of your prompt and the randomness factor in the generation process.
