Privacy Policy

Overview

Transfon Ltd, together with its software products (collectively referred to as "Transfon," "we," "us," or "our"), operates as a technology platform headquartered in the UK. Transfon uses various technologies to collect information about website traffic, online advertising, mobile app traffic, and connected device traffic to deliver its products and solutions (collectively, "Solutions"). This privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") outlines how our technology platform and related services (collectively, "Services") collect, use, store, share, and otherwise process your Personal Information on behalf of online advertisers, website owners, data providers, and other businesses that use our Services. Ampbid is Transfon's technology platform for data management, digital marketing, and digital advertising.

Transfon Ampbid complies with the specifications and policies of the IAB EU Transparency & Consent Framework to adhere to EU data protection laws. Ampbid identification number within the Framework is 1370. For more information, visit IAB Europe’s Transparency & Consent Framework.

To opt out of certain data practices, visit our Opt-Out page.

Collection of Information

Data Collected by the Platform:

  • Unique cookie and device identifiers
  • Mobile device advertising identifiers
  • Pseudonymous identifiers derived from email addresses or phone numbers, or device and connection information
  • IP addresses
  • Web browsing history from advertising impressions
  • Interest information inferred from web browsing history
  • Interest information stored and/or used on the Platform by clients and partners
  • Location information
  • Browser and device type, version, and settings
  • Hashed email addresses, phone numbers, and other identifying information (or data derived from such information)

How the Platform Collects Data:

  • From partners sending ad requests on websites, mobile applications, smart televisions, video devices, and other media
  • Using cookies and pixels
  • From clients and partners uploading data to our Platform
  • From clients and partners using our technology to collect data
  • Through the delivery of advertising

Cookies:

Cookies enable us to recognize and store data about unique web browsers and devices, facilitating the advertising purposes described herein. Our cookie domain is ampbid.org. The ampbid ID (ARID) identifies web-browser profiles over time across sites, with a lifespan of one year from the time of the last ad interaction. This lifespan may reset upon subsequent interactions with our Platform.

We also engage in cookie syncing, which matches our ARID with clients’ and partners’ cookie IDs to transmit ad requests and other user or device data. Additionally, we use cookies to store opt-out preferences for users who opt out of targeted advertising.

Purposes for Processing Data:

  • Personalizing ads
  • Delivering ads
  • Limiting the frequency of ad displays
  • Measuring ad effectiveness
  • Reporting on ad campaigns
  • Attributing purchases or other actions to ads
  • Preventing malicious or invalid activity
  • Improving our Platform and services
  • Associating related devices

Security and Data Retention

We retain your information while you are a user of our services. For log records, we retain data for one year from the date of creation.

We may retain your information longer if required for actual or prospective legal or regulatory proceedings.
