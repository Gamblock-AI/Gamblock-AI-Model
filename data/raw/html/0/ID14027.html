Connected insights for your connected lifestyle

The importance of fast and consistent network connections has never been greater. As the industry standard for technology performance benchmarking, RootMetrics, an Ookla® company, believes you deserve real-world performance insights for the technologies that power your daily connected experience.

Providing insights to improve the end-user technology experience

We're driven by one fundamental goal: to provide actionable insights that help improve the end-user's technology experience. From our testing of real-world 5G performance and much more, we know that better, more comprehensive testing leads to a better end-user experience.

Real-world testing for real-world results

Our controlled, consumer-focused approach to measuring technology performance provides a real-world view of the end-user experience for the technologies that matter most to users. We use technology purchased off the shelf to test the same things consumers and enterprises alike use their technology for on a daily basis. We test the speed and reliability of mobile network operators, measure real-world 5G performance, and much more.

The standard for technology performance measurement

We're independent in our testing, unbiased and accurate with our results, and recognized by industries, press, and consumers the world over as the standard for technology performance measurement and analytics. We're passionate about technology and just as passionate about providing technology performance insights to help both consumers and businesses make more informed decisions in today's connected world.

You need actionable insights for the technologies that power your daily life

We're passionate about the importance that technology performance plays in the consumer's everyday life. We believe accurate performance insights can help improve the end-user's connected technology experience.
