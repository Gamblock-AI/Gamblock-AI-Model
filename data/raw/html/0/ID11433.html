OFFICIAL

MOTOGP™ 26

Season

Race with all the official riders, teams, bikes, and tracks of the season and experience the thrill of being on the grid. From authentic visuals to roaring engines, every detail pulls you straight into the heart of racing.

Discover the new rider ratings and how they evolve throughout the season, reflecting real-world performance. They are also featured in 100+ Collectible Cards, alongside the most iconic tracks. Complete goals across all modes and collect them all.

RATINGS

A new

generation of

riding physics

Built on advanced Rider Based Handling technology, the new physics delivers a more authentic and immersive riding experience. Body movement and weight shifting matter more than ever, just like in real racing, across every bike and game mode.

Whether you choose a full sim approach with the Pro Experience or a more immediate challenge with the Arcade Experience, you’ll feel greater control through corners, smoother adjustments mid-race and more natural rider animations. Get ready to build a stronger connection with your bike and enjoy a more intuitive and rewarding racing experience.

Immersive

Career

Start your journey from rookie to legend with a custom rider or step into the boots of a real MotoGP™ star to rewrite history. Take the spotlight in press conferences, set your objectives for the season, create rivalries or friendships, deal with teams in contract negotiations, navigate the waters of the riders transfer market, and choose how to develop your bike to climb to the top.

Ultimate

Race Off

Take a break from the paddock and train like real riders do. Get the full picture of the MotoGP™ champion lifestyle by competing in Motard, Minibike, Flat Track events and with the new Production Bikes in brand-specific events, each with unique characteristics and a distinct feel. Race across three original locations, including the new Canterbury Park, alongside Borgo Caselle
and Mont Lagard.

Race Together

Ride online with full cross-play* and improved matchmaking. Line up on full-sized grids of up to 22 riders and challenge players from all over the world in smoother, clearer, and more accessible races.


* Not available on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2

Graphic Editors

Show your attitude with powerful graphic editors. Customise helmets, numbers, and liveries, and take full advantage of Cross-Platform UGC** by sharing your creations across platforms.


** Not available on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2
