Business Services Privacy Notice


Mobilewalla respects the privacy of each individual about whom we process information. This privacy notice describes our product and service offerings and the practices we follow.  Mobilewalla’s clients include advertisers, advertising agencies, brands, publishers and other users (“Clients”).  The Privacy Notice applies when our Clients provide business data to us for processing or when our Clients incorporate our Business Services as part of their website, mobile application or services (“Property”).  

Mobilewalla helps its Clients deliver relevant marketing and advertising campaigns, provide insights, manage and monitor the success of campaigns, identify and reduce fraud, power analytics and business models and provide consumers with advertisements that are more likely to be interesting and applicable to them (“Business Services”).  


Mobilewalla sources its data from other information providers that operate throughout the adtech ecosystem.   Mobilewalla has no direct interaction with individuals who use our Clients’ Properties.

Mobilewalla’s Clients are required to provide notice to individuals and to obtain the appropriate authorizations and lawful bases for Mobilewalla’s processing as set forth in this Privacy Policy and to provide a privacy policy of their own. Mobilewalla honors the IAB Europe’s Transparency and Consent Framework.  

For information about Mobilewalla’s Site and Website Services, please visit the Mobilewalla Website Privacy Policy here.

General

1.  Mobilewalla’s Business Services


We provide solutions to help our business Clients manage, use, and optimize their customer data and to help them identify and reach potential new customers using analytical tools and marketing and advertising strategy services. Stated differently, we design, build, and run processes and solutions for our business Clients to help them enhance and manage their data, and we provide the information to help our Clients better understand their customers and identify potential customers who are like their best customers. 

Our data helps our Clients implement marketing lifecycle improvements, new ways to connect with consumers, and mobile app and website experience optimization. When we provide data management services by processing data on behalf of our Clients, we process our Clients’ and their partners’ information according to contracts with our Clients. In that capacity, we are acting as a service provider, sometimes called a processor. 


For a company to use Mobilewalla’s products and services, it is required to enter into a contract with us.  The terms of those contracts govern the use of personal information Mobilewalla collects, processes, sells, shares and discloses to Clients. 

In addition to disclosures of information in the ordinary course of business, we may disclose personal information as necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to respond to law enforcement or a regulator, such as in response to a subpoena. 


Mobilewalla does not provide services or data to individual consumers and does not make our data available for purchase online. 

2.  Personal Information We Collect and Use


Mobilewalla collects data in order to provide the Business Services and for other specific purposes as described in this Privacy Policy. Mobilewalla’s Business Services contain only the information required for its intended use.  Mobilewalla’s Business Services are designed for a specific use by our Clients that have a legitimate need for the service. 

Some data may be considered personal data in some jurisdictions and sensitive personal data in other jurisdictions. Our Business Services are not directed to children under 16 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information on such users.  The Business Services contain information about individuals and households in the U.S. and are developed from many sources.

When interacting with the Property, providing the Business Services, or receiving data from other information providers (sometimes called “data brokers”) Mobilewalla may receive the following:

•    Device Information, such as device type and model, network provider and connection type, browser type, language, operating system, cookie identifiers, mobile carrier, mobile IDs, MAC address, IMEI, unique identifiers, hardware type, device specifications, operating system, IP address, location, Internet service provider, hashed email address, and other actions taken through the use of the Business Services.
•    Information about the Property, such as Property ID, app name, website, mobile website URL, app ID, and bundle ID.
•    Information Provided By Clients and Other Third Parties, such as demographics, age, interests, preferences, gender, zip or postal code, advertiser ID, and Device Information.

The Mobilewalla Business Services we license to Clients do not include specific details about a purchase, detailed financial information, credit information, or information identifying or revealing a person’s past, present, or future, physical or mental health condition.  

Collection and Use of Minor Data:  We ask our data suppliers not to provide any data to us that is associated with anyone younger than 16.  We do not knowingly collect, process, store or transfer any data to third parties that is related to anyone younger than 16. 

Collection and Use of Geo-Location Data:  Mobilewalla does process “movement data” that includes mobile advertising ID (MAID), latitude and longitude, IP Address, and timestamp. We do process information related to visits to commercial locations, such as restaurants, retail stores, and car dealerships (e.g., pseudonymous mobile advertising ID (MAID or device ID), latitude and longitude, timestamp and duration of visit).  We do not knowingly collect information related to visits to sensitive locations such as places of worship, domestic abuse shelters, drug and alcohol treatment centers or reproductive health clinics. 


Collection and Use of Deidentified Data:  Mobilewalla processes deidentified information (as that term is defined in applicable US statutes) that are used to build our marketing models.  Mobilewalla commits to maintain and use such information in its deidentified form and not attempt to reidentify the information.      

Collection and Use of Retail Purchase Data:  Mobilewalla does not collect individually identifiable information regarding retail purchases at a granular level (i.e., at a “SKU”- level).  Instead, Mobilewalla may processes identifiable retail purchase information at category levels (e.g., “health & beauty products,” “clothing,” “electronics”).    

Offshoring:  Where permitted by law, third parties under contract to Mobilewalla may have access to the information in Mobilewalla’s marketing and advertising data products to assist in processing the data. Some of these third-party service providers or contractors may reside outside the United States.

5. Cookies, Social Media, Analytics & Advertising


For the use of cookies and other Technologies on our Site and Website Services, please see our Website Services Privacy Policy. We, as well as our Clients and third parties that provide content, advertising, or other functionality as part of the Business Services, may use cookies, pixel tags, local storage, and other technologies (“Technologies”) to automatically collect information from your devices. We use Technologies that are essentially small data files placed on your computer, tablet, mobile phone, or other devices (referred to collectively as a "device") that allow us to record certain pieces of information whenever you visit or interact with Properties, websites, applications, messaging, and tools, and to recognize you across devices.


Cookies. Cookies are small text files placed in visitors’ computer browsers to store their preferences. Most browsers allow you to block and delete cookies. However, if you do that, the websites, applications and Business Services may not work properly.


Pixel Tags/Web Beacons. A pixel tag (also known as a web beacon) is a piece of code embedded on websites, applications, services and advertisements that collects information about users’ engagement and activities. The use of a pixel indicates, for example, that a user has visited a particular web page or clicked on a particular advertisement.


Social Media Integration. Our Clients’ Properties and other third party sites may include social media features. These features may collect your IP address, which page you are visiting on our site, and may set a cookie to enable the feature to function properly. These social media features are either hosted by a third party or hosted directly on the Property. Your interactions with these features are governed by the privacy policy of the company providing it.


Analytics. Our Clients and other third parties may also use Google Analytics to collect information regarding visitor behavior and visitor demographics. For more information, please visit www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/ . You can opt out of Google’s collection and processing of data generated by your use of the Services by going to http://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.


Uses of such Technologies fall into the following general categories:
•    Operationally Necessary. Cookies, web beacons, or other similar technologies that are necessary to the operation of the Business Services, Properties, websites, applications, and tools. This includes Technologies that are required to identify irregular site behavior, prevent fraudulent activity and improve security or that allow you to make use of our functions such as shopping-carts, saved search, or similar functions;
•    Performance Related. Cookies, web beacons, or other similar technologies are used to assess the performance of Business Services, Properties, websites, applications, and tools, including as part of standard analytic and measurement practices to help understand how visitors use the Business Services, Properties, websites, and applications and interact with messaging, or view an item or link;
•    Functionality Related. Cookies, web beacons, or other similar Technologies that offer enhanced functionality. This may include identifying you when you sign into a Property, website, application or Business Service or keeping track of your specified preferences, interests, or past items viewed so that we, our Clients and third parties may enhance the Business Services, Properties, websites, applications, and tools;
•    Advertising or Targeting Related. First-party and third-party cookies and web beacons deliver content, including ads relevant to your interests, through our Business Services, Properties or on third party websites and applications. This includes using Technologies to understand the usefulness to you of the advertisements and content that has been delivered to you, such as whether you have clicked on an advertisement.


If you would like to opt out of the Technologies, you may do so by blocking, deleting, or disabling them as your browser or device permits. If you wish to delete cookies or limit the kinds of cookies that can be placed on your device you need to check out the settings in your browser, use the ‘Help’ tab or look for settings like ‘Options’ or ‘Privacy.’ From there, you may be able to delete cookies, block them or control when they can be placed.


We may use third party APIs and software development kits (“SDKs”) as part of the functionality of our Business Services. APIs and third party SDKs may allow third parties including advertisers and Clients to collect your personal information to provide relevant content, products and services.

8.  Other Privacy Information


Transfer of Data Outside Your Territory: Where business needs exist, Mobilewalla may process, store and transfer your personal data to entities outside the US and EU/EEA.  However, your personal data will not be transferred unless a valid transfer mechanism is in place legitimizing such a transfer.  In the case of transfers referred to in Article 46, 47, or the second paragraph of Article 49(1), this will typically involve EU model clauses (a.k.a standard contractual clauses) or the EU-US Privacy Shield Framework.  Safeguards afforded by the EU model clauses may be accessed here EU Model Clauses.  We exclusively use global, top tier cloud-based service providers.


European Union Model Contracts: As a global company, Mobilewalla complies with privacy laws of many countries. Mobilewalla has executed EU and UK approved standard contractual clauses with its EU and UK Clients and partners to ensure ongoing compliance with the international transfer principle as set out in the EU and UK’s General Data Protection Regulation. 


Accuracy: Mobilewalla uses best efforts to ensure the information we compile, and process is as accurate and complete as possible. Mobilewalla responds promptly to questions from Clients and consumers about the accuracy of information. 


Data Retention:  Mobilewalla collects all data from third-party sources. These third-party sources may provide the following data elements: Location Data, Hashed Email Addresses, Internet Protocol (IP) Addresses, Mobile Advertising Device IDs (MAIDs, IDFAs) and various socio-economic data like age and gender.

We use these data elements in various combinations to produce a variety of products that we make available to our clients and business partners.   These products include audience segments, data extracts and analytics/reporting.

Mobilewalla deletes the data it receives from third-party sources within ninety (90) days of receipt. The products that we produce using this data are retained in an archive status for no longer than three (3) years. We delete products that have reached their three (3) year retention limit from our data archive on a rolling daily basis.

Security: Mobilewalla maintains security procedures designed to keep information we own, license and process from being accessed by any unauthorized person or business. We use a variety of multi-level security systems to control access to our services and data products. All users at client locations, as well as all Mobilewalla associates, must have the appropriate access credentials and be authorized to access certain data and applications. 


Mobilewalla conducts risk assessments and regular audits on our internal and external information systems to assess our ability to maintain the integrity of client and Mobilewalla data. Our enterprise security operations center maintains real-time monitoring for information system vulnerabilities and unauthorized access attempts into our internal systems. We also maintain electronic access and physical security for our facilities.  


We take reasonable precautions to ensure that our security procedures are adequate for the protection of our computer systems and data, but this does not eliminate the possibility that our security will be breached. If a security breach causes Mobilewalla leadership to believe individual consumers are at actual risk for identity theft or other fraudulent activities that may cause substantial harm or inconvenience to consumers or if such breach otherwise requires public notification, Mobilewalla will post a security alert notice on our website (www.mobilewalla.com) that will provide relevant information for consumers and Mobilewalla Clients to consider to protect against fraudulent and other harmful actions that may result from the unauthorized access. 


Compliance: Mobilewalla participates in industry efforts to establish fair and workable guidelines above and beyond current laws and regulations and believes such actions are an effective way to protect privacy in the marketplace. We also support legislation and regulatory efforts to introduce fair and workable guidelines that protect privacy of consumers. 


We actively work to ensure that such guidelines are consistent with, and complement established self-regulatory measures and that they enable the consumer to continue receiving the benefits that appropriate information use provide. 


When Mobilewalla provides Business Services to our Clients, we process their information in strict accordance with the terms and conditions of the contract. Mobilewalla works closely with our Clients to ensure the processing we perform is in accordance with all the laws governing those activities. 
Awareness: Mobilewalla is committed to privacy education. We provide education to our associates about the issues, guidelines and laws surrounding individual consumer privacy issues, corresponding responsibilities, responsible data collection and use and Mobilewalla’s privacy policies and practices.
Privacy Policy Changes: From time to time Mobilewalla may update and revise our privacy policy based on changes in our business environment and changes to applicable law. We urge consumers and Clients to periodically visit our website to understand any changes that may have occurred.
