Indonesia Travel Guide

Simply one of the most Fascinating & Diverse Travel Destinations in the World

It is impossible to introduce all the wonderful places that are worth a visit in Indonesia. The world’s largest archipelago with 17,000 islands spans almost 2mio km2, extending 5,120 kilometres (3,181 mi) from east to west and 1,760 kilometres (1,094 mi) from north to south. It’s much larger than most people would imagine! But we have handpicked some of the most stunning destinations for you to explore.
