Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this image converter tool completely free?

Yes, all our image tools are 100% free to use. You can convert, compress, and edit images without any registration or hidden charges.

2. Do you store uploaded images on your server?

No, we respect your privacy. All files are processed securely in your browser and are not stored on our servers.

3. Which image formats are supported?

Our tools support popular formats such as JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, and PDF conversions.

4. Will image quality be reduced after conversion?

Our converters are optimized to maintain high quality. However, file size reduction may slightly affect quality in some formats like JPG.

5. Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes, many of our tools allow batch conversion and merging multiple images into a single PDF file.

6. Do I need to install any software?

No installation is required. All tools work directly in your web browser on desktop and mobile devices.
