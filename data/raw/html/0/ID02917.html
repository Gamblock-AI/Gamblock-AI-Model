     Maple Grove Cycling Price Match Promise*

Buy with confidence knowing that our buying power provides a great selection of products at very competitive prices. We will price match any local competitors price at the time of purchase if that price is less than our store price. If you see one of our products offered for less, after your purchase and before 10 days, we will provide a store credit or an MGC gift card on the difference. 

*Excludes mail order, outstate, internet, close-out, one-of-a-kind, damaged, and clearance items.
