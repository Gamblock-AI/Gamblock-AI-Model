Mars Snacking

Inspire Moments of Everyday Happiness

When you create snacks that inspire happiness, there’s pride in knowing they help make everyday moments feel a little brighter. 

A snack shared with a friend, a pause in a busy day, or a simple smile sparked by something delicious—these small moments add up to something meaningful.

Today, our iconic snacks like M&M’S®, PRINGLES®, SNICKERS®, CHEEZ-IT®, EXTRA®, Skittles®, POP-TARTS® and KIND® are enjoyed in more than 180 countries.

Behind every product is a team of passionate Associates working to make each snack even more enjoyable—from improving how we source ingredients and sharing our products responsibly, to investing in the places we call home. We’re committed to protecting the planet we depend on and giving back to the communities where those joyful moments begin.

Snacks may be small, but they carry meaning. They can spark connections, create memories, offer comfort, and add a touch of pleasure to the day. From a quiet moment of indulgence to a lively celebration, our products help shape brighter days—one delicious bite at a time.

Because when a snack inspires happiness, it’s never just a snack—it’s part of someone’s day, someone’s story, and sometimes, someone’s smile.

AMERICAN HERITAGE

The AMERICAN HERITAGE® Chocolate brand was developed in 2006 by Mars Chocolate North America (now Mars Wrigley) to help educate consumers about the history of our multicultural nation through the engaging story of one of our most beloved foods…chocolate! With a flavor profile drawn directly from the archives of chocolate's historic past, AMERICAN HERITAGE Chocolate provides an artisanal, versatile way to eat, bake or drink a sweet taste of history. Rainforest Alliance Certified™ and made with no preservatives, AMERICAN HERITAGE Chocolate currently comes in four NEW unique formats: Tasting Squares, Tablet Bars, Grated Chocolate for baking/drinking, and Gourmet Hot Cocoa. AMERICAN HERITAGE Chocolate is sold in over 150 fine gift shops at historic sites, museums and historic inns across the U.S. and Canada. The brand can also be found on Amazon.com, at M&M's World in New York City and Las Vegas, and at the Ethel M chocolate store in Henderson, Nevada.
