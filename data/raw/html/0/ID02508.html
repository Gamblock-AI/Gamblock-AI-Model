Knowing More About Kinobi

What is Kinobi?

Kinobi is a student success SaaS system provider for higher education institutions looking to move beyond traditional academic-focused education and embrace a more comprehensive approach to holistic education. Our system helps universities prepare students to be job-ready by simplifying non-academic activities, such as career and internships, mentoring, student life, and more, to empower student success.

What makes Kinobi different?

Kinobi offers a highly customisable and tailored solution designed to meet your institution’s unique needs. Our product seamlessly integrates with your existing systems, ensuring a smooth transition and enhanced functionality. Moreover, we deliver exceptional value with competitive pricing that maximises impact without compromising quality.

Why choose Kinobi to support your institution?

We proudly support over 60 global clients, including prestigious universities, government bodies, and educational institutions, with our dedicated account manager consistently available to deliver personalised service whenever required.

How does Kinobi ensure data security and privacy?

Kinobi takes data security and privacy very seriously. We are proud to be certified under ISO 27001, the international standard for information security management, ensuring that we maintain the highest level of data protection. Additionally, our services are hosted on AWS, which provides industry-leading data security measures, including encryption, access controls, and regular audits. These certifications and practices demonstrate our commitment to safeguarding your institution’s sensitive information at all times.
