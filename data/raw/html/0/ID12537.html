Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Lupon Media (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides technology and services that enable website owners (“Publishers”) to monetise their content through digital advertising.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information through:

  • our websites (e.g.,luponmedia.com,adxpremium.services)
  • our advertising technology and analytics services made available to Publishers (“Lupon Media Services”)

Our goal is to provide simple and transparent information about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you visit a Publisher website using Lupon Media Services, or when you interact with our own websites:

1.1 Technical Identifiers

  • Cookie identifiers
  • IP address (used only for coarse location such as country/city)
  • Browser type, user-agent, operating system
  • Device type
  • Ad request data (e.g., ad unit, bid request info)

1.2 Advertising Identifiers

  • Mobile advertising IDs (IDFA/AdID)
  • Other pseudonymous identifiers created by our systems or by identity partners (e.g., Universal IDs)

1.3 Publisher Information

If you are a Publisher using Lupon Media Services, we may collect:

  • contact information (name, email, phone)
  • account login details
  • payment information (managed by a third-party payment provider)
  • information about your websites and ad setup

1.4 Information You Provide to Us

When you contact us, subscribe to a newsletter, or request support, we collect the information you choose to provide (e.g., name, email, message content).

Important Note

We do not collect information that directly identifies Readers, such as names, emails, phone numbers or addresses, unless voluntarily submitted on our own websites.

2. How We Collect Information

We use commonly-used technologies:

  • cookies
  • pixels
  • scripts
  • SDKs (mobile)

These allow us to:

  • understand advertising performance
  • detect invalid traffic
  • measure viewability
  • prevent fraud
  • run frequency capping
  • deliver more relevant ads depending on user context

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate business purposes:

3.1 Advertising Operations

  • delivering and measuring ads
  • selecting ads based on context, device type, browser, or general location
  • analytical reporting for Publishers
  • improving ad relevance (within the framework of IAB TCF consent, where required)

3.2 Fraud Prevention & Security

  • detecting invalid traffic (IVT)
  • preventing misuse of our systems
  • protecting our partners and advertisers

3.3 Service Functionality & Improvements

  • providing Publisher account functionality
  • improving our technology
  • analysing service performance and reliability

3.4 Legal Compliance

  • responding to lawful requests
  • complying with regulatory requirements

4. How We Share Information

We may share pseudonymous information with:

4.1 Publishers

To enable measurement, reporting, and fraud prevention.

4.2 Advertising Partners

Including demand-side platforms (DSPs), ad exchanges, and analytics vendors that participate in the digital advertising supply chain.

4.3 Service Providers

Such as data centres, analytics services, payment processors, customer support platforms.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights of Lupon Media, Publishers, or third parties.

We do NOT share or sell personal information that directly identifies individuals (such as names, emails, phone numbers, or physical addresses).

5. Cookies & Choice

Readers can manage cookies through browser settings.
For interest-based advertising, Readers may use industry tools such as:

  • https://www.aboutads.info/
  • https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/
  • Mobile AppChoices App (DAA)

If cookies are disabled or cleared, the opt-out may need to be re-applied.

6. Data Subject Rights (EU/UK Residents)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • access your data
  • request deletion
  • request correction
  • object to processing
  • withdraw consent when consent is used
  • request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at:
 info@luponmedia.com

We may ask for additional technical identifiers (e.g., cookie ID) to process your request.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK)

We process data based on:

  • Consent— for personalised advertising where required (via IAB TCF CMP).
  • Legitimate Interest— for fraud detection, security, ad measurement, and ensuring service functionality.
  • Contract— for providing services to Publishers.
  • Legal Obligation— for regulatory or law enforcement requirements.

8. International Data Transfers

We operate globally.Data may be transferred to countries outside the EEA/UK (e.g., UAE, US) with appropriate safeguards in place, such as contractual data protection clauses.

9. Data Retention

  • Advertising identifiers: typicallyup to 18 months
  • Publisher account data: retained for duration of contract + legal retention periods
  • Support / contact data: as required to fulfil the request and for compliance
  • Non-personal and aggregated data may be retained longer

10. Children’s Privacy

Lupon Media Services arenot directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information processed through our systems.
