FAQ

Why Onescreener?
Because it’s a one-page website that gives influencers, content creators, musicians, artists and brands a solution to power their online marketing and to directly sell products and services. A page can be created, customized and published very easily within minutes, and shared anywhere online, specifically as a link-in-bio on Instagram and other social media platforms.
read more
What is the difference between Free and Paid plans?
Onescreener’s Free plan comes with all the main features like adding multiple Teaser links and Shop links (10% payment processing fee), and allows you to create and publish your page using a subdomain (e.g. onescreener.com/yourname). The Pro plan unlocks more design options like custom colouring and backgrounds, and includes a reduced payment processing fee of 7% on Shop links. The Premium plan ($7/month) gives you everything PLUS the option to connect your own domain (e.g. yourname.com), AND a low 5% payment processing fee on Shop links.
read more
Why do I need a bio link or link-in-bio?
Many social media platforms allow users to add only one external link to the bio part of their profile; this is mainly to keep users on that platform rather than exiting to other sites. Use an optimized link-in-bio like Onescreener because it turns that one link into MANY links, and to turn the link into a money-maker when utilizing Onescreener's Shop feature.
read more
How does my Onescreener get noticed?
The visibility of your page is optimized as you add your content and share your page's URL. When a visitor wants to access your media players, social media, Teaser and Shop links, contact information or promotional offers, for example, they do so via your Onescreener page, increasing its organic traffic and interactions thus making it rank it higher in searches.
read more
How do I get my own domain?
When you’ve created and published your Onescreener, you'll be automatically given a subdomain for your page (e.g. onescreener.com/yourname). Should you want to use your own domain name, simply click on the “Choose your own domain” button and follow the instructions to register it within the Premium plan.
read more
I'm not a designer. Can I make a Onescreener page?
YES! Onescreener has been built with YOU in mind. Creating a page is as simple as uploading a background image, adding the icons for your socials, choosing which main content you want to showcase (try a Shop link to sell stuff!), adding your name or logo and then sharing your page!
read more
What is the difference between a Onescreener page and a website?
A Onescreener page links your most important news and activities in just one click, keeping your audience in-the-know. It's very affordable (a Free plan, even!), easy to create, and is meant to be updated anytime on the go (using your phone!) with your latest exciting content. As the best link-in-bio, it works together with your website to help drive traffic to your business or brand.
