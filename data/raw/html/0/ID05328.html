On-Demand Loading

Assume you have a 128-picture slideshow. If you use simple HTML code for all of your images, the website will load and run much slower. For this case jQuery Image Slider has the "On-Demand Loading" option also known as "lazy loading" that loads only images that enter or are about to enter the viewport. For extensive image galleries — consider the challenge presented by a slideshow containing 128 pictures — WOW Slider introduces an intelligent solution in 'On-Demand Loading.' Tailored for optimization, this feature initiates loading of images only as they approach visibility within the viewer's browser window. Dubbed 'lazy loading,' this approach significantly trims initial page load times and enhances overall performance by reducing unnecessary data transfer. Consequently, users enjoy swift access to content without bogging down their browsing experience under the weight of preloading an entire gallery's worth of assets.
