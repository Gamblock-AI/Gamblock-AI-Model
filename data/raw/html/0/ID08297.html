About Us

Chennai Paints, a family owned company is dedicated to providing quality service to its customers. Established in the year 2007 the company has made a prominent impact in the paints and construction chemicals sector, capitalising on nearly 6 decade of experience from their senior partners.

We have highly dedicated and experienced technicians with an innate urge to excel. We have made a big inroad in the “paints and construction chemicals” industry and have the best clientele list in our fold.

With our rich experience, committed personnel and your enduring support, we are confident of carving out a niche to catapult Chennai Paints to new heights.

In the scenario of rapid progress of technical advancement, Chennai Paints is well determined to put utmost efforts to continue to satisfy our customers with the products of the highest quality level at competitive prices.

Why Choose Us

Quality Paints at Competitive Prices

The corporate purpose of Chennai Paints is to develop, manufacture and market “Paints & Construction Chemicals” products for domestic and industrial use and exceed its customer’s expectations in terms of quality, service, value and safety through constant innovation. Always delight customers by providing them with quality products We at Chennai Paints supply outstanding quality products combined with optimal environmental friendliness and belive that the present environment issue can be soarted out by minimising and to conserve our resources which will secure the future development through sustainable and profitable growth.

All the products manufactured by Chennai Paints are guaranteed free from any added lead, mercury, arsenic and chromium

Trusted Expertise

We offer reliable and productive partnership with our, dealers, suppliers and employees through market driven behavior.

Instill a "CAN BE DONE” attitude, nurture team spirit, learn and strive to improve continuously to achive a high level of satisfaction with employee.

Our first preferences is to protect and be committed towards the safety of our products, operations and environment

Heritage and Quality

We always provide products that meet stated standards and constantly endeavour to provide timly delivery of quality products on time, every time. We strive constantly to improve our processes to understand the ever changing customers needs, demand and choices as a valuable input for reviewing our performance standards of all our products.

All our products are manufactured at the facility in chennai, South India each stage being controlled by tightly defined parameters and are commited to continual improvement in quality in all aspect of our business processes.

Building Success Together

We are always ready to serve our customers with assistance, when required by them to enable them to derive the expected benefits form our products.

Our name is synonymous with best quality products, competitive prices and excellent sales services. This policy has helped us to develop enduring relationship with our custmores.

Keeping customers in our fold continuously right from our inception through our dedicated hard work, committed quality and service to our customers, we are known for our innate capability for the quality of our products and delivering the goods as per your specific requirement on regular basis without any delay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products eco-friendly?

Chennai Paints takes environmental responsibility seriously. We offer eco-friendly paint options that minimize environmental impact while maintaining vibrant colors and high quality.

How can I find out more about your products and services?

Visit our website or contact us directly for a consultation. We're happy to discuss your specific needs and recommend the perfect solution for your project.

What type of Paints and Construction Chemicals do you offer and what makes you different?

We offers a variety of paints & construction chemicals, including wall paints, primers, putty, ready-mix wall plaster, block joining mortar, & tile adhesives. We prioritize customer satisfaction with high-quality, affordable products & exceptional service for lasting results.

Do you provide technical support?

Our team of experienced technicians is happy to answer your questions and guide you in choosing the right paint and application methods for your project.
