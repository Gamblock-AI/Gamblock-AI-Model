FAQ:
What’s the difference between Fall Rec & Competitive?

Rec:
This is our “in house” league for players of all abilities ages 3-15. All games are played on Saturdays at Gingerella Field with 1 practice per week. Fall season will have 8 weeks of games. No player assessments required. Teams are formed based on age.

Competitive:
This is our “traveling” league for players U8-U15 who are ready for and desire a higher level of competition and play. Our teams play in “Superliga” which is a statewide league that plays with the rules of USA Youth Soccer. This league has 2 practices per week and 1 weekend game. They play a total of 8 games: 4 home, 4 away with statewide travel required. Player assessments will be held for team placement. Teams formed based on birth year and ability.

Our goal for all players to have the opportunity to play soccer at a level and commitment that fits them best. And most of all to HAVE FUN!
