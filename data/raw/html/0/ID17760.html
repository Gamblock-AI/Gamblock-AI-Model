The Hour Of Joy - Demo

A downloadable game for Windows

PLEASE NOTE: This project is FAN MADE and not affiliated with Mob Entertainment.

Try the first few minutes of "The Hour Of Joy" fangame!

Play as a Playtime Co. employee and attempt to find an exit to the factory, all while the experiments try to kill you and everyone around you!

BTW after the intro, the game kinda takes awhile to load so BE PATIENT!

Also it's just a demo, expect some bugs .______.

Controls:

G - Kick

C - Slide

Space - Mantle

At some point I may update the demo to include the second floor of the offices, as the first floor is meant to be a tutorial section.
