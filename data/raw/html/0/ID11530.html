The Kayana

The Kayana’s boutique villas offer the ultimate in privacy and tranquility. Located in Bali’s top-rated Seminyak and Lombok’s Kecinan districts, every villa features its own entrance and private pool. Spacious outdoor living and dining areas combined with lush natural surroundings, lavish interiors and elegant contemporary décor create a sense of luxury and harmony with nature. The Kayana boutique villas are discreetly positioned along a winding pathway, flanked by frangipani trees and tropical foliage.
