5. Weighting test in cases in which we use legitimate interest

According to the above, the data processing analyzed based on legitimate interest could be considered lawful, provided that it is carried out in a proportionate manner for the purpose pursued.

According to its jurisprudential interpretation, the proportionality analysis is composed of three elements:

  • suitability judgment: Whether the measure is likely to achieve the proposed objective;
  • Judgment of necessity: If the measure is necessary, in the sense that there is no other more moderate measure for the achievement of such purpose with equal effectiveness;
  • Proportionality judgment in sense: If it is weighted or balanced, because it derives more benefits or advantages for the general interest than harm to other goods or values in conflict.

For this reason, proportionality is analyzed below for each of the processing operations based on legitimate interest:
