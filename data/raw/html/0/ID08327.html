Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, and process.

How long does it take to get my resume done?

We typically deliver ATS-optimized resumes within 72 hours. For urgent requests, we offer expedited services. The timeline depends on the complexity of your background and the level of customization needed. Once you place your order, we'll confirm the exact delivery date.

Are your resumes really ATS-compliant?

Yes, absolutely. Every resume we create is engineered specifically to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). We use industry-standard formatting, strategic keyword placement, and proven optimization techniques that have helped thousands of professionals get past automated screening systems. Our ATS compliance is verified and tested.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes, revisions are included. We offer unlimited revisions to ensure your resume perfectly reflects your experience and meets your expectations. Your satisfaction is our priority, and we'll work with you until the document is exactly what you need.

What countries do you serve?

We serve professionals globally! Our expertise spans over 42 countries including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Pakistan, UK, Canada, Australia, and many more. We tailor resumes to meet regional hiring standards, recruiter expectations, and ATS systems specific to each country. Whether you're applying locally or internationally, we've got you covered.

Can you handle my specific industry?

Absolutely! We have expertise across 50+ industries including technology, finance, healthcare, consulting, engineering, marketing, sales, and many more. Our team understands industry-specific keywords, formatting preferences, and what recruiters look for in your field. We craft resumes that resonate with your industry's standards.

Is my information confidential?

Yes, your privacy and confidentiality are paramount. All information you share is handled with utmost discretion and protected under strict confidentiality agreements. We never share your personal details or career information with third parties without your explicit consent.
