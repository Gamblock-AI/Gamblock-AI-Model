Biofoam Packaging & Compostability

Tempo is proud to now offer Biofoam packaging solutions a significant step forward in sustainable protective packaging.

Biofoam is a plant-based foam material that offers protective performance while supporting environmentally responsible disposal options, including composting where facilities exist.

 

With the introduction of Biofoam, Tempo expands its commitment to innovative packaging solutions that balance performance, cost efficiency, and environmental responsibility.
