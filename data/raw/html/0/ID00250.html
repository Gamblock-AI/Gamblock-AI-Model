Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Alta Global School (AGS)?

AGS is a blended-learning school for Pre-K to Grade 12, designed to prepare students for scholarships to top Indonesian and international universities.

Is AGS online or offline? Locations?

AGS provides flexible learning options: online, hybrid, or fully offline. Our online programs cater to students nationwide, while physical locations include Jakarta (MSIG Tower & Mall Taman Anggrek), Tangerang (Citymall), Bandung (BAC Buah Batu), and Karawang (Rasinda Mall). Students can choose the best option based on their needs and proximity.

How do I enroll in AGS?

Enrollment begins with a free consultation, then Students undergo academic assessments and interviews to ensure the program aligns with their goals. A detailed proposal, including costs, is provided afterward.

Is AGS accredited?

AGS is a certified Learning Center under Indonesia’s PKBM (Community Learning Center) framework, ensuring students gain proper academic credentials and can pursue their studies at top schools or universities.

What curriculum does AGS use?

AGS integrates the national curriculum for formal certifications and adopting international standards, including Cambridge Primary (Grades K-6), IGCSE (Grades 7-10), and A/AS Level (Grades 11-12). This approach ensures academic excellence and global recognition.

How does AGS ensure social interaction for online students?

AGS emphasizes building social connections through regular meet-ups, collaborative projects, and a student council. Activities like Bootcamps and community events ensure students develop social and leadership skills while forming lasting friendships.

How is AGS different from Schoters?

Unlike Schoters, which focuses on short-term preparation programs, AGS offers a comprehensive academic curriculum with year-round studies and custom pathways for students. At AGS, students gain both academic excellence and social-emotional growth, while Schoters primarily helps students prepare for specific milestones like university admission tests or scholarship applications.
