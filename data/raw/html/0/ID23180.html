DooPlay is created to facilitate the work of creating content with requires little effort, uses powerful tools that provide data, texts and images from very reliable sources, also gives you all the options to customize your website without touching lines of code.

Dooplay 2.5.5 has a persistent cache system, which allows you to minimize queries to the database, it is the fastest and most stable version we have achieved, it also has new and improved options that will give you absolute control of your website.

Dbmovies our import tool, has also had very important changes, providing improved access to a large volume of content in different languages.
