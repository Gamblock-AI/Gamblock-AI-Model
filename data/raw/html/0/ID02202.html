What Cookies Do We Use

We use 3 different kinds of cookies: technical, analytics and profiling cookies.

Essential cookies are essential for the proper functioning of our website, they are used to provide you the requested services, and enable key features like session management, and user preferences.

Analytics cookies are used to measure website traffic and performance by collecting aggregate data on the number of users and their interactions with our website (e.g., how long you stay on the website).

Profiling cookies are used to track users actions and behaviors to create profiles and deliver personalized services and targeted advertising.
