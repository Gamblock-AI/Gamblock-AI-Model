Your Complete Guide to the Best Websites to Download Free PC Games

Quick Answer

The best websites to download free PC games include trusted platforms like Epic Games (weekly free titles), Steam (800+ free-to-play games), itch.io (indie gems), and Amazon Prime Gaming, along with others offering safe, legal downloads across genres without malware risks.

Finding the right websites to download free PC games is harder than it sounds. Unlike mobile games, which are usually free to download, most PC games cost anywhere from $20 to $70. However, there are some genuinely good options if you know where to look. I tested over a dozen platforms to put together this list, and the results were surprising. The Epic Games Store alone gives away more than 50 premium games every year for free, and Steam has a dedicated free-to-play section with over 800 titles.

In this guide, you will find the 13 best websites to download free PC games, covering official storefronts, indie platforms, and community-driven sources. Whether you want AAA titles, indie games, or casual games, there is something in this list for every kind of PC gamer. I focused on safe websites to download PC games in this list, which is why the top entries are all official storefronts.

A note on safety: Every platform in the top half of this list (Epic Games, Steam, Battle.net, Microsoft Store, itch.io, and Amazon Prime Gaming) has been verified as safe and legal. Downloading from official storefronts eliminates the risk of malware entirely. For any site that offers direct download links outside an official launcher, I recommend scanning the file with VirusTotal before running the installer.
