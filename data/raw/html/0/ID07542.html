  • Advocacy
    • Campaigns

    Respect public dentistry

    Victorian public sector dentists are paid on average 20-30% less than their counterparts interstate. The Victorian Government is yet to make a formal offer to improve the pay and conditions of Australia’s lowest paid public sector dentists.

    EBA negotiations

    The ADAVB advocates for equitable employment conditions for public sector dentists, including through enterprise bargaining agreements.

      Private health insurance

      The ADA believes that those with extras cover should all receive the same dental rebates regardless which dentist they see.

      Public oral health waiting times and workforce capacity

      We highlight the urgent need for increased investment in the Victorian public dental sector. Despite oral healthcare being an essential component of overall health, affordable services are becoming increasingly inaccessible, with marginalised communities being the most significantly impacted.

Speakers

Dr Robert Mayne (Orthodontist)
Dr Mayne completed his undergraduate and postgraduate training in orthodontics at the University of Melbourne and afterwards gained his membership by examination in the Royal Australasian College of Dental Surgeons and fellowship of the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh. He received the Elsdon Storey Special Merit Award for his orthodontic research as a postgraduate student and is certified with the Australasian Orthodontic Board.
Previously a Senior lecturer at LaTrobe University in Bendigo, he continues to contribute to teaching at LaTrobe University and the University of Melbourne. He is an active member of the ASO, and enjoys private specialist practice in Shepparton.

Dr Anita Lim (Oral Medicine Specialist)
Dr Lim grew up in Hobart and completed her Dentistry degree at La Trobe University, Bendigo, in 2013. Anita served in the Royal Australian Air Force for 10 years in dental, officer training and senior health administration roles. She completed her Doctor of Clinical Dentistry (Oral Medicine) at The University of Melbourne and registered with The Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) in the specialty field of Oral Medicine in 2024.
