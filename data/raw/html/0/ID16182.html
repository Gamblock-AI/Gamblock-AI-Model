Kumoo(formerly Meitu Yunxiu) is an AI photo editing tool designed for batch portrait retouching and background processing, available on both web and Desktop platforms. Utilizing advanced AI technology, it allows you to automatically batch process images, improving efficiency and quality. This application is ideal for scenarios requiring fast processing of large volumes of images, especially for commercial purposes such as product displays in online stores.
