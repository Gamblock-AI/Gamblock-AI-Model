PRIVACY POLICY - Maxetise AB


  1. ABOUT US


    Maxetise AB, registration number 559203-1982, known as "Maxetise", "our", "us", and "we", facilitates connections between advertisers, referred to as "Buyer(s)", and publishers, referred to as "Client(s)", through the use of "header bidding" technology on our independent platform dedicated to publishers ("Platform"). This platform aids our Clients in monetizing their digital spaces, allowing them to continue providing content to users ("you", "your").


    Our Platform serves as a conduit between Buyers and you, the user, navigating the complexities of accessing personal data for Buyers via our Client's services.


  2. IAB EUROPE TRANSPARENCY & CONSENT FRAMEWORK


    Maxetise is an participant in the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework, adhering to its established Specifications and Policies. Our identifier within this framework is 1291.

  3. SCOPE OF OUR PRIVACY POLICY


    Maxetise primarily serves as a data processor for the Platform, managing data on behalf of our Clients. Additionally, we handle certain personal data in our role as a data controller. This Privacy Policy addresses our data handling activities both as a data controller (applicable where you are a Client representative or utilize our Platform services) referenced in section 6, and as a data processor noted in section 5.


    This document outlines the types of personal data Maxetise or our Clients might process, the purposes of such processing, and the choices and rights available to you. Please review this Privacy Policy thoroughly to understand its provisions.


    For any inquiries or concerns about our privacy practices, please reach out to us at privacy@maxetise.com, or you may contact the relevant Client directly.


  4. DATA COLLECTION METHODS


    Data Collection Overview


    Maxetise employs various technologies such as cookies, tags, and mobile SDKs to gather personal data and store it on your device. A Software Development Kit (SDK) is a set of tools used by our Clients to integrate our Platform within their mobile applications (iOS and Android).


    Cookies are small text files consisting of a sequence of characters, saved onto your device. They enable us to identify and remember your browser.


    A Maxetise tag is a compact snippet of code embedded in a Client’s website source code, providing them access to and control over our Platform.


    Cookie Opt-Out and Withdrawal of Consent


    To maintain transparency, Maxetise offers a cookie opt-out policy that enables any Internet user to discontinue use of our Platform, ensuring your browser remains unidentified by our systems. For effective opt-out, a single cookie will remain on your device to confirm and sustain your opt-out choice. This option persists solely on the device where it was set up and remains active as long as the opt-out cookie is not deleted by you or your browser.


    To opt-out, click here. A confirmation page will display, verifying that the opt-out process is complete, and no further cookies will be placed on your computer.


    You may also prevent the placement of cookies by disabling the cookies function in your browser settings. Blocking cookies might impair certain functionalities on websites you visit.


    As a representative of our Client when accessing the Platform, we save certain details in cookies on your computer for purposes such as login, session management, and preference settings.


    Should you choose to reject the use of our cookies, you must deactivate cookies through your browser settings. Without cookies, some aspects of the Platform may not work as designed, possibly preventing login and other interactive functions.
