ABOUT NEPAL POLYTECHNIC INSTITUTE (NPI)

LEARN SOMETHING NEW, AND GROW YOUR SKILLS

Nepal Polytechnic Institute (NPI) is the one of the leading Technical Colleges of Nepal that aims to produce skilled human resources who lead in agriculture, engineering, medical and veterinary sciences throughout the nation and the abroad. Since its established time, NPI has been moving forward to sharpen its graduates not only handling modern technology but also with creative ideas for problem solving, critical thinking, comprehensive design, deliver internationally adaptable skills.
Affiliated to Purbanchal University (PU) and Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT), NPI offers Pre-Diploma to Bachelor Level with a highly qualified and experienced faculty team. Our primary aim is to enhance the overall development of the learners who can cope the global world with their knowledge and skill.

NPI Offers

AN INTERNATIONAL APPROACH

Since the time of its establishment, NPI has got a good reputation in providing quality technical education and vocational training. Besides regular academic programs it has completed varieties of employment oriented short-term training courses in partnership and collaboration with renowned national and international organizations.

By the time, many international organizationssuch as UNDP, ILO, SDC, Helvetas, ADB, Alliance etc. have been working with us in partnership for providing vocational trainings for unemployed youths from underprivileged societies of Nepal.

NPI has also done so many international tieup with different universities from UK, Australia & India. Recently the chairman, vicechairman & managing director of this institution have visited Queensland University, Australia, Hamro College, Australia and RGIT, Australia for collaborating in faculties and students sharing and exchanging.

ABOUT NEPAL POLYTECHNIC INSTITUTE

NPI is situated in Bharatpur, Chitwan, known as the medical city and proposed capital of Nepal. It serves as a central hub for students from Mechi to Mahakali. Nearby attractions include the Narayani River, Devghat Dham, Sauraha in Chitwan National Park, Bikram Baba Mandir, and Manakamana, drawing thousands of tourists annually.
