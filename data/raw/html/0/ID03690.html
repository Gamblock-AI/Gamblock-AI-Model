 Pilates Power is a boutique studio with 10 Reformer beds, situated at the back corner of Tunstall Square in Doncaster East, Melbourne. Our studio is just around the corner from cafes, shops and restaurants. There is ample parking conveniently located behind the studio so you will always have peace of mind arriving at the studio. With floor to ceiling frosted glass windows, natural light floods the studio providing an environment that enhances your overall experience as you work out.

  Pilates Power prides itself in providing an intimate space for anyone to practice Reformer Pilates. Every session is different and each instructor from our amazing team dedicates their time to produce challenging yet effective programs to suit every individual. You can be sure that in every class you will enjoy a full body workout regardless of age, gender, or fitness level. We provide dynamic reformer classes for all as well as private 1:1 sessions for individuals who feel may benefit from a more personal approach.

  Our studio not only provides a safe space for all, it has also become a second home to many as we have built strong relationships, forming a close community sharing the same passion for Pilates. We strongly believe in helping every single person feel welcomed and loved at Pilates Power. Every one who walks through our doors matters to us and we will ensure that we help you on your journey to achieve your fitness goals. Our studio is located in Doncaster East at a very short distance from Doncaster, Boxhill North, Blackburn North, Park Orchards.
