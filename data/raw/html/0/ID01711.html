When should my child first visit the dentist?

At Fresno Dental Care, we recommend bringing your child in for their first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth emerging. Early visits are essential for establishing a positive relationship with the dentist and laying the foundation for a lifetime of healthy dental habits.

How often should I visit the dentist?

At Fresno Dental Care, we recommend visiting the dentist every six months for routine check-ups and cleanings. However, based on your individual oral health needs, our team may suggest a customized schedule to ensure your smile stays healthy.

Do you offer emergency dental services?

Yes, at Fresno Dental Care, we understand that dental emergencies can arise unexpectedly. We prioritize emergency appointments and strive to accommodate you as quickly as possible, addressing your pain and concerns with prompt, professional care.

Can you help improve the appearance of my smile?

Absolutely! At Fresno Dental Care, we offer a range of cosmetic dentistry services, including teeth whitening, veneers, and orthodontics, to enhance your smile’s appearance. During your consultation, we'll discuss your aesthetic goals and recommend the best treatment options tailored to you.

What payment options do you offer?

At Fresno Dental Care, we believe everyone deserves access to quality dental care. We accept most major insurance plans and offer flexible payment options, including financing plans for services not covered by insurance. Our team is here to help you navigate your options and make your dental care affordable.
