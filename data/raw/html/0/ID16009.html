INAUGURATION OF SERVICE LEVEL STANDARDS UPGRADE AT JURANGMANGU STATION



South Tangerang – October 14, 2024 Inauguration of Service Level Standards (SPM) upgrade at Jurangmangu Station was held on Saturday, October 12, 2024.

The revitalization of Jurangmangu Station is a collaboration between the Ministry of Transportation, South Tangerang City Government, PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero), and the Public Company, PT Jaya Real Property Tbk.

This project goes beyond merely upgrading the station’s facilities, as Jurangmangu Station is part of the development of a Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) area, which will add value for Commuterline users. These improvements, in line with Service Level Standards, are part of a roadmap for integrated, modern, and pedestrian-friendly rail transportation system

Currently, Jurangmangu Station is integrated with the bustling Bintaro Jaya Xchange Mall via an underground tunnel. The station also has convenient access to the Jakarta–Serpong toll gate.

The enhancement of Service Level Standards at Jurangmangu Station will provide added value to the Bintaro area, especially in Tangerang Selatan, and is expected to increase public interest in using public transportation.

Jaya Real Property Wins Award for the Most Popular Brand in 2024

At the awarding ceremony entitled "Jawa Pos 7 Most Popular Brand of The Year 2024" which was held at Balai Kartini, Jakarta on Tuesday (3/9), PT Jaya Real Property Tbk (JRPT) won a prestigious award as one of the best brands in the Residential Property Sector category.

In this event, Jawa Pos recorded the 7 most popular brands for 60 select categories based on a survey conducted by Jawa Pos together with Infovesta, one of the leading research and data processing companies in Indonesia.

The survey involved 19,000 respondents from various locations; starting from DKI Jakarta (15.8 percent), East Java (10.9 percent), West Java (10.0 percent), Banten (9.0 percent), Central Java (8.9 percent), and others. This data collection was carried out through a closed questionnaire with a multistage area random sampling method, where respondents were asked to choose the brand they first thought of for each category.

With the acceptance of this award by Tina S. Hadisumarto as the Head of the Directorate of PT Jaya Real Property, Tbk (JRPT) JRPT, this achievement marks the success of JRPT which always strives to present innovative products that are in line with consumer needs.

Grand Unveiling of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya
A New Era of Hospitality in Bintaro

JAKARTA, Indonesia, — July 17, 2024: DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya, celebrated its grand opening on 11th of July, marking a significant milestone in the hospitality landscape of Bintaro. The event was held at the Grand MapleTree Ballroom and was attended by esteemed guests, dignitaries, and key stakeholders, all of whom gathered to witness the unveiling of this new hotel addition to the Hilton portfolio.

 

The evening began with a warm welcome from the Master of Ceremonies, setting the tone for an elegant and memorable event. Distinguished guests included Mr. Benyamin Davnie, Mayor of South Tangerang, and several prominent figures from PT Pembangunan Jaya, such as Mr. Candra Ciputra, President Commissioner, Mr DR. Eng. H Fauzi Bowo, Mr M.A. Ismail Ning, Mrs. Vivian Setjakusuma, Commissioner, and Mr. Trisna Muliadi, President Director. Mr. Sebastian Goldmann, Country General Manager of Hilton Indonesia and Timor Leste, was also in attendance, underscoring the significance of this new venture.

 

Ibu Lintangsari Handayani, General Manager of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya, expressed her excitement and gratitude in her opening speech. "The presence of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya will offer a fresh alternative for business and leisure travelers, with modern facilities and the warm hospitality that DoubleTree by Hilton is known for," she said. "As the first international hotel chain in the Bintaro area, we are confident that our strategic location, connected to Bintaro Xchange Mall, BXSea, and BX Rink, will attract both domestic and international tourists."

 

The hotel boasts state-of-the-art amenities, including a spacious ballroom with videotron facilities, making it an ideal venue for MICE and wedding events. Guests were treated to a spectacular food parade led by the Executive Chef, showcasing a meticulously curated menu that highlighted the culinary excellence the hotel aims to offer.

 

A key highlight of the evening was the introduction of Hilton's Travel with Purpose program, an ESG initiative focused on creating positive environmental and social impacts. This was followed by a heartwarming fashion show featuring children from Yayasan Sayap Ibu, in collaboration with local designer D'Craft Indonesia. The children, including Umay (14), Anisa (12), Meymey (13), Inami (6), Ari (13), and Khaleed (14), received a warm round of applause for their participation.

 

The grand opening ceremony also featured an engaging door prize segment and a captivating performance by renowned artist Marcell Siahaan, adding to the evening’s festivities. DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya is set to redefine the hospitality experience in Bintaro, offering guests unparalleled comfort, convenience, and world-class service.

Hilton Unveils its Newest Addition to Greater Jakarta



JAKARTA, Indonesia, — June 19, 2024: Leading global hospitality company Hilton (NYSE: HLT) announces the opening of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya, marking its entrance into one of the region's dynamic and promising cities. Owned by PT Jaya Real Property Tbk, DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya is strategically connected to Bintaro Xchange Mall 2 and in close proximity to local attractions such as the BX Sea oceanarium and BX Rink ice skating rink, as well as Jurang Mangu train station. The hotel is also a 35-minute drive from Soekarno Hatta International Airport, making it an ideal choice for both business and leisure travelers.

 

"We are thrilled to announce the opening of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya, which not only underscores our commitment to providing elevated hospitality, but also represents a milestone for the dynamic city as it welcomes its first full-service hotel. We look forward to offering our guests an exceptional experience that mirrors the unique spirit of Bintaro, blending the warmth of the Indonesian people with the thoughtful hospitality of the DoubleTree by Hilton brand,” said Ibu Lintangsari Handayani, general manager of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya.

 

Elegantly appointed rooms for elevated comfort


As the first full-service hotel in the city, DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya offers a comprehensive suite of facilities to cater to the varying needs of each guest. The hotel comprises 184 meticulously designed rooms, including four Deluxe Suites and a Premium Suite. Inspired by the local Betawi culture and the natural beauty of the Bintaro tree, each room tells a story of tradition meeting contemporary luxury. Magenta-hued floral accents, inspired by the locally farmed Vanda Douglas orchid, and natural materials pay homage to the area's flora, crafting a unique, culturally rich, and visually striking guest experience.

For those seeking relaxation, guests can also rejuvenate themselves at the outdoor swimming pool, which offers a panoramic view of the city, or the fully equipped 24-hour fitness center. Guests can also look forward to enjoying the brand’s signature chocolate chip cookie welcome upon check-in.

DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya’s culinary offerings include OPEN} Restaurant, its all-day dining restaurant where guests can enjoy a modern take on authentic Indonesian recipes. Guest can also convene over delectable light bites and coffee at THE KOFFEE, the hotel’s lobby lounge.

 

Flexible meetings and events spaces


DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya elevates event hosting with an array of sophisticated and versatile venues. These include a sleek boardroom for intimate meetings, four unique meeting rooms, and dedicated bridal suites, all designed with meticulous attention to detail. The highlight is the Grand MapleTree Ballroom, which comprises three interconnected ballrooms and is equipped with a state-of-the-art LED videotron, offering a perfect setting for large-scale events that can host up to 1,000 guests. This can be further expanded by combining it with the four meeting rooms, transforming the space into the Ultimate MapleTree Ballroom, which can accommodate up to 1,200 guests.

Additionally, the hotel boasts the WattleTree, a multi-functional area that can accommodate up to 80 guests and ideally suited for weddings, birthdays, and special occasions. Bathed in natural daylight and featuring a terrace, WattleTree creates a picturesque backdrop for unforgettable moments. All event spaces are conveniently located on a single floor, providing effortless accessibility from both the hotel lobby and the adjacent shopping mall.

In celebration of its opening, DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya is pleased to offer Hilton Honors members 1,000 Honors points per night until 14th of September, 2024.

DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta Bintaro Jaya is part of Hilton Honors, the award-winning guest-loyalty program for Hilton's 24 world-class brands.
