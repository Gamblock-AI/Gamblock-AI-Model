    BUILDING COMMUNITY

    The Farming Secrets Soil Learning Center is a hub of resources for farmers & growers who are on the regenerative journey. A platform created by soil lovers for soil lovers!

    The goal of Farming Secrets Soil Learning Center is just that: creating a central online space bringing together resources, education, content and inspiration, to better your growing practices, boost profits and product quality with an emphasis on sustainability and promote freedom from climate change.

    In this blog post, we break down what Brix really tells you—in plain farmer words. Brix is more than just a number; it’s a quick snapshot of plant performance. From photosynthesis and nutrient density to stress levels and quality potential, Brix readings reveal how resilient your crops are and whether your system is moving in the right direction.
