3. Legal Basis For Use Of Your Personal Data

The laws of some jurisdictions, such as the European Union and the UK, require companies to tell you about the legal basis for processing your personal data.  To the extent those laws apply, we may rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: where use of your personal data is necessary to provide you with Versant Services under a contract, for example the relevant Terms of Service.
  • Legitimate interest: where use of your personal data is necessary for our or others legitimate interests and where the use is not outweighed by your rights and interests.  Below are some examples of such interests:
    • providing Versant Services
    • improving our Services and developing new ones
    • recognizing and better understanding our users, including across platforms
    • conducting security and fraud prevention activities
    • marketing and promoting our content and services
    • building and managing business-to-business relationships
    • conducting compliance and risk management activities
    • providing and managing access to our systems
  • Legal obligation: where use of your personal data is necessary to comply with laws and regulations such as those relating to anti-bribery and corruption and anti-money-laundering, complying with requests from government bodies or courts, or responding to litigation.
  • With consent: we may ask for your consent to process your personal data in a certain way.  Where we rely on this basis, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time – please see the Your Rights and Your Choices sections below to do this.

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights with respect to your personal data.

California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia residents, in addition to residents of Canada, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, may have the following rights depending on your jurisdiction:

  • Right to Know/Access: the right to access certain personal data we hold about you, in some cases in a portable format, if technically feasible, including
    • Specific pieces of personal data we maintain about you.
    • Information about the categories of personal data we process about you and how we process it (for California and Oregon residents).
  • Right to Correct: the right to update or correct inaccurate personal data we maintain about you.
  • Right to Delete: the right to delete certain personal data we have collected from or about you.
  • Right to Restrict: the right to restrict the use of or object to the processing of your data if you are in the UK, EU, or Switzerland.
  • Right to Receive Specific List of Third Parties: for Minnesota and Oregon and Rhode Island residents, the right to receive a list of the specific third parties, other than natural persons, to which we have disclosed personal data.
  • Right to Opt Out: the right to opt out of the “sale,” “sharing,” or use of your personal data for “targeted advertising.” You can find out more about how to opt-out in the “Opt Out of Sale of Personal Data and Processing for Targeted Ads” section below.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination/Retaliation: the right not to be discriminated or retaliated against (as provided for in applicable law) for exercising your rights under applicable laws.

Residents of certain other countries may be able request one or more of the above under applicable law. 

Exceptions to and Impacts of Exercising Privacy Rights

Please note: Your rights and our responses will vary based on your state or country of residency. Please note that you may be located in a jurisdiction where we are not obligated, or are unable, to fulfill a request. In such a case, your request may not be fulfilled.

Many of the above rights are subject to exceptions and limitations.

Some Versant Services can only be provided if we have your personal data, therefore deletion of your data will result in termination of such Services.
