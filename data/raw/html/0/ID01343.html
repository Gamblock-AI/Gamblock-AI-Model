Commitment to Safety

Distance Trucking is proud to be on of the safest carriers in the industry. Safety of our teammates remains the top priority of our firm. In line with our commitment to safety, Distance Trucking partnered with Teletrac, the leader in transportation industry safety technology, to install AI Quad Dashboard Camera in all our trucks. With this system we will ensure that all drivers are protected in case of an issue and we will be able to assist them quickly and efficiently when the need arises.
