FutureFit: Digital Conference - Coaches

Start timeApr 27, 5:00 PM

End timeApr 27, 7:00 PM

Add to calendar

During the 2025/26 season The FA are hosting a series of FutureFit: Digital Conferences aimed at supporting the grassroots network ahead of the implementation of changes to youth football in 2026/27.

Join us on Monday 27th April 2026, where The FA will be hosting an evening workshop covering a variety of themes relevant to Grassroots Coaches. The workshop will explore the incoming changes in more detail, including why they are being introduced and how you can best prepare yourself for success in 2026/27.

If you have any further questions relating to this session please email FutureFit@thefa.com.
