Our company, Natív Hirdetés Ltd., has created the |related| intelligent content recommendation and native advertising system (hereinafter: System) to provide a solution for our contractual partners (hereinafter: Contractual partner) to efficiently utilize unused spaces on their websites and to assist users visiting our Contractual partners' websites (hereinafter: Users) in finding the most valuable content for them.

For the secure, lawful, and efficient operation of the System, it is indispensable that we process the personal data of Users. During data processing, we always act in accordance with the principles of good faith, fairness, transparency, accuracy, and accountability, respecting and enforcing the rights of users related to data processing. We have designed our data processing in accordance with the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter: GDPR), Act CXII of 2011 on the right to informational self-determination and freedom of information (hereinafter: Infotv.), Act CVIII of 2001 on certain aspects of electronic commerce services and information society services (hereinafter: E-com tv.), Act V of 2013 on the Civil Code (hereinafter: Ptk.), and Act XLVIII of 2008 on the basic conditions and certain restrictions of economic advertising activity (hereinafter: Grtv.).

Our goal is to ensure that personal data is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes of data processing. During data processing, we ensure the confidentiality of the personal data we handle and prevent unauthorized access.

4.1. Scope of personal data processed

The System exclusively stores online identifiers provided by the devices and equipment (computer, mobile phone, other internet access device - hereinafter collectively referred to as: computer) used by Users, thus the IP address and cookie identifier of the Users' computers. Furthermore, the System automatically logs the IP address of the computer initiating access; the day and time of access/request; the name of the visited page or file; the referral to the page from which access to the System's services was made; the type and version number of the browser used by Users; and the operating system used by Users (hereinafter collectively referred to as: data recorded in the log file).

The IP address is a unique network identifier, a unique set of numbers that identifies Users' computers to send data to other computers and receive data from them. Our System automatically records the IP address of Users when they visit the Contracting partner's website.

A cookie, also known as a web cookie, is a small file used to store useful information and improve the browsing experience, which is placed on Users' computers when they visit a website. A cookie, as a sequence of characters, is not capable of identifying Users in any way on its own, but only of recognizing their computer and uniquely identifying their browser.

4.2. Purpose of data processing

Our Company processes the IP address and log file data of Users for the purpose of ensuring the security and legality of the System's operation. By storing the IP address, we can protect the System's data, traffic, and Users from potential abuse, illegal use, and hacker attacks.

We manage cookies in the System to improve the quality of the System's advertising and content recommendation services. With the help of the placed cookie, returning visitors can be recognized, and the repetition and frequency of the same advertisement display can be determined for them.

Personal data is processed and used exclusively for the above purposes, until the purpose is achieved. The use of collected personal data for purposes other than the intended purpose is only possible with the prior, explicit consent of the Users.

8. Users' rights and enforcement options

Users have the right to be informed about whether their personal data is being processed. If so, they have the right to access their personal data, correct inaccurate, incorrect, or incomplete personal data, request the deletion of personal data, and, in certain cases, restrict their use, unless this is restricted by law.

Users are entitled to request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their personal data if the processing of their data is based on their consent.

Users may restrict the use of their personal data in cases where they dispute the accuracy, validity, or completeness of their personal data, or if the data processing is unlawful and the Data Controller opposes the deletion of the data, or the Data Controller no longer needs the personal data for the purpose of data processing, but the Users require them for the submission, enforcement or protection of their legal claims; or if the Users have objected to the data processing.

In case of clarification of personal data accuracy, the restriction may last until the accuracy of the personal data is verified, and in case of objection, until it is determined whether our Company's legitimate grounds prevail over the Users' interests.

Furthermore, Users have the right to the portability of their personal data provided voluntarily or for the performance of a contract, that is, to receive a copy of their data in a machine-readable and conventional format, and to transmit this data to another data controller without hindrance.

Users may object to the processing and use of their personal data for the benefit of our Company. They have the right to withdraw their consent to the use of their data for specific purposes at any time. In case of withdrawal of consent, the processing and use of the data prior to the withdrawal shall be considered lawful.

Our Company shall comply with the request of Users, if it is well-founded, without undue delay, in the shortest possible time, but no later than one month after the receipt of the request, which may be extended by a further two months if necessary.

Our Company shall inform the affected Users and all those to whom the personal data has been previously transmitted for data processing purposes about the restriction, deletion, and correction of the processed personal data. The notification may be omitted if it does not infringe upon the legitimate interests of the data subject in light of the purpose of data processing.

We ask Users to contact us if they believe that their rights related to the protection of their personal data have been violated so that we can examine their complaints and remedy any possible infringement.

If Users believe that our Company or the Data Processor indicated in the Notice processes their personal data in violation of the law or the provisions of the European Union regulation, they are entitled to turn to the Court. In this case, Users may decide to initiate the lawsuit before the court according to their place of residence or domicile.
