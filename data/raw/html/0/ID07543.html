Advanced Digital Denture Course - Three-day Clinical & Technical Certification Course

Over three days this course will cover the complete treatment, from start to finish, of a live edentulous patient utilising BPS clinical and technical techniques in the digital realm.

The efficiencies and benefits of CAD/CAM technology is fast gaining traction in the treatment of edentulous patients and now, more than ever before, a thorough understanding of the principals of BPS will provide the confidence and guidance to those choosing to use the digital platform to treat their patients.

Course participants will follow the step-by-step BPS system, incorporating 3shape software and the Ivotion Denture System, to produce and issue precision complete digital dentures.

Learning Objectives...

Primary impressions using impression materials, frame cut back tray (FCB)
     and centric tray
✓  Scan and design custom closed mouth trays and Gnathometer CAD
✓  Final functional BPS/SEMCD impressions incorporating Facebow CAD
     and Gnathometer CAD
✓  Design digital try-in according to BPS® set up principles in 3shape software
✓  Evaluate the digital try-in
✓  Learn how to finish digital dentures
✓  Issuing of digital dentures manufactured on the Programill® 7 (PM7)
✓  Come away with a complete understanding of BPS principles
     and digital denture workflows

Who should attend...

All who want to gain a better understanding of digital denture workflows. Suitable for dentists, dental prosthetists, clinical dental technicians and dental technicians.

What To Bring...

Participants are required to bring their loupes, protective clothing, waxing and grinding instruments.
