Case Studies

Proven Success, Trusted by Industry Leaders

Expanding Market Reach in India & UAE for Consumr.ai

Challenge: Consumr.ai sought a strategic partner to help establish a strong foothold in India and the UAE by identifying ideal customer profiles (ICPs), increasing engagement, and securing high-value demo appointments with top-tier organizations. Challenges faced:

  • Limited brand presence in the Indian and UAE markets.
  • Identifying and reaching the right decision-makers within enterprises.
  • Crafting an effective sales outreach strategy to generate traction quickly.

Solution: RevAvenues partnered with Consumr.ai to develop and execute a comprehensive market entry strategy, ensuring high-quality engagements and measurable growth.

  • ICP Identification & Market Analysis – Conducted research to pinpoint the most relevant industries and decision-makers for Consumr.ai’s platform.
  • Targeted Outreach & Messaging – Built and trained a dedicated outreach team to create persuasive sales pitches and proposals tailored to enterprise clients.
  • Multi-Touch Engagement Plan – Developed a systematic approach, leveraging multiple communication channels to maximize touchpoints and secure high-value demo appointments.
  • Relationship Building & Sales Enablement – Identified key Points of Contact (POCs) within enterprises, leading to meaningful discussions and business opportunities.

Results:

  • 1500+ Unique Touchpoints – Established a strong presence through targeted outreach.
  • 70+ Demos in 3 Months – Secured meetings with decision-makers at leading enterprises.
  • 30% Incremental Revenue Growth – Direct impact on business growth through pipeline expansion and successful conversions.

Looking to expand into new markets? Partner with us to drive high-value engagements!

Scaling Quantacus from Referrals to a Thriving Business

Challenge: Quantacus had no paying clients and relied solely on brand references for outreach. The company struggled to secure consistent revenue and establish a strong market presence without a structured sales approach. 

Solution: RevAvenues developed and executed a comprehensive outreach strategy targeting legacy and new-age D2C brands. This included:

  • Building a targeted brand database and identifying key decision-makers.
  • Personalized outreach through customized proposals and strategic pitching.
  • Booking demo appointments to showcase Quantacus’ value proposition.

Results:

  • 1,000+ unique touchpoints and 70+ engaged prospects in just 90 days.
  • Successfully onboarded six paying clients, including major brands like Swiggy, Arata, Bluestone, Jasora, Pepe Jeans, and Clovia.
  • Achieved ₹6 Cr ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) Year-To-Date (YTD).
  • Established market credibility in India, leading to expansion into the U.S. market.
  • Created a repeatable sales process for sustained growth.

Ready to scale your business like Quantacus? Let’s build your winning outreach strategy today!

Driving Weekday Revenue for GoRugYourself

Challenge: GoRugYourself (GRY), India’s first DIY rug-making concept, faced low weekday footfall and revenue, relying mainly on weekend traffic from social media marketing and retail clients. The goal was to create a sustainable weekday revenue stream. 

Solution: RevAvenues partnered with GRY to develop a corporate engagement strategy, targeting employee engagement and admin teams of large organizations. 

  • Introducing weekday workshops as a creative team-building activity for corporate employees.
  • Direct outreach to corporate decision-makers to showcase the benefits of hands-on engagement.
  • Positioning GRY as an innovative employee wellness experience for companies looking to enhance workplace culture.

Results:

  • 700+ unique touchpoints with corporate decision-makers.
  • Successfully hosted 60 weekday workshops over 9 months.
  • Achieved 30% incremental revenue growth by tapping into corporate clients.
  • Secured weekday workshop bookings with major corporations like McKinsey, Google, and Michael Page.
  • Established a new revenue stream beyond weekends, increasing overall business stability.
  • Strengthened GRY’s brand presence among corporate clients, creating long-term opportunities.

Looking to expand your business reach? Let’s craft a winning strategy together!
