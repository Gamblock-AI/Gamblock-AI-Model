C. Bechstein B 212 Grand Piano

The C. Bechstein B 212 is a handcrafted "salon" grand piano—at 2.12 m (≈ 6' 11") long, it bridges the intimacy of a home instrument and the presence of a concert-ready piano. Built in Germany with around 420–700 hours of meticulous labor, it features the same engineering excellence as Bechstein's larger C 234 and D 282 models—like an optimized cast-iron frame, nine-layer pinblock, and violin-inspired spruce soundboard for superb sustain and clarity. The piano's celebrated "Gold Action" delivers a sensitive, responsive touch often praised for its symbiosis with the performer. Professionals and advanced amateurs alike relish its refined tone—rich, transparent, warm—and consistent temperament across registers. Whether gracing a chamber hall or music room, the B 212 stands as a masterpiece of German craftsmanship and acoustical artistry.

Casio Digital Pianos

Casio digital keyboards offers the perfect balance of affordability, convenience, and versatility when a full sized acoustic piano is just not an option. They are compact, lightweight, and doesn't require tuning, making it ideal for smaller spaces or people who need to move it around. With a wide range of sounds and features, such as built-in rhythms and recording capabilities, they provide an exciting opportunity for creativity and practice without the cost or maintenance of an acoustic piano. Plus, you can plug in headphones for quiet practice, making it perfect for apartment living or late-night sessions. We have many options to select from, like the AP-S200, just under $1,000. And to even get closer to and acoustic feel and sound, we have the GP-310 and GP-510 which were designed in collaboration with C. Bechstein. These are amazing digital pianos. Come visit us and try them for yourself.
