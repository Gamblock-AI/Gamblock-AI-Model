About Us

We are an organisation of professional, genuine North American actors who are each affiliated with at least one industry union and are legally eligible for work in the UK. We are an invaluable resource to casting directors on both sides of the pond, and act as a central hub of activity for our members by organising industry and social events tailored to North American actors.

BECOME A MEMBER

We welcome all American and Canadian actors with legal right to work in UK to apply for membership and join us. Here are some benefits of joining:

  • You’re included in the UK’s recognised casting pool of professional and genuine North American actors.
  • We send regular emails to individual casting directors with members’ photos and details of what you have been doing.
  • Your Spotlight page identifies you as a genuine North American with the legal right to work in the UK. Casting Directors searching Spotlight for real North Americans will find you first.
  • We send you emails with casting calls that come to us directly.
  • We have a wide social media network where we will post casting notices and that you can use to communicate with fellow members.
  • We send a members’ newsletter out, giving you all the latest news about our events, workshops, discounts, performances and more!
  • There are a variety of discounts and deals provided to our members from headshot photographers, showreel makers, etc.
  • We run social events and theatre trips as well as workshops with Casting Directors and guest speakers.
