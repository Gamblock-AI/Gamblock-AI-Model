About us

About Give Tech Back, Inc.

Give Tech Back, Inc. (Federal EIN: 45-1226377) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to bridging the digital divide through sustainable technology access, digital literacy, and workforce empowerment.

 

Founded on the belief that technology is not a luxury—it’s a lifeline, we repurpose, rebuild, and redistribute donated hardware and software to underserved communities, schools, veterans, and individuals with disabilities across the United States.

 

Our journey began with a simple, urgent question: What happens to yesterday’s technology when tomorrow arrives? Rather than letting functional tech be discarded or forgotten, we give it new purpose and new hands—empowering people where the need is greatest.

 

What We Do

Refurbishment & Redistribution: We securely wipe, repair, and redeploy laptops, desktops, tablets, and accessories to students, low-income families, and nonprofit partners.

 

Digital Skills Training: We provide free training programs in computer literacy, cybersecurity basics, and job-readiness to equip recipients with the tools they need to thrive.

 

Sustainability Focus: By diverting thousands of pounds of e-waste from landfills annually, we reduce environmental impact while expanding digital inclusion.

Custom Solutions: We support community centers, disaster recovery shelters, mobile learning labs, and veterans' groups with tailored tech stacks and consulting.

 

Why It Matters

In today's hyperconnected world, access to technology isn't optional—it's essential. From applying for jobs and attending school to scheduling healthcare and staying in touch with loved ones, digital access defines opportunity. Yet millions of Americans still face a digital desert.

 

Give Tech Back is driven by technologists, educators, veterans, and community advocates who’ve experienced that gap firsthand—and are committed to closing it. We don’t just deliver devices—we deliver dignity, opportunity, and the chance to participate fully in a digital economy.

 

How You Can Help

Support from individuals, corporate sponsors, and community partners fuels our mission. Whether you’re donating used equipment, volunteering your IT skills, or funding a program, you’re directly impacting lives.
