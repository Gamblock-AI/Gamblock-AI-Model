About TripZilla

TripZilla.com is the flagship publication of TripZilla, Southeast Asia's Leader in Digital Travel Media that has a media reach of 25 million every month. The TripZilla brand also has under its wing TripZilla.sg, Singapore's No.1 Travel Portal, as well as TripZillaSTAYS, a collection of the finest stays in Southeast Asia.

Our magazine creates and curates useful, value-adding travel stories to inspire readers. By sharing some of the best guides and tips from travellers, TripZilla.com builds a virtual travel hub to facilitate the travel community in every step of their holiday planning. While travellers from around the globe can enjoy the content, we specialise in catering to those within the Asia region.
