Social media has a strong influence on the health of today’s youth, affecting them both positively and negatively. On the positive side, social platforms help young people stay connected with friends, express themselves, and find supportive communities. Many teens use social media to learn about mental health, fitness, and healthy habits. Educational content, awareness campaigns, and peer support groups can encourage openness and reduce feelings of isolation. However, excessive social media use can also harm youth health. One major concern is mental well-being. Constant comparison with edited images and idealized lifestyles can lower self-esteem and increase anxiety, stress, and depression. The pressure to gain likes and approval may lead to unhealthy validation-seeking behavior. Cyberbullying is another serious issue that can deeply affect emotional health and confidence. Social media can also impact physical health. Long screen time often reduces physical activity and disrupts sleep patterns, especially when youth use their phones late at night. Poor sleep can affect mood, focus, and overall development. In some cases, unhealthy trends related to dieting or appearance may promote harmful behaviors. Overall, social media is a powerful tool that needs balance. With guidance, awareness, and healthy limits, youth can enjoy its benefits while protecting their mental and physical health.

How Does This Summarizing Tool Work?

Our summarizing tool uses an AI-based algorithm that firstly detects the best sentences from the paragraph and understands the text then proceeds to summarize the content.

After selecting the best lines from the text, the algorithm gives a score to each selected line based on its accuracy, structure, optimization, quality, length, and other technical factors. Based on the score, the text summarizer understands the importance of each line and compares it with the rest of the text.

By using advanced machine learning and NLP models, AI Summarizer allows you to summarize text according to the main concept of the content just like Editpad text summarizer does.

What is a summary?

A summary is a short version of a longer text that concisely covers only the most important points. Typically, it serves as an overview of the content, conveying the main ideas without unnecessary details.
