At Mozilla.ai, we believe the next generation of AI solutions should be trustworthy, transparent, and controllable. That’s why we’re creating the infrastructure and products to build intelligent systems that align with your goals.

A MOZILLA.AI PRODUCT: ANY-LLM

Simplified Management and Analytics for all your LLM Providers.

Simplify building AI apps with unified monitoring and management for both cloud-based and local models. Built on top of any-llm library, this platform provides you with a single place to manage all your provider keys securely, and monitor usage analytics for costs, tokens, and performance.
‍
Privacy-first design. No lock-in. No new SDKs.
Now in Beta and free to use.

A Platform You Can Trust, Backed by Open Source.

Octonous is built on a transparent, open-source foundation developed in collaboration with a global network of contributors and maintained with rigorous quality controls.

This community approach means our platform can be independently audited, improved rapidly, and adapted as your needs change, without locking you into a single vendor.

Open Tools

Our open-source libraries provide a unified foundation for AI, supporting flexible agent orchestration, model selection, evaluation, or local single-file execution without runtimes.
Scale AI your way!

any-agent

One interface to run, test, and compare different agent frameworks, so you can choose the best one for your needs.

Learn more
any-llm

Switch between LLM providers with a single configuration change, unlocking flexibility without added complexity.

Learn more
any-guardrail

A common interface for guardrail models, allowing you to quickly change between different models.

Learn more
mcpd

Easily manage MCP servers across multiple environments, streamlining deployment and control.

llamafile

Seamlessly distribute and run LLMs locally through a single-file executable, with no installation required.
