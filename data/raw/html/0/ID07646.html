TheWrong
Biennale.


An international digital art biennial as a decentralized platform for contemporary new media culture, built to connect artists, curators, institutions, brands and audiences worldwide.

Every edition of The Wrong presents exhibitions as pavilions, online projects, screenings, and embassy events that explore the evolving language of digital art.

Its global, open structure makes it a unique space for experimental practices, networked collaboration, and critical engagement with art in the digital age.

The Wrong has been awarded an honorary mention by the European Commission's S+T+ARTS initiative, and has joined the IBA - International Biennial Association as its newest institutional member.

Since its inception in 2013, The Wrong Biennale has featured over 14000 artists and curators, with their work showcased in over 1000 pavilions, embassies, and institutions, shaping contemporary art and digital culture worldwide.
