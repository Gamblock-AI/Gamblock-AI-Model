Be for Everyone

At eBay, “Be for Everyone” is more than a value—it’s a guiding principle shaping how we innovate, support employees, and serve millions of buyers and sellers. Belonging fuels creativity, engagement, and long-term performance, making it good for people and business.

As a global marketplace with 134 million buyers, we thrive when diverse voices influence how we design, decide, and deliver. Inclusion helps us anticipate needs, reduce friction, unlock unique inventory, and build trust across communities.

We also invest in employee well-being, manager capability, and belonging—recognizing that supporting people in life’s key moments drives retention, innovation, and business continuity.

For customers, we design experiences that reflect global diversity—improving discoverability, supporting sellers of all sizes, and ensuring safety, accessibility, and responsible AI.

By embedding inclusion into how we lead and operate, we foster innovation, strengthen the marketplace, and create lasting value for employees, customers, and shareholders.

Our Communities of Inclusion (COIs)

With 12 groups worldwide, our Communities of Inclusion (COIs) provide spaces where employees can come together, share experiences, and make an impact inside and outside eBay.

Access aims to celebrate our whole selves through accessibility programming to educate, advocate, and provide safe spaces for conversations and community building around mental health, physical disabilities, neurodiversity, and chronic illness.

Asian Employees at eBay (AE2) is a thriving community of Asian American, Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander (AANHPI) eBayers. In addition to the annual Lunar New Year celebration, AE2 hosted both in-person and virtual events for the Mid-Autumn Festival and AANHPI Heritage Month.

Black Employees at eBay (BEE) is a diverse community focused on the support and development of people of African descent. BEE’s 2024 Black History Month program, “Past, Present, Future,” celebrated Black innovators, including an Inclusive AI Panel with leading Black AI innovators who are building tomorrow's models. The community's mission is to foster dialogue and champion the culture and heritage of its members.

CARE is a community that supports those who care for others. Throughout the year, it provides supportive connections, meaningful events, and valuable resources to help our caregiver employees feel appreciated and know that they have a place at eBay.

DESI - Develop, Empower, and Support the Indian Subcontinent Community to create a vibrant community of team members through inspiration, creativeness, and togetherness. Our flagship global Diwali celebrations in 2024 centered around the rich and opulent history of the Indian subcontinent.

EMBRACE aims to recognize and welcome people from across the globe, to celebrate our cultures and uniqueness, and to put in the hard work to understand cultural, racial, and other topics that affect our communities.

Honor recognizes and supports service members and veterans from all branches of the global armed and defense forces, law enforcement and national security services, first responders, and their families. In 2024, Honor continued its veteran apprenticeship program, helping service members successfully transition to meaningful, full-time employment.

MENA builds community by celebrating and showcasing the beauty of Middle Eastern and North African cultures. Through events, mentorship, and cultural programs, we raise awareness, spark pride, and empower members and allies alike to thrive and lead with confidence.

Unidos exists to unite eBay’s Hispanic, Latino, and Latinx communities, including employees, sellers and buyers. Unidos delivers year-round programming to celebrate heritage, language, and culture.

United in Pride is a global alliance of LGBTQ+ eBayers and allies dedicated to keeping eBay a safe haven for all. Along with digital events, in-person Pride 2024 celebrations spanned the globe, including events in Zurich, Toronto, Dublin, San Francisco, Seattle, and Salt Lake City.

Women@eBay aims to promote gender balance and equality within the company. The community honored Women’s History Month with global keynotes, a fireside chat with eBay’s own executive leadership team, and movie screenings.

Young Employee Society (YES) is a community for the young and young-at-heart at eBay. We’re here to fuel innovation, connect people, and empower early-in-career talent, all while building a culture of belonging. Through mentorship, networking, and growth opportunities, YES helps members develop personally and professionally while shaping the future of eBay together.

Gender Pay Equity Statement

Our annual study of our gender pay equity, looking at total compensation (including salary, bonus, and annual stock award value), resulted in a gender pay ratio of 99.7% both in the U.S. and globally.

We continue to invest in training our hiring and leadership teams, and enhancing our ability to make compensation decisions by leveraging data-driven insights during compensation reviews. In addition, we rigorously examine the pay of those newly hired, transferred, or promoted. We are steadfast in our commitment to a workplace where everyone’s pay is a direct reflection of their role, contribution, and the impact they make.
