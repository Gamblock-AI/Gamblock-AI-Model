What is UC3?

Article summary

UC3 unites European football’s governing body UEFA and European Football Clubs (EFC), representing more than 800 top European clubs, around a new vision for managing commercial rights to UEFA club competitions (the “UCCs”).

UC3 is the commercial entity responsible for generating revenues from the UCCs and creating value for our partners. Our organisation oversees the management, sales and delivery of all commercial rights (including media, sponsorship and licensing rights) for UEFA’s elite men’s and women’s club competitions.

Powering football together

Article summary


At UC3, partnerships are more than just business – they’re collaborations that drive the beautiful game forward. Our trusted broadcasters and sponsors play a vital role in the success of every competition, from the roar of the fans in the stadium to the unforgettable moments shared by millions watching on TV around the world.

Working with UC3 means joining a legacy of excellence, innovation and global impact. Our partners benefit from unparalleled exposure, access to iconic events and the prestige of being aligned with one of the world’s most respected sports organisations and its premier club competitions.
