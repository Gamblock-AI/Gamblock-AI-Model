Welcome to Global Grey

Welcome to Global Grey - a one-woman project that offers a curated library of classic and hard-to-find public domain ebooks across a wide range of subjects, from spiritual traditions and philosophy to folklore, history, and classic fiction. Available in PDF, EPUB, and Kindle formats, free to download, and with no account required.

Each ebook is formatted by me, and I also maintain the site - if you want to support this work, please consider giving a small donation. It really helps with the costs (and of course, it's always nice to have the work you do appreciated and valued).
