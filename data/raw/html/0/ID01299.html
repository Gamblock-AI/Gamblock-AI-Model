DISCOVERY KARTIKA PLAZA HOTEL

Discovery Kartika Plaza Hotel is The Heritage 5 star oceanfront in the heart of Kuta, Bali. Inaugurated in 1990 and built in a 10 hectares land with integrated concept of Hotel and Shopping Mall.

Features 316 spacious rooms that radiate an elegant authentic Balinese furnishing, with views across lush tropical gardens toward the Indian Ocean, The Rooms, Suites and Villas at Discovery Kartika Plaza Hotel are perfectly positioned to enjoy Bali’s famed sunset.

Discovery Kartika Plaza Hotel is offering one stop hotel destination concept with incomparable services and facilities for all guest travel needs and purposed. With 3 restaurants, 4 bars, Discovery Spa and health club, outdoor and indoor kids club, 5 conferences rooms including Ballroom and 3 outdoor multi-purpose function venue.

Located in prime area of Kuta, within close reach to various tourist attractions, shopping mall of Discovery Mall, dining, entertainment center, and it is a 5 minute drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport.

Guest Notice

Kuta Beach Enhancement Project

Please be informed that the Government of Bali Province is currently carrying out a beach improvement project along the Kuta Beach area. The project involves sand replenishment and the construction of breakwaters aimed at conserving and enhancing the coastline.

During this period, there may be some temporary impact on the beachfront area in front of our resort. We sincerely appreciate your kind understanding as these important works are underway.

Once completed, this government-led initiative will contribute to the beautification and long-term preservation of Kuta Beach, ensuring an even more enjoyable experience for all guests in the future.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.
