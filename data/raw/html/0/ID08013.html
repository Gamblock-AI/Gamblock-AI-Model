The Voice of Europe’s Universities

The European University Association (EUA) is the collective voice of the universities of Europe, institutions that have shaped our world for a millennium. For over two decades, EUA has played a key role in building university communities across the whole of Europe and creating a coherent system for European higher education and research. EUA is a community of over 900 members and affiliates. It represents universities and national rectors’ conferences in 48 European countries, as well as affiliated organisations and networks based both in and beyond Europe.

About EUA

As the largest association of higher education institutions in Europe, EUA is the sector’s platform for horizon scanning, strategizing, communicating with policy makers and shaping public discourse.
Who we are

To achieve EUA’s vision of ‘Strong universities for Europe’, we support our members to excel in the service of society.

Discover what drives EUA
Our members

EUA counts universities from across Europe, as well as national rectors’ conferences and national university associations, among our members.

Meet Europe’s universities
Organisation

EUA is governed by a Board, Council and General Assembly, supported by a Secretariat, and guided by a variety of working groups and committees.
