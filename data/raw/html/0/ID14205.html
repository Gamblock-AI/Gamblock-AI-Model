      About the Event

      Connect Run 2025 aims to connect high-school students with top universities while fostering a sense of community through a cultural run. This event provides a unique platform for students to explore educational opportunities, establish friendships, and enjoy a memorable day. Our goal is to create an annual tradition that celebrates the achievements of graduate students and encourages interaction among high-school students across the UAE.

      Yeshee Sports Events

      Upcoming Events

      CONNECT RUN 2025 - Run into the Future

      • Date: March 17, 2025
      • Location: Expo City Dubai
      • Distance: 3km
      • Registration Fee: 100 AED per student
      • Highlights: Connect with universities, make new friends, enjoy a fun and engaging day
      • Vendor Booths: Booths available for vendors to rent at 25,000 AED per day, providing an opportunity to sell products to event attendees.
      • University Booths: Booths available for universities to rent at 25,000 AED per day to connect with students and showcase educational opportunities.

      Mission Statement

      FAQs

      Who can participate?

      High-school students from across the UAE.

      How much is the registration fee?

      The fee is 100 AED per student.

      What is the race distance?

      The race distance is 3km

      How do I apply for the scholarships?

      Details will be provided during the event

      What should I bring on the day of the event?

      Please bring your registration confirmation, a water bottle, and comfortable running attire.

      Can parents attend the event?

      Yes, parents are welcome to attend and support their children

      Is there parking available at Expo City Dubai?

      Yes, there is ample parking available for participants and visitors

      Are there refreshments available at the event?

      Yes, there will be water stations and snack booths available throughout the event.

      How can I rent a vendor booth?

      Vendor booths are available for 25,000 AED per day. Please contact us for more details and to book your booth

      Can universities rent booths at the event?

      Yes, universities can rent booths for 25,000 AED per day to connect with students. Contact us for more information and to reserve your space.
