Executive Chairman, MNC Group

PT MNC Asia Holding Tbk or MNC Group was established in 1989 as a securities company and listed on IDX in 1997. MNC Group expanded into media in 2001 and officially entered into tourism & hospitality in 2007.

Today, MNC Group is Indonesia`s leading investment group with 3 strategic businesses: Media & Entertainment, Financial Services, and Tourism & Hospitality. MNC Group`s vision is to become an important pillar for national economic growth and a leader in technology-related development.
