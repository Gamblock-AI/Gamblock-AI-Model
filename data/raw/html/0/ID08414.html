St. Maarten VIP Services

Princess Juliana International Airport

Fast & secure transit. Every time. Everywhere.

AirStMaarten offers two types of VIP Services to travelers who want a hassle-free transit through Princess Juliana International SXM Airport, without standing in line. Services include expedited immigration, customs and security screening, porter assistance, fast luggage retrieval, lounge access, and escorted transfers to flights or waiting vehicles.

Premium VIP Services

With our Premium VIP Services we meet and greet clients at the aircraft door on arrival, and escort them through Immigration, retrieve their luggage and clear them through Customs. For outbound flights booked with the Premium VIP Package, we meet you curbside at arrival, check you in, escort you through Customs & Immigration and the Security Checkpoint. From there we take yu to the boarding gate or to the VIP Lounge.

  • Meet & Greet at the Aircraft Door
  • Fast Track Service

Regular VIP Services

With our VIP Services clients meet our agent inside the Transit Hall at the Transit Information Desks after deplaning. The agent assists them from there. This service does not include access to the VIP or Princess Sky Lounge. VIP Lounge access requires a per-person entrance fee.

  • Meet & Greet at the Transit Desks
  • Fast Track Service
(*) Note ~ The newly rebranded Princess Sky Lounge at SXM Airport is now open and available for access at $65 USD per person.
