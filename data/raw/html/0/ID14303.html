Indonesian Journal of EFL and Linguistics, e-ISSN: 2503-4197, p-ISSN: 2527-5070, is a Journal of Language Learning, Linguistics, and Literature. The journal is published twice a year; May and November.

Indonesian Journal of EFL and Linguistics is accredited (SINTA 2) by the Indonesian Ministry of Higher Education with the Decree Number 225/E/KPT/2022 which is valid for five years since enacted on Volume 6, Issue 2, 2021 to Volume 11, Issue 1, 2026.

CALLING FOR PAPERS

Indonesian Journal of EFL and Linguistics is calling papers for the issue of Vol. 11, No. 1, May 2026.

Focus and Scope; TEFL, ELT, Language and Linguistics, Morphology, Phonology, Syntax, Semantics, Pragmatics, Discourse Analysis, Critical Discourse Analysis, Socio-linguistics, Literature, and Anthropological linguistics.

Deadline submission: February 10, 2026
