Setiabudi One is home to a wide range of delectable cuisine. From the flavorful Indonesian selection to the fresh and delightful European and Western delicacies. It's the place that brings the best of cuisine to please everyone's palate. It’s cozy and comfortable ambience is perfect for gathering.

Located at the Kuningan Central Business District. It is an ideal place for many occasions, perfect for hangout with friends, families, and work meetings. It's the place that has many experience for dining, coffee break, fitness, beauty treatment and entertainment to please everyone's desire.
