Our Two-Step Plan

Our Two-Step Plan means that at Canva, you have outsized impact. 

As Canva grows, so does our ability to make a difference. More resources mean more opportunities to create positive change.

Every time someone new joins our team and ships a feature, or hits a goal, they’re contributing to both Step One (growing our company) and Step Two (using that growth for good). So when you join Canva, your impact goes further and means more.

Acknowledgement of Country

Canva acknowledges the deep connections of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities to Country. We pay our respects to the Gadigal people of the Eora nation, who are the Traditional Owners of the land our offices are on in Surry Hills, Sydney.

As a visual communications company, we recognise, and are inspired by, the rich traditions of storytelling in the First Nations cultures of Australia.  We also recognise the continuing contributions of indigenous communities around the world. We’re committed to continually learning from these powerful traditions and amplifying them wherever we can.
