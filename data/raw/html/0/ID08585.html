Welcome to Shramjivi College

“An investment in knowledge always pays the best interest”- Benjamin Franklin

By attending school, you’ve already made your first investment and gained a great deal of knowledge and wisdom. Now, it’s time to take on a new major academic venture which will determine your destiny and mold you into a structure as a responsible citizen of the nation. Every student wishes to pursue his/her chosen courses in most prestigious colleges and universities.
Being among the city’s greenest and sound campuses, the greenery fosters creativity and provides an excellent learning environment for the stakeholders. The S. S. P. M’s Shramjivi College of Pharmacy is situated in the campus of Adarsh Mahavidyalaya. Omerga is connected to other important cities like Hyderabad, Mumbai, Solapur, Latur, Gulbarga, and Humnabad via a major highway road line that runs through Pune, Hyderabad, and Mumbai.
The city has a population of over 49,000 and a calm, intellectual atmosphere for students, the city is a great hub for education.

vision

Shramjivi Shikshan Prasark Mandal’s Shramjivi College of Pharmacy, Envisions the quality pharmacy education to create globally recognized and competent pharmacist with sound infrastructure, prolific academic atmosphere and comprehensive commitment

Mission

  • M: 2 - Value based education
  • M: 3 - To create an effective collaboration with pharmaceutical industries, business and communities in order to make to education responsive to bring requisite changes among the students / stakeholders
