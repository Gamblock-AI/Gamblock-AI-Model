Features

SCOUTASTIC is the leading platform for planning and managing scouting activities. Scouting reports are created directly in the platform and analysed using powerful AI to identify patterns and provide in-depth insights. Thanks to an exclusive integration of player data from transfermarkt.de, users also have direct access to valuable information. The combination of AI-supported analysis, report statistics and transfermarkt.de data provides a strong basis for decision-making. Top clubs worldwide rely on SCOUTASTIC to take their scouting to the next level.

PARTNER

SCOUTASTIC – exclusive Transfermarkt Data Partner

Exclusive Transfermarkt Data Partner

Big data is becoming increasingly important in football. Transfermarkt.de is one of the most important and largest databases in the world of football. SCOUTASTIC has exclusive rights to the official Transfermarkt.de data. The football information is used as the basis for the scouting software. This provides football scouts with all the important data on a player. Furthermore, SCOUTASTIC offers an API. Clubs that use SCOUTASTIC can thus legally and securely create further scouting analyses using Transfermarkt data.

Scouting with Soccerdonna.de Data

With the help of the comprehensive database for women’s football data Soccerdonna.de and all the features of SCOUTASTIC, we also make scouting in women’s football easier. Create shortlists, find new talents and write scouting reports. With the help of the editor, missing data can be easily added for complete data management. Switching between the men’s and women’s sections with just one click enables seamless collaboration between both scouting areas.

GENERAL

GENERAL

Write Reports and Analyse them with AI

Whether it’s a report on a player, match or tournament: With SCOUTASTIC, you can write reports based on your own templates. View the statistics for all reports and see what ratings a player receives on average or how they develop over time. With the help of artificial intelligence and the latest LLM technology, relevant content is extracted from the reports, filtered and then displayed – adapted to the respective club jargon.

Shortlists

Our shortlists give a unique insight into potential additions to your team. This feature allows you to create speculative lineups and categorise comparable players within positions by colour coding them. This enables you to compare different players and formations to find the best possible team for every situation. Use shortlists to make your transfer decisions based on in-depth data and analysis.

Search and Find

With our advanced search, you can find exactly the player who will strengthen your team according to the criteria you have in mind. Filter the search results according to subjective impressions from your own reports or using objective data sources. Search the shortlists you have created and filter by assigned tags. You can also compare selected players in a direct overview and discover who fits best into the team.

Area Search

With our area search, you can easily create your scouting plan. The area search allows you to search for matches around you or in specific locations and offers various filter options to refine your search results. This allows you to find relevant matches nearby, optimise your schedule and ultimately see more players in action. Whether you’re planning your weekend or looking for new players on the go, the map search ensures that you’re always in the right place at the right time.

Flexible Data Sources

All basic data is already included in SCOUTASTIC. In addition, further data sources can be flexibly connected and evaluated, including dynamic Internet content and social media data.

SQUAD MANAGER

SQUAD MANAGER

Plan your squad easily on a virtual pitch. Be as flexible as possible! Create different budget scenarios and plan loans, departures and new signings. The squad planner automatically analyses for example the age distribution, market values and salary structure of your squad, giving you an optimal overview of your team. In the pitch view, you can assign character types, squad roles and individual labels, display contract data and make adjustments using drag & drop.

SPORT DIRECTORS

SPORT DIRECTORS

Coach Scouting

Our software offers you a powerful tool for coach scouting. With this feature, you can search specifically for talented coaches who perfectly match the requirements of your football club. Filter by various criteria such as experience, track record, playing style and much more. Find the ideal coach who can lead your team to success and optimise your scouting processes effortlessly. Easily organise your favourite coaches in shortlists and keep track of potential candidates.

Compare Players

SCOUTASTIC allows you to discover players who are similar to a player you have selected. Using advanced algorithms, we analyse extensive player data and find players who are similar to the selected player in terms of playing style, position, strengths and weaknesses. Identify potential reinforcements who will fit seamlessly into your team and significantly improve your scouting efficiency.

DATA SCIENTISTS

DATA SCIENTISTS

REST API for Accessing Transfermarkt & Soccerdonna Data

Export Option for Platform Content

Business intelligence made easy: Our highly configurable scouting platform allows all content, such as reports, notes, or shadow teams, to be integrated directly into your company’s BI platform via the REST API. Authorisation management ensures that sensitive content is only visible to authorised user accounts.

ORGANISATION

ORGANISATION

Data Security

Multi-level rights management, role concept and maximum data security: SCOUTASTIC offers a wide range of options such as ticket orders, appointment approvals, content visibility, private data and targeted sharing, allowing you to optimally map every club-specific workflow. Whether you want to run it in the club’s data centre or as software-as-a-service, the decision is entirely up to you!

Scouting Calendar

An optimised calendar for all scouting applications: More than just a simple planner, the SCOUTASTIC calendar allows you to manage appointments efficiently and share them with colleagues for better coordination. Appointment coordinators can easily assign tasks and link appointments to players, matches, coaches, competitions and training sessions. You can filter by user groups, display ticket order status using colour coding, and assign scouting assignments. Thanks to easy integration with any calendar application, you always have your team’s schedule at your fingertips, no matter where you are.

Ticket Management on a Whole New Level

SCOUTASTIC is the ideal platform for managing ticket orders. With different user roles, tickets can be requested, ordered and approved. Email notifications remind users of personal tasks, while customisable email templates simplify the ordering process.

DATA-SCOUTS

DATA-SCOUTS

Perfect Interplay

At SCOUTASTIC, you have the opportunity to seamlessly link subjective impressions such as reports and evaluations with objective data. We understand that, in addition to pure statistical data, personal observations and assessments also play an important role in the scouting process. Our platform allows you to combine these subjective impressions with objective data and make a comprehensive assessment. Create a comprehensive picture of players and coaches by not relying solely on pure numbers, but using a combination of subjective impressions and objective data to make the best decisions for your team.

ACADEMY

ACADEMY

Discover tomorrow’s talent today. With SCOUTASTIC, you can efficiently organise not only professional scouting, but also youth scouting. You can integrate data from third-party providers or intuitively create players, teams, competitions and matches and enter them into the tool. The change in age groups after the season has also been taken into account. With just a few clicks, you can determine which players should move up to the higher U-team.

CLUB-IT

CLUB-IT

Flexible Hosting

We offer flexible hosting that meets your individual requirements. Our cloud hosting solution complies with the strict requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and ensures that your data is stored securely. However, if you prefer an on-premise option, we can also accommodate you. We adapt to your preferences and offer you the option of choosing the hosting environment that best suits your needs. You have control over your data with the hosting option that best suits your business.

Single Sign-On (SSO)

With our single sign-on (SSO) feature, we offer you the option of setting up centralised authorisation management for your company. You can give your employees easy and secure access to our platform with a single login process. This not only simplifies the management of access rights, but also increases the security and efficiency of your IT infrastructure. With our SSO feature, you retain full control over access rights and ensure a smooth workflow in your company.

Seamless Data Migration

Our many years of experience with various providers such as ISF, ISB, SAP Sports One and Soccerlab enable us to ensure smooth data migration. We understand the challenges that can come with changing software providers. That’s why we make sure that the transition is as easy as possible for you. We support you in seamlessly transferring your existing data into our software so that you can immediately benefit from our advanced features.
