Smartphone Monitoring

Smartphone monitoring through Corrisoft’s AIR® suite transforms traditional supervision by moving beyond basic GPS tracking to create deeper accountability and engagement. By providing participants with an AIR Mobile device—or through smartphone apps like AIR Verify and AIR Check In—this approach enables two-way communication, scheduled and randomized biometric check-ins, facial recognition, and passive GPS monitoring. These features allow participants to self-manage calendars, request travel exceptions, and immediately notify supervision officers if circumstances change in real time. Smartphone monitoring encourages participants to carry their device at all times, reinforcing accountability and reducing opportunities for evasion. It integrates reminders, compliance alerts, and zone-based accountability while offering educational and support resources, resulting in stronger behavior change outcomes, fewer compliance failures, and improved participant-staff engagement.

Unlike rigid, outdated monitoring systems, this mobile-first approach fits more naturally into participants’ daily lives—making compliance easier and more consistent. Whether your agency supports probation, treatment courts, pretrial release, or residential reentry, Corrisoft helps modernize your correction system with adaptable smartphone monitoring and GPS tracking technology. With tools built for flexibility and real-time response, it supports a more human, responsive, and effective model of supervision.
