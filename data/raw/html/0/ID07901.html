  • What is the Fit-for-Purpose Framework?

The Fit-For-Purpose Framework is a tool for business owners and product managers to
  • make clear strategic marketing decisions, 
  • greatly improve customer satisfaction and business outcomes.
The F4P Framework includes guidance on:
  • segmenting your market by customer purpose, 
  • determining the criteria customers use to make a selection amongst competitors,
  • determining meaningful metrics and KPIs.
The F4P Framework combines both narrative- and data-based approaches to sensing customer segments, their criteria and assessing fitness.

  • What is there in the F4P Framework for me?

F4P Framework will help you with:
  • More actionable market research
  • Better OKRs
  • Better personas
  • Better product requirements

Fit-for-Purpose Framework answers the following questions:

How do you know whether a product change is an improvement?

How to build products that your customers need?

How do you create the right product-related metrics?
