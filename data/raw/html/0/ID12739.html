What personal data is collected on this website?

For the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, VIADS ADVERTISING, S.L. collects and processes the following personal data, which will depend on the different products or services requested on this website:

On what legal basis are these data processed?

Your personal data are processed on the following legal bases:

  • Execution of a contract with VIADS ADVERTISING, S.L., for the contracting of services and/or products and managing the requested services.

How will your data be used?

On this website, there may be different forms. In each of them, the information collected will be used in the following way:

  • Subscription forms to content: The data provided will be used exclusively to activate the subscription, send the newsletter, and keep you updated on news and occasional offers exclusive to our subscribers.

Duration of personal data retention

The personal data provided will be kept for the shortest possible time considering the reasons for which the data processing is necessary, as well as the legal obligations to retain the data for a specific period, such as labor, tax, or anti-fraud legislation which require the preservation of personal data for a determined period or until the interested party withdraws their consent.

Subsequently, the data will be deleted according to the data protection regulations, which implies their blocking, being available only at the request of Judges and Courts, the Public Prosecutor's Office, or the competent Public Administrations during the period of prescription of the actions that could derive and, after this time, they will be completely deleted.

  • Client data: The period of retention of personal data will vary depending on the service contracted by the client. In any case, it will be the minimum necessary, and may be maintained until:
    • 4 years: Arts. 66 et seq. General Tax Law (accounting books...).

    • 10 years: Art. 25 of the Law on Prevention of Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism.

Interested persons have the right to:

  • Request its rectification or deletion.
  • Request its cancellation.
  • Request the limitation of its processing.
  • Oppose the processing.

Request the portability of the data. If you have granted your consent for a specific purpose, you have the right to withdraw the consent given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to its withdrawal.

In case you feel that your rights concerning the protection of your personal data are violated, especially when you have not obtained satisfaction in the exercise of your rights, you can lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Control Authority. To exercise these rights, you can write to hr@viads.com. The forms can also be presented in person or sent by letter to the contact addresses of the responsible party indicated above.

In case of acting through legal representation, you must also provide the ID and document accrediting the representation of the representative.
