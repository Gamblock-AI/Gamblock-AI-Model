Start Your Healing Journey With the 7 Essentials System®

The practical & proven plan for holistic healing.

If you are here, you may be feeling overwhelmed, confused, or unsure of what to do next.

A breast cancer diagnosis — or the fear of recurrence — can leave you navigating a maze of conflicting information, urgent decisions, and emotional uncertainty.

At Breast Cancer Conqueror®, we believe healing is not about chasing a single treatment or protocol. It is about restoring the internal environment that allows your body to function, repair, and thrive.

The 7 Essentials System® is the foundational roadmap we use to guide women through an integrative, whole-body approach to healing, prevention, and long-term resilience. Developed through lived experience, clinical practice, and decades of research, this framework helps you address root causes while supporting your body alongside conventional care.

Whether you are newly diagnosed, currently in treatment, navigating life after cancer, or seeking prevention strategies, this page will help you understand where to begin and how to move forward with clarity and confidence

Below, you will find a guided overview of each Essential and suggested starting points to support your next steps.

How to Use This Framework

You do not need to implement everything at once. Healing is a layered process.

Start by exploring the Essential that most closely aligns with your current needs. Over time, you will begin integrating multiple pillars as your healing journey evolves.

If you are unsure where to start, we recommend beginning with:

  • Essential #1 (Nutrition & terrain support)

  • Essential #4 (Emotional healing)

  • Essential #7 (Understanding testing & monitoring)

These three pillars create the foundation for informed, empowered decision-making.
