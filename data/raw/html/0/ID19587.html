Welcome to the Heart of New England

At the crossroads of the region’s major arteries, and encompassing New England’s second largest city; building on a four hundred year tradition of participatory citizenship that helped birth our nation; reflecting the commitment to hard work and innovation that drove the industrial revolution, and continues to drive a boom in entrepreneurism, technology, and healthcare, Heart of New England Council is proud to support the Scouting program in central Massachusetts and help grow the leaders of tomorrow. 

Our Council is comprised of two districts: 

QUINAPOXET District (Blue area in map below) 
SOARING EAGLE District (Red area in map below)
