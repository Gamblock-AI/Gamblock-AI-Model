Setting Sail from Spring 2027

Amangati

Amangati, Sanskrit for ‘peaceful motion’, presents a matchless experience on the water. The luxury motor yacht exclusively welcomes just 94 guests, comprising 47 airy and expansive suites, each with a private terrace opening to uninterrupted vistas. At Amangati’s heart, the Selora Marina and Lounge offers a seamless connection to the sea, complemented by shaded decks and alfresco spaces. Dining follows the seasons, while the Aman Spa, the largest in luxury yachting, crowns the experience with a two-storey wellness facility comprising a Japanese serenity garden and ocean-facing treatment rooms that restore balance.

Discover more

In Flowing Rhythm

Days aboard Amangati find their own natural cadence. Mornings invite movement and light. Afternoons linger over conversation, swimming and unhurried pleasures. And evenings settle into music, dining and calm reflection. Every experience feels intuitive, allowing space for relaxed connection.
