      Klub Bunga Butik Resort

      make your holiday unforgettable experience

      Located on the gentle slopes of Panderman Hills, situated at 1000 meters above sea level, Klub Bunga is impressively lanscaped in a spectacular 12 hectares of lush tropical greenery. Quietly breathaking views of mount Semeru, mount Welirang and mount Arjuno, where the legendary mount Bromo sunrise is only an hour away. The resort interior details reflect a rich culture heritage contrasted by modern architecture, creating an aesthetic impression to delight, the visual sense.
