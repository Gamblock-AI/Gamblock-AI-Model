MetaX Privacy Policy

Last Updated Date: April 1, 2022.

METAX SOFTWARE PTE.LTD., Ltd. (“MetaX”) is a data-driven Connected TV (CTV) advertising and operation company. We know how important privacy is to our users, and we strive to be clear about how we collect, use, disclose, transfer, and store (collectively, “process”) your personal information.

This Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy”) explains our personal information processing practices, and applies to all MetaX products, software, services and websites (collectively, the “Services”) and our corporate website at www.MetaXSoft.com (“Site”). This Privacy Policy applies regardless of whether you use a computer, mobile phone, tablet, TV, set-top box or other smart device (“Device”) to access our Services or our Site.

It is important that you read the Privacy Policy carefully because it provides information on how your personal information is processed anytime you use our Services.

It is also important that you check back often for updates to the Privacy Policy. If we update the Privacy Policy we will let you know in advance of any material changes, by placing a notice on the relevant Services or by e-mailing you, when appropriate. The most current version of the Privacy Policy will always be available here https://www.metaxsoft.com/privacy-policy. You can check the “Last Updated Date” posted at the top to see when the Privacy Policy was last updated.

Opt-Out From MetaX Personalized Advertising

Depending on the Device, application, web browser and service you use, you may be able to opt out from our personalized ads. Please follow the below instructions:

   To opt-out from your web browser in any devices:

If you opt-out, you will still see ads, including contextually tailored ads (i.e., ads tailored to the current website or app you are viewing) and ads based on your current location; they just may not be relevant to your interests.

The opt-out mechanism will only opt you out of personalized advertising delivered by MetaX in this browser or in applications on this Device. It will not opt you out on other devices or in other browsers you use.

Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default.  If you prefer, you can usually choose to set your browser to remove or reject browser cookies.   Please note that if you choose to remove or reject cookies, this could affect the availability and functionality of our Services. Opt-out information is stored in a cookie If you delete your cookies, this information will also be deleted, and you will need to renew your opt-out choices.

   To opt-out from an in an app environment:

If you opt-out, you will still see ads, including contextually tailored ads (i.e., ads tailored to the current website or app you are viewing) and ads based on your current location, they just may not be relevant to your interests.

If you want to disable MetaX personalized ads, please refer to your operating system settings.

Some Device system software are providing mechanisms that allow users to opt-out of the use of information about their usage of apps to deliver targeted ads to their Device. For more information, or to opt-out using these mechanisms, you may consult your Device settings, contact the manufacturer or retailer of your Device, or the operator of the application. Please note that a Device setting may disable personalized ads from all providers, and not just for MetaX.

If your Device’s advertising identifier is reset, you will need to renew your opt-out choice for that device.
