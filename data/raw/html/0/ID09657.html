ITF Recognition of Coach Education System

The objective of the ITF`s Recognition of Coach Education Systems programme is to ensure that coach education delivered in member nations meets the minimum ITF standards.

ITF recognition is based on criteria and minimum standards for coach education agreed upon by the ITF Task Force on Coach Education and subsequently endorsed by the ITF Coaches’ Commission and the ITF Board of Directors.

For further information on this process, please click here or contact us at education@itftennis.com
