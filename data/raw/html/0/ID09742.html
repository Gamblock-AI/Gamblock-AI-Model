Sound Purpose: Value-led. People-Focused. Science-Based.

At HARMAN, sustainability is central to our strategy. We set ambitious, science-based goals to reduce emissions, source renewable energy, and foster a culture of inclusion and innovation. Our program is built on transparency, measurable impact, and long-term value for our stakeholders.
