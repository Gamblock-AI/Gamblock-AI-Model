Pesan Rektor

Assalamu’alaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuh,

On behalf of Sunan Kalijaga State Islamic University (UIN Sunan Kalijaga), Yogyakarta, it is my honour and privilege to welcome you to our official website.

As one of Indonesia’s leading Islamic universities, UIN Sunan Kalijaga is steadfastly committed to integrating Islamic values with modern sciences, fostering innovation, and promoting inclusivity in education. Guided by our motto, “Integrative-Interconnective,” we aim to harmonize traditional wisdom with contemporary knowledge, nurturing well-rounded individuals ready to address global challenges.

UIN Sunan Kalijaga is a place where knowledge converges with values, and innovation propels progress. With its current leadership tagline, “Empowering Knowledge,” this university is dedicated to fostering a transformative educational experience that empowers individuals and communities alike.

At UIN Sunan Kalijaga, we believe that education is not merely a pathway to personal and professional success but also a powerful instrument for shaping a better, more just, and sustainable future. By integrating Islamic principles with modern sciences and interdisciplinary approaches, we strive to produce graduates who are intellectually advanced, ethically grounded, and socially responsible.

Our campus is a vibrant and diverse community where students, faculty, and staff collaborate to advance academic excellence, uphold ethical values, and contribute meaningfully to society. With a wide range of programs across various faculties, UIN Sunan Kalijaga offers abundant opportunities for academic growth, research, and community engagement, all grounded in a spirit of openness and collaboration.

I invite you to explore this website to uncover the opportunities and resources available at UIN Sunan Kalijaga. Whether you are a prospective student, a researcher, or a valued partner, I warmly welcome you to join us in our journey of empowering knowledge and shaping the future. Together, we can make a meaningful and lasting impact on society and beyond.

Thank you for visiting our website. May your journey with UIN Sunan Kalijaga be inspiring and fulfilling.

Wassalamu’alaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuh
