New categories announced for CRN Channel Awards Asia 2026

There are five new categories for vendors and partners to consider this year.

Aaron Raj
10 February 2026 • 3 min read

Share

The CRN Channel Awards Asia 2026 is back with more categories this year to recognize and celebrate the success of the channel ecosystem in the Asian region. Taking place in Singapore, this year’s awards will have 30 categories for vendors and partners to participate in.

The prestigious awards recognize and reward the outstanding achievements of the individuals and teams responsible for driving the Asian IT channel forward. From vendors and distributors to marketplaces and resellers, the awards honour the people powering successful channel businesses across the region.

Among the most popular categories in 2025 included categories for cybersecurity vendors, channel managers, AI innovation as well as women leaders from both the vendor and partner ecosystem.

With a fully independent and rigorous judging process, the CRN Channel Awards Asia continue to stand among the most respected and sought-after honors in the global IT industry, firmly established as the Oscars of the IT channel.
