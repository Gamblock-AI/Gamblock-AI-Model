About Us

GlobeTrek Engineering Corporation is a trusted Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter of Civil Engineering & Scientific Laboratory Equipment.

Globetrek Engineering Corporation is one of the Few Indian organizations which constantly working with the Goal to developed Most advance Testing Equipment’s in India which match the International Quality.  We had started our Journey from Developing Most Advance Test Sieves in the market . Over the period of time we have diversified our range of product from Testing Sieves to Soil testing equipment’s to Rock , Concrete Cement, Highway , Aggregate, Metal , Bitumen , Survey & oil-Petroleum Testing Equipment’s .

Globetrek is exporting its products and Innovative technology to more than 34 Countries all over the Globe through the dealer network of Civil Laboratory Equipments & also provide OEM services to various International brands .

Our Services

Globetrek Engineering Corporation offers installation, Training and Calibration services to our clients for all the products manufactured by us.

For any new Civil Engineering laboratory equipment, it is essential to install the equipment correctly to ensure long term performance of the testing systems. Team Globetrek  offers a full installation and commissioning service maximizing the return on investment in equipment.

Globetrek trained engineers have extensive product and test application knowledge coupled with world-wide experience in the installation of complete testing laboratories. This work is normally carried out on-site in the customer’s testing laboratory following installation of equipment.

Some of our Focused products which requires calibration services annually are Compression Testing Machine, Total Stations, Digital Levels, Automatic Levels, Theodolite, Test Sieves, Cube Moulds etc. Our highly trained service engineers carry out calibration at our fully equipped laboratory.
