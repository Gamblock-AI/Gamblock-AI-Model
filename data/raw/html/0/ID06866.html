with us

YouVersion exists to connect everyone, everywhere,
to God’s Word every day. And volunteers play a key
role in making this vision become a reality.

Could this
be your next
step ?

You were made by God, on purpose, for a purpose. God gave you unique skills and abilities to impact people all over the world. And when you serve with YouVersion, you can deepen your relationship with Jesus while helping people seek God every day.

‍

Find a serving opportunity that aligns with your passions, talents, and schedule by looking through the list below. And don’t worry—we offer specialized training so you can consistently serve with confidence.
