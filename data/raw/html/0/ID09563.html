Signup

How to Sign up

  1. Visit http://www.whiz-mate.id and click the menu sign up.
  2. Fill out the membership registration form with name, e-mail, and desired password.
  3. The validation link will be forwarded to the registered e-mail. Guests are required to click that link to verify their account.
  4. After the validation is completed, click the menu log in to view member dashboard. In it guests can view various information such as their membership number and their electronic membership details
