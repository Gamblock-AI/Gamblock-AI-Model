    Cookies Preference Center

    Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer's hardware or your mobile device. Cookies assign an anonymous identifier to your browser and automatically provides information to the cookie sender. If you access Marcel via your browser, we use persistent cookies to authenticate you. When you sign in to the platform using your Lion Login, we store a unique ID number, and the time you signed in, in an encrypted persistent cookie on your device. This cookie allows you to move through the platform without having to sign in again on each page and saves your sign-in information so that you do not have to sign in each time you use the Marcel platform. While you may still use Marcel if you reject cookies, you will have to log in each time you visit certain pages on the site.
