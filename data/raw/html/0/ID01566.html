End of Term 1 and early dismissal

A busy and productive Term 1 ends on Thursday 2 April, and students will be dismissed early at 2:30pm. Grandparents & Special Friends Day will be celebrated on the last day of term from 11:30am to 1:30pm. Term 2 commences on Monday 20 April.

READ MORE

Pink Stumps Day

Our annual Pink Stumps Day will be held on Wednesday 1 April in support of the McGrath Foundation. We’re looking forward to coming together by wearing pink on the day, participating in cricket-themed activities and our traditional Year 6 vs teachers cricket match. Donation portal now open.

READ MORE

School Tours - book now

Bookings are now open to come and see our school in action. We welcome families interested in enrolment at EHPS in the 2026 and 2027 school years to attend a school tour. Tours are conducted from March to November and are held weekly during school terms. Phone our Office to secure your place.

READ MORE

Entertainment Membership supports EHPS

Support EHPS by purchasing an Entertainment Membership. The current offer is a Buy One Membership, Get One Free. Outstanding value for money considering the membership is basically paid for with one or two visits to restaurants.
