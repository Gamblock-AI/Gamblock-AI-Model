RE-ELECT REP. QUANTÁ CREWS BACK TO THE HOUSE!

Hi! I’m Quantá, your representative for Legislative District 26, serving Central West Phoenix and Glendale.

 

I moved here in 2005 to attend Grand Canyon University, and this community became my home. I know what it means to work multiple jobs just to stay afloat, keep a roof over your head, food on the table, and care for your family. That lived experience is what drives my work.

 

Securing community victories in a Republican majority legislative has it's challenges but together we  have delivered. iN 2025, Arizona House Democrats passed a bipartisan $17.6 billion budget that invests in K-12 education, transportation, kinship care, services for our elders all while protecting Medicaid, expanding cancer care, supporting working families with free college tuition and skiled trades training, and lowering childcare costs. Has it been perfect? No. But together we have made real progress and it kept our state running.

 

I’m running for re-election to continue this work and fight for policies that bring stability, opportunity, and relief to our community
