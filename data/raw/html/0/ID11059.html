NEWS

JAKARTA (INDONESIA), March 31, 2026 – The brilliance of the stars is about to illuminate all of Asia! F✦FOREVER, consisting of the members of F4 - Jerry Yan, Van Ness Wu and Vic Chou — along with Ashin, the lead vocalist of the "King of Concerts" band, Mayday, will hold a highly anticipated phenomenal concert in Indonesia titled "F✦FOREVER 1st World Tour” which will take place for two days on Friday-Saturday, May 29-30, 2026, at the magnificent indoor venue of the country, Indonesia Arena, in the GBK Senayan area, Jakarta, promoted by Color Asia Live and presented by Panin Bank.Tickets for this concert only available through the page www.fforeverindonesia.com or the Loket.com application and website starting Saturday, April 4, 2026 at 10:00 AM WIB (GMT+7/Jakarta Local Time).With nearly 300 people and a spectacular production budget, the concert will boast high-end specifications with Four-Point Star Stage: An exclusive stage design that allows the performers to connect with fans from all sides. The concert fully showcases the musical charm of the four stars, connecting the entire concert with the chapter of “Faith + Friends + Fans + Family + Fixed stars” that will transform the venue into a universe filled with stars, lighting up the City of Stars with the fans.David Ananda, Managing Director of Color Asia Live, said, “This isn’t just a concert; it’s a historic celebration. Bringing Jerry, Van Ness, Vic, and Ashin together on one stage in Jakarta is a dream come true for millions of their fans in Indonesia.” Not only that, David continued, "With a magnificent indoor venue in the country such as the Indonesia Arena in the GBK area, Senayan, Jakarta, we want to provide a spectacular and magnificent concert experience, with a concert production concept that will be made on a large scale like other F✦FOREVER concerts around the world."Meanwhile, Sugiono Sutanto Janis, Director of Business Banking & PIKK, stated that this concert is a long-awaited concert for the public, and PaninBank, as the main sponsor of the "F✦FOREVER 1st World Tour," is committed to providing added value to customers through exclusive experiences tailored to their lifestyle. This international-class concert aligns with PaninBank's commitment to delivering premium and meaningful experiences. Support for the "F✦FOREVER 1st World Tour" is one of the events commemorating PaninBank's 55th anniversary.For information and details on how to obtain tickets through PaninBank, please visit your nearest branch or call Panin at 1500678 or 60678.
