Everything You Want to Know

How quickly can I get my policy issued through ZenInsure?

We offer one of the fastest policy issuance processes in the Philippines. Most policies are issued within just a few hours after your documents are verified and payment is completed.

What types of vehicles does ZenInsure cover?

We cover all kinds of Cars, Motorcycles, and Trucks. We also offer CTPL insurance - a legal requirement for all vehicle owners.

How much can I save with ZenInsure?

You can save up to 60% on your car insurance renewal compared to traditional market rates, thanks to our strong partnerships and tech-driven efficiencies.

Can I choose monthly payments instead of a full upfront payment?

Yes! ZenInsure offers flexible payment options - choose between easy monthly installments or a one-time full payment, whichever suits you best.

What makes ZenInsure different from other insurance providers?

We’re 100% digital, focused on fast service, personalized claims support, and affordable insurance plans tailored for today’s online-savvy and intelligent customers. We also offer complete backend support for mortgaged vehicles.

What documents do I need to get insured?

Generally, you need a valid government ID, your OR/CR for the vehicle, and a copy of your previous insurance (if applicable). Our team will guide you step-by-step. For new vehicles, we just need the sales invoice, rest our team will take care of.

How can I get assistance if I have a claim?

We offer dedicated VIP concierge support - our claims desk personally assists you through every step of the process to ensure a quick and hassle-free experience - a complete Zen experience. We also provide a personalized online claim tracker, the first of its kind in the country.

Is ZenInsure available across the Philippines?

Yes! Whether you’re in Manila, Cebu, Davao, or any part of the Philippines, you can access ZenInsure services easily online or via phone or in person.
