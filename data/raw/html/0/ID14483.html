Tampa’s Premier Waterfront Convention Center¶

Located in the heart of Downtown Tampa, the award-winning Tampa Convention Center offers 600,000 square feet of flexible event space, state-of-the-art amenities, and stunning waterfront views -- all under the Florida sun. 

Recognized as the Best Convention Center in the Southeast by the Northstar Stella Awards and several other prestigious awards, it’s the ideal destination for large conferences, conventions, trade shows, and corporate events.
