      Luxury Hotels and Resorts Choose TOTO Products

      Across the globe, luxury hotels and resorts choose TOTO to deliver unforgettable guest experiences. Our luxury products blend performance, comfort, and design — elevating hotel spaces and setting a new standard for the premium guest experience. From intuitive smart toilets and WASHLET bidet seats to sculptural faucets and artfully crafted lavatories, TOTO products add elegance to every detail.

      Architects and designers rely on TOTO to create spa-inspired environments that feel both indulgent and effortless. Whether planning resort-style retreats or seeking refined upgrades, TOTO offers designer solutions that align with the most sophisticated global design trends.
