CENTRAL and CONNECTED

A transit-oriented development strategically positioned just a few steps away from Bunderan HI, Thamrin Nine redefines convenience and connectivity with 4 railway connections – MRT, LRT, Commuter Line and the Airport Train.

In the future, the complex will also have direct access to Grand Indonesia and Dukuh Atas MRT Station, making it the most connected superblock in the CBD.

BUSINESS MEETS LIFESTYLE

Thamrin Nine provides a seamless, integrated workplace experience with signature features and facilities designed for convenience and reliability.

The luxury experience begins as soon as you arrive at the grand lobby with VIP drop-offs and destination-controlled lifts, continues with washrooms, showers and smoking balconies at every level and ends with a myriad of F&B, sports and entertainment facilities to wind down after your work day. Thamrin Nine has everything you need to fit into your schedule 24/7, so you can have the freedom and flexibility to live, work and play any way you please.

A MONUMENTAL DEVELOPMENT

Thamrin Nine redefines mixed-use developments in the capital of Indonesia. A first-of-its-kind, world-class superblock with a complex breadth of offerings that includes luxury offices, F&B spaces, sports and entertainment facilities and a range of hospitality offerings, including PARKROYAL Hotel & Serviced Suites, Pan Pacific Hotel and the first Waldorf Astoria Hotel in Indonesia.

Thamrin Nine features a retail podium, a low-density, luxury residential property, and two mixed-use towers: Autograph and Luminary. The former has reached unprecedented heights, becoming the tallest building in the Southern Hemisphere. Both towers are also the tallest buildings in Indonesia.

KPF / ARCHITECT

KPF is a unified architectural practice focused on the design of buildings of all types and scales, in all geographic regions. KPF was founded in 1976 by A. Eugene Kohn, William Pedersen, and Sheldon Fox. The firm consists of 550 staff led by 30 principals, with offces in New York, London, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Seoul, and Abu Dhabi.

WATG / ARCHITECT

WATG is an International design practice that is diverse, innovative, and entrepreneurial. The company brought together design talents from diverse cultural, global and regional backgrounds, resulting in a vibrant studio environment where individuals team up, learn, collaborate and inspire one another.

PUTRAGAYA WAHANA / DEVELOPER

Thamrin Nine is developed by PT PutraGaya Wahana (PGW),
a dynamic Indonesi an development group founded in 1993, known for the iconic UOB Building in the Thamrin Corridor.

Unique for its integrated approach and broad mix, Thamrin Nine will, for the first time, offer Jakartans the choice and convenience of other world-class cities. Coupled with a vision for a sustainable future, Thamrin Nine redefines working, living and playing in the heart of Jakarta.
