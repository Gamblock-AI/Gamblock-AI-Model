FAQ

NordProtect is a comprehensive identity theft and cyber protection service brought to you by Nord Security. It provides all-around protection against identity theft, including credit monitoring, dark web surveillance, security alerts, identity theft recovery, cyber extortion protection, and online fraud coverage.

Once you get your NordProtect subscription, you’ll need to set up your account. At this stage, you can add the information you want us to monitor (such as your email address, SSN, phone number, and credit card number). The more assets you add to the dashboard, the more comprehensive your online protection will be.

The platform will update regularly with information about your credit activity, credential security, dark web exposure, and other metrics related to the safety of your identity. You’ll be able to keep an eye on this information in one place and take action to secure your accounts when needed.

NordProtect scans the dark web 24/7 to ensure your personal information isn’t leaked on cybercriminal forums and marketplaces. What it tracks will depend on how much information you’ve provided, and it may include:
  • Your email addresses (work and personal)
  • Social Security number (SSN)
  • Phone numbers (US)
  • Credit or debit card numbers
NordProtect’s credit monitoring scans credit reports from a major credit bureau and notifies you about new activity that could indicate a higher risk of identity theft. This activity may include an inquiry into your credit, a new account in your name, or a delinquent account. Find out more on our credit monitoring feature page.

You have numerous rights under the FCRA, including the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report(s). Consumer reporting agencies are required to investigate and respond to your dispute but are not obligated to change or remove accurate information that is reported in compliance with applicable law. While this plan can provide you assistance in filing a dispute, the FCRA allows you to file a dispute for free with a consumer reporting agency without the assistance of a third party.
