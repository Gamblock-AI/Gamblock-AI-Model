It’s the 10th year of ASB hosting the Jarul Book Awards! The Jarul Book Awards are a unique and special recognition of children’s literature in India. They’re the only Indian book awards where students get to choose the winner, making it a truly democratic and child-centric initiative. The awards celebrate contemporary Indian picture books and empower children to read, voice their opinions, and select the best books. Congratulations to the winners this year and thank you to our many collaborates who make all this possible!
