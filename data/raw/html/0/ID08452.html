Know More

Nestled in the heart of Kolkata's vibrant Sudder Street, Astoria Hotel offers a prime location for leisure, business and medical travellers alike. Just a stone's throw away from the bustling Park Street Metro Station, the hotel provides easy access to the city's iconic landmarks, including the Indian Museum, New Market shopping area, and the Eden Gardens, all within a leisurely 15-minute stroll. Additionally, cultural gems like the Victoria Memorial are within reach.
At Astoria Hotel, our dedicated staff is committed to ensuring your stay is comfortable. We have round-the-clock assistance at our front desk and our rooms have modern amenities.
Astoria Hotel prides itself in blending contemporary comforts with warm hospitality. We have recently undergone renovations in order to make your stay more comfortable.
Location of Astoria Hotel makes it convenient to the following places:

  • Park Street Metro Station 500 meters
    (Offering seamless connectivity all around the ‘City of Joy’ including Howrah and Sealdah station and Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport)
  • Esplanade 700 meters
  • Sealdah 2 km
  • Howrah Station 5 km
  • Kolkata (Chitpur) Railway Station 7 km
  • Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport 14 km
