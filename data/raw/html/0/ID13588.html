What is a coach bus?

Coach buses are larger and more comfortable than regular buses, often with reclining seats, air conditioning, and onboard amenities like toilets. They are ideal for longer journeys.

What is the meaning of express bus ?

Express buses make fewer stops and travel directly between major towns/cities, offering faster travel times compared to regular buses.

How much is the luxury bus in Malaysia?

Luxury bus fares vary depending on the route, operator, and amenities. Expect to pay more than regular buses, typically ranging from RM50 to RM150+ per journey.

Is there any discount on online bus booking?

Yes, you can save up to 50% on bus tickets with redBus Malaysia. Additionally, there are redDeals that offer discounts and cashback offered by redBus partners from time to time. Please refer to the redBus website or download the app to know more about the latest offers and related T&C, if you are constantly on the lookout for the cheapest bus ticket booking platforms.

Can I cancel my bus ticket?

Yes, you can, provided that the bus operator supports it as not all operators offer a cancellation option when you book your tickets with them. You might get charged a percentage of the bus fare for canceling the bus ticket. You can visit our “Contact Us” page to stay updated on contact information that could help improve your bus booking experience.

Can I reschedule my bus ticket?

Yes, bus tickets can be rescheduled on redBus given that the bus company agrees with it. You can simply add your ticket number and email address, choose a reschedule date, and get confirmation on the same.

I missed the bus. Do I get a refund?

redBus will issue a refund if your bus trip is cancelled by redBus or by the offiical bus operator partner. However, refunds are not available for missed buses due to any other reasons.

What is the cancellation policy?

The cancellation policy differs from operator to operator. Generally, travelers can cancel up to 24–48 hours before the trip, but a small fee will be applicable on cancellation.

What is the price for an online bus ticket in Malaysia?

The bus ticket costs in Malaysia range between RM10 and RM140. However, the bus fare may vary depending on the bus operator, type of bus, bus routes, and availability. An express bus fare will be different from that of Coach bus tickets. You can find coach bus price and express bus prices both when you search for buses on your route. You can cheap bus tickets with discount offer codes.

What is the process of booking bus ticket online?
  • Choose your platform and sign up/log in (optional).
  • Enter your travel details (origin, destination, date).
  • Select a bus operator and preferred bus type/service (e.g., Express, Luxury).
  • Choose your seat and fare (promotions may apply).
  • Make a secure online payment using credit/debit card, e-wallet, or online banking.
  • Receive your e-ticket via email or app notification
Which bus is best in Malaysia?

Malaysia has a number of bus operators to choose from. It depends on your priorities and budget. Consider factors like comfort, amenities, travel time, and price. You can take a pick from the most popular ones like Transtar Travel, Sani Express, KKKL Express, Bus Asia, and more when traveling within Malaysia or to Singapore.When you book your bus tickets with redBus, you can choose from 30+ bus operators. 

Where to book buses in Malaysia?

Bus booking in Malaysia can is made easy with online bus ticket booking apps and at the bus terminal. You can visit the bus ticketing counters at any bus terminal or choose to book your ticket online. 

Is it safe to book bus ticket online?

Yes, reputable platforms use secure payment gateways and data encryption. Look for user reviews 

Can I track the location of my bus booked via redBus?

Several buses have GPS tracking system and hence you can track the location of your bus online. While booking your tickets, ensure to check the GPS tracking amenities listed for the particular operator. 

What are the payment methods for bus ticket online booking?

You can choose from various payment methods like Debit, Credit card, netbanking, FPX, Boost, Touch N Go wallet and more

How to pay bus fare in Malaysia?

There are several payment methods --Online payment: Pay securely when booking online with credit/debit cards, e-wallets, or online banking.-Cash payment: Pay directly to the bus conductor on board.-Cashless payment options like Touch 'n Go and MyKad are becoming increasingly common on certain routes.

Are there special services for elderly or disabled passengers?

Some operators offer priority boarding, wheelchair access, and assistance for elderly or disabled passengers. Always check with the operator in advance.

Are there discounts for students or frequent travelers?

Yes, many operators offer discounts for students, frequent travelers, or groups. Check the specific operator's promotions during booking.
