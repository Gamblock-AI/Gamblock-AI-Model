OUR BRANDS


Apple Premium Partner

iStudio is Singapore’s largest Apple Premium Partner, bringing a boutique Apple Experience closer to you. iStudio opened its inaugural boutique at Terminal 3 of Singapore Changi Airport in 2007; being one of the first Apple Premium Resellers located in a Duty Free Departure/Transit Hall. iStudio is proudly Singapore’s first and the only Apple Premium Partner to offer tax-free purchases for patrons departing from the Singapore Changi Airport, also available on iShopChangi. In 2016, they launched their eCommerce, istudiosg.com, reaching out to larger audiences in Singapore and beyond, offering a wide variety of exclusive online promotions only at iStudio.


Tech Lifestyle Brand

Since 2018, EpiCentre under Elush, is one of Singapore’s leading Multi-Brand stores that provides a safe haven for technology enthusiasts with a wide array of consumer electronic devices and gadgets to explore and discover. EpiCentre was the first Apple Premium Reseller in Asia, and a world-class digital lifestyle brand committed to enrich your digital lifestyles with innovative value and awesomely great hospitality. Carrying a wide range of products from iOS to Android devices, EpiCentre continues to enrich digital lifestyles in Singapore. You may find EpiCentre’s Concept Store at Amazon SG.

About Elush Retail:

We are an organization of companies that begun operations in 2003. We currently operate and manage brands such as iStudio (Apple Premium Partner) and EpiCentre (Tech Lifestyle). We previously represented Nike, Celio and Tous in the Singapore market. Elush Retail Group is not a company. The brands are managed respectively by:

iStudio (Apple Premium Partner) – Elush (T3) Pte Ltd
EpiCentre – Elush (T3) Pte Ltd
Live Out Loud – Elush (T3) Pte Ltd
REFIT – RefitAsia Pte Ltd
Tous – Elush Jewellery (RC) Pte Ltd
Celio – Eternal Lush (S) Pte Ltd
Nike – Fitness & Become Pte Ltd

In 2007, Elush Retail Group was appointed Apple Premium Reseller. To date, Elush has expanded to 22 stores – 18 iStudio storefronts, of which 4 are within Singapore Changi Airport and several along Orchard Road as well as multiple in the heartlands.

In December 2016, iStudio revamped their website to launch their eCommerce business: www.istudiosg.com, offering exclusive online promotions and giving patrons the opportunity to purchase Apple products online to reserve their stock before heading to the respective stores for collection or to opt for free shipping, only applicable within Singapore.

With best retail practices, good teamwork, dedication to service excellence and constant improvement, Elush Retail Group has won several Excellent Service Awards (EXSA). This is a national award held annually and recognises individuals who have delivered quality service. The Singapore Retailers Association and SPRING Singapore are part of the organising committee of the event.
