Who Are We

Quran.Foundation is a deeply committed, purpose-driven non-profit organization dedicated to building platforms and supporting projects that aim to transform every human through Quranic guidance.

With a heartfelt mission to help individuals connect with the Quran, we offer Quran.com—a trusted resource serving millions for over a decade, and QuranReflect for deeper connection. We empower individuals to connect, reflect, and grow spiritually.

Rooted in authenticity and guided by a visionary purpose, we bridge tradition with technology to inspire hearts and ignite meaningful change. Whether you’re exploring or deepening your journey, Quran.Foundation makes the Quran’s timeless guidance accessible, transformative, and deeply personal.

Our Platforms

Quran for the web

Quran.com is a trusted platform for connecting with the Quran, offering reading, recitations, translations, tafsir, reflections, study tools, and a user-friendly experience. Serving millions globally, our mission is to make the Quran accessible, helping individuals connect deeply with Allah's words.

Quran for iOS

The Quran.com iOS app brings the trusted experience of Quran.com to your fingertips, offering a seamless and intuitive way to read, explore, and understand the Quran. Designed for ease of use and deep engagement, the app provides access to authentic Quranic text, translations, and study tools anytime, anywhere. We continue to enhance the app with new features, ensuring a smooth and enriching experience for users seeking a deeper connection with the Quran.

Quran for Android

The Quran.com Android app brings the Quran to millions of users worldwide, offering a seamless and intuitive experience for reading, listening, and understanding the Quran. Designed for ease of use, the app provides powerful tools to enhance engagement, including translations, recitations, and tafsir. We continue to refine and expand its features, ensuring that the Quran is always accessible at your fingertips.

QuranReflect

QuranReflect is our dynamic platform designed to foster deep engagement with the Quran through reflection and discussion. Alongside lessons from qualified teachers, we welcome personal insights that undergo expert quality review, led by Shaykh Hammad Fahim and our resident scholar Dr. Sohaib Saeed. Our mission is to inspire meaningful conversations and deepen the world’s relationship with the Book of Allah.

Visit Website
Quran Foundation API Portal

Our updated secure APIs are designed to empower Muslim App developers to build on top of the same backend that powers our apps. Our APIs provide the functionality and content on Quran.com and QuranReflect, facilitating easy access to comprehensive ayah and surah level content. Our user authentication enhances your App's functionality, enabling the sync of bookmarks, notes, reflections, reading history, streaks, and more. By integrating with our APIs users can enjoy a rich connected experience across Quranic apps, and you can focus on innovating unique solutions that the world needs.

Our Scholars

As Resident Scholar & Chief Content Officer at Quran.Foundation, Dr. Sohaib Saeed oversees Quran.com’s content, ensuring accuracy, depth, and clarity in presenting the Quran’s message. Recognized as a highly respected scholar in the field of Tafsir, he brings expertise in classical and contemporary Quranic studies to bridge scholarship with modern accessibility.

Through research, content curation, and collaborations, Dr. Sohaib plays a key role in making Quranic reflection and understanding more engaging and impactful for a global audience.
