    Prometric 's main services

    CBT

    CBT（Computer Based Testing）is a method of administering exams using a computer. Prometric 's proven CBT offers exam opportunities that are rigorous and fair at high-quality, convenient Test Center located in 160 cities across 47 prefectures.

    About CBT

    IBT

    Prometric 's IBT service allows test takers to take the test remotely under the same rigorous conditions as Test Center, with proctors monitoring the test. Candidate can take the test on their own computer at home or in the office with an internet connection.

    About IBT

    Computer-based testing for universities

    Prometric solves the challenges universities face, such as conducting tests nationwide, accommodating international students, and reducing the burden on faculty.

    About CBT for universities

    Test Development Service

    To maintain and improve the quality of your exams at a high level, our test development experts will accompany you from test specification design to result analysis.

    About Test development services

    About Prometric

    About us

    Prometric is a global leader in testing with over 35 years of experience.

    With the mission of cultivating the talent of the future, we provide the fairness and high level of security required for high-stakes exams such as national exams and public qualifications, and offer reliable service. From creating exam questions to delivering CBT, we support exam organizers with world-class services.

    • See company profile

    Commitment to Quality

    Prometric strives for customer satisfaction and provides services to customers accurately, promptly and with integrity.
    To this end, we operate our quality management system effectively and continually improve it.

    • See our commitment to quality

    About achievements

    Prometric supports Candidate around the world, delivering 7 million tests annually in over 180 countries. In Japan, we boast a track record of delivering 2.5 million tests annually and over 40 million tests in total.
