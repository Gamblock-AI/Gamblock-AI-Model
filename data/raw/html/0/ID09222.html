Message From President & CEO

Honda’s foundation is built on dreams and innovation. We prioritize original ideas, technologies, and designs, fueled by passion and unconventional thinking. By fearlessly tackling challenges, Honda has grown to provide diverse mobility solutions. Our goal is to empower people worldwide who strive for change and take on new initiatives, thereby contributing to the advancement of mobility and daily life.
