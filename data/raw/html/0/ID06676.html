What Is a Mini Travel Router? Why Do You Need One?

Staying connected on the go is part of everyday life. ASUS mini travel routers give you a more secure and reliable way to get online when using public WiFi or multiple devices — at home or on the road. With support for public WiFi (WISP mode), 4G/5G mobile tethering, and router mode, you can turn café, hotel, airport WiFi, or your phone’s hotspot into your own private, encrypted network. Connect once and easily share a fast, secure connection across all your devices.

Compact and easy to carry, ASUS mini routers are ideal for leisure travel, business trips, small spaces, and outdoor use. They can also work as an ASUS AiMesh router to extend your home network. Advanced users can further customize their setup with third-party open-source firmware.*
