TAG Registry

The TAG Registry is the official database for the global TAG Community, including leading brands, agencies, publishers, and ad tech providers.

Companies across the industry rely on the TAG Registry to determine trust and transparency in prospective partners, and to quickly screen and qualify those partners.

Before a company can be listed in the TAG Registry, it must complete the “Verified by TAG” process to ensure that each member of the TAG Community really and truly is who they say they are.
