    IC Philanthropy

    In 2009, IC Philanthropy was established by the IC Council to teach tennis to disadvantaged children. In some communities, the sport provides an alternative path from life on the streets and the dangers of drugs and crime.

    Our programmes also support young people with disabilities to improve their physical and mental skills whilst enjoying the company of others.

    Please visit the IC Philanthropy website to find out more about our projects.
