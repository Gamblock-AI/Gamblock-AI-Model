Elevated Padma Experience Awaits

For the past 16 years, Padma Hotel Bandung has been dedicated to providing unparalleled hospitality,
offering guests an immersive experience amidst nature with world-class service and exceptional amenities.

We are proud to announce the temporary closure of Padma Hotel Bandung,
effective on 20 April 2025, as part of a major transformation and expansion project.

This upcoming transformation will introduce new and refined accommodations, upgraded facilities, and rejuvenated amenities, ensuring an elevated guest experience while preserving the hotel’s signature warmth and charm.

We eagerly anticipate to unveiling a reimagined Padma Hotel Bandung
that will set new standards in luxury and excellence, in near future.
