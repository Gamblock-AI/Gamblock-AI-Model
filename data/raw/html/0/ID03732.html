What conditions can chiropractic care help with?

Chiropractic care focuses on the assessment and management of musculoskeletal conditions. This commonly includes back pain, neck pain, joint and muscle discomfort, and movement-related pain. Care is tailored to each individual and may help improve mobility, reduce discomfort, and support everyday activities.

Is chiropractic treatment safe?Tabea2026-02-06T12:43:00+00:00

Is chiropractic treatment safe?

Chiropractic care is considered safe when provided by a qualified and registered chiropractor. A thorough assessment is carried out before any treatment begins to ensure care is appropriate. If chiropractic care is not suitable, or if referral to another healthcare professional is needed, this will be discussed with you.

What happens at my first appointment?

Your first visit involves a detailed case history, physical examination, and discussion of your symptoms and goals. A personalised care plan will be explained, and you will always have the opportunity to ask questions before deciding how to proceed. Find out more here.

Do I need a GP referral to see a chiropractor?Tabea2026-02-06T12:44:58+00:00

Do I need a GP referral to see a chiropractor?

No, a GP referral is not required. Chiropractors are primary healthcare professionals, and you can book directly. If necessary, we may communicate with or refer you to your GP or another healthcare provider as part of your care.
