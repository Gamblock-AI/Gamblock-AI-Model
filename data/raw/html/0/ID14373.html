ABOUT US


Visoka (earlier known as Kalbhor Construction Company) came to life in 1975 when the Founder Member & Director, Mr. Vitthal Kalbhor decided to dedicate his professional life to the development of our motherland.

The company was established during the era when the socio-economic and industrial development of Pimpri Chinchwad was in its nascent stage. The company leveraged this progressive phase and began working on the infrastructure, road development and industrial turnkey projects. Bajaj Auto was the first client. Later on, John Deere, Škoda, Volkswagen, TE Connectivity, JSW joined the clientele portfolio.
