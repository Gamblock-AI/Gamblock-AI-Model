Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDF2Go

Is PDF2Go safe? Are files encrypted and auto-deleted?

Yes, PDF2Go is a secure and trusted online PDF editor operated by QaamGo Web GmbH, a German company founded in 2016 and compliant with GDPR. All file transfers use secure HTTPS connections, and your files are encrypted at rest while stored on our servers. We do not access or share your documents, and they are automatically deleted within 24 hours after processing. You can also delete your files manually at any time. PDF2Go gives you full control, strong data protection, and reliable service from a long-established provider.

How many free credits do I get and what counts as a credit?

When you use PDF2Go without registering, you automatically get a handful of free Credits to test most premium tools. After you create a free account, you receive 30 welcome Credits to explore all features. A Credit represents one premium action, for example, converting, compressing, or editing a file. Once your Credits are used up, you can upgrade to a paid plan at any time for unlimited access to all tools and faster processing or just wait a day to get your Credits refilled.

How fast does processing take?

Processing on PDF2Go is designed to be fast and efficient. Most files are converted, compressed, or edited within seconds, depending on file size, tool type, and server load. Simple tasks like merging or rotating PDFs are usually done almost instantly, while large or complex files may take a bit longer. Premium users enjoy priority processing for the fastest results possible - no waiting, just instant productivity.
