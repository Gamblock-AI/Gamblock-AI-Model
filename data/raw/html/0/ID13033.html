TaurusX is committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this document (“Privacy Policy”) carefully and with attention.

Section I. General Provisions

1.1 Who we are

TaurusX is a global programmatic advertising technology provider. We develop and operate various advertising platforms that serve targeted ads, plan campaign and optimize ads, whether by itself or through third party partners.

1.2 This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information, including personal information, may be collected, used, and disclosed by TaurusX in connection with its advertising technology platform and all advertising-related services delivered through it.  with respect to the following instances:

(i) use of this website located at www.taurusx.com (the "Website" or the "Site"), and

(ii) how personal information is collected, used, and processed through our technology (including, but not limited to, its SDK, API and real-time bidding systems) on behalf of the online advertisers, publishers, business partners, and other businesses that use TaurusX advertisement materials and/or inventory or services (hereinafter referred to as “clients”).

If you have any comments or queries about this Policy, or how we use your information, please contact us using the contact details provided below.

1.3 Our Services and Compliance with Law

In delivering our services, we typically process personal information on behalf of our clients as a data processor or independent controller. We do not directly interact with the owners of personal information unless the owner of information is our clients. . Rather we receive personal information from various sources, such as applications partners, data vendors or clients (“Sources”), which may include third party apps, sites and services that you use.  

If you would like to understand how those apps, sites and services collect, use, and share data from your interactions with them, please refer to the privacy policies of the apps, sites, and services you use (TaurusX is not responsible for how those third-party apps, sites and services collect and use data). If you would like to read the information regarding our services and possible use of personal information, please read Section II of this Privacy Policy. If you would like to know about the information collected solely on this Website, please read Section III of this Privacy Policy. If you are our client, please refer to our agreement and then to this Policy (if applicable).

2.3 How We Use User Information

The information that we collect about you, both personally identifiable information and non-personally identifiable information, may be used by us, or shared by us as set forth in this Privacy Policy or otherwise with your consent.

We primarily use the user information we collect to enable our clients to find and purchase advertising space, including the following purposes:

(a) Advertising presented through our Service can be based on limited data, which is intended to enable processing activities such as

Selection and delivery of an advertisement based on real-time data (e.g. information about the page content, device information, app type, non-precise geolocation data etc.)

Real time data, as referenced above, may be used for positive or negative targeting.

Control the frequency of advertisements shown.

(b) Create profiles for personalised advertising, which is intended to enable processing activities such as:

Associate data collected, including information about the content and the device, such as: device type and capabilities, user agent, URL, IP address with a new or existing advertisement profile based on user interests or personal aspects of the user.

Establish retargeting criteria.

Establish positive or negative targeting criteria.

(c) Use profiles to select personalised advertising, which is intended to enable processing activities such as:

Select advertisement based on a personalised advertisement profile.

Select an advertisement based on retargeting criteria.

Select an advertisement based on positive or negative targeting criteria tied to a profile.

(d) Measure advertising performance, which is intended to enable processing activities such as:

Measure how brand suitable or safe the content of the digital property where the advertisement was served was.

Measure the percentage of the advertisement that had the opportunity to be seen and for how long.

Measure how many devices engaged with an advertisement, for how long and what was the nature of that engagement (click, tap, hover, scroll etc.).

Determine how many devices an advertisement was served to.

Measure/analysis the characteristics of the device the advertisement was served to.

(e) Understand audiences through statistics or combinations of data from different sources, which is intended to enable processing activities such as:

Provide aggregate reporting to advertisers, publishers or their representatives about the unique reach of online services and/or the audiences of their advertisements, through panel-based and similarly derived insights.

Create market research aggregate reporting.

(f) Develop and improve services, which is intended to enable processing activities such as Service improvement or new Service development.

In particular, our clients use two types of information in order to deliver end users a targeted advertisement material: our client’s information and information that we provide in the course of our services delivery. 2.7 Opt-Out Options for Targeted Advertising

TaurusX respects your control over personalized advertising experiences. Below are methods to stop receiving targeted advertising that our clients may apply using our technology. Note that technical implementations may vary by device，platform or jurisdiction.

(a) Your Ad Choices Control in the apps

If you consented to targeted advertising within a specific app, withdraw consent directly through that app's privacy settings. Look for options labeled "Advertising Preferences" or similar in the app's settings menu.

You can also opt-out of having your Mobile IDs used for certain types of interest-based mobile advertising (also called “cross-app advertising”), including those performed by TaurusX, by accessing the settings on your IOS or Android mobile device, as follows:

On an IOS device, if you are using iOS 14.5 or above, you can click on "Ask the App Not to Track" in the app's asking pop-up window or manage your "Tracking" settings in [Settings] - [Privacy] . On Android devices, your settings may allow you to “Opt out of Interest-Based Ads.” When you opt out using this setting on a device, TaurusX will not use information collected from that device to infer your interests or serve ads to that device that are targeted based on your inferred interests.

Please be advised that this policy is not intended to serve as your user manual for your device, and you should always contact the manufacturer to obtain updated guidance.

(b) Your Ad Choices Control for the Web

You can opt-out of many of the publishers, platforms and service providers we work with that support onlinetargeted advertising from cookies or similar technologies by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance’s consumer education and opt-out page at http://www.aboutads.info (or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance’s opt-out page at http://www.youronlinechoices.eu if you are located in the European Union). Some of our publisher clients have their own opt-out mechanisms that are linked from their websites or their online-posted privacy policies. You should review the privacy policies of those companies for these opt-out links if you no longer wish to receive targeted advertising from a certain company, or multiple companies.

Please be advised that this type of opt-out is cookie-based, which means that if you block cookies, upgrade your browser or delete your cookies, you will need to opt-out again. Also, in order for your opt-out choice to be effective, you must ensure that your browser is set to accept third-party cookies. Some browsers block third-party cookies by default, and you may need to change your browser settings to accept third-party cookies before opting out. If you use a different device or browser, or erase cookies from your browser, you will need to renew your opt-out choice. Even if you opt-out, TaurusX may continue to collect data for other purposes and you still will receive advertising from TaurusX  Ad Service when you visit websites of a publisher client who uses our Platform – but such advertising will not be targeted to you.

(c) Additional Choices

You may opt-out from receiving promotional emails from us, such as emails to inform you about events and new services, by following the “unsubscribe” instructions in any promotional email you receive, or by contacting us at dpo@taurusx.com.

(b) Data Subject Rights of The Residents of The European Economic Area

If you are a resident of the EEA, you have certain data protection rights. TaurusX endeavors to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your personal information, thus to enforce all rights provided to you under the GDPR.

The GDPR gives certain rights to individuals in relation to their personal data. Accordingly, TaurusX are happy to offer transparency and access controls to help users take advantage of those rights.

You (as a ‘data subject’) may send official requests to us regarding your personal data via email to the following address: dpo@taurusx.com. At any time, you have the following rights:

Right to access – This right allows individuals to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them is being processed and provide access to such personal data. It also allows individuals to request details of the processing of their personal data including, without limitation, categories of recipients to whom the personal data have been or will be disclosed and purposes of processing.

Right to rectify – This right allows individuals to rectify any inaccurate personal data about them.

Right to restrict processing – This right allows individuals to block or suppress processing of personal data under certain circumstances.

Right to be forgotten – This right is also known as the “right to erasure”. It is an individual’s right to have personal data erased or to prevent processing in specific circumstances.

Right of data portability – This right allows individuals to move, copy or transfer personal data from one place to another in a secure manner without interrupting the integrity and usability of the information.

Right to object to processing – This right allows individuals to object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing, profiling, and providing for purposes of scientific or historical research and statistics.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact dpo@taurusx.com .

(c) Right To Lodge a Complaint With A Supervisory Authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

2.9 SUBSECTION FOR UK USERS ONLY

After Brexit, the UK is no longer regulated domestically by the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which governs processing of personal data from individuals inside the EU. Instead, the UK now has its own version known as the UK-GDPR (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation). The new UK-GDPR took effect on January 31, 2020.

Under the UK-GDPR, individuals can exercise: the right to be informed; the right of access; the right to rectification; the right to erasure; the right to restrict processing; the right to data portability; the right to object to processing; the rights in relation to automated decision making and profiling

If you would like to exercise any of these rights (as a resident of UK), please contact us with a reasonable proof that you are subject to these rights.

Please note, as we already stated most information we deal with is anonymized when we receive and process it, therefore, we cannot identify you individually, you need to provide us with some additional information to enable us to identify the user information you are referring to, it may be technologically impossible to identify your information, although we will try to accurately fulfill your request. Please also read regarding our responsibility for the actions of our clients in Section I.
