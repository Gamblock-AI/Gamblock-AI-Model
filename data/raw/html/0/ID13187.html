Privacy Policy for Alke Analytics

Introduction

This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when using our web analytics service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and processing your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

Data Collection

Our service collects and processes the following types of data:

Navigation Data

  • Visited pages on the website
  • Session duration
  • Traffic sources and visit origins
  • Browsing behavior
  • Other data related to your navigation

Technical Data

  • Device type
  • Web browser and version
  • Operating system
  • Screen resolution
  • Browser language
  • Other data related to the technology used by visitors

Advertising Data

  • Displayed advertisements
  • Available targeting information
  • Interactions with advertising content
  • Other data related to advertising

Other Data

  • Country-level geolocation for statistical purposes only
  • IP address (processed for security purposes)

Use of Cookies

Our service uses cookies and similar technologies to collect some of the data mentioned above. For users subject to GDPR, cookie processing strictly respects the consent signals transmitted by the website publisher. Users can modify their cookie preferences at any time through the settings provided by the website publisher.

Purpose of Processing

The collected data is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing accurate audience statistics to website publishers
  • Analyzing user behavior to improve user experience
  • Optimizing advertising delivery
  • Ensuring service security
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent activities

Data Retention

The collected data is retained for a period of 26 months. After this period, it is automatically deleted or anonymized.

Data Sharing

The collected data is shared only in aggregated form with the website publisher. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data Hosting and Transfer

All data is hosted and processed exclusively within the European Union, in compliance with GDPR requirements.

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, you can contact us at: support@alketech.eu

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or the processing of your personal data, you can contact us at: support@alketech.eu
