We know that the very idea of having a custom-made piece of jewelry can seem overwhelming, but we’ve simplified the process to just three easy steps:

Dream

Whether you have a clear idea or none at all, we can help you create your dream ring. Start by creating your own design profile page. Then visit the Inspiration Galleries, collect some ideas, and save them to your page.  Finally, reveal your personal design profile by taking our style Questionnaire. This is a fun way for our design team to learn about you and your style.

Meet

Our fun, free design meeting provides all the answers. You’ll enjoy working with the design team as together you develop design concepts. They’ll also provide pricing information and help with your budget concerns. Tour of the showroom and advanced manufacturing shops to see how the behind-the-scenes magic happens. It’s a great experience, and we are with you every step of the way.

Create

Now the truly exciting part! Our award-winning team begins building your custom design! CAD/CAM model making, casting, and all of the precise, intricate work begins. Your personal design consultant will text you updates, including photos and videos of your design being crafted, allowing you to see just how we create your dream ring. You can even help polish if you’d like—how cool is that!

What makes a design “travel-inspired?”

Wherever I travel, I keep my eyes attuned to finding the interesting detail—a sculpted bit of architectural embellishment, some intriguingly rendered wrought iron, maybe a remnant of fresco. That’s the easy part. What’s more subjective, and therefore harder, is to convey the feeling of a location. Sitting still a while in a quiet courtyard or along a busy boulevard, a cappuccino or aperitif close at hand, is the perfect way to allow the ambient atmosphere to seep into your consciousness in a way that can be expressed through design.

Ethical Sourcing Policy

When it comes to protecting the planet, each piece of the puzzle is important. We believe by committing to the following practices, we can make a difference in the world and our community.

.

Recycled Metals

Christian Caine is committed to ethical sourcing and environmental sustainability in all aspects of the creation of our designs.  We use 100% recycled materials in our manufacturing processes and require the same for all our suppliers.

Non-Conflict Diamonds

Christian Caine is committed to selling conflict-free, ethically sourced diamonds. We believe it is unacceptable to tolerate conflict diamonds and human suffering in any way.

All polished mined diamonds sold by Christian Caine comply with the Kimberly Process, which ensures rough diamonds are:

-Exported and imported with a government-validated certificate stating the diamonds are conflict-free.

-Transported between signatory countries in a sealed, tamper-proof container.

-Sold with a statement from the seller on all invoices guaranteeing the diamonds sold are conflict-free.
