What are Cookies?

Cookies are IT data saved in files and stored on the end device of the user of the Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group Websites, which the browser sends to the server each time you connect to the Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group Websites from a given end device – when you visit various sites on the Internet (i.e., your computer, tablet, smartphone, etc.).

A Cookie usually contains the name of the website from which it originates, its duration; Cookies generate a unique number used to identify the browser from which the website is accessed and other data needed. In this Policy, information on Cookies also applies to other similar technologies used on Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group Websites.

Other technologies similar to Cookies (also referred to as: “other similar technologies”) For the purpose of ensuring efficient use of the Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group’s Websites, we also use technologies other than Cookies, such as Local Storage, which is used to store data saved during use of the Websites in a dedicated part of the browser’s memory. The data in Local Storage can only be accessed by the website from which the data was saved in your browser. The data in Local Storage is stored by the browser when the browser is closed.

Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group uses Cookies or other similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • to collect aggregate statistics that allow us to understand how people use Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group Websites (including: our services and applications) and to help us improve their functionality and content.

Ringier Axel Springer Polska Group Websites may include:

  • Permanent cookies – which remain on your device as long as they have a set lifetime or until they are deleted.
