Transforming the world through inclusive education

ICDE envisions a future where everyone has access to quality education and lifelong learning opportunities regardless of their circumstances.

Since 1938, we have championed accessible education, challenged barriers, and promoted learning innovation. As the leading global network in inclusive, sustainable, and scalable education, our community is committed to borderless collaboration.
