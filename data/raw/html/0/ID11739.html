Fast Scanning

iCanScan automatically detects the edges of your paper, receipt, or ID card and straightens the perspective so every scan looks professional. You can instantly resize your captures to standard formats such as A4, ID card, or receipt, and the app even removes unwanted elements like fingers in the frame—leaving only the document, clean and sharp.

Smart Cleanup & Editing Tools

Beyond just scanning, iCanScan helps you prepare documents the way you need them. You can take a handwritten form and clean it up to get a reusable blank template, perfect for refilling digitally. Adding a signature or a photo to any document is just a tap away, with auto filter. And with built-in markup tools like a highlighter, pen, and eraser, you can review, annotate, or correct your scans without needing any extra software.

Flexible Output & Control

Whether you need a quick receipt scan or a full report, iCanScan adapts to your workflow. Choose your output format with full control over page setup—pick A4, A3, portrait, or landscape layouts, and even insert page numbers for multi-page documents. When you’re ready, you can download your scans as PDFs, JPGs, or PNGs, giving you maximum flexibility to share or store your files.

Accessible Everywhere

iCanScan works right in your browser, which means there’s no installation required and you can use it instantly on both desktop and mobile. Save your files locally or upload them directly to Google Drive to keep your documents in sync across devices. And with premium, you enjoy scans without watermarks or ads, while keeping your files private and secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up to use iCanscan?

No. You can start scanning immediately as a guest without login. However, guest users have limited features. Creating a free account unlocks extra features like Google Drive save, OCR, and more scans per day.

What is included in the free quota?

Guest: 5 scans/day, 5 downloads. Free sign-up: 10 scans/day, 10 downloads/day, with OCR and merge/split features.

What are the benefits of Premium?

Unlimited scans, no ads, no watermark, Google Drive save, advanced features, and priority support.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by secure gateways. We do not store your card or bank details.

Can I try Premium before purchase?

Yes. You can use guest or free accounts to test features. Referral rewards also unlock Premium for free.

Can I use iCanscan on mobile and desktop?

Yes. Our web app works on desktop (Chrome recommended) and mobile browsers. No installation required.

Are my documents private?

Yes. Files are processed securely in your browser. Cloud saves go directly to your Google Drive — we never keep them.

Will my scanned documents have a watermark?

Yes, for guest and free users. No watermark for Premium users.

Can I cancel my Premium subscription anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime, and benefits last until your billing cycle ends.

What if I need help?

Premium users get email support. Free users can access our Help Center and FAQ.
