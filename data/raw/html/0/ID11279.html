HOW THE QR MENU SERVICE WORKS ?

  • The client scans the QR code and the electronic menu is downloaded to his phone
  • The waiter receives a new order notification
  • Accepts it and enters it into the automation system of your institution
  • The client receives a notification about the start of the cooking process.

WHAT ARE THE ADVANTAGES OF THE QR MENU?

  • Detailed information with a photo for each position
  • Possibility to order on site or arrange delivery
  • Language is automatically selected
  • Tipping the waiter through the service
  • Built-in payment system will allow you to pay for an order without a terminal
  • Recommendations of dishes to order
  • You can leave reviews and ratings for the institution and dishes
  • Easily order a taxi to the institution
