Live Chat

WhatsApp

Connect with others who have experienced similar situation through our online peer support spaces, including daily live chatrooms and 24/7 forums spaces.

Grant’s Story

Anonymous (caller)
I got so good at lying about gambling, now I help others open up through the Peer Aid programme. You can talk to someone who understands. Call the National Gambling Helpline now for free support, self-exclusion and gambling blocking software.
Lisa
“The fact that I’ve used that service [GAMSTOP] with the blocking software and the counselling has given me three big pillars to stand on.”
