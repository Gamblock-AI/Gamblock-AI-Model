Trending Topics

Science

National Wildlife Week

National Wildlife Week, observed each April, celebrates native wildlife, promotes conservation efforts, encourages habitat protection, and raises awareness of the importance of preserving biodiversity for future generations.

Social Sciences and Humanities

National Volunteer Week

National Volunteer Week brings attention each April to the individuals who give their time and energy to others, celebrating the role of service in strengthening communities and encouraging broader participation in volunteer efforts.

Health and Medicine

Autism Awareness Month

Autism Awareness Month highlights each April the experiences of individuals on the autism spectrum, promotes understanding and acceptance, and encourages inclusive support across schools, workplaces, and communities.

Business and Management

Financial Literacy Month

Financial Literacy Month focuses each April on building practical money skills, from budgeting and saving to managing debt, while encouraging informed financial decision-making at every stage of life.

Physical Therapy and Occupational Therapy

Occupational Therapy Month

Occupational Therapy Month is marked each April with a focus on the everyday impact of therapeutic support, highlighting how occupational therapists help people adapt, recover, and navigate daily life with greater confidence and capability.
