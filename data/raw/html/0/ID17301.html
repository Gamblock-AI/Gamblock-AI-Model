Features & Capabilities

Get instant football coverage with Camel Live. Follow live scores, dive into detailed player and team statistics, and track upcoming fixtures — all in a lightweight Chrome extension built for fans who want fast, reliable updates. Perfect for following leagues worldwide or keeping tabs on your favorite team.

Key features include: live scores with instant updates across leagues; in-depth stats for player performance and team rankings; fixtures & schedules so you never miss a kickoff; and a fast, easy-to-use interface.
