Creating Breakthroughs That Change the World

We’re not afraid to push boundaries and break rules to solve the world’s most complex challenges.

Tree Rings Rewrite Oregon Fire History

By analyzing fire scars inside tree stumps, UO ecologist James Johnston found that Douglas fir forests in Oregon’s Western Cascades historically burned more often than believed. The findings may guide more resilient forest management.

Read Between the Rings

See Also:

  • Institute for Resilient Organizations, Communities, and Environment
  • Ecosystem Workforce Program

Advancing Human Performance in Ballet

UO students in Sports Product Management redesign the traditional ballet pointe shoe for better comfort and durability, centered around dancer needs and modern product thinking.

Watch Students Weave Innovation and Tradition

See Also:

  • Sports Product Management Program
  • University of Oregon Portland

Designing Biomedical Scaffolds to Heal Muscle Injuries

UO researchers build microscopic scaffolds that help regenerate muscle cells, an alternative to muscle transplants that often result in scar tissue and impaired function.

See How Scaffolds Promote Cell Regeneration

See Also:

  • Knight Campus for Accelerating Scientific Impact
  • The Wu Tsai Human Performance Alliance at Oregon

Weekend Sleep Boosts Youth Mental Health

Adolescents and young adults who catch up on sleep on weekends are at lower risk of depressive symptoms than those who don't, according to research led by Melynda Casement, a UO clinical scientist and licensed psychologist.

Learn About the Link Between Sleep and Wellbeing

See Also:

  • Department of Psychology
  • Center for Translational Neuroscience

From Classroom to Communities

Students in the Sustainable City Year Program partner with local towns and cities to solve urban challenges, gaining hands-on experience while helping communities create a livable future.

Learn About the Projects Across Oregon

Impact In Motion

See how we’re turning bold ideas into real-world progress.

  • The History Hidden in Playing Cards

    A UO doctoral student explores the stories behind karuta playing cards, gaining insight into art, culture, and world history through this Japanese tradition.

    See the Cards That Connect Past and Present

  • Virtual Reality Affects Real Emotions

    UO researchers discover that playing a virtual reality game to find a lost dog increases feelings of altruism and empathy, which could motivate people to help others in real life.

    Explore How Immersive Games Shape Feelings

  • Demystifying the Tax Code

    In preparation for Tax Day, a UO professor specializing in tax law shares advice and answers questions to simplify the filing process.

    Get Answers to Your Tax Questions

  • Sending Stolen Artifacts Home

    After a collector donated ancient artifacts to the UO, an anthropology professor recruited students to help return them to burial sites in Cambodia and Thailand.

    Follow the Treasures Back to Southeast Asia
