Our journey as a national hotel management company in Indonesia began in 2007. We are more than just a hotel operator, we offer a full range of services and strategies to ensure our properties grow to their highest potential. Powered by a dynamic, innovative, and creative young team, we bring together a diverse range of experts in the hospitality industry. We operate each hotel with meticulous attention to detail. This commitment has allowed us to become one of the fastest-growing hotel operators in Indonesia, thriving even through challenging periods such as the global pandemic. Azana Hospitality caters to various market segments, from dynamic budget travelers and tech-savvy business guests, to families and luxury-seeking travelers. With brands such as Azana, Azana Essence, Front One, Votel, and Azana Hotels Collection, we always create the right experience for every journey. We are committed to elevating every guest experience, ensuring comfort, care, and a true sense of home in every stay.
