Our mission and purpose

Through our work and our partnerships, we help ensure that the next generation of professionals is equipped to help build a world that is safer, more efficient, more inclusive and more sustainable. Our approach, the standards we set and the quality we guarantee, inspires confidence in those who aim to build a better world.

Who we are

We are a nonprofit, ISO 9001 certified quality assurance organization. Through the accreditation of academic programs, recognition of credentials and assessment of student learning, we support excellence in education worldwide. Our services and global partnerships help equip professionals to confidently face the future. Together, we aim to contribute to a world that is safer, more efficient and more sustainable.
