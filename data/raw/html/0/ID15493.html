Not sure yet?

Here are some frequently asked questions to help you decide.

Why join E.ON Next?

Because we want to help you get that energy feel-good that you won't get anywhere else. Plus:

  • Exclusive discounts on home energy tech. T&Cs apply.

  • Your own dedicated Energy Specialists, providing you with expert service whatever your query.

  • The Energy Switch Guarantee, which means you'll get a safe and speedy switch to us.

How do I join E.ON Next?

Easy-peasy, we'll just need to find the right tariff for you. Follow our step by step process and you'll be enjoying positive energy before you know it.

Do I need to provide anything to my existing supplier when I join E.ON Next?

No, we'll handle all of that for you, including sending them your final meter readings. We do recommend clearing any outstanding balance with them to help make your switch go through as smoothly as possible.

When will you need my opening meter readings?

We'll let you know when we need your opening meter readings, which you can give to us through your online account. If we don't get your readings then we'll estimate them for you

How long does it take to switch energy supplier?

It can take up to 5 working days to process your switch. We'll let you know how the switch is going and when you need to give us any information or meter readings.
