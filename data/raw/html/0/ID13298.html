    Welcome
    Creating exceptional spaces, our commitment to style, elegance, and integrity is paramount in every aspect of our developments.
    PT Plaza Indonesia Realty Tbk was established since 1983 with a vision to build high quality prestigious properties in premium locations. Operating since 1990-1991, The Company started with the building of now iconic Plaza Indonesia complex, consisting of Plaza Indonesia Shopping Center and the Grand Hyatt Hotel Jakarta. Since the landmark development of the Plaza Indonesia complex, our commitment to style, elegance, and integrity has been prominent in the development and ongoing development of a wide range of residential, commercial, and lifestyle properties in Jakarta.
