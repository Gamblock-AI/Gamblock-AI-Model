My name is Farzana and I, along with my cousin Preya, are helping Suad collect donations so she can purchase essentials in Gaza. Aid has been restricted and prices have skyrocketed. 

Suad is computer engineer and a new mother who has given birth to a baby boy named Khaled. Her son suffers from respiratory and intenstinal infections which require constant care. Along with this, Suad's mother in law needs surgery to replace both her knees. 

In Suad's words: "Hello to all my friends and compassionate supporters around the world.

My name is Suad, a young Palestinian woman from Gaza. I am an engineer who graduated from the College of Engineering in 2021, driven by passion and dedication. I volunteered in various technology companies and participated in numerous technical competitions, achieving the second position in my batch. This success earned me a one-year contract as a teaching assistant at my university immediately after graduation. I then worked as a university lecturer, teaching over 300 students, and progressed rapidly in my career to become a Software Quality Assurance Team Lead in a private technology company.

In early September 2023, I was honored with the "Outstanding Employee" award—a moment of pride and achievement. On October 3rd, I discovered I was pregnant. My husband and I were overwhelmed with joy, eager to build a future for our child. But everything changed on October 7th when war erupted in Gaza.

Within days, we were forced to evacuate our home multiple times, fleeing bombardments, artillery shelling, and ground invasions. During one of our displacements, we narrowly escaped death after a nearby bombing left us buried under rubble. We emerged bruised, choking, but alive.

Our suffering continued as we moved to Rafah, where we lived in a cramped tent shared with my husband's family. We spent weeks enduring cold rain, inadequate shelter, and contaminated food and water. I developed severe gastroenteritis and constant stomach pain, while my unborn child suffered from the lack of essential vitamins and proper medical care.

We fled Rafah as the situation deteriorated, moving to Deir al-Balah only to face the same cycle of suffering. Prices soared, food and clean water became scarce, and every day was filled with fear and desperation.

On June 3rd, 2024, I gave birth to Khaled in a chaotic, overwhelmed hospital. The lack of resources was unimaginable, with children sleeping on floors and essential medical care out of reach. Shortly after his birth, the hospital itself was bombed. We fled once again, with nothing but the clothes on our backs.

Khaled’s health quickly deteriorated. He suffered from chest allergies due to inhaling dust and smoke from nearby bombings. With power outages plaguing us constantly, providing him with essential nebulization sessions became nearly impossible. Kind strangers with solar panels allowed us to use their nebulizers, but nighttime treatments were often missed.

I tried to find shelter for Khaled in a small room at a relative’s house, but we were forced to flee barefoot in the middle of the night after a nearby building was bombed. We lost everything we had left, and once again, we were homeless.

The relentless cycle of fear and hunger continues. Khaled, who is now 10 months old, recently developed severe gastroenteritis due to contaminated food. His fever spiked dangerously, and I was forced to walk 20 kilometers to reach an overcrowded hospital. Khaled’s condition has only worsened. We can’t afford his formula or diapers anymore. Basic hygiene items are a luxury we cannot afford.

Every day, the prices of essential items climb higher, making it impossible to provide Khaled with the care he needs. His health continues to decline, and I am desperate to give him a chance at life.

Adding to our suffering, my mother-in-law’s health has severely deteriorated due to the physical toll of constant displacement. She now requires urgent and costly surgery to replace both knees.

Despite everything, I am fighting to keep Khaled safe and healthy. But I cannot do it alone. I need your help to provide Khaled with a chance at life. I need your support to escape this never-ending nightmare.

Every donation, no matter how small, brings us closer to safety. Your kindness and generosity could be the reason Khaled survives. If you cannot donate, please share our story. Help us reach someone who can make a difference.

Thank you from the depths of my heart for standing by us in these desperate times.

With endless gratitude,

Suad."
