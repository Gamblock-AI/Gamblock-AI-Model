Why water filters are a life changer

Can you drink the water from the tap in your house? No? You are in good company.  World-wide, 4.4 billion people [see this report] do not have access to safe drinking water at home.  Every year more than half a million children pass away because of a constant exposure to bacteria in dirty drinking water.

We think that everybody should have access to safe drinking water in their house. It is our mission to provide safe drinking water to everybody everywhere by manufacturing and selling affordable yet high quality water filters.  Nazava water filters are very durable and WHO tested. With a Nazava water filter you can turn dirty water from most sources into clear, fresh, safe drinking water.

Our water filters provided safe drinking water to half a  million people while also reducing household expenses, increasing available time, reducing deforestation and greenhouse gas emissions.
