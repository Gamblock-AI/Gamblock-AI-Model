Nail Artists/Beauty Therapists Role Description

This is a full-time or part-time role for a Beauty Therapist at OMH Therapies. The Beauty Therapist will be responsible for providing hair removal and nail treatments for our clients. The Beauty Therapist will also be responsible for advising clients on skincare and beauty products, maintaining a clean and sterile work environment, and managing inventory. The Beauty Therapist will work with a team of other therapists to provide exceptional customer service and create a welcoming and relaxing atmosphere for clients. This is a self-employed position with a minimum of 2 days a week required. To apply send your CV to info@omhtherapies.com or for more info call 0131 220 1301

Qualifications

  • Certificate, Diploma or equivalent Level 2 or 3 in Beauty Therapy.
  • Excellent knowledge of skincare and beauty products.
  • Demonstrated expertise in waxing, threading and nail treatments.
  • Excellent attention to detail and communication skills.
  • Ability to work in a team and provide exceptional customer service.
  • Ability to manage time and maintain a clean and sterile work environment.
  • Understanding of health and safety regulations and willingness to adhere to them.
  • Willingness to learn and adapt to new techniques and technologies in the field of beauty therapy.
