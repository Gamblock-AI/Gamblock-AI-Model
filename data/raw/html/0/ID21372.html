ABOUT US

Imagine if your restaurant have a smart device that show restaurant advertisement and menu?

We have been developing a simple yet powerful table signage placed in famous restaurant, bars, or cafes to help your ads reach potential customer while expand the restaurant menu function.

We provide a safe digital environment for advertising. Expand visibility of ad banner and/or video ad by up to 100%. Reach more customers at their favorite place. Add coupons or voucher code to attract loyal customers. At the same time, use the device to show the best menu available at your place.
