What's Inside The Park?

Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) is one of the world's tallest monumental statues. As Cultural Park, GWK offers a unique blend of Balinese heritage, cultural performances, scenic dining, and breathtaking views. Whether you're here to explore, learn, or simply enjoy, your unforgettable Bali journey starts here.
