About MelodyInbox

Welcome to MelodyInbox — your modern, fast, and ad-free MP3 download platform designed for music lovers around the globe.

🎵 What We Do

MelodyInbox helps users discover and download free MP3s quickly and legally. By indexing music under Creative Commons licenses or with direct artist permission, we ensure that all downloads are safe, respectful of rights, and fully compliant with legal guidelines.

✨ Why Choose MelodyInbox?

  • No ads, no pop-ups, no distractions
  • No account required — just search and download
  • MP3 quality from 64kbps to 320kbps
  • MP4 support for music videos up to 4K
  • Mobile & desktop friendly — use it anywhere
  • Privacy-first design: no trackers, no logs, no cookies

📱 Perfect for Everyone

Whether you're a casual listener, a student editing a project, or a content creator looking for royalty-free music, MelodyInbox is your go-to solution.

⚡ Our Technology

We leverage modern APIs and open data to provide real-time search results and quick access to downloadable MP3 or MP4 content. Our backend is designed for speed, and our frontend is optimized for usability and accessibility.

✉️ Get in Touch

Have a suggestion or spotted an issue? We'd love to hear from you! Please reach out through our contact form or report problems via our DMCA policy.

📚 Legal Compliance

We take copyright seriously. All indexed files on MelodyInbox are either:
