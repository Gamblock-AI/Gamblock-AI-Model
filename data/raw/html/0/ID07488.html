To register for a new CoinDepo account, you only need to create a password and verify your email address. To create a CoinDepo account, press here.

The verification process takes only a few minutes.

Email Verification is the process of checking and authenticating your email address to make sure it is authentic and associated with a real person.

Identity Verification provides us with information about who is the real owner of the account. This prevents anyone else from managing your CoinDepo account on your behalf and prevents your account from being scammed.

To complete the Identity Verification, we will require your full name, date of birth, address, mobile phone number, and a photo of your personal document. You will need to upload a photo of your passport or national ID.

Know Your Customer (KYC), and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations exist to limit or mitigate the impact of money laundering, terrorism funding, corruption, and other forms of financial crime. KYC and AML are mandatory for regulated entities deemed at high risk of facilitating financial crime.

The CoinDepo Identity Verification process, which includes the submitting of a document confirming your identity, is required to comply with KYC/AML rules and to protect your account from unauthorized access.

You can withdraw funds from your CoinDepo account to any external wallet at any time unless they are part of the collateral for a loan taken under a CoinDepo Credit Line.

You can deposit supported cryptocurrencies and stablecoins by transferring them from any external wallet to your CoinDepo Current Compound Interest Account wallet address of the respective asset. Once your deposit funds to your Current Compound Interest Account, you can instantly transfer them to any type of CoinDepo Interest Account with a higher interest rate using the “Transfer Between Accounts” feature.

All you need to do is transfer CoinDepo-supported cryptocurrencies or stablecoins from any external wallet to your CoinDepo account. You can choose from six types of CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts to deposit your assets, with annual interest rates from 12% to 18% for cryptocurrencies and from 18% to 24% for stablecoins.

To earn 18% per annum on cryptocurrencies and 24% per annum on stablecoins, choose the CoinDepo Annual Compound Interest Account.

By keeping (reinvesting) regularly paid compounding interest in the account of your choice, your effective annual interest rate (APY) will always be higher than the specified APR.

All you have to do is purchase any CoinDepo-supported stablecoins with USD, EUR, or GBP from any licensed crypto exchange and transfer them into your CoinDepo account. You can choose from six types of CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts with annual interest rates from 18% to 24% to deposit your stablecoins.

To earn 24% per annum on stablecoins, choose the CoinDepo Annual Compound Interest Account.

By keeping (reinvesting) regularly paid compounding interest in the account of your choice, your effective annual interest rate (APY) will always be higher than the specified APR.

Compound interest is the interest on the assets you deposit, accrued based on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods. Compound interest is calculated by multiplying the initial principal amount by one plus the annual interest rate raised to the number of compounding periods minus one.

At CoinDepo, compound interest can be calculated and accrued daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually, depending on the compounding period (type of Compound Interest Account) you choose.

To get the highest interest rate (18% APR for crypto/24% APR for stablecoins), choose a CoinDepo Annual Compound Interest Account to deposit your assets. 

The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) is the fixed annual rate of return on your CoinDepo Interest Account, excluding the effect of compound interest.

APR is calculated as follows: APR = Periodic Rate x Number of Periods in a Year 

The Annual Percentage Yield (APY) is the real annual rate of return on your CoinDepo Interest Account, taking into account the effect of compound interest. By keeping (reinvesting) the regularly paid-out compounding interest in the account, your effective rate of return (APY) is always higher than the APR.

APY is calculated as follows: APY = (1 + Periodic Rate)n – 1

Where: n = Number of Compounding Periods per Year

Interest earned is paid out into your account the day after the compounding period ends at 14:00 UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). The first day of the compounding period is the date the funds are deposited into the interest account. For example, if you choose a Current Compound Interest Account, the compounding period is equal to one day; that is, the first payout of earned interest to the account is made on the next day at 14:00 UTC after the funds are deposited.

At CoinDepo, the compounding period can be one day, week, month, quarter, half-year, or year, depending on the type of Compound Interest Account you choose.

You can withdraw your funds and earned interest from any of the CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts at any time.

Please note that if you make a partial withdraw during the compounding period or make a full withdrawal before 14:00 UTC on the interest payout date following the last day of the compounding period, you will not be paid interest on the withdrawal amount for that compounding period.

CoinDepo has no minimum amount limit for depositing into a Compound Interest Account.

Go to the "Borrow" page in your CoinDepo Account, select the asset to borrow, the LTV level, and the loan amount within your available Instant Credit Line limit. No paperwork or credit checks are required. The loan is immediately credited to your CoinDepo Current Compound Interest Account, and you can use it indefinitely.

You can withdraw your loan to any external wallet or spend it with a CoinDepo Сredit Сard, earning unlimited crypto cashback up to 8% on every purchase.

You can borrow an unlimited number of times within your credit line limit (50% LTV of your portfolio balance in CoinDepo).

CoinDepo does not require you to freeze your assets in a collateral account when borrowing. As a result, all your funds remain in CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts, and you keep earning interest on all of your assets even when you borrow. 

You can borrow up to 50% LTV of your portfolio balance in CoinDepo.

Loan-to-Value (LTV) is the ratio of the loan amount to the value of the borrower's portfolio held in CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts, which is the collateral for the loan.

The Effective Annual Rate (EAR) is the actual cost of a secured loan to a borrower in a year. In CoinDepo, this is the difference between the amount of interest paid on a loan and the amount of compound interest accrued per year on the collateral held in the CoinDepo Compound Interest Accounts.

Since you cannot borrow more than 50% of the value of your portfolio in CoinDepo, your effective annual interest rate on the loan will always be negative. This means you earn during this time more than you pay for using the loan. Learn more

CoinDepo does not charge any fees for issuing a loan, or for full or partial repayment of a loan.    

You can use the loans taken under the CoinDepo Instant Credit Line for an unlimited time.

The only thing that is required of you is to pay, on the 5th day of each month, the interest for using the credit line, accrued based on the balance of your loan for the previous month.

You can repay loans taken under the credit line in parts or in full, as well as take new loans at any time.

Below you can find our top security recommendations to help you protect your account from unauthorized access:

1. Use a strong password for your CoinDepo account and change it regularly.

2. Never give your password to anyone.

3. Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication), preferably Google Authenticator.

4. Bookmark www.coindepo.com to be sure the CoinDepo domain you are visiting is genuine.

5. If possible, complete Identity Verification for your CoinDepo account.

6. Use a secure password manager.

7. Make sure that your Internet connection is secure.

8. Install antivirus software and trust only secure apps/programs.

9. Put a lock on your phone.

10. Use a unique email for your CoinDepo account.

11. Regularly check official messages from CoinDepo for security updates.

12. Never call any phone number from someone claiming to be CoinDepo Customer Support. 

13. Never send any funds to anyone claiming to be a member of the CoinDepo team. 

14. Constantly check your network of contacts for potential security threats to avoid social engineering attacks.

15. Identify and avoid phishing and malware campaigns.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is the industry standard for securing online accounts. 2FA adds a second layer of protection to your CoinDepo Account. When logging in, you will be required to enter not only your password but also a verification code generated by your authentication app.

You can set up 2FA on your CoinDepo Account using the Google Authenticator app or the Authy app. Google Authenticator is available on Android and iOS mobile devices. After enabling it, you will only be able to log into your CoinDepo Account with the code generated by the app. This code is updated every 30 seconds.

We strongly advise all of our users to enable 2FA on their accounts. Despite using the highest security standards, CoinDepo cannot be held responsible for unauthorized users who may access your account if you are not using 2FA.

After logging into your CoinDepo Account, you can enable 2FA by clicking the button "Enable 2FA" on the https://app.coindepo.com/profile/security/settings page and following the instructions.

Yes, all your funds in your CoinDepo Account are fully insured in storage, transfer, and E&O (errors and omissions). Errors and omissions insurance (E&O) is a type of professional liability insurance that protects against claims of inadequate work or negligent actions. 

To protect against unauthorized withdrawals of your funds by third parties, CoinDepo uses a multi-layered security system to verify the user initiating the withdrawal, which includes:

  • Security Verification (via Email Verification Code)
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Withdrawal Request Notification
  • Withdrawal Security Hold

You can request a full or partial withdrawal of your assets from any CoinDepo Compound Interest Account at any time. CoinDepo may initiate the withdrawal process immediately if possible, but as a rule, all crypto withdrawals are subject to the Withdrawal Security Hold and are processed the next business day, no later than 19:00 UTC. For example, if your withdrawal request is placed on Monday before 23:59 UTC inclusive, it will be processed no later than 19:00 UTC on Tuesday.

During the Withdrawal Security Hold period, the withdrawal status is displayed as “Pending” on the “Transactions” page in your CoinDepo account, and you can track the progress there. Withdrawals may be subject to manual verification to reduce the risk of fraud. If additional confirmation is required from you, you will receive an email notification. Funds will not be debited from your account balance until the withdrawal is fully processed.

Withdrawal Security Hold is designed to protect users from fraudulent requests and human error, such as if the destination wallet address is entered incorrectly, by allowing users to cancel their request while it is in the “Pending” status. In addition, it gives CoinDepo time to verify and ensure that the assets are returned to the correct person without any fraudulent or suspicious activity.

For users who have been in the crypto space for a while, these measures may seem over the top. However, these security layers allow CoinDepo to prevent your accounts from being compromised and any fraudulent withdrawal attempts. The security of your assets has been, and always will be, our top priority.
