How to install Android APP?
Installation Guide
Since Google Play does not allow our app to use certain key features such as live match video streaming, we recommend downloading the original version. Simply follow the steps below to complete the installation in just a few moments.

1.Download the APK via Google Chrome.

2.After downloading, you will usually see an “Open” option, or you can open it from the “Downloads” folder in your file manager.

3.You need to enable “Unknown Sources” to open the file. Tap “Settings”.

4.Enable “Unknown Sources”.

5.Repeat the installation process and tap “Install” in the prompt.

You have successfully installed the Nowgoal App on your phone.
