Welcome To FLITS

Having 15 Years Experience

Future Link Institute of Technological Studies (Pvt) Ltd (FLITS) is the leading training provider in Colombo, paving the way for a bright future in Technology and Engineering, Business Management, Information Technology, English, and other related fields.

Conducted by the best lecturers in the industry, FLITS fosters a student-focused learning environment, which has led to a significant increase in student enrollment in both weekday and weekend classes, providing more time and space for effective learning.

FLITS is a registered institute under the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC) of Sri Lanka and is also approved by City & Guilds – UK.

Our mission is to provide today’s youth with the opportunity for vocational training, enabling them to earn while they learn and pursue their studies both academically and vocationally. Unlike traditional education systems, FLITS bridges the gap between education and employment, empowering students with practical skills for a successful career.

Under the guidance of Managing Director Mr. Roshan Abeysiriwardana and Director Mrs. Nuwanthika Fernando Abeysiriwardana, FLITS continues to transform vocational education and create skilled professionals for the future.

What We Do

FLITS is a learner-centric provider of nationally & internationally recognized industry-relevant Engineering qualifications, Business Management, Information Technology, Accountancy, etc. Through its Faculty, FLITS offers an array of skills development courses; not teaching, but molding its students to take on tomorrow’s challenges in each relevant field.  Under the watchful guidance of Head of the centre Mr. Roshan Abeysiriwardana,

We equip you with all theoretical and practical aspects of engineering in the form of module practices which goes in line with the theory during our entire course.Students can obtain job training in well-known companies linked with FLITS We provide the facility of internships in Middle-East countries to our scholars who have at least completed 1 year course in our institute.We offer on the job training facilities which will help the scholars to Earn & Learn.
