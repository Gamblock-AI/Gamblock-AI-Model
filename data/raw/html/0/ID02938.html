    ABOUT

    We have been part of Selangor and Klang Valley community for more than 25 years. From our humble beginning in 1994 at Petaling Jaya, we expanded our business portfolio from Bumiputera-Muslim product trading towards community pharmacy.

    ​

    We pioneered the concept of modern and traditional medicine integration under one roof which later became trendsetter in Malaysia pharmacy concept.

    ​

    Our mission is to contribute towards community through good medicine dispensing practice, providing accurate health advice and creating health awareness. We provide value-for-money alternative service compared to clinics.

     

    We strive to become the pharmacy of choice for customers and talents through good management practices in accordance to industry standards.
