Challenge
Students hit a wall

That stuck moment when nothing makes sense, no steps, no clues, just confusion. That’s when students start to believe, “I’m not a math person.”

Support
Scaffolding that builds confidence

StepGuide breaks complex problems into multiple, bite-sized steps that use foundational concepts to guide students to the final answer. Success at each step builds momentum and confidence.

Growth
Mistakes become learning opportunities

When wrong answers trigger guidance, and not punishment, students begin to view mistakes as a natural part of learning. The StepGuide approach fosters a growth mindset and helps them ultimately achieve mastery.
