Today NOT JUST A LABEL (NJAL) represents over 50,000 designers from more than 150 countries and is an infinitely expanding destination devoted to facilitating growth in the fashion industry.

NJAL provides designers a digital platform where they can connect with a global audience, showcase and retail their designs directly from their studios, thus providing a unique shopping experience in the luxury space.
