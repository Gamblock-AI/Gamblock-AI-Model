JUST THE WAY WE LIKE IT

Rooted in Tomorrow

WE'VE ALWAYS DONE THINGS DIFFERENTLY.WE WERE FOUNDED BY FARMERS WHO BELIEVED THATYOU WORK BETTER WHEN YOU WORK TOGETHER.

As the world changes around us, our cooperative has remained rooted in this belief, allowing us to grow and achieve far more than our founders had envisioned. And we're proud to say, we're just getting started.

COMMITTED TOCOOPERATION

Being a co-op means our owners have their boots on the ground. This unique business model allows us to use the power of teamwork, shared resources and decades of know-how to help our members and communities thrive.
