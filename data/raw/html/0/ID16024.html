The user-friendly way to get apps

Uptodown is the largest alternative app store specializing in Android. We develop tools and services so that everyone can easily download software legally and securely. We love working on our project, and we think you would too. Join our team and help us make Android even better!

Our values

It is clear to us that the foundation of our project is based on the well-being of our team and the passion to work toward an exciting goal.

A robust project

After 20 years of experience in the industry, we are still in constant growth, self-financed, and without the help of external investment.

We are all important

All team members have a say in day-to-day operations. Feeling an integral part of the project is fundamental to innovate and continue growing.

For a better mobile industry

We are one of the world's leading players in mobile app distribution. We fight against the big gatekeepers to promote a fairer industry for users and developers.

Without forgetting our roots

Although we are an international company, we maintain our main offices in Malaga, where we were born and grew up. We always keep in mind our origin as a Spanish and European project.

Benefits of working at Uptodown

The best results are obtained when the work of all the people who are part of the company is valued. The vote of confidence we place in our entire staff, together with the tools we provide to bring out the best in each of its members, is the best formula for Uptodown to be considered by its employees as a great project worth being part of.

“100% of employees think this is a great place to work” - Great Place to Work Spain 2022/2023 Certification

Flexible calendar

We do not want you to sacrifice your personal life for your professional life, so we look for the best solution for each employee.

Remote

Free training

We have a large budget to train you in subjects relevant to your profession in the way that best suits you.

Medical insurance
