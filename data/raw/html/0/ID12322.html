Looking for inspiration and easy ways to level up your Stories? Instagram Story apps may be the answer you’re looking for.

While Instagram Stories content may be temporary, their impact isn’t. Stories offer social media marketers and brands a golden opportunity to reach and engage with Instagram’s 2 billion monthly active users.

We’ve compiled a list of top 20 apps that will make designing, editing, and animating your Instagram Stories a breeze. And to make the most of these tools, we spoke to Halley Morlock, the Co-founder of Halehaven Media, and Celine Rosalina, Marketing Lead at Villa Ventures, for their top tips on creating engaging Instagram Stories.

Key Takeaways

  1. Instagram Story apps help you create professional-looking content without needing a design degree. From customizable templates to fun stickers, these tools can transform your Stories into eye-catching visuals!
  2. Jump on viral sounds or templates to connect with your audience. Quick adoption can give your Stories a trendy edge and show you’re in the know.
  3. Apps like Hootsuite let you schedule your Stories and analyze their performance. Track replies and engagement to understand what resonates with your audience and optimize your strategy.

What are Instagram Story apps?

Instagram Story apps are software tools that allow you to create next-level content for Instagram Stories.

These apps help marketers, brands, and content creators produce high-quality, professional-looking Stories with cohesive themes and styling, without hiring a designer.

Instagram Story apps usually offer features like:

  • Customizable Instagram templates – To quickly create designs
  • Text tools – With various fonts and styles
  • Filters & effects – To enhance photos and video clips
  • Stickers/graphics – To make Stories more fun and inviting
  • Video edit features – Including trimming, cutting, adding transitions, or music
  • Animations – To make videos more dynamic

Using apps to create more dynamic and visually appealing Stories gives your Instagram posts a better chance of standing out from other users and more importantly, competitors.

5 tips for using Instagram Story tools from social media managers

1. Start with your strategy

Before downloading a dozen apps to your phone, consider the goals of your marketing strategy.

Rosalina shares, “I always consider current trends and my overall content strategy before choosing which app to use. This helps me decide which tool best suits my content’s practical needs and aesthetic goals.”

2. Take your audience behind-the-scenes

Stories are a good place to get real with your audience. The ephemeral nature of Stories lends well, too. “Show ’em what your life really looks like” says Morlock.

“Share your story. It’s a goldmine for showcasing your personality, your hot takes, and sneaking in reminders about your offers while giving that BTS peek” notes Morlock.

Source: Halley Morlock

3. Add a little rizz

Despite the casual nature of Instagram Stories, don’t forget that they’re a key tool for educating your audience about your brand and products.

Using insta Story apps to add elements like stickers, text effects, and animated transitions can give your content a polished edge and stand out from the sea of Stories from everyday users.

4. Jump on trends

Just like on other platforms, joining social media trends is a good way to engage your audience on Stories.

However, sometimes it can take longer to create content and get it approved than the lifespan of a trend. Luckily, many Instagram Story apps like Tezza and Mojo are constantly releasing updates that include trending templates and sounds.

“If you’re falling short on ideas, riffing on a trend is a good option. Is there a viral article, meme, or video making waves? Jump on Stories and share your take” says Morlock

Source: Tezza

5. Keep things simple

Rosalina says that even with many tools at your fingertips, you should be careful not to overcomplicate your Stories.

“Create content that is bite-sized, visually engaging, and authentic. Keep things simple—Instagram Stories are about building a connection with your audience, so keeping the content real and relatable is important.”

Source: Celina Rosalina
