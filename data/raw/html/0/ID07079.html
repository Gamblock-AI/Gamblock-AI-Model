Release on November ASEAN Statistical Highlights 2025 Download Now This fourth edition of the ASEAN Statistical Highlights provides quantitative information
on ASEAN macro-economy, trade, investment, socio-demography and connectivity
with comprehensive tables and various infographics. This edition continues to showcase
essential ASEAN statistics, offering an updated overview of the region’s latest trends and
developments. Recent Release ASEAN SDG Indicators Progres Report 2025 Download Now The ASEAN SDG Indicators Progress Report 2025 aims to provide information
on the current status of the selected SDG indicators in comparison with
the baseline period by showing the progress made during the period of 2016 to 2023.
Based on identified trend, the report provides further assessment on whether
each indicator is on track to meet its target by 2030. Released Last Year: ASEAN Statistical Yearbook 2023 Download Now The ASEAN Statistical Yearbook 2023 provides a wide range of key statistical indicators of ASEAN
and its member states which covers population, education, health, employment, macroeconomic,
trade in goods and services, foreign direct investment, transport, tourism, agriculture, manufacturing,
and other social indicators.
