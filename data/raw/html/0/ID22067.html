TV Shows Countdown is here to be your guide through the ever-evolving world of television schedules and seasons. What are the TV Seasons? In the television industry, the categorization of TV seasons by the time of year can vary, but generally, it's aligned with the traditional meteorological seasons. Here's a more accurate breakdown:
  • Winter season — This usually starts in December and runs through February. It's a common period for new shows to premiere, especially right after the holiday season in early January.
  • Spring season — This spans from March to May. It often includes the conclusion of the shows that began in the winter and the start of some new series.
  • Summer season — Traditionally from June to August, the summer season used to be a time for reruns and less high-profile shows, but in recent years, it's become more popular for new series and continuing shows.
  • Fall season — The most traditional and significant in TV scheduling, it runs from September to November. This is when most new shows premiere and existing shows return with new seasons.
Not every TV series aligns perfectly with these timelines, as they may start or end at various points within these months. Some series conclude within the same season they start, while others may extend into the next.
