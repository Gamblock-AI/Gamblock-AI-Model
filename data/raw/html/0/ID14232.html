About us
Aiming for better experiences for communities
Max-Link Group was founded in 2004, with a core focus on the transportation sector.
Started with one division, today it has diversified with four companies.
The group has been providing comprehensive solutions to various industries.
The endeavour of the Group is to create better experiences for communities in countries it operates in.
Not limiting to a specific segment, it has spread its wings in government contracts such as toll collections,
and pay and park and has been adding value to clients such as National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), MSRDC,
Cantonment Board and municipal corporations.
