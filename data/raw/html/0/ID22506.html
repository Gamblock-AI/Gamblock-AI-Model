Freecash tracking issues – how to fix & avoid!

To earn on Freecash, it is very important that you make sure the website and app are tracking the offers and surveys you complete.

I often get asked about this, so I wanted to add a section here to explain exactly how to ensure Freecash offers & games track (and what to do if they don’t).

Here's a quick checklist you should make sure to follow:

  1. Allow tracking on your device
    If you are using the app or using your phone browser, make sure to allow tracking and ad personalization.
  2. Use Chrome (if possible)
    Freecash reports the fewest tracking issues on Google Chrome. So if you can, use Chrome when earning on Freecash (unless you use the app):
  3. Accept cookies and avoid ad blockers
    When prompted, make sure to accept cookies and avoid using ad-blockers/privacy extensions while using Freecash. If you do not accept cookies or use ad blockers, Freecash will not be able to track what you are doing.
  4. Use one device for the entire offer
    When starting a new offer, make sure to complete the entire offer on the same device you started with. Don’t switch phones/tablets mid-progress.
  5. Only complete offers you have never completed before
    Some offers are available on many different GPT sites. Only do app/game offers you’ve never installed before – previous installs often cannot track/reward.

If you follow the above steps, most tracking issues will be resolved.

However, what if an offer still doesn’t credit?

Then, first of all, be aware that it can take up to 72 hours for rewards to show. If it has been more than 72 hours, you can contact Freecash's support or the support of the specific offer (you can find it via Profile → Earnings in progress).

One thing I also make sure to do is to always double-check after starting an offer. I sometimes hear people say they spent hours completing an offer to find out it did not track.

That is why you should, in my experience, always first just do one or two steps of the offer, and then go to the “my offers” section and make sure the steps have been checked.

If the green tick marks are not showing up, then it might not be tracking properly, and you should make sure to go through the steps above.
