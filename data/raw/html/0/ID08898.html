Sea and Google Deepen Partnership to Deliver AI-Powered Innovations for Consumers Globally

Sea today announced an expanded strategic partnership with Google to advance the development of AI-powered innovations across its digital entertainment, e-commerce, and digital financial services businesses. Building on a fruitful, long-running history of collaboration between Sea and Google, the partnership on AI will focus on advancing thoughtful, scalable solutions that better serve consumers and small businesses across Southeast Asia and beyond. By combining Google’s AI leadership with Sea’s innovative ecosystem and deep understanding of local markets, both companies aim to unlock new opportunities in digital entertainment, e-commerce, and digital financial services – helping more people participate in and benefit from the digital economy.

Jan 22, 2026

Shopee Charity Supports Over 200 Families in Taiwan with Grocery Vouchers to Ease Financial Pressure During School Holidays

Shopee Charity, in partnership with four nonprofit organizations in Taiwan, distributed “Family Essential Vouchers” worth NT$2,400 per household to more than 200 disadvantaged families across Taiwan, helping to ease financial pressure during the winter school break. To bridge the “meal gap,” Shopee Charity worked closely with four nonprofit partners to ensure support reaches families most in need. Each eligible household received vouchers valued at NT$2,400 to help cover lunch expenses typically supported by schools, while also allowing families the flexibility to purchase essential supplies.

Jan 22, 2026

ShopeePay and SeaBank Indonesia Deliver IDR 1.2B+ in Donations Through Peduli Programs

ShopeePay and SeaBank continue to strengthen their commitment to supporting social and humanitarian initiatives in Indonesia through the ShopeePay Peduli and SeaBank Peduli programs. Through these initiatives, the two platforms have collectively raised more than IDR 1.2 billion in donations, reflecting strong user participation across both ecosystems. The contributions include IDR 100 million in donation-matching from ShopeePay and IDR 250 million from SeaBank, in addition to user donations. The donations were distributed in collaboration with trusted social and humanitarian organizations.

Jan 20, 2026

Shopee Indonesia Hosts Shopee Super Awards 2025 to Honor 42 Ecosystem Partners Driving Innovation

Shopee Indonesia has once again held the Shopee Super Awards 2025, an awards ceremony recognizing the dedication, hard work, passion, and journey of Shopee Sellers, Creators, and Partners who have contributed to the development of Indonesia’s digital economy throughout 2025. This year, Shopee Super Awards 2025 presented awards to winners and nominees across 42 categories, covering a wide range of segments. Entering its latest year, the Shopee Super Awards 2025 carries the spirit of innovation, growth, and collaboration, while serving as a manifestation of the #ShopeeAdaUntukSemua (#ShopeeIsThereForAll) commitment to supporting an inclusive and sustainable digital ecosystem.

Dec 11, 2025

Sea Brings AI Experiences to More Shopee Users Through OpenAI Collaboration

Sea is expanding its collaboration with OpenAI to bring AI experiences to more Shopee users across Southeast Asia. Following earlier promotions for Shopee VIPs, the 12.12 Birthday Sale will offer VIPs in five markets three months of free access to ChatGPT Go. Beginning January 2026, all Shopee users in these markets will also be able to redeem one month of free access, making advanced AI tools more widely accessible. These initiatives underscore Sea’s efforts to bring AI touchpoints and experiences closer to the communities we serve, and to empower our users to realize the benefits of new technology like AI in their everyday digital journeys.
