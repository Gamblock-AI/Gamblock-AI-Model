        Privacy Policy

        Last updated September 10th, 2024

        Rockerbox, Inc. ("we", "our", and "us") offers a technology platform that helps marketers understand the value of various offline and online channels. For digital ads we do this by tracking media exposure (the users who saw an ad), media response (i.e., the users who clicked an ad) and conversion (i.e., the action a Client hopes a user will take - an online purchase, a registration, or similar). Rockerbox is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains our privacy practices regarding the information that we collect, use, share, and secure from visitors to https://www.rockerbox.com, (our "Website"), our technology platform (the "Platform"), our services ("Services") and any information collected from those who use our Platform and Services (our "Clients").

        We collect information, including Personal Information, in various ways when you use our Website, and Services and when Clients leverage the Platform via their respective websites. As used in this Policy, "Personal Information" means any information which, either alone or in combination with other information, identifies you as an individual, such as your name, mailing address, and email address.

        INFORMATION COLLECTED VIA OUR WEBSITE AND FROM CLIENTS

        Information Collected Automatically When You Use Our Website and Services: Through the use of cookie technology, Web beacons or other technologies (as described below), we may track and collect information about your computer and software when you visit our Website. This information can include your IP address, domain server, type of computer, type of web browser, access times; and referring websites addresses. This information does not personally identify you. Among other things, this information assists us to design and arrange our web pages in the user-friendliest manner and to continually improve our Website and services to better meet the needs of our Clients and prospective Clients.

        Information You Volunteer: In order for you to access certain services and information that we offer via the Website or otherwise, we may require you to provide us with information that personally identifies you, such as your name, mailing address, email address and home and business telephone numbers ("Personal Information"). If you communicate with us by email, or otherwise complete online forms, surveys, or the like, any information provided in such communication may be collected as Personal Information.

        Information We Collect When You Contact Us: When you contact our support team or communicate with us in any other way, we'll collect whatever information, files, materials, or data that you volunteer at that time.

        Information We Collect From Clients and Prospective Clients: When you become a Client, we will collect an email address and ask you to select a password so that you may login to our Platform. We may also ask you for your phone number, title, company address and similar information. We will also collect billing information. We will also collect information for current and prospective Clients to help us market our services to them and will provide options to opt-out of such communications within those communications as required by law. Before we use your Personal Information for a purpose that is materially different than the purpose we collected it for or that you later authorized, we will provide you with the opportunity to opt out of such use. For more information you may contact us at privacy@rockerbox.com.

        Cookies and other Tracking Technologies: Rockerbox employs cookies in order for its server to recognize a return visitor as a unique user. Cookies are small text files placed in the 'Cookies' folder on a visitor's hard disk and allow Rockerbox to remember the visitor. The cookies placed by Rockerbox's server are readable only to Rockerbox, and cookies cannot access, read or modify any other data on a computer. All web-browsers offer the option to refuse any cookie. If a visitor refuses the Rockerbox cookie, Rockerbox does not gather any information on that visitor, but doing so may require you to re-enter certain information at each visit, or prevent us from customizing the Website's features according to your preferences. You may also delete a cookie manually from your hard drive. For more information about cookies, please visit http://www.allaboutcookies.org/cookies/.

        How We Use Information Collected via the Website and from Clients

        Rockerbox may use your Personal Information to register you to use Rockerbox's services and/or products, contact you to deliver certain services, materials or information you may have requested (e.g., a request for information by clicking on our (i) "Contact Us" tab, or (ii) "Request a Demo" tab on the Website), verify your authority to enter into certain areas of the Website, improve the content and general administration of the Website and our services, provide you with notices regarding our services, and, with your permission, administer Client accounts as described above and provide you with updates and information about Rockerbox and its services. Generally, you will be given the opportunity to "opt-in" to receive Rockerbox newsletters, white papers or specific marketing material.

        We may also use information collected to monitor and analyze trends and track usage and user movement on the Website, develop, improve, and refine products, services, and functionality. As with other sites and services, we may also use this information to prevent fraud or other unauthorized or illegal activity as well as to promote safety and security. In short, we reserve the right to use this information in order to carry out any other purpose for which the information was collected.

        INFORMATION COLLECTED VIA OUR TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM

        Overview of Data Collection via the Platform

        Rockerbox's Services include data collection, user mapping, and user path analysis - the results of which are delivered solely to our Clients in aggregated form. Data collection is a process that centralizes, cleans and normalizes data from each of the Client's marketing vendors and each source where a consumer engages in a desired behavior (for example: a purchase which occurs on a Client’s website). Our Clients, who are generally businesses seeking to market a product or service, use this information in order to help them optimize marketing programs.

        In order to provide the Services, Rockerbox works with Marketers, Marketing Vendors and Third Party Data Providers.

        Marketers: Rockerbox offers its services to digital marketers that advertise to consumers and may work with them directly or indirectly through an Advertising Agency they employ.

        Marketing Vendors: The companies that provide marketing services to Marketers. This may include digital marketing such as Search Engine Marketing, Email Marketing, Display Advertising or others. It may also include vendors that provide offline marketing such as for television, radio or mail.

        Third Party Data Providers: Rockerbox works with companies that assist in stitching user data together from disparate sources. For example, companies that provide offline purchase data for Marketers.

        What data does Rockerbox collect via the Platform?

        Our Platform receives data each time a Client's ad is viewed or clicked, including the following:

        • A Unique ID - Our systems collect a pseudonymous unique identifier relevant to the source of data. For browsers this will be from a unique user id created by Rockerbox and placed into a cookie. On mobile devices, we use the mobile advertiser ID provided by the mobile O/S (i.e., the - Apple ID for Advertising (IDFA) or the Android Advertising ID (AAID). On a Connected TVs, we use the subscriber ID provided by the connected TV manufacturer. None of these IDs may be used by Rockerbox to identify an actual person, but they allow our Platform to unique identify a browser, computer, TV or mobile device over time.
        • Ad serving and log file data - Our systems also obtain information about the ad that was served including ad campaign meta-data provided by the Client, the date/time the ad was served, the user agent and IP address (which helps us infer city, region or country), the referrer and the landing page URL of an ad clicked on.
        • Client provided data - In addition to the above information our Clients may provide us additional identifiable information about you including a unique user identifier created by each Client, your email, your address, the total amount spent on a purchase, whether or not you are a returning customer or other relevant dimensional data (e.g. a segment you belong to or relevant to an event completed on their site).

        How does Rockerbox use the data collected via the Platform?

        Rockerbox primarily uses the data collected about your browser or device to provide our Services, which include:

        • User-path management - Our platform collects data about your browser or device over time to create what we call a "user path". A user path helps our Clients better understand whether their ads are engaging and driving users to visit their sites and/or purchase their products and services, among other things.
        • User segmentation - Our Platform will collect "meta-data" over time which enables our Clients to place their users into profile segments such as "new customer" and "existing customer" which helps them better understand the effectiveness of their marketing programs.

        What Tracking Technologies does Rockerbox use on the Platform?

        Rockerbox collects data in various ways. Some Clients allow us to place tracking pixels and JavaScript tags onto their websites. In these cases, we use cookies to uniquely identify users over time as ads are viewed, clicked on and other actions performed on a Client's website. Other Clients simply pass data to us directly, or via their market vendor or third-party partner such as their ad server.

        We also engage in cookie syncing and other forms of ID synchronization. ID Synchronization occurs when Rockerbox provides known user identifiers to a 3rd party data provider who then identifies the user within their dataset and map their unique identifier to the identifier provided by Rockerbox. Rockerbox engages in ID synchronization with 3rd party data providers in order to identify users that Rockerbox thinks as separate users which that provider may know is the same user and to augment user path data; that is to add knowledge of non-digital media stimulation, media response and conversions to user paths.

        On a mobile device we will use cookies within a browser app or we will collect data through server-to-server (postback) integrations with individual application owners and the mobile analytics companies that they have integrated with.

        HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION WE COLLECT

        We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent other than:

        • Third Party Service Providers - With third party agents and service providers (e.g., email marketing providers, database vendors and survey vendors) who work on our behalf and use data only as directed by us, provided that such third parties subscribe to the Principles or provide at least the same level of privacy protection as required by the Principles;
        • Marketing Analytics and Optimization - Rockerbox shares aggregated data collected via the Platform with Clients and their marketing partners in order to inform Client media plans and strategy.
        • Lawful Requests - To comply with laws or to respond to lawful requests and legal process (provided that Rockerbox will endeavor to notify you if Rockerbox has received a lawful request for your information). Protection of Rights and Property - To protect the rights and property of Rockerbox, our agents, customers and others including to enforce our agreements, policies and our terms of service.
        • Emergency - In an emergency, including to protect the personal safety of any person; and
        • Corporate Restructuring - For the purposes of a business deal (or negotiation of a business deal) involving sale or transfer of all or a part of our business or assets (business deals may include, for example, any merger, financing, acquisition, divestiture or dissolution transaction or proceeding). In the event of an insolvency, bankruptcy, or receivership, Personal Information may also be transferred as a business asset. If another company acquires our company, business or assets, that company will possess the Personal Information collected by us and will assume the rights and obligations regarding your Personal Information as described in this Policy.

        We may transfer Personal Information to companies that help us provide our Services. Rockerbox is potentially liable for the onward transfer to third parties of Personal Information of EU and Swiss individuals pursuant to the EU-US and Swiss-US Privacy Shield programs, respectively.

        OUR SECURITY MEASURES

        Rockerbox maintains commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to help protect the information we process in an effort to prevent loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction. We utilize encryption/security software to safeguard the confidentiality of Personal Information we collect from unauthorized access or disclosure and accidental loss, alteration or destruction. Once we receive your transmission, we make reasonable efforts to ensure security on our systems. Unfortunately, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. As a result, while we strive to protect your Personal Information, we cannot ensure or warrant the security of any information you transmit to us or from our online services, and you do so at your own risk.
