OUR MISSION IS TO USE TECHNOLOGY FOR THE CONSERVATION OF ENDANGERED SPECIES & ECOSYSTEMS

Wildlife Protection Solutions (WPS) deploys technology globally to help conservationists protect and monitor the world's ecosystems and biodiversity. In addition, WPS raises awareness about conservation through the creation of innovative education and entertainment experiences.

Our Programs

  • Conservation

    Our conservation solutions use advanced AI to enhance continuous, real-time monitoring of protected areas using a variety of camera systems, remote sensors, and software. We implement technology that provides conservationists with immediate landscape scale awareness so protected area managers and law enforcement officials can proactively and adaptively respond to the many challenges facing endangered wildlife and ecosystems today.

    LEARN MORE

  • Education

    Our education and outreach initiatives raise funding for and awareness of the plight of endangered species and ecosystems globally. Leveraging the latest innovations in AI, cinematography, virtual reality, and storytelling, we weave together unforgettable experiences that create empathy, excitement, and ultimately support for the natural world we all share.
