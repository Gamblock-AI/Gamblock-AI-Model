THE BMW M SAFETY CARS.

SAFETY SQUAD.

SAFETY SQUAD.

BMW M hits the gas with the new BMW M5 MotoGP™ Safety Car and BMW M5 Touring MotoGP™ Safety Car. The current season also saw the new BMW XM Label Red MotoGP™ Safety Car on the track. In addition, the BMW M2 MotoGP™ Safety Car is part of BMW M's safety fleet as well. Also available: the BMW M3 Touring MotoGP™ Safety Car and the BMW M 1000 RR Safety Bike. The BMW M 1000 RR is the first M model from BMW Motorbikes and will be seen in a racing version in the Superbike World Championship. The BMW i4 M50 Safety Car leads the field in the electric motorcycle series FIM Enel MotoE™ World Cup, which takes place on the support programme for MotoGP™.

Dynamics and performance: MotoGP fascinates Fans from all over the world every season, led by BMW M Safety Cars.
Franciscus van Meel

CEO of BMW M GmbH

HYBRID POWER FOR THE PREMIER CLASS.

23

HYBRID POWER FOR THE PREMIER CLASS.

2025 in action: The BMW M5 Safety Car is the new lead car in MotoGP™. Based on the BMW M5 Sedan, BMW M continues with this innovative model to rely on cutting-edge hybrid technology, as it did with the predecessor BMW XM Label Red Safety Car. The sedan is complemented by the BMW M5 Touring Safety Car.

The BMW M5 MotoGP™ Safety Cars also feature a special graffiti-design livery. It is derived from the design of the BMW M Hybrid V8 race car, which BMW M Motorsport competes with in the FIA World Endurance Championship with races like the 24 Hours of Le Mans and in the IMSA series in North America.

IT DOESN’T GET ANY CLOSER.

For those who want to make their visit to MotoGP™ an unforgettable experience, the BMW M MotoGP™ Experience is just the ticket. As an exclusive guest in the VIP Village, you not only get to follow the race from the best vantage point, but also meet world champions, interact with current MotoGP™ riders and get up close with the mechanics as they perform their duties in the pit lane. As a highlight, you will experience the circuit as never before during a lap in the passenger seat of one of the current BMW M Safety Cars.

THE BMW M AWARD.

Keys for the BMW M2 CS: Marc Márquez wins BMW M Award 2025.

THE BMW M AWARD.

The BMW M Award is all about speed! Riders who regularly burn fast laps into the track in qualifying not only win the coveted trophy at the end of the season, but also an exclusive high-performance car from BMW M. 

The winner of the 2025 BMW M Award in MotoGP™ is Marc Márquez. The record holder in this special competition has once again claimed the coveted prize for the fastest qualifier of the season – for the eighth time. His prize: a new BMW M2 CS.
