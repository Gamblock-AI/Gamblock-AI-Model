Parks & Trails

put yourself on the map

Explore over 27,000 acres of parks & trails

Go to a place where time is not of the essence. Where worries fall by the wayside. A place where you can log miles on a well-trodden trail or escape your thoughts off the beaten path. Where you can let nature be your guide.

Parking & Passes

Most parking across Three Rivers is free and passes are required for certain activities.

Find yourself in the parks using Three Rivers GeoPDF maps on your mobile device with the Avenza app! Learn more.
