Notable Projects

Residential Court Construction

Legendary Sports Construction has completed numerous custom backyard builds across Nevada, including pickleball, tennis, basketball, and multi-use layouts. Each project is designed for optimal performance, safety, and visual appeal—proving our ability to craft high-quality courts even in tight or uniquely shaped spaces.

Commercial Court Construction

Our team has delivered premium athletic surfaces for HOAs, schools, parks, and recreation centers throughout Nevada. From multi-lane pickleball facilities to durable basketball and tennis installations, our commercial projects highlight our commitment to long-lasting quality and professional-level play.

THE LEGENDARY PROCESS

We build multi-use game courts throughout Southern Nevada. Our team is committed to making your dream court or sports construction project a reality. Let our experience, expertise, and passion for designing, building, and maintaining your court guide us to create the Legendary solution you deserve.

DESIGN

Our team of experts can help you choose your preferred location, colors, dimensions, and accessories for your residential court, ensuring every detail surpasses your expectations. You’ve imagined the perfect court – now it’s time to bring it to life.

BUILD

Once your design is finalized, our dedicated professionals will seamlessly execute every aspect of the project, making sure your space is flawlessly built. With a focus on timeliness and cost-effectiveness, we are committed to delivering exceptional results. Get ready to experience the transformation of your space as we make your dream court a reality.

PLAY

After your home court construction is complete, it’s time to play – the most exciting part! We pride ourselves on not just creating remarkable courts but forging backyard courts where memories are made and legends are born.

MAINTAIN

Our team is committed to building a relationship with you for the life of your court. You’ve invested in the best quality court. Now it’s time to keep it looking beautiful and playing great for all your future BBQs, gatherings, holidays, and more!

Court Construction — Serving Southern Nevada & Arizona

At Legendary Sports Construction, we’re proud to serve homeowners, schools, HOAs, and communities across Southern Nevada and Arizona. Whether you live near the bright lights of Las Vegas or the sun-soaked suburbs of Phoenix, our team delivers custom, high-quality athletic courts built to thrive in desert climates.

Court Construction and Resurfacing Services - Southern Nevada

We create courts engineered to endure Southern Nevada’s intense heat and desert conditions. Expect UV-resistant surfaces, advanced drainage, and durable construction—ideal for everything from backyard basketball or pickleball to community sports facilities.

Court Construction and Resurfacing Services - Arizona

Across Arizona — from Phoenix to Scottsdale, Mesa, and beyond — we build courts that stand up to prolonged sun, high temperatures, and seasonal challenges. Our heat-tolerant, UV-stable coatings and resilient surfacing systems ensure your court stays functional, safe, and vibrant for years to come.
