Current Issue

Vol. 7 No. 2 (2025): Vol. 7 (2025) No 2

This issue explores the synergy between cultural tourism and the fields of Visual Communication Design, social sciences, humanities, and the arts. It focuses on how visual strategies and design narratives strengthen local cultural identity amidst globalization. Through an interdisciplinary lens, this volume examines the role of art in preserving heritage and its impact on social structures. These articles aim to provide creative solutions for sustainable tourism development while enriching the academic discourse on visual communication as a medium for sustaining traditional values and local wisdom.
