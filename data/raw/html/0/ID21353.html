Strictly Necessary

Always Active

These cookies are essential to enable visitors to move around the website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas of the website. Without these cookies a service a user requests, like shopping baskets, e-billing, authentication, or video content delivery, cannot be provided.

Functional

These cookies allow the AMC website to remember choices a user makes (such as username, language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features. These cookies can also be used to remember changes a user has made to text size, fonts and other parts of web pages (some parts of the site may not work, or may not work as well, but the core essential features are still serviceable).

Marketing & Research

These cookies collect information about how visitors use a website, for instance which pages visitors go to most often, and if they get error messages from web pages. These cookies don’t typically collect information that identifies a visitor. Information these cookies collect is often aggregated. It is used to improve how a website works.

Targeted Advertising

These cookies are used to deliver relevant ads. They are also used to limit the number of times a user sees an advertisement as well as help measure the effectiveness of the advertising campaigns. They are usually placed by advertising networks with the website operator’s permission. They remember that a user has visited a website and this information is shared with other organizations such as advertisers. Quite often targeting or advertising cookies will be linked to site functionality provided by the other organization, but if a cookie is used for both targeted ads and third-party site functionality, it should be considered a targeted ads cookie.
