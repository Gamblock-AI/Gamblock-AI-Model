2. Username and Password

Access to the user's account will only be possible with the user's unique username and password.

3. Data Collection and Use

We collect non-personal information through the use of cookies (e.g., managing user sessions) for the operation of the site, analyzing site usage, detecting and preventing malicious behavior, and improving the content and organization of the site. Additionally, we use the collected non-personal data to advertise our business on other sites, including through the THEADX platform. When doing so, we act as a customer of the platform using our own data, without mixing it with the private data of other platform customers. You can learn how to opt-out of interest-based advertising by the platform on the THEADX user options page.

5. Information sharing

Except for the reasons outlined in this Privacy Statement, we will not rent, share, or sell personal information collected through THEADX.COM to third parties. We may share customers' personal or non-personal data with affiliates, subsidiaries, vendors, or other third parties for marketing purposes and to conduct activities on behalf of customers if they approve. We may disclose your information to third parties if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to comply with the law, protect the physical safety of any person, or to address suspected or actual illegal activities, fraud, or potential threats. Information, including any personally identifiable information (PII), may also be transferred to a successor entity in the event of a business merger, consolidation, asset sale, bankruptcy, or other corporate change.

6. Information about children

This site is not created or designed to attract children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from individuals under the age of 13.

7. User Choices

We will provide reasonable opportunity for individuals to access, update, or delete PII about them that we have in our possession. We will not use PII provided to us for purposes incompatible with the purpose for which it was provided without obtaining authorization from the subject of the information. For sensitive information, as required by the Safe Harbor Principles, we will obtain authorization prior to sharing the information or using it for a purpose other than that for which it was collected.

8. Use of Cookies by the Platform

While the specific cookies used on the THEADX Platform may change from time to time, this section explains the purposes for which the THEADX Platform uses cookies. These cookies allow our customers to target ads to users, evaluate how well their ads are performing, and see users' preferences.

  • The platform uses cookies to distinguish between unique web browsers and store data for advertising purposes, including interest-based advertising.
  • Cookies are collected via pixel codes
  • The Platform uses cookies to enable ad buyers to track users' responses (clicks and conversions) to ads purchased on the platform.
  • The platform uses cookies to store ad delivery and reporting data for customers, such as which ad was shown, how many times a specific ad was displayed, and how recently an ad was shown to a specific web browser.
  • The platform uses cookies to track users' responses (clicks and conversions) to ads purchased on the platform.
  • The platform uses non-unique value cookies for technical purposes such as server load balancing.
  • The platform uses unique cookies to store opt-out options for users.
  • Some browsers or other software may be configured by default to block third-party cookies.

9. What Information Do We Collect and Use?

  • The THEADX Platform allows clients to collect and use Platform data
  • The Platform ingests information provided by third-party sources to allow clients to use such data to target ads. To do this, clients and data providers may use ID synching to enable the use of this information through the Platform.
  • THEADX does not collect, use, or allow its clients to transfer to or use on the Platform, data that, by itself, directly identifies an individual, such as name, address, phone number, email address, or government identifier. We also prohibit certain categories of sensitive data from being collected, used, or transferred on the Platform.
  • The Platform may ingest Precise Location Information when, for example, an app makes such information available through the Platform.

10. How Do We Collect Information?

Our technology employs cookies to gather information from your web browser during your visits to websites that utilize our services. Tracking codes sourced from Theadx data services are embedded within our clients' websites. These codes facilitate the placement of cookies on your web browser. Cookies are small text files that are stored on your web browser. Through the use of cookies, we analyze your browsing behavior on the website and deliver targeted advertisements. Theadx cookies do not store any personally identifiable data, such as your name, surname, or phone number.

11. For What Purposes Do We Use Information?

THEADX uses the information collected on the platform to provide services to clients, primarily to operate, manage, maintain, and develop the Platform. THEADX allows customers to collect, purchase, and sell non-PII data collected via cookies, such as URLs visited, by adding pixel codes to customers’ websites. They may use this data for retargeting campaigns. All visitors from the EEA can block these cookies. Sometimes THEADX staff provides "hands-on" assistance to clients using the platform for these purposes, such as helping clients structure ad campaigns to best meet their goals. Specific advertising-related purposes for which THEADX clients can use Platform Data on the Platform include:

  • Interest-based advertising (to opt out of interest-based advertising on the platform, visit the user options page below).
  • Limiting the number of times a user sees a specific ad.
  • Showing ads in a particular sequence.
  • Customizing ads for a specific location.
  • Showing ads related to the content of the webpage on which they appear.
  • Determining how users respond to ads, for example, by reporting aggregate statistics about the effectiveness of online advertising campaigns.
  • The platform may use Platform Data for cross-device mapping to deliver or measure ads across relevant devices on behalf of our clients.
  • Typically, companies using technology like the THEADX Platform own the data they provide to and receive from the Platform. While the THEADX Platform is designed to use only certain types of data and prohibits clients from bringing directly identifiable data into the Platform, clients manage the data they remove from the Platform according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws, rules, or regulations.

12. Third-Party Providers

Third-party providers, including Google, may display ads to you on the internet based on your visits and use of our site and application. These third parties use technologies such as cookies and device identifiers when delivering their advertising services. This process ensures that ads are shown that are tailored to users' interests.

13. How Long and Where is Platform Data Stored?

THEADX uses generally accepted industry security standards to protect the data on the Platform. Platform Data is generally collected or deleted within 30-60 days, but may be retained on the platform for up to 18 months from the date of collection before deletion. Collected data is used for reporting and analysis and may be retained on the platform for up to 540 days. When customers remove their data from the Platform, its storage and retention are subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws, rules, or regulations.

14. Disabling Cookies and Identifiers

Our visitors can opt out of Google's use of cookies or device identifiers by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com/anonymous?hl=en). Alternatively, they can opt out of third-party providers' use of cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page (https://thenai.org/opt-out/) or control the use of device identifiers from their device settings (https://support.google.com/My-Ad-Center-Help/answer/12155656?sjid=2742183457655051580-EU&visit_id=638590686298807633-24968087&rd=1#mob&zippy=%2Cmobil-cihazlar)

15. Newsletter Emails

THEADX.COM ® may send promotional emails, campaign announcements, newsletters, greeting messages, and special offers related to the user’s products. Users can unsubscribe from the email list at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link within the email, and they will no longer receive these emails.

16. Information Sharing

Demographic information about users may be shared with site advertisers, but this does not involve the transfer of users' personal information. If THEADX.COM ® provides any service (such as payment infrastructure) through a third party, the necessary information for the service will be provided to that third party. The party may not use this information for purposes other than providing the service.

17. Cookies

THEADX ® tags users visiting partner websites using anonymous cookies. Users tagged by THEADX ® are assigned an anonymous identifier within the cookie. Personal information such as names or addresses is never stored in cookies. Browser cookies retain data about products viewed by visitors who have visited one of the partner websites where THEADX ® displays ads.

18. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on visitors' computers. Typically, a cookie contains the domain name it came from, its "lifetime," and a randomly generated unique number or another identifier. Cookies can also store basic information such as visitor preferences.
