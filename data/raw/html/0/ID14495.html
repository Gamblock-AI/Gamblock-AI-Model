2026 Researcher Incentive Program
We sincerely thank security researchers around the world for their continued attention, support, and contributions to TECNO Security Response Center (TECNO SRC). To further encourage more security researchers, TECNO SRC will launch the Annual Researcher Incentive Program for the 2026 calendar year.I. Key Achievement Eligibility (meet either condition):•Three (3) or more Critical vulnerabilities submitted and confirmed : USD 500•Five (5) or more High-risk vulnerabilities submitted and confirmed : USD 300Rules:•The qualifying vulnerabilities must cover at least two categories among Web / Mobile / Device, as defined in the TECNO SRC reward scope;•Each researcher may receive each reward tier at most once during the 2026 calendar year;•Applications must be submitted via email no later than December 15, 2026. Late applications will be considered forfeited.II. Continuous ContributionEligibility:Within 2026, a researcher must submit at least one (1) valid vulnerability per month for six (6) consecutive calendar months, with all submissions confirmed by TECNO SRC.Reward: USD 100Rules•Vulnerabilities must fall within products and services covered by the TECNO SRC reward policy;•Consecutive months are calculated on a calendar-month basis and may start from any month;•If no valid vulnerability is submitted in any calendar month, the consecutive period will automatically reset;•TECNO SRC reserves the right to revoke eligibility for this reward in cases of submission behavior that clearly deviates from the purpose of legitimate security research;•Applications must be submitted via email no later than December 15, 2026. Late applications will be considered forfeited.III. Annual Appreciation Eligibility:Accumulated Base Contribution Points ≥ 100 within 2026Rules•This incentive is intended to recognize researchers who demonstrate long-term and continuous participation in TECNO SRC;•Specific reward formats will be announced through official TECNO SRC channels in late December 2026;•The contribution point calculation period runs from January 1, 2026 to December 15, 2026, based on final vulnerability approval dates.IV. General Terms• All incentives described in this announcement are additionally independent rewards and do not replace standard TECNO SRC vulnerability bounties;• All incentives are subject to final review and confirmation by TECNO SRC, and notifications will be sent via email;• TECNO SRC reserves the right of final interpretation of this incentive program. Any rule adjustments required due to business needs will be announced through official channels at least 15 working days in advance.
Get your exclusive CVE ID
TECNO officially became a CVE Numbering Authority (CNA) on March 19, 2024, and has the qualification to issue CVE ID numbers for the security vulnerabilities in TECNO products. 1. Reporting requirementsVulnerability type: mobile application/terminal vulnerabilityVulnerability level: medium or aboveVulnerability status: confirmed to be fixedNote: If the vulnerability report is ultimately rewarded according to the Web rules, it will not be included in the CVE ID allocation range.2. Application Content2.1 Vulnerability Details* Vulnerability report number* Vulnerability name* Affected products and versions* Vulnerability type* Vulnerability severity* Vulnerability brief description2.2 Researcher’s name and email addressThis content is used for external announcements and thanks. If there is no special explanation, the account information of TECNO Security Response Center will be used for public disclosure.2.3 Application channelsEmail: security.tecno@tecno-mobile.comOnline workspace: TECNO Security Workspace3. Result Confirmation3.1 For vulnerabilities that qualify for CVE ID application, TECNO Security will notify security researchers by email of the CVE number, issue an e-certificate, and publish acknowledgment announcements on the official website of the TECNO Security Response Center.3.2 For vulnerabilities that are not eligible for CVE, TECNO Security will notify the security researcher by email of the results and reasons.4. Supplementary InstructionsTECNO Security Response Center has the final right to interpret this application within the scope of the law and also welcomes your valuable suggestions and opinions from security researchers.Revision Records:Revision DateDescription2024-12-31Added reporting requirements for CVE ID assignments
TECNO Security Author Program: share your security research and bug bounty practices v2.1
TECNO Security Response Center is committed to protecting product security and guarding user privacy, we cooperate with outstanding security researchers around the world. We hope to give security researchers a bigger opportunity and space for growth, and spread more technical, in-depth, and insightful security voices!This is a program that showcases the knowledge accumulation of security researchers and shares personal security technology research and vulnerability bounty experience. We want to have more in-depth technical communication with all security researchers and spread more security voices of outstanding researchers in the TECNO Security Response Center.1. Article TopicTopic 1: Security Technology Research (Web Security, Mobile Application Security, Device Security, Privacy Security), Sharing security-related technical knowledge you have researched and analyzed, and you can add practical application cases, etc.Topic 2: Security Tool Research (Open Source Software Research, Self-research Tools), sharing the use skills of various security tools, paying attention to innovation and practicality, including scene introduction, use ideas, application cases, etc.;Topic 3: Bug Bounty Practices Sharing, sharing your experience of vulnerability bounties, including the process of vulnerability mining, reproduction analysis, security protection measures, etc.2. Writing Requirements① Original security technology content sharing, including Cutting-edge Technology knowledge research and Security Tool Research. Every article needs about 1500-2500 words, such as the Technical Points of mobile phone security penetration testing;➡️Writing Points: This topic is a technical sharing article by security researchers. The article needs to contain key points of security-related technical knowledge and focus analysis, etc. It needs to present the author's technical knowledge accumulation, thinking, and viewpoint output. The article needs to be clear in structure, smooth in logic, and complete in content.➡️For example: Security Hazards in the Use of startActivityForResult and setResult② Sharing of security vulnerability bounty practices, including the process of vulnerability mining, reproduction analysis, security protection, etc. Every article needs about 1500-2500 words, such as remembering the experience of mining the first mobile high-risk vulnerability;➡️Writing Points: This topic is a sharing of vulnerability mining experience by security researchers, which needs to be original, real, detailed, and effective; the vulnerability report in the article needs to be accepted and fixed, complying with vulnerability disclosure standards, and specific product service information cannot appear; You must not submit vulnerability reports that have not been accepted by other platforms or have been accepted but not disclosed or have not yet been fixed, otherwise all consequences will be borne by the author himself.➡️For example: An In-depth Analysis of Path Traversal Vulnerability: Dirty Stream Attack3. Submission Guidelines① Submission Requirements📢Articles are submitted through the official mail of the TECNO Security Response Center or Google form, the title of the email is "Technology Community Article submission + article title", and the email address is security.tecno@tecno-mobile.com, and the Google form is Submission entrance of Security Blogs.📢We will reply within 5 working days to confirm whether we have received the article. If there are holidays, it will be postponed. If no reply is received, please confirm whether the email has been sent normally and resend the email; if you still do not get a reply after resending, you can ask via social media accounts: Twitter@TecnoSRC, Facebook@TECNO Security Response Center. You also can ask via Slack Workspace - TECNO Security.📢All articles submitted to the TECNO Security Response Center, regardless of whether they are adopted or not, will send an audit notice within one month; Once the article is accepted, it cannot be re-submitted to other platforms. Once discovered, the submission qualification will be canceled.② Format Requirements👉Please provide your registered email address, registered nickname, etc. at the beginning of the article for confirmation and reward distribution.👉The article needs to be saved in Word format and sent via email attachment. If it contains code that can be published, please use the Markdown format.👉If there are pictures in the article, keep them in the article, save the clear pictures in JPG/PNG format, and then add them to the email attachments.👉Please write and submit in English.4. Article Rewards💌Second: Exclusive ColumnWe will set up an "exclusive column" for excellent researchers and excellent serialized articles. The "exclusive column" articles will be given priority for publication, and the author will receive an exclusive honor badge.➡️Exclusive Column Author Qualifications: The author actively contributes high-quality articles with significant technical value and has published at least three articles.🧑‍💻Third: Security Training OfficerWe will provide security training opportunities to excellent authors. They can train new security researchers online or on-site, share their own security research, knowledge, and experience, and interact with other security researchers. In addition, the security training officer can get an exclusive honor badge.➡️Security Training Officer Qualifications: We will select security training officers based on the quality of published articles and training willingness and ability, and they can get training officer registration opportunities and host training.🎯Author Honor BadgeTo thank and encourage all security researchers who have contributed, we will issue author honor badges to outstanding security researchers.➡️Security Training Officer: Quality authors who are selected as Security Training Officers can get;➡️Exclusive Column Author: Authors who have high content and quality articles and have published more than 3 articles can get;➡️Excellent Security Research Author: Researchers who submit original security knowledge technical articles and are accepted 2 or more can get;➡️Excellent Security Practice Author: Researchers who submit security vulnerability mining experience articles and are accepted 2 or more can get;➡️Most Potential Security Author: The security researcher who submits the most times each quarter will get this badge;➡️Popular Writer of the Year: Researchers with the highest number of reads/likes on their published articles can obtain this badge;➡️All-round Security Writer: Researchers who publish at least 3 articles of different types can obtain this badge;⏱️Badge distribution time: According to the time when the badge is obtained, it will be issued through the official website and notified by email.5. Rights and Responsibility Requirements① The submitted articles need to be first published in the TECNO Security Response Center, and articles that have been published on personal blogs or other platforms will not be accepted. If deliberately concealed, once discovered, they will lose the qualification for submission.② Once the article is accepted, the author is obliged to modify it according to the suggestions of the TECNO Security Response Center until it is completely finalized;● If the author does not reply for a long time or refuses to modify, resulting in the article not meeting the publication requirements, we have the right to withdraw the acceptance decision;● If the author's communication and cooperation attitude is poor, we have the right to decide never to accept his articles.③ Once the article is accepted, the TECNO Security Response Center will have the right to publish, edit, and promote the article, and the author has the right to sign.④ Once the article is accepted and finalized, it will be published in the blog section of the TECNO Security Response Center, the TECNO security community section of the DEV website, etc.⑤ After the TECNO Security Response Center publicly releases the promotion, the author can share and repost the article to get more readings.⑥ Every author has the copyright of the submitted article and must not infringe on any third party's legal rights and interests. The content must comply with laws and regulations.⑦ If there are any rights disputes or legal risks in the submitted article, the author himself will bear them independently.6. Guidelines about the Refer-a-Friend Program
The new year has arrived, the TECNO Security Response Center has prepared a gift for everyone—rewards for referring friends!This Refer-A-Friend program means that when you refer friends to the TECNO security response center (TECNO SRC), you can get credits for your referrals. See the guidelines and terms below for activity details and reward criteria.● Activity start time: January 1, 2025Q: How does the Refer-a-Friend program work?A: You can earn credit rewards by your referral to register on the TECNO SRC website, and you can earn more credits if they submit the first valid report.To participate in it, you need:① Share the referral link with your friend (“Referee”) through social media or email.② The referee should register the account successfully on the TECNO SRC website and appear in the referral list of your dashboard.Q: How many times can I send referral links during the activity?A: During the activity, there is no limit to the number of invitations each person can send, and you can share it with anyone.Q: What rewards can I get through the referral program?A: During the activity, we offer you two reward tiers:① 5 credits for each referee. You can earn up to 250 recommended credits per year.② Every time the recommended person submits the first valid vulnerability report, you can get an additional reward of 100 credits. During the activity, you can get up to 5,000 recommended report credits. (Each person can receive a maximum of 5,250 credits per year.)Q: What will I get if I recommend more than 50 friends?A: Each person can recommend up to 50 friends a year. When you recommend the 51st friend to register, you will not get any reward;Note:● The recommended reward pool will be refreshed every year. ● After the refresh, you can continue to recommend friends to register to earn new referral rewards. Please pay attention to your referral number in time. ● We encourage you to recommend friends with bug bounty experience to register, so that you can get more report rewards.Q: When will I receive the referral credits?A: The credits will be issued automatically into your TECNO SRC account once someone registers on our website using your referral link.Q: How can I redeem my referral credits?A: All the platform credits can be redeemed into cash through our Swagshop for now, please check the rules of how to exchange the credits-Complete Guide To Redeeming TECNO SRC Credits. Q: What can I do if I haven’t received my reward?A: Please reach out to us, and we'll investigate promptly.Q: When will the referral program end?A: This activity will officially start on January 1, 2025, and the activity cycle will be 1 year. If we decide to cancel the program or make any changes, we will issue an official notice through the official website and social media channels.Q: Can I transfer the reward to others?A: No, all the credits are bound with one account, no transferring can be done.General Terms● TECNO SRC reserves the right to adjust or cancel or adjust the Refer-A-Friend guidelines or the whole program for any legitimate reason at our discretion and in particular if we suspect that there has been any misuse of this promotion in any way. Misuse of the promotion can include but is not limited to someone creating multiple or non-genuine accounts; and non-compliance with applicable law or regulation in any jurisdiction.● TECNO SRC will withhold rewards from any individual if it reasonably suspects fraudulent activity. TECNO SRC reserves the right to immediately suspend or terminate your use of the services without notifying you, and without further liability to you, including without any liability in respect of you no longer being able to participate in or benefit from any Programme. All decisions made by TECNO SRC are final.● Participation in the Refer-A-Friend promotion is subject to maximum referral amounts for both you and the friend you refer. These maximum referral amounts are set out in our Refer a Friend FAQs and are subject to change from time to time and according to your referral list. We reserve the right not to provide you with advance notice of any such changes.● Personal data supplied during this program promotion will only be processed as set out in TECNO SRC Privacy Rules. Also, users are deemed to have accepted and agreed to be bound by this guideline and terms, as well as TECNO SRC Privacy Rules and Platform Agreement upon using the Refer-A-Friend functions or services.Can’t find what you are looking for? If you need to get in touch with TECNO SRC, don’t hesitate to contact us via the below ways.● Email: security.tecno@tecno-mobile.com● Slack: TECNO Security Workspace● Twitter: @TecnoSRC● Facebook: TECNO Security Response Center
