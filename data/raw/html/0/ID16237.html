ISBN United States

The ISBN (International Standard Book Number) is the most important identifier your book can have. As the official ISBN Agency for the United States and its territories, Bowker provides identifiers that help authors get their books discovered.

Discover More

Barcode

A barcode is a graphical representation of your book’s ISBN and in the United States, the price information. An ISBN is the most widely used format for identifying your book in the publishing industry.

Discover More

Books in Print

Books In Print® is the leading bibliographic database for publishers, retailers and libraries around the world. From the enriched metadata that is supplied by publishers, Books In Print provides a valuable resource for retailers in the process of making smart purchasing decisions.

Discover More

Syndetics Unbound

Turn your catalog into a dynamic, information and feature-packed resource. Syndetics Unbound provides a modern, attractive and mobile-responsive user interface for any and all online library and retailer catalogs.
