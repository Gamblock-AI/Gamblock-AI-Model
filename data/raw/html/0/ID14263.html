    AGGCON Equipments International Limited

    AGGCON Equipments International Limited is one of the leading and fastest growing infrastructure equipment rental companies in India. We see ourselves as a rental partner and follow modern trends that helps our clients build more sustainably. We have an integrated operations network in all states and union territories of India. AGGCON keeps you going with a large fleet size of latest infrastructure equipments and a team of experts, skilled operators, and maintenance staff. We provide all equipments including specialized equipments on rental basis across India including the latest technology equipments and cranes.
