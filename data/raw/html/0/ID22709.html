Common Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering. Can't find your answer?

Tint laws vary by state and window position. Our lighter shade (35%) is legal nearly everywhere. Darker shades (5%, 10%, 15%) are typically legal for rear windows only. Since TintRebel is removable, you can always peel it off for inspections and reapply after. Check your state's specific VLT requirements before ordering.

No. TintRebel uses no adhesive on the glass itself. The tint slides into the window channel and is secured by an adhesive strip at the top edge only. Your glass, defroster lines, and antenna lines remain completely untouched. Remove it anytime with zero residue.

With normal use, TintRebel lasts 3-5+ years. The nano-ceramic film won't fade, bubble, or turn purple like cheap dyed films. Since it's removable, you can take it off, clean it, and reapply if needed. Many customers have used the same pieces on multiple vehicles.

Yes. The average install time is about 1 minute per window. Roll your window down, peel the backing, slide the tint into the channel, press the adhesive strip, and roll the window up. No heat gun, no soap water, no squeegee, no special tools. If you can peel a sticker, you can install TintRebel.

Every piece is computer-cut using precise templates for your exact year, make, and model - we cover 17,000+ vehicles. Fitment is guaranteed. If anything doesn't fit perfectly, contact us and we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund.

It depends on your priorities. For maximum privacy and heat rejection, go with 5% or 15%. For a balanced look that's legal on most windows, try 35%. For subtle UV protection without changing appearance much, 35% is great. Our 15% is the most popular choice among customers.

Professional shop tinting costs $150-$400, takes 1-3 hours, and uses permanent adhesive that can damage defroster lines on removal. TintRebel is a fraction of the cost, installs in minutes, and removes cleanly. The ceramic film quality matches or exceeds most shop-grade options.
