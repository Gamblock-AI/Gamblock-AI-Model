Energy economics

bp’s economics team produces the Energy Outlook, which makes projections to 2050 and beyond

Energy Outlook – 2025 edition

The Outlook uses two scenarios – Current Trajectory and Below 2° – to explore the speed and shape of the energy transition out to 2050 and to help shape a resilient strategy for bp.

Energy Outlook 2025

Statistical Review of World Energy

From 1952 until 2022, bp’s economics team also informed the energy economics community through its Statistical Review of World Energy, which provided historic data on world energy markets.

 

The Statistical Review’s new custodian, the Energy Institute (EI), published the 2025 edition of the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy on 26 June, providing free-to-access data on global energy production, consumption, trade and emissions for 2024.

 

Any enquiries about the Statistical Review should be directed to the Energy Institute.
