About Us

Greetings

Dear valued customer and partners,

Since its establishment in 11 March 1978, PT Jaya Beton Indonesia has been honored to play an important role as a pioneer in the precast concrete industry in Indonesia. Through the years, we have been involved in supporting numerous projects throughout the country across range of sectors.
