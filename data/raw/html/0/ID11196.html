    Rental Collection Overview

    Travel with all the luxuries of home

    Togetherness at your next destination

    Traveling with large, multi-generational groups—ranging from friends to family—can be a deeply enriching experience, creating cherished memories that bridge generations. A luxury vacation home at a Marriott International Rental Residence provides the perfect backdrop for such an adventure. Here’s a glimpse into how our guests can make the most of their time together.

    Explore our Locations

    Enjoy a life luxury around the globe

    When you book a stay at one of Marriott’s Branded Luxury Residences, you’ll not only enjoy the same extraordinary amenities and services that you’ve come to expect from one of Marriott’s luxury hotels, you’ll also enjoy all the comforts of home. With all the space you need for your family or small group gathering, from multiple bedrooms, expanded living and dining areas, and fully stocked kitchens to stand-alone villas with personal plunge pools and expansive outdoor space. Choose from thrilling locations across the globe and the most celebrated brands in hospitality, all offering bespoke service and world-class amenities. Indulge in culinary masterpieces, lavish spa treatments and more, all mere steps away, then escape into the privacy of your sublime home away from home.

    Go beyond a suite experience

    Unlike a standard hotel suite, you’ll enjoy access to everyday amenities including a kitchen with the finest luxury appliances, in-residence washer and dryer, high speed Wi-Fi, separate bedrooms, walk-in closets, and in some destinations, even your own private plunge pool. Enjoy the extra space without the worry of tidying up with twice daily housekeeping services. Prepare a meal in your fully stocked kitchen or order in-room dining to enjoy on your spacious outdoor terrace overlooking the ocean.

    Celebrating something special? Your dedicated Residential Concierge can handle every detail, from hiring a gourmet chef for a private dinner party in your Rental Residence to orchestrating an unforgettable outing to one of the area’s unique adventures.

    When booking one of our Rental Residences Collection, you can take advantage of special hotel offers and promotions, including seasonal packages, complimentary breakfast packages, resort credits, complimentary nights and more.
