FAQ: Website Builder

Is it easy for a beginner to build a website?

Yes, it's easy to build a website without coding or web design knowledge. If you can point and click and use something like Microsoft Word, you can build a website with Webnode.

How long does it take to build a website?

You can make a website fast with our free AI website builder. In just a few seconds, you'll have a draft you can further expand and customize with an easy-to-use editor. This process takes far less time than the traditional web design process, which can take weeks or more.

Can I create a website for free by myself?

You can build and run your website for free for as long as you want, including the use of our AI website generator and user-friendly website editor. If you want to remove Webnode branding, need a domain, more bandwidth or other additional features, you can upgrade anytime to a Premium Plan.

Is Webnode suitable for small businesses?

Absolutely. You can use AI to create a small business website in just seconds and customize it to suit your needs. Add online booking, create profesional mailboxes, connect your website to your socials and many more features as your business grows.

What kind of websites can I build with Webnode?

If you can imagine it, you can create it. Build a business site that showcases your services. Open an online store to sell products. Build a website or blog about your favorite hobby or special interest.

Can I use my own domain with Webnode?

Yes, all successful websites use their own domains and we can walk you through the process of purchasing and using yours.
