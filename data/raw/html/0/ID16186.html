Wise Connect

Uniting the cross-border payments community

Our global Wise Connect series brings together senior payments leaders from around the world to shape the future of fast, low-cost, transparent cross-border payments. Now in our fourth year, these events in London, San Francisco, and Singapore attract key attendees from banks and businesses, spanning transaction banking, treasury, and international payments to product innovation, retail payments and more.

What to expect

  • 200+ senior payments professionals from across the APAC, EMEA or North America
  • 5 hours of practical sessions on enabling cross-border payments innovation
  • 3 hours of meaningful networking with your peers

 

 

Don't miss out on...

  • Innovation: Explore the trends and technologies transforming cross-border payments, from AI-powered sanctions screening and fraud detection to verification tools that ensure payments reach the right recipient every time.
  • Bank and platform case studies: Discover how industry leaders are investing in seamless payment experiences, from infrastructure upgrades to intuitive platform design, to win customer loyalty and market share.
  • Regulation: Get practical guidance from regulators and experts on what matters now: meeting G20 targets, navigating ISO 20022, and more.
  • Global payments as a growth strategy: Find out why international payments aren’t just an important revenue opportunity, and how your bank or business can leverage them to extend customer lifetime value.

Europe - London
Tuesday, 14 April 2026

Convene 22 Bishopsgate

Wise Connect will once again return to London, welcoming over 300 global payments leaders from banks and businesses across Europe. Get the inside track on the trends and innovations shaping fast, low-cost payments, from regulations and technology to key industry partnerships.

Last year’s event attracted expert speakers from the likes of Raiffeisen Bank International, Itaú Unibanco, JP Morgan, and more – all offering crucial insights into how the industry can deliver great customer experiences to everyone, everywhere.

Location to be announced soon – Central NYC

Wise Connect is landing in NYC this June. Our invite only, highly curated conference is built for real conversation on what’s next in fast, low-cost, transparent international payments. You won’t want to miss it.

In 2025, over 150 payments innovators gathered at San Francisco’s Commonwealth Club to gain insights on succeeding in cross-border payments. Attendees heard from industry leaders including Upwork, Melio, Gusto, and Bank of America, alongside our in-house experts, as they explored why nearly half of American small-to-mid-sized enterprises view cross-border payments as a barrier to global expansion.
