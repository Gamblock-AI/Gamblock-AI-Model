    a breath of

    spring

     

    Springtime is the time to see nature at its rebirth. Come and enjoy our trilliums, mayflowers, adder-tongues and many other wild flowers, the forest is blanketed with their natural beauty. Take a walk through the bunny trail and enjoy the serenity of the forest. 

    ​

    summer

     

    Relax under the summer sun on our beach or lawn, sipping cold drinks with a view of Lake Cecebe. For thrill-seekers, we offer canoeing, paddleboarding, and swimming. Navigate Lake Cecebe and the Magnetawan River to historic locks and dams. Explore the "Dam Trail" or journey through our new lock to Ahmic Lake for boating fun. Children will love our playground and beach activities at the resort.

    fall

     

    Fall is known to be one of nature's prettiest times of the year. Viewed here is Echo Rock, on Lake Cecebe, well known for its three echoes and the magnificent surrounding colours. Located near here is the remains of the steamship Winona, which burned and sank in 1936.
