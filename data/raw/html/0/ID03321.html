What is official.link?

"official.link" is a domain (TLD) that acts as an official link directory for linking links and lets people and businesses create landing pages with no code or low code options.

Why do I need an official.link?

With some basic and advanced customization, an official link in the official link directory connects all the other links, projects, social posts, and feeds inside a dedicated landing page. This makes it possible to manage business and personal landing page experiences in one place and track the stats and growth of the links and pages from the dashboard.

Having everything in one space is a wonderful thing! Sharing one link with clients, friends, and family could be a better option.

How to get started with an account?

To get started with an account at official.link, there is a registration process through signup.
Step 1: Click or tap the "Register" or "Get Started" button.
Step 2: Fill in the details.
Step 3: Confirm the email address with the account activation link.
For direct signup, continue with your Google account, such as Gmail or Workspace Mail.

How to create a link with official.link?

To create a link with official.link, first, log in to your account.
Once logged in, visit Dashboard, scroll down, click/tap on "Create Link," select Link Type, and start creating.

How many links can I create with the official link directory?

With a free individual account, 02 biolinks and 01 HTML/CSS/JS-based static web link page can be created with the official link directory.

Can I optimize my official.link page for search engines?

Yes! You can easily customize the SEO title, description, and keywords for each of your official.link pages. Simply go to your dashboard, click or tap on three dots next to your link, Edit, then navigate to Settings to find the SEO options. You can add multiple keywords by separating them with a comma.

The link verification process is currently in development. We are working to create a secure and reliable system for our users. We will share an update on our website and social media channels as soon as it's ready.

What is statistics retention data with official.link?

Statistics data retention is the process of keeping track of information about clicks, countries, locations, devices, referrers, browsers, operating systems, and visitor languages on the dashboard of bio links and static sites at official.link. Measuring clicks and statistics is useful for determining how an official link page is expanding. The growth of your connections with various networks and search engines is essential, as it provides important insights into your priorities.
