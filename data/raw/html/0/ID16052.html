Join Us

Be part of the initiative to help the future of our children, by helping them today.

We at Vedantu, in our quest to help innumerous children who have been adversely affected by the pandemic, have come up with the ‘Help India Learn’ initiative. We have launched this initiative to progress our vision of taking the positive impact of education to a level where ‘for profit’ edtech can not reach today. This initiative is being set up with a long term view point of helping needy children with continual education.

Our immediate priority is to
help children who have lost one or both of their parents in the pandemic. Our ❤️ heart goes out to them and are committed to help them to the best of our abilities.

Under this initiative, Vedantu will not only support the identified affected children with immediate needs of food and medical support, but will work as a long term academic mentor by adopting these children academically to ensure their continual growth.

Vedantu pledges ₹15 Crore towards securing the future of 12,000 children impacted by the pandemic by ensuring their educational and academic needs are met. 

A part of funds will be disbursed through the partner organizations to help with the immediate needs of the affected children namely: food, shelter, devices for continued education, and school fees.

We will support these children with completely free education for multiple years on Vedantu. We are also committed to transparently showcase the academic progress and learning outcomes of the affected children whom Vedantu academically adopts. 

Partner with us

You have the power to ensure India’s education won’t stop. You can partner with us in the following ways 

Post School

Help children with after school learning support from Grade 6th - Grade 10th and competitive exam preparation for JEE & NEET, by giving out scholarships up to 100 percent.  

Financial Aid to Support
Continuation of School

Support children, with school fees, including but not limited to tuition fees. We will also help with any other charges of the school expenses on books, uniform, and academic-related supplies.

Sponsoring Digital
Devices

Support children who are unable to take online classes due to the unavailability of a smart device, by providing a Mobile/Tablet to enable online learning. 

Content Learning Partner

Collaborate with Schools or Not-for-profit organizations to enable institutes/schools with our learning content for their online classes. Keeping in mind, a lot of these institutes are struggling with quality content, Vedantu can help such bodies with the specially designed content.

Partnership to use Vedantu’s Interactive Learning
Platform  (WAVE)

Collaborate with Schools or Not-for-profit organizations to enable institutes/school online learning by giving temporary license to use our LIVE interactive technology. For the institutes struggling with the online setup, WAVE can be used to make the online classes interactive and seamless.
