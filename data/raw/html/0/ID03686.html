PIK Avenue

PIK Avenue is a premier shopping and entertainment destination located in Pantai Indah Kapuk, Jakarta. It offers a wide range of luxury retailers, restaurants, and entertainment options. PIK Avenue is connected to Swissotel and Mercure hotels, providing convenience for visitors. The mall features upmarket retailers, specialty food outlets, and popular cafes. What’s on PIK Avenue today? You can contact us or read our news & updates for more information. PIK Avenue is a great place to spend time shopping, dining, or enjoying entertainment activities.
