I need support

You’ve come to the right place for free, confidential advice and support. Take a look at all the ways we can help steer you in the right direction.

Support comes in many forms

No matter how you’re affected by gambling – whether you’re worried about your own or someone else’s gambling, or you’re impacted by someone’s gambling – we’re right here for you.

I want to help myself

There are lots of strategies and tools you can use to take control of your own gambling.

I’m affected by someone else’s gambling

You and your family’s wellbeing and financial security is important. Find out all the ways you can look after yourself and your family.

I need to talk to someone

You can choose from many options, from online chat to face-to-face counselling – even financial assistance. It’s free and confidential.

I want to know what works for others

Many people have faced gambling-related issues and can offer support and advice about what worked for them. Meet them on the online peer-to-peer forum.

Help for Aboriginal people

Free, confidential, and culturally safe services are available if you're worried about your gambling or someone else’s.

I need support in my own language

What language are you most comfortable speaking? Support is available in over 40 community languages, including 中文 (Mandarin and Cantonese), Tiếng Việt (Vietnamese), عربى(Arabic) and Italiano (Italian).
GambleAware acknowledges Aboriginal people as the traditional custodians of the land and we pay respects to Elders past, present and emerging. GambleAware is an inclusive support service.
