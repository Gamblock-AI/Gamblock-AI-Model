Website FAQs

A website is critical to your business.

You may have some initial questions about a website. Here a few that we commonly get.

How much does a new website cost?

A website design is quoted based on the needs of your business and although every website is a bit different and can require different components; we design and develop websites specifically for your small business. We’ll ask many questions, assess your needs, and give you a quote based on that assessment.

How long does it take?

Typically, six to eight weeks turnaround time is common, but the speed of website design is set by you. How much content and input you can provide during the beginning, your availability with feedback, and how soon your images and text is ready – all this affects the speed of your website design completion. A more complex site can take more time to develop then a brochure website.

I’m on a strict budget. Do you have any low-cost website design options?

You get what you pay for. We have a one page website option that can help your business get started. It is our lowest price option. The way we handle all of our website design projects requires the same foundation of work. This allows us to scale the website when you are ready to grow.
