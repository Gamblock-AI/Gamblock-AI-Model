Privacy Notice

Effective Date: December 20, 2024

1. Privacy Commitment

We, at Cluep Inc. (“Cluep”, “we”, “us”, “our”), prepared this Privacy Notice to help you understand what we do in the advertising space but also to provide you with transparency into how we collect your personal data, how we use it, and who we share it with. We use privacy technologies and techniques designed to minimize how data is processed and used for advertising. This helps prevent anyone from being able to tie information back to you personally. This Privacy Notice also describes your choices when it comes to your personal data as well as your rights. We are also available to answer questions you may have about your personal data. Feel free to contact us at privacy@cluep.com.

2. Who We Are & What We Do

We would like for you to get to know us. Cluep is a technology company in the digital advertisement space. Our clients are advertisers, media agencies, and brands that hire us to help them with their digital advertising campaigns to deliver ads on mobile apps or mobile websites to individuals that we believe are most likely to find them relevant. We partner with ad exchanges and publishers (including publishers of mobile websites and mobile in-apps) to deliver you with relevant ads.

You may be wondering how we are able to serve you with relevant ads? We use publicly available data that you post on social media, such as Twitter. For example, through your public posts, we may be able to tell that you like coffee. We may know this by the keyword(s) and/or hashtags you used or pictures you posted.

We collect this data only when you provide consent through social media platforms and publishers (e.g. websites) to do so. Generally, social media platforms provide tools and settings for you to make choices about what content to publicly share or not share. (See “Withdrawing your Consent” and “Your Rights Regarding Your Data” below for more information about your choices). If you provide consent through the settings on your social media platform, our artificial intelligence (AI) technology will help us understand your interests, such as you are someone who likes artisanal coffee, amongst other predicted interests.

Our technology is designed to process, decide, and serve advertising without the need of storing and profiling data that can tie back to you personally. In other words, we do not collect personally identifiable information about you such as your name, email address, physical address, social identity number, a picture of your face or any information that will identify who you are.

Using probabilistic predictions of what you are interested in, we try to send advertisements to mobile apps and websites we think you may find relevant. Publishers and ad exchange platforms, on behalf of publishers, give us general information about their visitors. For example, they may inform us that a male in New York between the age of 20 - 30 is visiting a website or using their App. They will know this information using pixels, tags, ad identifiers and cookies and they will share this information with us if you provide consent for them to do so. Our AI technology will take this information and compare it to the information we collected from social media. If our technology predicts that there is a good probability that the visitor on the website or App is the person on the social media platform that liked artisanal coffee, then we will buy ad space on the website or App and display the relevant advertisement to that person. During this process, we still do not know your identity.

3. Scope

Our Privacy Notice was created to provide transparency into the various ways we collect personal data. At Cluep, we collect personal data or pseudonymized data about (1) individuals we intend to send targeted advertisements to; (2) clients that use our services; and (3) visitors to our website (www.cluep.com).

Since our business activities may change and the privacy regulatory landscape may evolve, so might this Privacy Notice. We encourage you to review this Notice from time to time.

4. Other Links

When you click on one of these links, you are visiting a website operated by someone other than us, and the operator of that website has a different privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of these third-party operators.

5. If We Send you Targeted Ads

  1. How We Collect Data

    During the integration phase of our technology with either a social media platform, publisher or ad exchange, the partner will share certain personal data with Cluep based on your settings, preferences, and consent. For example, social media platforms generally provide tools and settings for you to make choices about what content to publicly share and not share. Publishers may also provide you with choices, such as opting in or out of cookies or asking you for consent to share your data for targeted ads. (See “Withdrawing Consent”, below, for more information about your choices and how to manage them). We respect your choices. For example, we will not collect any information about you if you did not provide consent to be served with targeted ads. Cluep Inc. participates in the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework and complies with its Specifications and Policies. Cluep’s identification number within the framework is 973.

    Please see Who We Are & What We Do for a detailed description of how we collect information.

  2. What Data We Collected & How We Use It

We intentionally do not collect personally identifiable data about you that will allow us to know your identity. For example, we do not know your name, email address, phone number, or what you look like. We do not know who you are.

    On social media platforms, we search for and collect data that is relevant to the ad campaign our client is running. We collect text, such as keywords from your public posts, such as “coffee”, “tired”, “energy boost”, and images you post, such as the coffee cup in your photo. Our technology refrains from collecting faces on images or other personally identifiable data.

    Publishers and ad exchanges, with your consent, may share with us your Device ID, Advertising ID, and Internet Protocol (IP) address so we can track how many ads we sent you and avoid continuously sending you the same ad. Your location data, which may include GPS coordinates, may be shared with us if you provided consent to share this information. If you decline the sharing of this information, publishers and ad exchanges will not share it with us. We keep your Device ID, Advertising ID, IP address and approximate GPS coordinates for the campaign flight plus 60 days after which we permanently delete it (see Retention).

    We do not use your personal data to automatically make decisions about you that will have a significant effect on you or have a legal effect concerning you.

7. Who Do We Share Your Data With

We do not share personal data with our clients on whose behalf we may serve interest-based advertising. To demonstrate to our clients the success of ad campaigns and the right audience reach, we limit the information we share to only usage statistics and demographic trends about individuals that interacted with the ads. For example, we inform them that the ad campaign received 100 impressions by males between the ages of 20 - 30. We will also tell them which websites had impressions and how many.

At the request of our clients and if we are contractually obligated to do so, we may share device IDs with a third party at the client’s request for analytics purposes. We will only share this pseudonymized data upon explicit instructions from our clients.

We limit who we share your personal data with and what we share with them to only what is necessary.

We share your personal data with the following parties:

  • Service providers: We disclose personal data to select service providers to help us perform functions related to our services. For example, we may use service providers to provide us with administrative, financial, or quality assurance services. These parties are bound by confidentiality and are limited in the way in which they can process, access or store personal data based on our instructions. These parties may be outside of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK) or Quebec.

  • Parent and affiliates: We do not disclose personal data we collect from advertising activities.

  • Business Transactions: We may transfer our assets, including your personal data, in the event of a merger or sale (including any transfers made as part of an insolvency or bankruptcy proceeding) involving all or part of our business or as part of a corporate reorganization, stock sale or other change in control. In such a case, you will be provided with notice. We will limit the disclosure of personal data to only what is necessary and limit the use of the information to the purpose for which it was collected before the transaction and in accordance with applicable laws.

  • Legal Requirement: We may also disclose personal data (to the extent that we have it) if we are required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to (i) comply with a legal obligation, including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements, (ii) protect and defend our rights or property, (iii) prevent fraud, (iv) act in urgent circumstances to protect the personal safety of individuals, or (v) protect against legal liability.

8. How We Protect Your Personal Data

We have implemented security measures to protect against unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure or destruction of data. This includes the necessary physical, electronic and managerial procedures to protect data integrity, access and use. This also includes the use of encryption, the use of role-based access controls using secure protocols, and two-factor authentication.

As a client of Cluep, you are provided with an account to log into. Although we do our best to protect your personal data, the internet is not completely secure. It is your responsibility to ensure you keep your password safe and not share it with anyone else. Should you notice suspicious activity in your account, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

We may share your personal data with an entity outside of your jurisdiction. For example, if you reside in the EEA, UK or Quebec, your personal data may be processed in a different jurisdiction, such as the United States or other provinces within Canada where some of the third parties we engage are located. While we take appropriate safeguards to protect your personal data, when your data is stored or processed in a different jurisdiction, it is subject to the laws of the foreign jurisdiction including to foreign governments, courts, law enforcement and regulatory agencies. These jurisdictions may not have the same information protection laws as the country in which your personal data was collected.

When transferring personal data outside of the EEA and UK, we enter into contractual arrangements (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) with service providers that offer you a similar degree of protection you would get in the jurisdiction your personal data was collected.

By not opting out of the use of your personal data, you consent to your personal data being stored and processed in a country outside of where you reside.

11. Advertising and Marketing Choices

You have choices about how and when you receive advertisements. The following information will provide you with information about advertising and marketing so that you can make informed choices about how to control your personal data.

  1. Cookies

    We use cookies on our website to understand how website visitors interact with our website and to store your preferences. A cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is sent to your web browser and uniquely identifies you. Please see our Cookies Notice to learn more about the type of cookies we use on our website, as well as how to manage your cookie preferences.

  2. Interest-Based Advertising

    We engage in Interest-Based Advertising (IBA) when you give consent to social media platforms and publishers to share data about you for targeted advertisements. We use this information to place you in general interest and purchase categories (e.g., beauty buyers, coffee buyers, organic product buyers), which helps us determine what types of advertisements may be of interest to you and then serve you with these advertisements. We may draw inferences from your online activities and use these inferences to gain insights about your general interests.

  3. Withdrawing Consent

    Your choice is in your control. You can manage your privacy preferences in a few ways including making changes to your browser settings, opting-out of interest-based advertising in your mobile devices or mobile app settings if available, and/or using third party unsubscribe functionalities to change your preferences within these platforms.

    On Your Device

    You may limit ad tracking in mobile app environments on your device. For either Android and iPhone users, your devices offer a way for advertisers to uniquely identify you in order to track your activity across applications and websites you consume on your mobile device. For the most effective and up-to-date methods for opting out of interest-based advertising, please refer to the instructions provided by your specific device manufacturers. The following information is solely for informational purposes only, and we cannot guarantee that the methods below are the most current:

    • Apple instructions for how to Limit Ad Tracking on devices such as iPhones and iPads
    • Google instructions for how to express choice on Android devices

    Configuring your device settings to disable tracking tells the system not to share your unique identifier and lets apps know you do not want to be tracked between apps. Apps use this identifier to gather information on you, including the apps you’ve downloaded, your search history, your purchase history, your recent locations and more. Where Cluep receives a signal to limit ad tracking, we will not serve interest-based advertising in the mobile app environment of that device.

    With the Publishers (Websites)

    Publishers must ask for users' consent in order to collect, use, and possibly share users' personal data with ad technology providers in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Publishers must obtain users' consent before using their personal data to place personalized ads or share such personal data with third-party partners. Therefore, as an end user, you may withdraw or not provide consent directly with the publishers.

    With Cluep

    To withdraw your consent directly with Cluep, please see Your Rights Regarding Your Data or contact us at privacy@cluep.com.

12. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

Depending on where you reside, applicable privacy laws may give you certain rights relating to your personal data and our processing of it:

  • Your right of access. You have the right to ask us for copies of your personal data.

  • Your right to rectification. You have the right to ask us to rectify personal data you think is inaccurate or incomplete. We may, however, need to verify the accuracy of the new data you provide to us.

  • Your right of deletion. You have the right to ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances. In some instances, such as legal reasons, we will not be able to fulfill your request for deletion. If we cannot fulfill your request, we will notify you with the reason.

  • Your right to restriction/objection of processing. You have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as if the processing of your personal data is unlawful, if you contest its accuracy, or if we no longer need your personal data for processing however it has been retained as permitted by law.

  • Your right to data portability. You have the right to ask us to transfer your personal data to another organization in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

  • Your right not to be subject to automated decision-making. You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or significantly affecting you.

  • Your right to revoke consent. You have the right to revoke consent to the processing of your personal data, even if you had previously consented. However, revocation of consent will not impact the legality of processing before revocation. In some circumstances, if you revoke consent, we may not be able to provide you with services. Please also see Marketing and Advertising Choices for consent choices you have.

  • Your right to lodge a complaint. You have the right to lodge a complaint about our processing with a supervisory authority or regulator in the jurisdiction where you believe your rights have been violated. If you are concerned about our personal data practices, we would appreciate hearing about it first to try to resolve your concerns. You can contact us through the methods listed under Contact Us

To exercise your rights, please email us at privacy@cluep.com. If you are an individual residing in the EU or UK, please send your request to our data protection representatives at cluep@datarep.com (see Contact Us). We will respond to any request within 30 days. If we need more time to fulfill your request, we will let you know.

Please note that your rights are not absolute as there may be exceptions that exist in the applicable law. Furthermore, since we refrain from collecting personally identifiable data about you, we may not be able to fulfill your request as we may not be able to identify you in our system. We will ask you to provide us with information that we may have in our systems, such as your device ID, which we may be able to find.

As required by law, you will be required to prove your identity. Depending on your request, we may ask you for your name and device ID. We may also ask you to provide a signed declaration confirming your identity. If you designated an authorized agent to submit a request to exercise your rights, we will require verification that you provided the authorized agent permission to make a request on your behalf.
