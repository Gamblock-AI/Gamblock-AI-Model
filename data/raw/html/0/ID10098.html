We sincerely thank you for visiting and welcome you to For You Telecare Family Service (FUTFS) in the Korean Canadian Community. Since its establishment in 1999, our Centre has been a steadfast presence in the Korean Canadian community in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) and Canadian Society for over 27 years, offering counselling, education, and outreach services to support those we serve. We provide a wide range of educational programs and customized services for individuals and families across all generations, including children, youth, young adults, adults, seniors, persons with disabilities, and hospice patients. You are also invited to participate in our free educational programs, fundraising initiatives, and a variety of community activities. We look forward to your continued support and engagement as we work together to build a caring and connected community.
