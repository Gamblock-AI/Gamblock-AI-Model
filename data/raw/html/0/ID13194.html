ViSTARS Inc. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2025
Last Updated: December 10, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to ViSTARS Inc. (“ViSTARS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). We are a Delaware-registered ad-tech company delivering programmatic advertising technology, DSP in-housing solutions, analytics, and marketplace media trading.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, use, and safeguard personal information (“Personal Information”) across our websites, software platforms, advertising technology, and related services (“Services”).

2. Scope & Applicability

This Policy applies to website visitors, clients, publisher partners, data partners, and any global users. “Personal Information” includes any data identifying an individual under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

Contact details, billing information, support requests, onboarding documents.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

We and our partners collect IP addresses, device information, browser types, advertising identifiers, statistical IDs, eTags, cache objects, log data, referring URLs, cookie IDs, RTB metadata, and cross-device identifiers. Third parties may use this data across other websites for ad targeting, measurement, and fraud prevention.

3.3 Data From Third Parties

Publisher inventory data, anti-fraud scoring, measurement signals, and publicly available business data.

3.4 Restrictions on Data Sharing

We do not send PII to PubMatic; do not sync identifiers; delete identifiers upon request; and do not drop identifiers in COPPA-regulated environments.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process data under GDPR bases (contract, legitimate interests, consent, compliance) and U.S. laws including CCPA/CPRA, COPPA, NAI, and DAA.

5. How We Use Data

5.1 Service Delivery

RTB, targeting, measurement, fraud detection, frequency capping, cross-device analysis.

5.2 Communications

Support, billing, updates, and marketing (where consent is required).

5.3 Legal & Security

Enforcement, compliance, and incident prevention.

6. Advertising, Cookies & Identifiers

We use cookies, pixels, statistical IDs, cross-device technologies, eTags, cache objects, and mobile identifiers.

6.1 Third Parties

SSPs, DSPs, anti-fraud vendors, and analytics providers.

6.2 Required Disclosures & Opt-outs

NAI: https://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/

DAA: https://www.aboutads.info/choices

EDAA: https://youronlinechoices.eu

AppChoices: https://www.aboutads.info/appchoices

PubMatic Opt-Out: https://pubmatic.com/opt-out/

6.3 Third-Party Identifier Use

Third parties may place identifiers on your device and use them for targeting across other websites. ViSTARS complies with PubMatic’s Privacy Policy and NAI principles.

7. Data Sharing & Transfers

We share data with service providers, cloud vendors, exchanges, and DSP clients. International transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and appropriate safeguards.

8. Your Rights

8.1 GDPR

Access, delete, correct, restrict, object, portability, and withdraw consent.

8.2 CCPA/CPRA

Right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/share, and limit sensitive data use.

8.3 Opting Out of Tracking

Opt-outs must be performed per device and browser using the tools listed above.

8.4 PubMatic Compliance

We adhere to PubMatic, NAI, DAA, IAB TCF, and all Applicable Privacy Requirements.

9. Children’s Data (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect data from children and do not drop identifiers on COPPA-regulated inventory.

10. Data Security & Retention

ISO/SOC security controls, encryption, monitoring, and retention aligned with contractual, regulatory, and fraud-prevention needs.

11. Cookie Notice

Please refer to our full Cookie Notice page for detailed identifier practices.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy periodically.
