WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT

Samsung collects your personal data in a variety of ways.

Information Collected From Your Devices

We collect, use and share the following information from Samsung’s systems, your device(s) and the applications and websites you visit:

  • Device Information: Device identifiers and device characteristics (such as device manufacturer, model, operating system version, IP address, Advertising IDs, device connection status, selected language, country code and service provider).
  • Device Usage and Log Information: Diagnostic, technical, error and usage information, including the dates, time and duration of your use of your device, apps and content you have on your device or hardware connected to your device, and how you use and interact with those apps and content.
  • Location Information:
    • Non-precise location data and information about the area in which you are located, such as country, city, postal/zip code, latitude and longitude co-ordinates and microcells.
    • With your separate consent, we may process precise location data (such as using detailed latitude and longitude co-ordinates).
  • Ad Delivery Information: Information relating to user consent preferences and adverts viewed, such as campaign IDs, ad type, placement and the date, time, the contextual information in which the ad was delivered and generalised location of the ad viewed.
  • Ad Interaction Information: Information about your interactions with Samsung Ads (e.g., the ads you click on).
  • Device Viewing History: If you have a device that operates Samsung’s Tizen operating system, and provide your separate consent to both Interest-Based Advertisements, and Viewing Information Services, we may process information relating to content or ads viewed, purchased, downloaded or streamed on your device including through broadcast TV, applications or hardware connected to your device such as set-top boxes or games consoles.
  • Samsung Account Data: Where you have logged in to your Samsung account on your device and with your separate consent to Improved Personalised Ads and the Customisation Service, information associated with your Samsung Account and/or collected through the Customisation Service, such as your Samsung Account ID, name, email address, age and app usage information.

Information You Provide Directly

We may obtain, process and share the following information that you share with us:

  • Support Information: Where you have contacted us we collect your name, phone number, email address, location and other device information.

Personal Data Collected From Third Parties

We may obtain, process and share personal data from third parties such as advertisers, content publishers, advertising agencies, ad publishers, ad networks and other data management platforms that manage first and third-party data, as well as commercially available sources such as our third-party service providers. This data can also be collected through cookies or similar technologies on your devices. The information obtained from third parties includes:

  • IP addresses, device identifiers and device characteristics.
  • Non-precise location data.
  • Information about specific audience interests, online behaviours and demographics that have been defined by a third party and can be matched to device IDs or other audience identifiers.
  • Contextual information about third-party applications and websites, and the types of content they provide.

We require these third parties to ensure that this information has been obtained in accordance with applicable law and can be provided to us for the purposes outlined in this Ads Privacy Notice.

HOW LONG DO WE RETAIN YOUR INFORMATION

We will not keep your personal data longer than is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. This means that information will be destroyed or erased from our systems, or kept in a form which can no longer be linked to you, when it is no longer required.

How long your data will be retained depends on the legal basis relied upon to process your data. For example, data we process to perform our contract with you requires us to keep the data throughout the time you use our Services. As long as you are an active user of our Services, we will retain and process this data. Data such as your account information and device information and identifiers fall into this category.

Data we process in accordance with our legitimate interests is only kept for as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected. For example, this data may be collected to perform analytics so we can develop and improve our ads offerings, or we may process data to keep a record of enquiries you have made to us to improve your customer service experience.

In addition, we may also process your data based on your consent. If you withdraw your consent, we will stop processing the data that relies on that consent, but it will not impact the processing of data collected prior to your withdrawal until the purposes for which such data was collected have been achieved.

Please note, although we aim to retain your data for as short a time as possible, your data may be processed longer pursuant to applicable law. For example, if a specific statute mandates we require a certain piece of data, we will comply and retain that data until the required retention period expires.

WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS

We offer you certain choices in connection with the personal data we obtain about you, which may vary depending on the type of device you have, the browser you use, the application or website you visit, and the service provider you use.

In accordance with applicable law, you have the right to ask us to provide details about what we have collected, and you can ask us to delete it or correct any inaccuracies. You can also object or ask us to restrict or limit certain processing, sharing, or transfer of your personal data, as well as to provide to you your personal data that we have collected so you can use it for your own purposes. We will not delete data that we are required by law to retain.

Additionally, you have the right to data portability which means you can ask us to provide your personal data to you. You also have the right to object to processing on the basis of legitimate interests, and to object to processing for direct marketing purposes, including profiling.

Where we rely on your consent to process your personal data, you have a right to withdraw that consent at any time. Where you withdraw your consent, we will stop processing the data which relies on your consent. This will not impact the lawfulness of the processing of data collected prior to your withdrawal of consent.

To make a request concerning your rights or to make an enquiry, contact us. See the CONTACT US section.

HOW TO CHANGE YOUR PREFERENCES

How to stop receiving personalized ads from us

If you opt out of receiving personalised ads, we will no longer use your data to deliver personalised ads. This will not reduce or prevent the delivery of generic or non-personalised ads from us, and the ads you see may not be relevant to you or your interests.

Please note that the menu path for privacy settings is subject to change and may vary depending on your device, the browser you use, and the application or website you visit. If you do not wish to receive personalised ads from us, there are a variety of ways to stop or limit them:

Device Opt-out process
Samsung Mobile Devices

On your device:

  • Selecting “Advertising Settings”within each Samsung Mobile Application and select “Configure” where you can review and change your choices at any time.
  • Select the “Opt out of Ads Personalization” option on your Samsung mobile device at Settings > Google > Ads.
Other Mobile Devices

Mobile device system software, such as Apple iOS, provides mechanisms that allow consumers to opt out of the use of information about their usage of mobile apps to deliver targeted ads to their mobile device. For more information, or to opt out using these mechanisms, consult your device settings.

Samsung Smart TVs, monitors & devices using Samsung’s Tizen operating system

On your device:

  • Via Privacy Choices App: Privacy Choices App on the Home Screen > “Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy ” > deselect “I consent to Interest-Based Advertising”.

or

  • Via Settings Menu: “Settings” > “All Settings” > “General & Privacy ” > “Terms & Privacy ” > deselect “I consent to Interest-Based Advertising” or “Settings” > “Terms & Privacy ” > deselect “I consent to Interest-Based Advertising”.
Industry Self-Regulation

In Europe the Samsung ad delivery platform supports industry self-regulation programs, including AdChoices, and adheres to the rules and regulations stipulated by the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (“EDAA”).

AdChoices is the self-regulatory program for online interest-based advertising, designed to give consumers a better understanding of, as well as greater control over, the ads that are displayed for them based on their online interests. To learn how to opt out of ad network interest-based advertising, please visit www.youronlinechoices.eu.

How to reset your Advertising ID

We display Samsung Ads by using a device identifier, known as “Advertising ID” such as “Google Advertising ID (GAID)” on Samsung mobile devices and “PSID” or “Tizen ID For Advertising (TIFA)” on devices that operate Samsung’s Tizen operating system. This is a string of characters that can be used to uniquely identify your device, and which is generated by the operating system on your device. We commit to comply with all applicable terms to use the Advertising IDs.

You can reset this identifier at any time, please see instructions below. Please note that the menu path for resetting your Advertising ID is subject to change and may vary depending on your device. If you wish to reset your Advertising ID:

Device

Opt-out process
Samsung Mobile Devices

You can reset your Google Advertising ID by selecting “Reset Advertising ID” on your Samsung mobile device at Settings > Google > Ads.

If you reset the Google Advertising ID, any data collected and associated with your previous Google Advertising ID will no longer be used in the delivery of personalised ads. It may take some time for our systems to update your information.

Please note that resetting your Google Advertising ID will not stop the delivery of personalised ads to your Samsung mobile device if you remain opted-in to personalised ads.

Other Mobile Devices

Mobile device system software, such as Apple iOS, provides mechanisms that allow consumers to reset the advertising ID. For more information, or to opt out using these mechanisms, consult your device settings.

Samsung Smart TVs, monitors & devices using Samsung’s Tizen operating system

You can reset your PSID by selecting “Reset PSID ” on your Samsung Smart TV at:

  • Settings > All Settings > General & Privacy > Terms & Privacy

or

  • Settings > Terms & Privacy

If you reset the PSID on your Samsung Smart TV, any data collected and associated with your previous PSID will no longer be used in the delivery of personalised ads to you. It may take some time for our systems to update your information.

Please also note that resetting your PSID alone will not stop the delivery of personalised ads to your Samsung Smart TV – you must also follow the steps outlined in the “How to stop receiving personalised ads” section above.

COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

We, along with certain third parties such as content providers and advertisers, may also use technologies called cookies, beacons or pixels and other tools (collectively known as “Tracking Technologies”) that communicate information from your device to a server. Tracking Technologies can be embedded in online content, videos and advertisements. They allow a server to read certain types of information from your device, know when you have viewed particular content or a particular advertisement and determine the time and date on which you viewed the Tracking Technologies and information about your device, including the IP address of your device. We, and certain third parties, use Tracking Technologies for a variety of purposes, including to analyse the use of Samsung Ads and to provide ads that are more relevant to you.

We may also engage third parties to serve ads on our behalf across the internet and provide analytics services to us. These companies may use their own, or third-party Tracking Technologies to collect information about your use of Samsung Ads and other websites and applications, including (but not limited to): your IP address, web browser, pages viewed, time spent on pages or in applications, links clicked and conversion information. These third parties are not controlled directly by Samsung, so we recommend that you check their privacy policies to understand how they use your data.
