Frequently Asked Questions

Why Choose Wink?

Wink is an AI-powered video and photo enhancement tool trusted by over 100 million users worldwide. Available on mobile, tablet, desktop, and web, it restores blurry videos, old footage, and portraits with Ultra HD quality in one click.

Is Wink Easy to Use?

Yes! Wink is designed for beginners and pros alike. With its intuitive interface and one-tap AI tools, you can instantly repair, upscale, and beautify photos and videos without any technical skills.

What File Formats Does Wink Support?

Wink supports a wide range of photo and video formats across all platforms.

On mobile and tablet, it handles PNG, JPEG, GIF, WEBP, HEIC, RW2, RAF, BMP, DNG/CR2/ARW, as well as MP4 and MOV videos.

The desktop version supports additional formats including TIFF, RAW, DCR, 3GP, MKV, AVI, and more.

On the web, you can edit JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP images and MP4, MOV, 3GP, M4V, FLV, AVI videos. Wink works seamlessly across devices, allowing you to enhance and restore content anywhere.

What's the Difference Between Wink VIP and SVIP?

VIP:
Available on both mobile and iPad. Grants access to VIP materials, supports batch processing of up to 9 items at a time, and includes a monthly bonus of 100 Wink Coins. Each cloud processing task allows up to 5 or 10 minutes of video per segment, with a daily free cloud processing quota of 50 images + 20 minutes of video.

SVIP:
Available across all platforms (PC, Web, Mobile, and iPad). Grants access to SVIP-exclusive materials, supports batch processing of up to 20 items at a time, and includes a monthly bonus of 300 Wink Coins. Each cloud processing task allows up to 60 minutes of video per segment, with a daily free cloud processing quota of 100 images + 60 minutes of video.

Cloud Processing:
The cloud processing feature authorizes your photos to be processed through our cloud-based AI models for optimal results.
