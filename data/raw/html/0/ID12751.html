We collect information from you when you register on our site, place an order, subscribe to a newsletter or fill out a form. In cases where users of our Services build or deploy ad creatives that may collect PII, they are bound by our Terms of Service and required to protect that information.


2. Non-personal information

We may collect non-personally identifiable information about users and website visitors whenever they interact with our Site. Non-personal identification information typically includes the browser name, type of computer or digital device and technical information about the users’ means of connection to our Sites, such as the operating system, the Internet service providers utilized and other similar information.

In order to serve digital advertisements developed using our Services, we collect and store information from users related to ad impressions, click-through rates, and other advertising metrics. We do not collect, store or process any personally identifiable information from audiences viewing or interacting with advertisements created or served using our Services. Our customers may, however, employ cookies or other technological means to collect anonymous and/or aggregated data about their own website visitors and users who view, click on or otherwise interact with advertisements created with our Services. See the Cookies Policy below for more information.

– To compile data from more than one grouping of ads and accounts to provide analytics that aggregate results of overall usage but obscures the data of any particular party, campaign, advertisement or user.
– To send periodic emails; the email address a user provides for order processing may be used to send you information and updates pertaining to your order, in addition to receiving occasional company news, updates, related product or service information, etc.

Cookies Policy

We use cookies to understand and save preferences for future website visits and to compile aggregate data about website traffic and interactions so that we can offer better Site experiences and tools in the future.

Cookies are small files that a site or its service provider transfers to your computer’s hard drive through your Web browser (if you allow) that enable the site’s or service provider’s systems to recognize your browser and capture and remember certain information. Cookies can be “persistent” or “session” cookies.

We use cookies to enable certain functions of the Site and Services, to provide analytics, to store user preferences. Users may use our Services to, and to enable advertisement delivery, including behavioral advertising, but must follow our Terms of Service. We use both session and persistent cookies on the Site and may use “essential cookies” to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of customer accounts.

You have the option to decline to have cookies placed on your computer and other digital devices. If you would like to delete cookies or instruct your Web browser to delete or refuse cookies, please visit the help pages of your Web browser. Please note, however, that if you delete cookies or refuse to accept them, you might not be able to use all of the features we offer, you may not be able to store your preferences, and some of our Site’s pages might not display properly.

Customers using our Services to develop and serve creatives and other interactive advertisements served by us have the option to place certain limited-use cookies on your digital devices. These cookies generally limit the number of and restrict the repetitive serving of advertisements to their website visitors. We do not create or place such cookies, instead of leaving it to the business judgment of our Services users. Please refer to the privacy policies of the applicable website or digital provider to determine whether our customers deploy cookies for their users and others viewing or interacting with creatives developed using our Services. You may be able to opt out of some advertising practices by visiting the following links: Network Advertising Initiative, Omniture, Digital Advertising Alliance and PrivacyChoice. Please note that opting out does not prevent the display of all ads to you.
